# Scientology: A History of Man

*A History of Man* (originally published 1952 as *What to Audit*) is [[l-ron-hubbard|L. Ron Hubbard's]] manual for **Technique 88** — the auditing procedure that addresses the theta being (the [[thetan|thetan]]) directly and traverses the **whole track**: the full 76 trillion years of a thetan's spiritual history. It is a companion volume to *Electropsychometric Auditing* and the *Individual Track Map* (the latter contained in the *Technique 88* lecture package), and is explicitly prerequisite to *Scientology 8-80*.

The book opens: **"A cold-blooded and factual account of your last 76 trillion years."**

## Overview and Thesis

The central claim: prior Dianetics audited only **current-lifetime** incidents (engrams from birth to present). Technique 88 unlocks a vastly more powerful procedure by auditing the **genetic entity** (the body's evolutionary track) and, most importantly, the **theta being** itself — whose track goes back 76 trillion years and whose accumulated incidents are responsible for most human aberration, illness, and spiritual limitation that current-lifetime auditing cannot reach.

Hubbard presents the contents as research findings verified through extensive E-Meter testing across many preclears, not as theology or belief: "I am not asking you to accept these incidents as true. The E-Meter says they exist."

## The Four Auditing Targets

Technique 88 addresses four distinct entities that are "in" every human being:

1. **Cellular injuries** — straightforward physical-body trauma; the most accessible target.
2. **The Genetic Entity (GE)** — the "somatic mind"; a composite of all cellular experience through the evolutionary line; carries the body's evolutionary history as a series of auditable incidents (see [[genetic-entity|Genetic Entity]]).
3. **Injected Entities** — synthetic "souls" packed into the preclear by electronics on the whole track; origin of "demon circuits" in the mind; generally do not need to be audited.
4. **The Theta Being (Thetan)** — the principal target; the "real I"; the [[thetan|thetan]] itself; its track goes back 76 trillion years.

The [[genetic-entity|Genetic Entity]] carries the complete evolutionary history of the body, not as biology but as a sequence of **traumatic incidents** that can be contacted and run on the E-Meter. Key incidents in the genetic line (each named for the creature or event involved):

The GE is **not** the "I" of the person — it is "the 'I' of the body," cowardly, stimulus-response, without higher goals. It enters protoplasm approximately two days before conception and remains with the body to its death.

## The Theta Line — 76 Trillion Years of Thetan History

The theta being's track predates the MEST universe. Hubbard describes several major **eras** and **types of incident** on the whole track:

### Separation and Entrapment
The thetan originally existed as part of a "Main Body of Theta," then individuated, created its own universes, and was progressively entrapped by invader forces using electronics. The whole track consists of **spirals** — the first beginning ~76 trillion years ago, each subsequent spiral shorter; the current spiral began approximately 40,000 years ago.

### Invader Forces
A recurring cosmological theme: various waves of **invader forces** — beings from heavy-gravity planets who developed electronics and used it to conquer and enslave. Invaders created most of the theta-line incidents through deliberate control technology. The **Fourth Invader** is specifically named as the creator of Facsimile One.

### Specific Named Incidents on the Theta Line

#### Chapter 9 Incidents

**Jack-in-the-Box**: A theta trap that opens unexpectedly, shocking the theta being; a facsimile trap. The basic "you looked and got caught" pattern.

**Obsession**: Electronic invalidation that forces the thetan to need facsimiles; origin of the addictive need for memory-pictures.

**Borrowing**: A retractor beam that steals facsimiles from another thetan's bank. Borrowed facsimiles show up on the E-Meter but "evaporate" when identified as borrowed.

**Nipping**: Two thetans clash with energy streams; one kills the other's MEST body. Source of tinnitus (ringing in ears).

**Blanketing**: A theta being envelops another — for emotional contact, sexual satisfaction, or energy absorption. Source of overwhelming emotional states. Running Blanketing incidents shows the preclear "once and for all that he is a theta being without a body."

**The Halver**: A device that projects half-light on one side, half-dark on the other, with a devil on one side and angels on the other — "rigged up with religious symbols." Effect: simultaneous sexual compulsion and religious compulsion, pitting the two against each other. Used as a community control mechanism. "It gave him a conflict, one side with the other, one being good, the other being bad." Origin, according to Hubbard, of the human pattern of oscillating between religious devotion and indulgence.

**Facsimile One (Fac One)**: The "first proven-up whole track incident" — first verified to produce measurable results when audited out across many preclears (eradicating asthma, sinus trouble, chronic chills). Created by the **Fourth Invader** ~1 million years ago. The "Coffee-Grinder" device emits a push-pull electronic wave ("baps") that: (a) knocks out the pineal gland's potential and deranges the glandular system; (b) installs a "summons to court" implant via the vagus nerve, explaining anxiety at legal threat and the "inexplicable collapse" of criminals upon arrest. Restimulated by pneumatic drills; attributed to Fac One (not silicosis) the mortality of workers using them. Replaced by the Halver ~100,000 years ago as "more efficient."

**Before Earth**: Incidents of the preclear being summoned before a council, condemned, and sentenced to be transported to Earth — not for wrongdoing but as recruitment. Designed to reduce the preclear to a more obedient colonist by degrading them down the Tone Scale.

**The Joiners**: Three types of incidents in which "souls" are packed together by electronics. Origin of injected entities and "demon circuits." Entities from these incidents convince the thetan it has done wrong; insane people are found to "run on their entities, not their thetans."

**The Ice Cube**: Method of transporting beings — frozen in ice, taken by saucer to a new area, dumped in the ocean. Explains chronically cold hands and feet. Two separate invader crews may be involved: one planting beings via Ice Cube, another operating a pre-existing between-lives station who then intercepts those beings.

**Between-Lives**: At death, the theta being leaves the body and goes to a **Between-Lives area**, where it is given a strong forgetter implant (spinning wheel of pictures; force screen; mirror arrangement) designed to erase past-life identity. "The report area for most has been Mars. Some women report to stations elsewhere in the solar system. The last Martian report station on Earth was established in the Pyrenees." (See [[between-lives|Between-Lives]])

**The Emanator**: A large, glowing radioactive body hanging in the air — "a sort of a god, an all-knower." Used as a recruiting trap: preclear enters out of curiosity, is coerced into agreeing to come to Earth, told his original body is "safe." When the original body disintegrates after thousands of years and the preclear finds it gone between-lives, he becomes "lost" and "homeless" — explaining a category of psychiatric presentation.

**The Double-Body**: Having a trance body preserved in pawn elsewhere while living on Earth. During operations or accidents, the thetan may switch to the other body. Explains what surgeons observe as near-death experiences and what patients report as learning "the secret of the Universe" — they have briefly been in the between-lives area and seen the implant apparatus.

**Theta Traps**: General category — mechanisms for reducing thetans from knowing beings to colonists/slaves: curiosity traps, awards/prizes (which are implants), retractor screens, ornate buildings. "All theta traps have one thing in common: They use electronic force to knock the thetan into forgetting, into unknowingness."

**Body Builder incidents**: After capture in a theta trap, the thetan was subjected to a series of devices to mold it into a body and reduce it to a stimulus-response mechanism:

- *Jiggler* — moved up/down eccentrically; thetan identifies with the post
- *Whirler* — spun left and right
- *Bouncer* — bounced until fixed on time track
- *Spinner* — chair device; probable source of "spinning" as slang for insanity
- *Rocker* — teetered left/right; "dramatized today by mystics" (rocking prayer)
- *Boxer* — hit from all angles to make the thetan a stimulus-response mechanism: "This is actually aberration itself. It is the psychologist's definition, though vaguely put, of a 'well-adjusted human being' — one who stimulates and responds without thought."
- *Faller* — installs fear of falling
- *Education* — hypnotic, stimulus-response instruction: "It is dramatized today in universities as it requires no skilled instruction."
- *Fly-Trap* — gummy material; thetan fights it, taken out by "do-gooders trained in religious sweetness and syrup." "Sunday school sometimes brings this, even as a second facsimile, into heavy restimulation."

### Supplement Incidents (from *Scientology 8-80*)
The Supplement section was added editorially after the book's initial publication, drawing on discoveries from *Scientology 8-80*:

- **Glare Fights**: Very early; two thetans contest using electrical energy
- **Mirrors**: Defense — energy reflected back at the attacking thetan
- **Exploding Facsimiles**: Death shock causes facsimiles to explode; explains anxiety stomachs
- **The Capper**: Electronic cap placed on a thetan; forces it to have facsimiles; deranges self-determinism
- **The Prover**: Group demands new thetan prove something; immediately invalidates the proof; drives thetan down the Tone Scale
- **The Iron Maiden**: 2–3 thousand years ago; magnetic shell; causes left/right directional confusion; "sexual and religious connotations"; "makes one think he must be dual to survive"
- **Theta Trap Posts**: A large magnet; thetan fights it, draws closer, eventually blankets it; every energy flow thereafter is turned back on the thetan; "probably a basic on the overt act phenomenon"; explains fear of anger and the experience of emotional "kickback"
- **The Parity**: Thetan made to sit opposite a mirror-image of itself; metal beam in both mouths; any activity triggers energy interchange confirming the overt-act mechanism; "peculiarly restimulated by marriage"

## The Transfer (Chapter 11)

**The Transfer** is described as "the single most important phase of Technique 88." It is the thetan's movement from outside the MEST body into it.

The thetan's positions:
- **Best**: Entirely outside and independent of any body
- **Second-best**: Just outside the body, monitoring it "at arm's length"
- **Worst**: Inside the body (which is to say, degraded to the level of an entity)

Most thetans are positioned "behind and above" the body. Cases where the thetan is in *front* of the body produce left/right reversal.

Types of Transfer:
- **Temporary Transfer**: Thetan does a DED (unjustified harm to another), catches the sorrow-waves of the body it wronged, merges temporarily in guilt/sympathy
- **Full Transfer**: Recurring; not permanent until the thetan fully merges with a body
- **Permanent Transfer**: The end of the thetan — GE and entities thereafter run the body; the thetan is essentially extinguished
- **Half-Transfer**: Thetan believes it can only run a MEST body; hasn't gone fully in but has degraded to that belief-level
- **Switch Transfer**: Thetan switches control to a person threatening "its" property
- **Control Transfer**: Thetan extends control to the environment and others; loses control gradually, interprets each loss as permanent reduction in ability

The reason thetans fixated on bodies: **boredom**. "One of the reasons he fixated on a MEST body was because he was terribly bored." Being immortal but unable to achieve its original goals, the thetan found MEST bodies attractive because they *change and die* — providing novelty. This set in motion the Permanent Transfer.

## Generalized Incidents (Chapter 10)

Key terminology for incident patterns:

- **Motivator**: An incident happening to the preclear that it dramatizes
- **Overt Act**: An incident the preclear does to another dynamic
- **DED** ("Deserved Every Darned Bit" — the word is described as a sarcasm): An unjustified harm the preclear does to another for which it has no motivator; must be post-hoc justified
- **DEDEX** (DED Exposed): An incident that happens to the preclear *after* a DED, on the same subject; disproportionate victimhood claims mask the underlying DED
- **Misassist**: Failed attempt to help another dynamic; highly aberrative
- **Degrader**: Chain of incidents where a low-toned person reduces a higher-toned person in order to then "help" them

## Key Anti-Religious/Anti-Mystical Statements

*A History of Man* contains some of Hubbard's sharpest attacks on established religion and mysticism:

> "India and 'join Nirvana' have given us 'techniques' WHICH ARE GUARANTEED TO GLUE A THETAN TO A BODY AS THOUGH RIVETED AND TIED WITH IRON BANDS. So beware of mysticism and its techniques, yogism and its nonsense."

On the social-control function of religious implants:
> "The Halver was rigged up with religious symbols... It gave him sexual compulsion all mixed up with religious compulsion so that an overdose of indulgence would send him to church, sometimes into a life of crime. It was a control factor used to keep the community fighting itself."

On the Rocker device: practiced today by "mystics who, not being quite low enough on the scale to die, finish the job by picking up old electronic implant motions." The Fly-Trap's do-gooders practice "religious sweetness and syrup." The Education device is "dramatized today in universities."

On electronics and social control: "Electronics alone can make a truly slave society." A handful of electronics experts "could bring entire nations into their command" — presented as a factual claim about invader capabilities, not a metaphor.

## Canon and Reception

Within Scientology, *A History of Man* holds **authoritative** status — it is part of the technical canon (the "Tech") and is cited across the Bridge as a foundational whole-track reference. The incidents described are treated as real events, not allegories.

**Critical reception**: The book has been widely cited by critics of Scientology as exemplary of Hubbard's cosmology in its most unguarded form. The claims about Mars, between-lives implant stations, invader electronics, and the Clam incident have been extensively satirized. No independent evidence supports any of the historical or cosmological claims.
