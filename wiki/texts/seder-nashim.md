**Seder Nashim** (Hebrew: סדר נשים, "Order of Women") is the third order of the [[mishna|Mishna]] and the [[talmud-bavli|Talmud]]. It deals comprehensively with Jewish family law, including betrothal, marriage, divorce, and the vows that affect familial relationships. 

It is highly dialectical and widely studied in yeshivas. All tractates in Nashim have Babylonian Gemara.

## Tractates of Nashim

1. **Yevamot (Sisters-in-law):**
   - *Subject:* The complex laws of *Yibbum* (Levirate marriage), where a man must marry his deceased childless brother's widow, and *Chalitzah*, the ceremony releasing them from this obligation. Also covers issues of prohibited marriages and Jewish lineage.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (122 folios).
2. **Ketubot (Marriage Contracts):**
   - *Subject:* Often called the "miniature Talmud" due to its breadth. It deals with the *ketubah* (marriage contract), financial obligations within marriage, dowries, the obligations of a husband to his wife, and seduction/rape.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (112 folios).
3. **Nedarim (Vows):**
   - *Subject:* The formulation, binding nature, and annulment of personal vows (*nedarim*). Included in Nashim because a husband or father has the right to annul certain vows made by his wife or daughter.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (91 folios).
4. **Nazir (Nazirite):**
   - *Subject:* The laws of the Nazirite vow, in which a person abstains from grape products, cutting hair, and contact with corpses for a specified period.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (66 folios).
5. **Sotah (Suspected Adulteress):**
   - *Subject:* The Temple ritual of the *bitter waters* administered to a woman suspected by her husband of adultery (Numbers 5). It also discusses liturgy recited in various languages and the consequences of sin.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (49 folios).
6. **Gittin (Divorce Documents):**
   - *Subject:* The stringent laws surrounding the writing, delivery, and validity of a *Get* (bill of divorce). It addresses complex issues of agency and intent in severing a marriage.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (90 folios).
7. **Kiddushin (Betrothal):**
   - *Subject:* The process of betrothal (*erusin* / *kiddushin*), how marriage is legally effected, the status of offspring from various unions, and the genealogical classes within Israel.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (82 folios).
