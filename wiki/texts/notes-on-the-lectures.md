**Notes on the Lectures of L. Ron Hubbard** is a compilation, made in 1950 by the staff of the **Hubbard
Dianetic Research Foundation** of California, of notes from **[[l-ron-hubbard|L. Ron Hubbard's]]** earliest
public lecture series — the **Los Angeles Lecture Series (20 Nov–1 Dec 1950)** and the Oakland series. First
published in 1951 (and reissued by the Church of [[scientology|Scientology]] with a Scientology framing), it
is one of the **founding documents of the Dianetics movement** — distinct from the books already in the wiki
([[dianetics|*Dianetics*]], [[science-of-survival|*Science of Survival*]], and
[[scientology-fundamentals-of-thought|*Fundamentals of Thought*]]).

## Place in the Corpus

The book captures Dianetics **in its first months as a public movement** (mid-1950, just after the May 1950
release of *Dianetics: The Modern Science of Mental Health*), in the spoken, lecture-hall form — Hubbard
working out and demonstrating his system before audiences and fielding questions. It therefore preserves the
**earliest articulation** of several ideas that later became central Scientology doctrine, and predates the
"thetan"/Scientology vocabulary almost entirely (the word *engram* appears hundreds of times; *thetan*
barely at all — a 1968 editorial overlay aside).

## Contents and Key Themes

The lectures, as compiled, move through:

- **Thought, Life, and the Material Universe** and **The Spectrum of Logic** — Hubbard's framing of the mind
  and of "infinity-valued logic" against absolutes.
- **The [[tone-scale|Tone Scale]]** — the emotional spectrum (here in its early Dianetics form, the
  predecessor of the *Science of Survival* Chart of Human Evaluation) and how a case's position on it governs
  behavior and survival.
- **[[arc-triangle|Affinity, Reality and Communication (ARC)]]** — presented as a mutually-dependent triangle
  (raising one raises the others), one of the lectures' central models.
- **The [[engram|engram]] and the reactive mind** — engrams as "fixed data" recorded in moments of pain/
  unconsciousness, the **reactive mind**, **circuits**, **valences**, **chronic somatics**, "the track,"
  locks, and "straight memory."
- **The [[eight-dynamics|Dynamics]]** (here the earlier set), with remarks on groups.
- **Auditing in practice** — accessibility, "stalled cases," different types of cases and methods, and the
  **Auditor's Code** and standard procedure.

## Significance

As a record of the **1950 lecture period**, *Notes on the Lectures* is valuable both to the Church (as early
source teaching) and to historians and critics (as evidence of how rapidly Hubbard was systematizing
Dianetics, and of the doctrine *before* the 1952 shift to Scientology and the thetan). It rounds out the
wiki's primary-source coverage of the foundational Hubbard corpus.
