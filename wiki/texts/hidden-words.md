# Hidden Words

*Populated from [[peter-smith|Smith, A Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith (2000)]]. All claims are from that source unless marked otherwise.*

## Description

The *Hidden Words* (*Kalimát-i-Maknúnih*) is a collection of Arabic and Persian verses by [[bahaullah|Bahá'u'lláh]], composed in Baghdad in 1274 AH (c.1857–8 CE) (line 16641).

## Identification and Framing

Bahá'u'lláh identified the text with the "Hidden Book of Fátima," which in Shí'í tradition had been addressed to the Prophet's daughter to console her after her father's death (lines 16643–16648). The work is presented as the "inner essence" of what had been revealed by the prophets of the past, "as a token of grace to the righteous," that they might be faithful to God's Covenant, live according to his Will, and obtain "the gem of Divine virtue" (lines 16649–16656).

## Summary of Content

[[shoghi-effendi|Shoghi Effendi]] described the text as "God's most precious gift to man" (line 16657, paraphrase; OCR damaged). Its ethical themes include (lines 16595–16678):

- Freeing oneself from the "prison" of self and the "fetters" of worldly attachments
- Bringing oneself to account each day
- Sorrowing only at separation from God; not fearing death
- Following divine law; submitting to God; accepting trials for God's sake
- God assures the reader of his love; human beings are noble and should cleanse their hearts to find the divine light within themselves
- The human heart is God's home and the human spirit his place of revelation
- Bahá'ís should not breathe the dust of others, vaunt themselves over the poor, commit iniquity
- They should eschew covetousness, envy, and malice; be forbearing with others
- They should avoid fellowship with the ungodly; distinguish themselves by their deeds
- They should "plant only the rose of love" in the garden of the heart; work to support themselves
- A "new garden" has appeared, inhabited by heavenly beings
- They should seek to learn the mysteries of divine love and wisdom; God is always close to them
- They should seize their chance before the "fleeting moment" of their lives is ended

## Significance

The *Hidden Words* is among Bahá'u'lláh's most widely circulated and beloved works. Together with the *Kitáb-i-Íqán* and the *Kitáb-i-Aqdas*, it is one of the core texts of the Bahá'í Faith. Shoghi Effendi produced an early English translation in collaboration with others.

## Canon and Reception

Canonical for Bahá'ís worldwide. The text is used extensively in devotional programs, the [[nineteen-day-feast|Nineteen Day Feast]], and Bahá'í holy day observances.
