**Seder Nezikin** (Hebrew: סדר נזיקין, "Order of Damages") is the fourth order of the [[mishna|Mishna]] and the [[talmud-bavli|Talmud]]. It is the bedrock of Jewish civil and criminal jurisprudence, detailing property law, torts, contracts, the court system, and penal law.

Nezikin is the most heavily studied order in the traditional Yeshiva curriculum due to its rigorous legal logic.

## Tractates of Nezikin

The first three tractates (the "Bavas") were originally a single massive tractate encompassing all civil law before being split due to size.

1. **Bava Kamma (First Gate):**
   - *Subject:* Civil matters involving damages and torts. It categorizes the primary causes of damage (e.g., the ox, the pit, the fire) and deals with personal injury and theft.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (119 folios).
2. **Bava Metzia (Middle Gate):**
   - *Subject:* Property law focusing on found objects, bailments, hiring workers, renting property, and stringent laws against usury/interest (*Ribbit*).
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (119 folios).
3. **Bava Batra (Last Gate):**
   - *Subject:* Real estate law, property ownership rights, zoning, partnership divisions, sales guarantees, and the laws of inheritance.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (176 folios — the longest tractate).
4. **Sanhedrin (Courts):**
   - *Subject:* The structure and jurisdiction of the Jewish court system (the Sanhedrin), rules of evidence and witness testimony, and the administration of criminal penalties (including capital punishment).
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (113 folios).
5. **Makkot (Lashes):**
   - *Subject:* A continuation of Sanhedrin. It deals with the punishment of lashes, the laws of conspiring false witnesses (*Edim Zomemim*), and the cities of refuge for accidental murderers.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (24 folios).
6. **Shevuot (Oaths):**
   - *Subject:* Oaths taken in a judicial context to resolve financial disputes, personal oaths, and the sin offerings brought for false or forgotten oaths.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (49 folios).
7. **Eduyot (Testimonies):**
   - *Subject:* A collection of legal testimonies delivered by later sages recording earlier rulings, unique for being organized by the sage who transmitted the law rather than by subject matter.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
8. **Avodah Zarah (Foreign Worship):**
   - *Subject:* Laws regulating interactions, commerce, and relationships between Jews and idolaters. It seeks to prevent Jews from inadvertently supporting or participating in pagan practices.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (76 folios).
9. **Avot (Fathers):**
   - *Subject:* Also known as *Pirkei Avot* (Ethics of the Fathers). A purely ethical and historical tractate tracing the transmission of the [[oral-torah|Oral Torah]] from Sinai and recording the moral maxims of the Tannaim.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No (as it contains no Halacha to dialectically dissect).
10. **Horayot (Decisions):**
    - *Subject:* The procedures and sin-offerings required when the High Court (Sanhedrin) or the King issues an erroneous ruling that causes the public to sin.
    - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (14 folios).
