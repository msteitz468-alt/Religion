# Scientology 8-8008

**Scientology 8-8008** is a doctrinal textbook authored by [[l-ron-hubbard|L. Ron Hubbard]] in 1952, serving as the required text for the **Philadelphia Doctorate Course** (PDC), which Hubbard delivered in Philadelphia in November–December 1952. The book presents the most comprehensive systematic statement of Hubbard's cosmological and soteriological doctrine prior to the OT materials.

## Canon and Reception

Canonical within Scientology. Part of the foundational "Bridge" sequence of Hubbard's writings. The Church maintains it as required reading for advanced training, particularly as the PDC companion text.

## Title Meaning

The title encodes the book's central goal:

> "The attainment of infinity (8) by the reduction of the MEST universe's apparent infinity (8) to zero (0) and the increase of the zero (0) of one's own universe to an infinity (8) of one's own universe."

Infinity placed on its side makes the numeral 8. The formula describes a double transformation: the apparent vastness and authority of the physical universe (MEST = Matter, Energy, Space, Time) reduced to nothing for the individual, while the individual's own creative universe — originally reduced to nothing — is expanded to infinity.

## Structure

The book is divided into two parts:

**Book One** (original 1952 text) — the systematic doctrinal statement, covering the theory of the thetan, the MEST universe, the ARC Triangle, the Tone Scale, processing methods, and the Standard Operating Procedures (SOP 3).

**Book Two** (1953 supplementary material, originally separate) — The Factors, Viewpoints and Dimension Points, Universes, Behavior of Universes, Terminals, Logic, Assists, Communication, and SOP 8 with appendices.

## Doctrinal Overview

### The Four Parts of Man

Hubbard presents the human being as a composite of four elements:
1. **The thetan** — the immortal true self; "awareness of awareness"; the actual individual
2. **The standard memory banks** — the analytical mind's storage
3. **The genetic entity** — the entity that built and animates the MEST body across the evolutionary line; stimulus-response; "cowardly, a thing of stimulus-response, without further will or goals than to grow a body"
4. **The MEST body** — "a carbon-oxygen engine built of complex electronic ridges around the genetic entity"

The thetan is the only true individual. One does not "have" a thetan; one *is* the thetan.

### The Theta-MEST Theory

The universe is composed of two types of substance: **theta** (the spiritual, life energy) and **MEST** (the physical universe). The thetan was originally godlike — capable of creating space, energy, and matter by postulate alone, and able to form its own universe. Through a long-term process of agreement with the MEST universe, the thetan progressively surrendered its creative powers, accepting the MEST universe as the only reality, and became trapped in bodies.

The MEST universe is described as:
- An **agreed-upon illusion** — not independently real but sustained by the collective agreement of thetans
- A **"police universe"** — operating on force and demanding compliance on pain of extermination
- A **major expanding trap** — designed to capture and hold thetans in matter indefinitely
- The product of thetans who forgot they created it: "Every planet in the MEST universe was once one or more people" (stated jocularly)

The goal of Scientology processing is to reverse this entrapment: to rehabilitate the thetan's original creative abilities, enable exteriorization from the body, and eventually attain the state of **Cleared Theta Clear**.

### The Eight Dynamics

The eight "urges to survive" expanding from self to infinity — covered more extensively in earlier works but systematically applied throughout 8-8008 as the organizing framework for processing assessment. See [[eight-dynamics|Eight Dynamics]].

### The Tone Scale

The emotional-behavioral scale ranges from -8.0 (body death and below, for the thetan) through 0.0 to 4.0 (the Homo sapiens range) to 40.0 (the thetan's optimal range). See [[tone-scale|Tone Scale]].

8-8008 adds nuance not in earlier works: the **two-position account**. Every person has *two* positions on the Tone Scale simultaneously:
- The **Body-Plus-Thetan position** (0.0–4.0): determined by social training and education; a stimulus-response scale; "false tone"
- The **actual thetan position** (Sub-zero to 40.0): the thetan's true condition beneath the social veneer

Most people's thetans are in fact at sub-zero — below body death — though their social training masks this. The course of auditing first drops the preclear from the false Body-Plus-Thetan tone to the actual thetan tone (which can appear as decompensation or irrationality), then raises the thetan back up through the full range. A "raving mad thetan" is, in Hubbard's formulation, "far more sane than a normal human being."

### The ARC Triangle

**Affinity, Reality, and Communication** form the three vertices of a triangle in which each vertex is interdependent with the others. See [[arc-triangle|ARC Triangle]].

8-8008's distinctive contribution: Communication is declared **by far the most important** of the three — "thousands of percent the senior in importance to affinity and reality." Affinity is a type of energy. Reality is agreement. But Communication is "the heart of life" and the primary modus operandi. The sole accurate test of case improvement is a communication change (including perception change). "A thetan can be what he can see. He can see what he can be."

### The Anti-Nirvana Statement

One of the book's most theologically striking passages (Chapter 5, "Identity versus Individuality"):

> "There is evidently no Nirvana. It is the feeling that one will 'merge' and lose his own individuality that restrains the thetan from attempting to remedy his lot."

This explicitly rejects Hindu and Buddhist dissolution-of-self soteriologies. The goal of Scientology is not merger with a greater whole (Brahman, nirvana, the Godhead) but the *rehabilitation of the individual thetan's own creative powers*. Identity, individuality, and the ability to create one's own universe are the highest attainable states.

### The Critique of Existing Religion

8-8008 contains Hubbard's most explicit theological critique of major world religions:

- **Hinduism**: "The Hindu sought to depart into his Nirvana by refusing to have anything to do with having. He sought thus to promote himself into *eng* [Chinese: emptiness]. He saw that as long as he retained a grasp on a body in any degree, he was having and thus was pressed into doing." Hubbard treats this as a misdirected insight that compounds the problem.
- **Indian mysticism generally**: Described as "booby-trapped" — containing essential truths alongside directions "certain to send the experimenter even more deeply into the unwanted state of becoming MEST." Mysticism's effect is "to deliver an entirely opposite result to any experimenter luckless enough to hope to reach cause by becoming an effect."
- **Conventional monotheism**: Hubbard attacks the conception of an anthropomorphic God: "More modern Man has fallen into the error of making God into the body of a Homo sapiens and posting him somewhere on high with a craving for vengeance and a pettiness in punishment matched only by the degradation of Homo sapiens himself." He contrasts this with his own claim: "There are Gods above all other Gods and Gods beyond the Gods of universes. But it were better, far better, to be a raving madman in his cell, than to be a thing with the ego, cruelty and jealous lust that base religions have set up to make men grovel down."
- **Logic and epistemology**: Described as "at best, rationalization" — an aberrative mechanism by which immortal beings are manipulated into agreeing they might die. "Anything which has wide acceptance and has been successful, wherever suns shine and planets swing, is based upon some fundamental truth. There is no argument here against the existence of a Supreme Being or any devaluation intended."

### "Deeply Religious Feelings"

Hubbard explains religious devotion psychologically within the framework: "A considerable reaction can be got from a preclear by making him conceive a feeling of devotion toward the 'older gods' who were here and who built this universe and who have left it to him. Deeply religious feelings are very often based upon this idea." Religious experience is here reinterpreted as an encounter with the track-memory of the thetan's own past creations and participation in the MEST universe.

### The Thetan's Cosmological History

The thetan has existed for **76 trillion years**. In its original state it was godlike — able to create space, energy, and universes by mere postulate. Over successive "spirals" (cycles of cosmic history), through a gradient of minor agreements, it progressively surrendered its creative power and became trapped in the MEST universe and in bodies. The current spiral is approximately 40,000 years. Each human being carries this entire history as facsimiles.

### States of Attainment

8-8008 specifies a ladder of attainment not present in earlier works:

- **Theta Exterior**: A thetan that is clear of its body and knows it, but not yet stable outside.
- **Theta Clear**: A being who is "reasonably stable outside the body and does not come back into the body simply because the body is hurt. No other condition is necessary."
- **Cleared Theta Clear**: A thetan who is "completely rehabilitated and can do everything a thetan should do, such as move MEST and control others from a distance, or create his own universe." After approximately 50 hours of Creative Processing, the Cleared Theta Clear "can, without the aid of a body, move objects, heal at a distance and do a thousand other 'interesting tricks' which could very well be viewed with considerable awe. For they have not been seen on Earth in recorded history, but have lived in legend."
- **Operating Thetan**: The goal of SOP 8 (Standard Operating Procedure 8); a higher goal than the Theta Clear. Defined as a thetan able to manufacture particles of admiration and force in abundance and to use theta perception without a body.

## Key Doctrinal Statements (Glossary)

8-8008 includes an unusually comprehensive in-text glossary (Chapter 27, SOP 3 definitions section):

- **Thetan**: "The beingness of the individual, the awareness of awareness unit, that quantity and identity which *is* the preclear. One does not speak of 'my thetan' any more than he would speak of 'my me.'"
- **Illusion**: "Any idea, space, energy, object or time concept which one creates himself."
- **Reality**: "That agreement upon illusion which became the MEST universe."
- **Delusion**: "Things not of one's own creation or of the MEST universe which locate one in time and space."
- **Certainty**: Tripartite — certain it is one's own illusion (highest); certain it is MEST reality; certain it is delusion.
- **Scientology**: "The science of knowing how to know."
- **Freedom**: "Ability to create and position energy or matter in time and space."
- **Slavery**: "Being positioned in another's time and space."
- **The Human Body**: "A carbon-oxygen engine built of complex electronic ridges around the genetic entity which animates it."
- **The Human Mind**: "The thetan plus the standard memory banks."
- **The Reactive Mind**: "The ridge automatic response system."
- **The Somatic Mind**: "The genetic entity plus the brain system of the body."
- **Mysticism**: "Many right ideas but the wrong way to go about it."
- **The Human Soul**: [defined only as "the preclear"]
- **Astral Bodies**: "Somebody's delusion."

## The Chart of Attitudes

A two-column chart mapping the optimal (27.0–40.0) and low-end (0.0) values for 16 dimensions of being. Upper/lower poles:

1. Survive / Dead
2. Right / Wrong
3. Fully Responsible / No Responsibility
4. Owns All / Owns Nothing
5. Everyone / Nobody
6. Always / Never
7. Motion Source / Stopped
8. Truth / Hallucination
9. Faith / Distrust
10. I Know / I Know Not
11. Cause / Full Effect
12. I Am / I Am Not
13. Win / Lose
14. Start / Stop
15. Differences / Identification
16. Being / Had

These serve as the basis for **Rising Scale Processing** — the auditor identifies where on each column the preclear believes themselves to be, then raises their postulate incrementally toward the optimum.

## Processing Methods

### Creative Processing

The primary processing method of 8-8008. The preclear creates mental **mock-ups** (illusions, images) and manipulates them in space and time — moving them near and far, changing their size, color, and nature, making them "disobey MEST laws," creating and destroying them. The goal is to rehabilitate the thetan's ability to create its own illusions, which in turn rehabilitates its perception of the MEST universe. Three categories:

- **Can't List** — things the preclear is unable to start/change/stop/create/alter/destroy on each Dynamic
- **Must List** — enforcements (what must be done to or by the preclear)
- **Desires** — cravings for sensation or identification that drew the preclear into MEST agreements

### Postulate Processing

Based on the principle that energy is produced by the thetan by postulating it into existence. The thetan's current weakness results from accumulated reverse postulates (counter-postulates made for "randomity"). Rising Scale Processing is a form of Postulate Processing: the preclear shifts their postulate upward on each column of the Chart of Attitudes.

### Standard Operating Procedure Issue 3 (SOP 3) — Book One

Seven steps from simplest to most difficult case, beginning with Step I (exteriorization by direct command: "Be three feet back of your head") down to Step VII (locate a part of one's body and recognize it as such). The auditor begins at Step I and descends until finding a step the preclear can perform.

### Standard Operating Procedure 8 (SOP 8) — Book Two

Advanced procedure with the goal of Operating Thetan. Seven steps with more elaborate procedures including **Spacation** (creating one's own space with eight anchor points), **Expanded GITA** (Give/Invest/Take/Accept Processing using a list of approximately 200 items ranging from "Viewpoint, Work, Pain" to "Christ, Faith, God, the Devil, Homosexuality, Eating Human Bodies"), **Present Time Differentiation**, and imitation of things.

### The Dichotomies (Book Two)

Twenty-nine paired dichotomies used for AC (alternating current) flow running, producing energy through "difference of potential." Examples: Survive/Succumb, Affinity/No affinity, Agreement/Disagree, Responsible/Not responsible, Faith/Distrust, I Am/I Am Not, Sane/Insane, Sympathy/Propitiation. The preclear alternately flows feeling-thoughts of each dichotomy pair until the flow "crackles."

## The Factors (Book Two)

A cosmological prose poem in 30 numbered "factors," dated April 23, 1953, "humbly tendered as a gift to Man." Key statements:

- "Before the beginning was a Cause and the entire purpose of the Cause was the creation of effect."
- "In the beginning and forever is the decision and the decision is TO BE."
- "The first action of beingness is to assume a viewpoint."
- "Thus there is space created, for the definition of space is: viewpoint of dimension."
- "And thus there is LIGHT. And thus there is energy. And thus there is life."
- "And the viewpoint can never perish; but the form can perish."
- "There are universes. The universes, then, are three in number: the universe created by one viewpoint, the universe created by every other viewpoint, the universe created by the mutual action of viewpoints which is agreed to be upheld — the physical universe."
- "Thus comes about death."
- "There is beingness, but Man believes there is only becomingness."
- "The particle of admiration is best of all."

## Hermeneutical Notes

8-8008 is a **primary doctrinal text**, not a commentary tradition. Hubbard's hermeneutical stance is anti-interpretive: the text is to be applied exactly as written, not interpreted. This is consistent with the general Scientological position on "Source." The book's own Scientology 8-8008 self-description confirms this: "It works only when used as stated. Disorganized fragments of this material, given other names and emphases, may be found to be harmful."

The text interacts with and explicitly critiques Hindu non-dualism, Buddhist nirvana, Christian monotheism, and classical Western logic/epistemology — positioning Scientology not as heir to any of these but as a superior alternative that reveals where they each went wrong.

## Interpretive Cruxes

1. **The "no Nirvana" statement** (Ch. 5) — Hubbard's explicit rejection of Hindu/Buddhist merger soteriology; represents the clearest statement that Scientology is not, as sometimes claimed, a synthesis of Eastern traditions.
2. **The "Gods above Gods" passage** (Ch. 18) — Hubbard simultaneously claims to believe in a "Supreme Being" while attacking anthropomorphic God-concepts; the precise theological status of the divine in Scientology remains deliberately ambiguous.
3. **The relationship between Theta Clear and pre-existing paranormal claims** — Hubbard's promise that a Cleared Theta Clear can "move objects, heal at a distance, and do a thousand other 'interesting tricks' which could very well be viewed with considerable awe" — these claims constitute a form of magical/paranormal promise that has not been verified and is a perennial point of controversy.
4. **The MEST universe as illusion** — Hubbard's idealist metaphysics is internally consistent but structurally similar to the Advaita Vedanta māyā doctrine he explicitly critiques; the relationship between these systems is a potential comparison page.
5. **"Deeply religious feelings"** as track-memory — Hubbard's psychological reduction of religious experience to facsimiles from the thetan's whole track removes any independent epistemological validity from religious experience.
