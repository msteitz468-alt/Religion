# Jap Sahib

A composition attributed to **[[guru-gobind-singh|Guru Gobind Singh]]**, opening the
[[dasam-granth|Dasam Granth]] and appointed for daily recitation after [[japji|Japji]] in the morning
[[nit-nem|Nit-nem]]. It comprises **199 brief stanzas** and is described as beginning "as an
invocation before battle"; it is an extended catalogue of the qualities and epithets of the divine,
its rhythm imitating sword-play (McLeod Appendix, agent 6 ~L766). It is to be distinguished from
[[japji|Japji]] — a different author, content, and function, despite the similar name.

Jap Sahib is one of the Dasam Granth portions universally treated as *gurbani* — recited in the
Nit-nem and during the consecration of [[amrit-sanskar|amrit initiation]] — and so falls on the
**authoritative** side of the [[dasam-granth|Dasam Granth's contested canon]].
