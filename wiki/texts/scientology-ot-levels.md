# Technical Bulletins of Dianetics and Scientology, Vol. XIV: The O.T. Levels

A 612-page compilation of [[l-ron-hubbard|L. Ron Hubbard]]'s **Hubbard Communications Office Bulletins (HCOBs)** and **HCO Policy Letters** constituting the official instructional framework for the advanced levels of [[scientology|Scientology]] — the **Operating Thetan (OT) Levels** and **Special Rundowns**. Published as Volume XIV of the Technical Bulletins series. Created as a PageMaker document (Aldus PageMaker 5.0; Acrobat PDF Writer 1.0 for Macintosh; creation date: 1 July 1995).

**Source note:** This volume assembles typed policy bulletins and checksheets for OT 1 through OT 8 and the major Special Rundowns. The "secret doctrine" handwritten materials themselves (the **"Data"** document with the Xenu narrative, the **Incident II** and **Incident I** sequence platens) are referenced in the checksheets but were distributed separately to students on a need-to-know basis; they do not appear as reproduced text in this volume. The narratological core (the Xenu/Galactic Confederacy cosmogony) is therefore only *alluded to* here, not stated directly, though its technical parameters (dates, volcanoes, "R6," body thetans) are extensively discussed.

**Textual provenance note (New OT 8):** The "New OT 8" section in this volume (lines 15695–16090 of the extracted text) is explicitly identified as a **debrief written from memory by a Free Zone practitioner** who attended the procedure on the *Freewinds* ship in 1991. It is **not** an LRH-authored original and explicitly breaks Church security ("I am breaking security as I disagree that this should only be released to an elite"). This makes it a **secondary source** for OT 8 content — potentially accurate but unverified against original materials.

## Canon and Reception

The OT levels are **canonical scripture** for both the [[church-of-scientology|Church of Scientology]] and [[free-zone-scientology|Free Zone Scientology]], though the Church regards the Free Zone's possession and use of these materials as a copyright violation and trademark infringement. The materials have been the subject of extensive litigation (see [[operating-thetan|Operating Thetan]] concept page for legal history).

Within the Church's hermeneutical framework (**the "Keeping Scientology Working" policy (HCO PL 7 Feb 1965)**), these materials must be applied **verbatim and exactly** — no interpretation, no deviation, no modification. This is the defining feature of Scientology's hermeneutics: an **anti-interpretive** reading mandate.

## Textual Provenance Notes

**GPM Platens (OT 2 and Clearing Course):** Pages 78–196 and 198–225 of the PDF are **blank** — only page numbers appear. These would contain the actual GPM item lists (the specific dichotomies for each of the 21 named GPMs and the Clearing Course items). The GPM material was deliberately excluded from this compilation. Only the structural descriptions and non-line-plot incident descriptions were captured.

**Handwritten Materials (OT 3):** The core "secret doctrine" handwritten documents referenced in the OT 3 checksheet — LRH Handwritten "Data" (the Xenu narrative), Incident II sequence, Incident I sequence, and List of Volcanoes — are **not reproduced as text** in this volume. Students received them separately as physical platens. The volume discusses the incidents' parameters and procedures in detail but does not reproduce the narrative itself.

**L Rundowns:** The L Rundowns section includes an editorial note stating that these materials are **reconstructions by former Church members** who delivered them before leaving the Church — not original documents from Flag Land Base. Their accuracy is described as high but unverified against originals. (Same applies to the New OT 8.)

**New OT 8:** Explicitly identified as a **Free Zone practitioner's memory reconstruction** from 1991 — not an LRH original. See original OT 8 (HCOB 1 Oct 1969) for the authentic Hubbard text.

## Key Theological Contributions

### OT 2 — Goals Problems Mass (GPMs)
Hubbard's cosmology of **implanted goals**: the reactive mind is structured not merely as traumatic engrams but as interlocking "goals-problems masses" — sequences of opposed goals that create irresolvable internal conflicts. The Clearing Course materials reveal these patterns as the product of a massive **galactic implant station** approximately 75 million years ago ("R6" or "CC materials"). See [[goals-problems-mass|Goals Problems Mass]].

### OT 3 — Body Thetans and the Xenu/Incident II Framework
The central cosmological teaching of Scientology. **[[body-thetans|Body thetans]]** (BTs) are defined in this volume as "a thetan who is stuck to another thetan or body but is not in control" (HCOB 5 Feb 1970, "Definitions, Sect. III"). A **cluster** is "a group of body thetans crushed or held together by some mutual bad experience." The OT III practitioner must audit out these accumulated BTs using two key incidents:

- **Incident II** ("Inc 2"): 75 million years ago — the R6 implant event; associated with volcanoes. The key document (LRH Handwritten "Data") is referenced in the checksheet but not reproduced in this volume. The parameters given in the HCOBs: "Inc 2 is R6 75 million years ago. … Only those in this Old Confederation have Inc 2 and R6." (HCOB 2 Oct 1968)
- **Incident I** ("Inc I"): approximately 4 quadrillion years ago — a universal incident shared by "all thetans in the Universe." "Both, all thetans on this planet and 21 nearby stars have in common." (HCOB 2 Oct 1968)

The **free-wheel warning**: "If the volcano bit is run as per III directions but the Inc I is not run on same thetan, R6 begins to run off on automatic, the being can't sleep for days, the body dies. That's the way it was designed." (HCOB 2 Oct 1968) — this is the theological justification for the confidentiality requirement; unprepared exposure to the material is said to be lethal.

### OT 6 / NOTs — New Era Dianetics for OTs
The largest section of this volume (lines 6760–15300). **NOTs** (New Era Dianetics for OTs) addresses a population of body thetans distinct from those handled in OT 3: dormant, confused, or mis-identified BTs requiring different auditing techniques. Key concepts introduced: BT perception, valence technique, collective identities, "solidity" of the body, revivication. The NED for OTs framework is the main content of what is now sold as OT 5 through OT 7 in the Church.

### OT 8 — Why Thetans Mock Up
The original OT 8 bulletin (HCOB 1 Oct 1969, "SECRET") addresses what Hubbard calls "the most plaguing question in history of Clearing": why thetans compulsively create pictures of bad experiences. His answer locates the compulsion in a fundamental principle about **havingness** and thetan creativity.

### OT 7 — Projecting Intention
**Original OT 7** is a structured rundown on the "rehabilitation of ability to project intention" — progressing from in-session processes ("Give me an unknown datum") to outdoor practice of placing thoughts and intentions into strangers, plants, and animals at distance: "Practice placing an intention into individuals until you can successfully and easily place an intention into or on a being and/or a body."

**New OT 7** is simply a continuation of NOTs solo auditing toward the EP: "When the Pre-OT has a transparent body and a clear area around it to some distance… and when he realized he is alive and very much himself the EP of NED for OTs has been reached. When this EP is reached then one will have achieved 'CAUSE OVER LIFE.'"

### OT 8 — Why Thetans Mock Up (Original, LRH)
The **Original OT 8** (HCOB 1 Oct 1969, SECRET) addresses what Hubbard calls "the most plaguing question in history of Clearing." His answer: thetans mock up (create mental pictures) because of a fundamental refusal to abandon locations under duress — "a thetan never totally gives up." When forced from a position in space, a thetan "begins MOCKS IT UP mentally... This, unwittingly, gives him a picture, an engram." Hubbard's definition: "An engram therefore is a refusal to leave a place at which force was exerted to drive one away." Power is defined as "the ability to hold a position in space." Practical corollary: engrams can be blown without running them by simply spotting the original location ("What point (location) is unsafe?") — "working with this you will see a door open to a higher level than Dianetic R3R. But realize that it is only for a high level thetan."

### New OT 8 (Free Zone Debrief, 1991)
Reconstructed from memory by a Free Zone practitioner who attended the Freewinds delivery. Claims it addresses "what truly is the relationship of the Supreme Being to each individual thetan." The debrief describes extensive preparatory processes reviewing one's relationship to "MEST and form and life units" and one's "true source and history from its origin as the theta body, the true 8th Dynamic."

### Special Rundowns
**L10, L11, L12** (reconstructions — see provenance note): "The L's were developed as a result of research into the levels above OT VIII" — implying OT levels beyond 8 exist. L10 restores **havingness** (EP: "Freedom from self-restraint"); L11 restores **doingness** (EP: "A New Life"); L12 restores **beingness** (EP: "certainty and unshakability as a being"). The L10 **8th Dynamic Rundown** lists religious concepts (God, prayer, gospels, sacred texts, hell, wrath of God, sutras, hymns, idols, faith, devil, miracles, paradise, sacrifice, angels) as items to be assessed on the E-meter and any charge erased — conventional religious beliefs are treated as potential aberrations.

The **L12 Character List** categorizes archetypal past-life identities to be audited out as valences, including "**THE ALMIGHTY**" (God, A Goddess, The Buddha, Pan, Diana, The Father of All, The Horned God) and "**THE EVIL ONE**" (The Antichrist, The Devil, His Satanic Majesty). This places divine and demonic identities as whole-track case items requiring clearing.

**Super Power**: Five rundown series addressing existential conditions and the 8th Dynamic. **Series 2** makes a striking apophatic claim: "God is not an object. When one labels god with an identity one immediately degrades the 8th dynamic into MEST terms. The 8th dynamic cannot be defined in terms of MEST. It can only be defined in terms of infinity which the number 8 symbolizes." The final **Series 5 ("Actual Super Power Process")** commands include "Get the idea that you are infinite"; its claimed EP is ecstatic laughter, speechlessness, exterior state, and floating TA "up to an hour." The widely reported "57 perceptions" figure does **not** appear in the Super Power materials in this volume.

**Theological tension**: Super Power's apophatic/infinite God directly conflicts with L12's treatment of God, Buddha, and the Devil as auditable past-life valences. Both appear in the same compilation without reconciliation. See [[scientology-god-8th-dynamic|Scientology and the 8th Dynamic]] (controversy).

## Hermeneutical Method

This volume exemplifies Scientology's defining hermeneutic: **verbatim technical application**. No passage is to be interpreted; every procedure is to be run **exactly as written**. The HCOB format (official bulletin from Hubbard) carries supreme authority. "Keeping Scientology Working" policy (reproduced in full in the OT 2 section) forbids deviation. The graded, confidential structure means *which* texts one may read is determined by one's position on the Bridge — a **gated revelation** model.
