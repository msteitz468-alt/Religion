# The Zohar (Sefer ha-Zohar)

*The Book of Radiance.* The foundational canonical text of Jewish mysticism (Kabbalah), comprising a vast running commentary on the Torah together with a rich body of homily, narrative, theosophic speculation, and ethical instruction. The Zohar exceeds the dimensions of a normal book; it is a literature, comprising more than twenty discrete sections, the bulk of which is a Torah commentary from Genesis through Deuteronomy. In traditional piety it was ranked alongside the Bible and Talmud as a divinely revealed source of religious truth.

The Pritzker Edition translation and commentary (Stanford University Press, 2004–) is the first scholarly English translation based on a newly constructed critical text from original manuscripts. Volumes I–IX (translated by [[daniel-matt|Daniel C. Matt]]) cover the main running Torah commentary from Genesis through Deuteronomy. Volume X (translated by Nathan Wolski) presents the Midrash ha-Ne’lam, the earliest stratum of the Zoharic corpus. The wiki has ingested the main Torah commentary in **Volumes I–IX** and additional material in **Volume X**:

## Structure of Volume XI (Joel Hecker): Midrash ha-Ne’lam and Zoharic Compositions

*Source: [[hecker-zohar-pritzker-vol11|Hecker Pritzker Vol XI]] — standards reingest 2026-07-18. Body lines 352–4768 of working extract; apparatus excluded. First-pass 2026-06-07 retained only as historical scaffolding; **Idra Zuta was misattributed to this volume** and is corrected here.*

Volume XI (translated and annotated by [[joel-hecker|Joel Hecker]]) is the first Pritzker volume to present a **variety of Zoharic genres** rather than continuous Torah commentary (I–IX) or Torah *Midrash ha-Ne’lam* alone (X). Hecker’s preface:

> This eleventh volume in the series is the first to present a variety of Zoharic texts, reflecting the range of genres within the Zoharic library. Many of these sections first appeared in print in the publication that was titled *Zohar Ḥadash*, or *New Zohar*; this title is misleading, however, in that the volume comprises some of the earliest Zoharic material.

**Verified chapter inventory (body):** (1) MhN Song of Songs · (2) MhN Ruth · (3) MhN Lamentations · (4) Zohar on Song of Songs · (5) Matnitin · (6) Tosefta · (7) Sitrei Torah. **Not in Vol. XI:** *Idra Zuta* (see Vol. IX Ha’azinu / ~3:287b–296b).

### Midrash ha-Ne’lam on Song of Songs (short)

Does not run the Song as continuous midrash. Opening: Rabbi Reḥumai on Isaiah 11:2 four *ruḥot* — only King Messiah has attained them; Ezekiel 37:9 “from four *ruḥot*” = **complete *ru’aḥ*** born of **love’s kiss** (spirit joined to spirit → four; man and woman → Messianic child). Large **ש (*shin*)** of Song 1:1 paired with large **ב** of Genesis, **מ** of Proverbs, **א** of Chronicles as book-openers; *shin* = upper chariot / three patriarch-columns. Body mostly Aramaic with epic-layer theosophy (Hecker preface), closing into non-sefirotic Ecclesiastes 12 anthropology. Diverse rabbis (not Rashbi’s Companions as default cast).

### Midrash ha-Ne’lam on Ruth (largest unit)

Opens Ruth 1:1 → Job 38:7: morning stars = angels of day; sons of God = angels of night; creation for divine glory (Isa 43:7). **YHVH letters as soul map:** *vav* = *ruaḥ*, *he* = *nefesh*, *yod-he* = *nishmeta* / *nishmeta de-nishmeta* (Father–Mother / Son–Daughter); left-side animal soul from serpent. **Household map:** Elimelech = *yod*, Naomi = *he* / *nishmeta*, Ruth = turtledove inversion, Orpah = left *nafsha* / folly. Dense digressions: Night path of souls; seven Geihinnom compartments under **Dumah**; Temple Mount subterranean fire; **R. Kruspedai** near-death (Angel of Death, Shekhinah, **Sandalfon**, beit din); Uzza/Aza’el and *ishim*; Orpah = Harafah / David–Ishbi–Nob; soul levels *nefesh / ruaḥ / neshamah / nishmeta le-nishmeta*; nut-grove hermeneutic; tablets dual Torah vs calf; Hell crevice of Upper Galilee butcher freed by son’s Shema; mezuzah/tzitzit/tefillin/Sabbath Bride; **R. Shim’on** annuls Lydda plague (“greater than Moses?” frame).

**Ruth narrative densifications:** Field of Apples / *khoh* (Boaz’s maidens as chariots); Grace after Meals (ten requirements; prodigy youth); midnight threshing floor / two redeemers–messiahs; *yibbum*–*ḥalitsah*; seven (semblance of) wedding blessings; soul as pledge / *shekheinot*; **ten martyrs** sealed (sale of Joseph); R. Peraḥiah afterlife tour / Shema windows; preexistence of soul; end of unit (pagination may shift ZḤ markers mid-stream).

**Print history (Hecker preface, attributed):** known but largely omitted from first Zohar prints (scattered Cremona 1558); contested as Zoharic by some 14th–16th-c. kabbalists; Thiengen 1559 as *Yesod Shirim* / *Tappuḥei Zahav*; Venice 1565 as MhN al Rut; *Zohar Ḥadash* 1658. Vehicle for thinking conversion and, often, **polemic against Gentiles**.

### Midrash ha-Ne’lam on Lamentations (Eikhah)

**Israel vs Babylon** eulogy contest over who may open the acrostic lament of the destroyed House. Israel prevails: orphans without Father or Mother; Mother’s bed overturned, throne collapsed; She wandering rooftops weeping (Isa 22:1). Body labels **Master / Matronita / Koh** (Tif’eret–Shekhinah–Israel as family); Mother receives the flogging (Isa 53:5). Hecker preface frames this as a subtle **anti-Christian Holy Family reframe** — that framing is **Hecker’s**, not bare body wording. Serpent enmity / Gen 3 as Temple-destruction template; Ecclesiastes 12 line-by-line on Israel/Temple/sages; compressed **ten martyrs** / Samael prosecution. Hybrid MhN cast (many rabbis; Aramaic; some theosophy).

### Zohar on Song of Songs (Zoharic Composition)

Hecker: **mature phase** of Zoharic authorship; sustained **Rabbi Shim’on–Elijah** exchange on Song **1:1–11**. Early homilies: Rashbi on **ascent** (sefirot/soul); Elijah on **Other Side** ruptures and repair. Body authorship claim (Elijah speaking): Rashbi’s words written **above** before the Ancient of Days; Elijah’s only **below**, by Rashbi’s hand. Load-bearing motifs: four radiances/names; kisses / Cain–Seth letters; Oral Torah as bridal adornment; *mashkona* Shekhinah; **letter mysticism** — masculine/feminine letters, “upright ones” (final *mem nun tsadi pe kaf*), *bakh* alphabets, 72 names as *yayin*; **Pittum ha-Ketoret** binds limbs against Accuser; Song 1:5 black/beautiful as East/West lamps and *yod* point; Moon diminution / two Temples as *eikhah*; *dimmitikh* triple reading (Song 1:9); cantillation/vowels as Song 1:11 adornments; human as dual image of Elohim; creation as vowel-pointing. Cordovero reception (among most profound; full-fledged composition vs casual parashah midrash) is **Hecker preface / Cordovero**, not body. Hecker notes practical fallout: **prohibition of tefillin on intermediate festival days** (preface; not dense in every body slice).

### Matnitin and Tosefta

First published in any language as **discrete units** rather than dispersed (Hecker preface). **Identical style:** oracular, hortatory, terse, sometimes rhythmic — “Awaken sleepy… How long shall you remain in the darkness of your desires?” Brief sefirotic summaries so dense they can seem to diverge from main-body consistency. Hecker positions them as **likely later** compositions written with many Zoharic texts already in mind (contra early-summary view). Hell economy densest in Tosefta (Dumah; **Samri’el** opens gates on Amen May His great name be blessed). Sample load-bearing units: nine names of Torah; Tree of Life vs Tree of Death / changeling-thought; White Head name cascade; ten sefirot *belimah*; nine glyphs; resurrection garment / dust of Holy Land; well in the field // Field of Holy Apples.

### Sitrei Torah (“Secrets of Torah”)

Mostly **Genesis-linked**, anonymous voice (not Companions epic as default). Central focus: power of the **demonic Other Side**. Opening: Gen 5:3 Seth alone in Adam’s likeness; serpent scum; letters jumbled (*tsadi–dalet* hunter; *qof–resh* monkey/evil); Cain from filth; restoration via *shin–tav*. Seven earths (Erets → Tevel); Babel as Other Side city/tower vs Zion. Lekh Lekha soul allegory (Abram/*abba-ram*; **Lot = cursed serpent**). Soul itinerary *nefesh–ru’aḥ–neshamah*; Gen 15 *maḥazeh*/circumcision; “Zohar, Radiance” litany / YAHDVNHY. Va-Yetse: Samael & Woman of Whoredom; ladder/Metatron; well Netsaḥ–Hod–Yesod; mandrakes Leah/Rachel = Upper/Lower World; rods/*atufim*. Close: **Secret of Secrets** — spirit perfected by righteous; Dwelling as perfecting the Spirit (Exod 25). Hecker: later MhN-style layers labeled Sitrei Torah in some prints **excluded** from this translation.

**Relation to prior volumes**: Complements running commentary (I–IX) and Torah MhN (X) by displaying **generic diversity** — Megillot concealed midrash + mature Song midrash + oracular Matnitin/Tosefta + Sitrei Torah Other Side secrets. *Zohar Ḥadash* misnomer holds for early material; Matnitin/Tosefta may be later dense distillates (Hecker). Hermeneutics: Sod in multiple keys (MhN hybrid, Rashbi–Elijah dialogue, oracular “mystical Mishnah,” anonymous ST). See [[book-of-ruth|Ruth]], [[song-of-songs|Song of Songs]], [[lamentations|Lamentations]], [[zoharic-afterlife|Zoharic Afterlife]], [[zohar-authorship|Zohar Authorship]].

## Structure of Volume XII (Wolski and Hecker): Zoharic Compositions

*Source: [[wolski-hecker-zohar-pritzker-vol12|Wolski & Hecker Pritzker Vol XII]] — standards reingest 2026-07-18. Working extract body lines 492–4906; digests A–H under `raw/commentaries/Zohar/_cache_vol12/digests/`. First-pass 2026-06-07 retained as historical scaffolding.*

Volume XII (Stanford, 2017) completes the Pritzker Edition. Translators: [[nathan-wolski|Nathan Wolski]] (Heikhalot Be-Reshit, Heikhalot Pequdei, Piqqudin) and [[joel-hecker|Joel Hecker]] (Raza de-Razin, Sitrei Otiyyot, Qav ha-Middah, Merkevet Yeḥezqel, Zohar al ha-Parashot, Tosefta-style passages), with a **joint Introduction**. Daniel C. Matt’s series Preface in this volume notes that Vol. XII “completes the realization” of Margot Pritzker’s dream of a full English Zohar.

**Introduction framing (Wolski & Hecker, attributed — not body):** multi-generic “Zoharic library”; many sections appear in *Zohar Ḥadash* (“New Zohar”), “a misnomer… since the volume comprises some of the earliest Zoharic material”; vast majority of the English is first-time; Heikhalot reimagine classical palace ascent as prayer theurgy under Malkhut; Piqqudin restored as contiguous composition (not Mantua’s scatter among *Ra’aya Meheimna*); Raza clinical notes + mutability via *teshuvah*; Sitrei maps YHVH letters → chariots; Qav = measuring instrument / *Botsina de-Qardinuta*; Merkevet stresses looking-glass prophecy; Parashot highlight Joseph-sale → ten martyrs theodicy and Moses’ two-sided staff; “Tosefta” label often misapplied.

### Heikhalot Be-Reshit and Heikhalot Pequdei (body; Wolski)

**Be-Reshit (~1:38a–45b).** Rabbi Shim’on: creation engraved “above and below… in one mystery”; seven halls above (supernal faith) match seven below; after Adam’s banishment halls are arrayed for righteous souls. Garden halls include converts’ souls (Onqelos above them), Messiah comforting sufferers, Akiva / Manasseh motifs, multi-colored pillar. Prayer halls progress through named spirits (e.g. Sapphire / Sappira) toward the seventh as Holy of Holies — **seventh with seventh** (Malkhut receiving from Binah / Will unto Infinity).

**Pequdei (~2:244b–268b).** Body states prior halls were taught “in a general way,” now elaborated: “All the arrangements follow a single principle: so that low can be comprised in high” (**2:244b**). **Fourth Hall = Merit (Zekhut)** — all cases of the world judged (merits, sins, punishments, rewards); nested lights (216); children’s diphtheria as Other-Side counterpart. Fifth = Love; Sixth = Desire/Ratson (Jacob takes by kisses); Seventh = Holy of Holies (Joseph/Yesod arrays the concealed hall). When halls join: complete name **YHVH Elohim**. Morning Amidah mapped hall-by-hall with prayer postures. **Seven impurity halls** parallel holiness: Pit (Satan), Destruction (Impure), Dumah (Enemy), Guilt/Ḥovah (vs Merit), Sheol (Uncircumcised), sixth (*shit* — Lilith / harlot), seventh “dregs of wine” / Netherworld. Close: seven abodes of defilement from the serpent. See [[heikhalot|Heikhalot]], [[sitra-ahra|Sitra Aḥra]], [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]].

### Piqqudin (body; Wolski)

Contiguous **47 commandments** (not Mantua’s scatter). Frame: Lev 22:31 — *observe* + *do* = *shamor* (female) + *zakhor* (male); **613** = entirety of male and female. **First commandment:** know the blessed Holy One **in general** then **in particular** (Exod 6:7 → Deut 4:39 *Know today… that YHVH is Elohim*). Dense theurgy: tefillin portions, Sabbath pearls/names, Pesaḥ cycle, omer → Shavuot (Wife→Husband), shofar/RH Accuser covering the moon, Yom Kippur afflictions, Azazel “dog bone,” Sukkot princes, **lulav = image of Adam** (47th). Folios span multiple Mantua loci (e.g. 2:25a opening; 3:98b–100b shofar).

### Raza de-Razin (body; Hecker)

Title in text: *Raza de-Razei de-Sitrei de-Oraita Qadishta Shelimta*. Lemmas: Exod 18:21 *And you, you shall behold*; Gen 5:1 read as **Book of Human Features**. Shekhinah instructs Moses; six characteristics — hair, eyes, nose, lips, face, hands. Letter system from *zeh sefer*: **zayin, resh, he, pe, samekh, final tsadi**. Core body claim of mutability:

> “Just as the appearance of the stars and constellations shift… so too the appearance of markings and lines in the skin of a human being change, depending on one’s deeds from moment to moment.” / “From moment to moment according to one’s deeds—thus they are engendered, inscribed, and transmuted.” (**~2:71a**)

Holy Spirit vs impure spirit rewrite external markings; examinations from age thirteen; *teshuvah* can switch letter dominance and rewrite veins/markings (nine-day fantasy case; adultery–serpent-neck theurgy). Close: Primal Adam’s book; Jacob’s beauty/lines = Adam; David’s days. Pagination: ~2:70a–75a then **ZḤ 35b–37c**. See [[raza-de-razin|Raza de-Razin]].

### Sitrei Otiyyot · Qav ha-Middah · Merkevet Yeḥezqel (body; Hecker)

**Sitrei Otiyyot (ZḤ ~1b–7b):** Rashbi on Ps 106:2 / Jer 10:7. Each YHVH letter actualized with a corresponding **chariot**; *yod* as supreme point in Will of Thought; nut on six pillars; Lamp metes measure; nine pillars under *yod*; telescoping sefirot/chariots/pillars continuum; Ein Sof where named.

**Qav ha-Middah (ZḤ ~56d–58d):** Opens as mystery of *Shema* unification; instrument **Qav ha-Middah** also called **Botsina de-Qardinuta** / Botsitsa de-Qardinuta (“Lamp/Spark of Adamantine Darkness”) — configures beauty, color, and permanence for the sefirot; all radiances ascend/descend only with this measure — “excluding above, above, where there is no limit.” Square permanence of YHVH. See [[ein-sof|Ein Sof]], [[sefirot|Ten Sefirot]], [[divine-name|Divine Name]].

**Merkevet Yeḥezqel:** Assembly of Rabbi Shim’on, El’azar, Abba (Ḥagigah 2:1 chariot rule invoked). Four four-faced creatures; quaternities pervading being; prophecy via **speculum that shines** / looking-glass / behind-screen mediation; *ḥashmal*; Metatron; Name chain Ehyeh–YHVH–Adonai.

### Zohar al ha-Parashot + Tosefta (body; Hecker)

## Textual Overview

The Zohar was composed in medieval Castile, Spain, toward the end of the thirteenth century, written in a unique artificial Aramaic modeled on the Babylonian Talmud and Targum Onqelos but enriched with neologisms, puns, and deliberate obscurities.

In his Translator’s Introduction to the Pritzker Edition, [[daniel-matt|Daniel C. Matt]] states that *Sefer ha-Zohar* (The Book of Radiance) “emerged mysteriously in medieval Spain (Castile) toward the end of the thirteenth century,” is “written mostly in a unique Aramaic,” and is “virtually a body of literature, comprising over twenty discrete sections,” of which the bulk is a running Torah commentary from Genesis through Deuteronomy. Matt notes that the author(s) “concocted a unique blend of Aramaic out of traditional sources, especially the Babylonian Talmud and Targum Onqelos,” peppered with “enigmatic expressions, puns, outlandish constructions, puzzling neologisms, solecisms, and traces of medieval Hebrew and Castilian.” He glosses the Zoharic oxymoron “new-ancient words” as alluding to “the dual nature of the Zohar’s secrets, recently composed yet ascribed to ancient sources.”

**Critical text (Matt’s method):** Matt reports that all previous translations rest on standard printed editions that “nearly all derive from the Mantua edition (1558–60), supplemented by variant readings from the Cremona edition (1559–60).” Examining fourteenth- through sixteenth-century manuscripts, he found “superior readings that had been rejected or revised by editors of the first printed editions,” and concluded that “certain manuscripts of older lineage reflect an earlier version of the Zohar, which was then reworked in manuscripts of later lineage.” On his account, “no such [complete reliable] manuscript exists anywhere in the world; in all likelihood it never did, since from the start the Zohar was circulated in sections or booklets,” and “probably no single complete Book of the Zohar existed until it was printed nearly three hundred years later in the sixteenth century, collated from various manuscripts.” For the first two volumes he identified “approximately twenty reliable manuscripts” (criteria: provenance, age, lack of scribal errors, legibility), held in Oxford, Cambridge, London, Paris, Munich, Rome, the Vatican, Parma, Toronto, and JTS. He chose an **eclectic critical text** rather than a diplomatic base because even for individual sections “there is no one ‘best’ manuscript.” The sixteenth-century Mantua and Cremona editors had already fashioned critical texts (Mantua from ten MSS, Cremona from six). Matt begins from Margaliot’s *Sefer ha-Zohar* (Vilna line based on Mantua), substitutes multi-witness better readings, and prefers older striking or creative formulations over later smoothing and restoration of exact Talmudic wording. He disclaims full recovery of “the original text of the Zohar,” noting the text “probably emerged over many years, written and distributed piecemeal,” while aiming to “scrape away some seven hundred years of accretion and corruption.” See [[matt-zohar-pritzker-vol1|Matt Pritzker Vol I]].

**Primary attributed speaker:** Rabbi Shim'on bar Yoḥai (Rashbi; 2nd-century CE Tanna, student of Rabbi Akiva), whose circle of Companions (*Ḥavrayya*) wander the hills of the Galilee sharing Torah secrets. This attribution is a literary fiction (see [[zohar-authorship|Authorship Controversy]]).

**Actual authorship (framed positions):** Scholarly consensus since Gershom Scholem (1941) identifies [[moses-de-leon|Moses de León]] (c. 1240–1305) as the primary author/compiler, with the text possibly being a collaborative circle product edited over several generations. Matt’s Translator’s Introduction defers “the complex question of authorship” to Arthur Green’s introduction to the same volume. Green frames the Zohar as first revealed around 1300, distributed orally and in small written fragments under the claim of ancient rediscovery from the circle of Rabbi Shim’on son of Yoḥai; he notes that fragments were first distributed by Moses de León, who claimed they were copied from an ancient manuscript—classic pseudepigraphy. Green records Isaac of Acre’s early controversy account (via Zacuto’s *Sefer Yuḥasin*), Scholem’s eventual single-author case for de León against Graetz’s dismissals, and contemporary scholarship (especially Yehuda Liebes and Ronit Meroz) that “parted company with Scholem on the question of single authorship,” accepting de León as writer or editor of long sections including the main narrative body while not treating him as sole author. Green further frames the work as “a work of sacred fantasy” and, following Scholem’s suggestion, a “mystical novel” in formation whose Galilean wandering circle likely masks a living Castilian brotherhood (Toledo, Guadalajara) with night study sessions.

## Structure of Volume I

*Scope of Pritzker Vol. I (Stanford, 2004; Matt):* **Haqdamah** (Proem), **Parashat Be-Reshit** (Gen 1:1–6:8), and **Parashat Noaḥ** (Gen 6:9–11:32). The following densifies body content from the 2026-07-18 fidelity reingest of that volume (Zohar body in Matt’s translation; apparatus and Green intro kept separate below and in the reingest-anchors subsection).

### Haqdamah (Proem)
The printed Haqdamah opens the work (Mantua pagination from **1a**). Traditional reception links the book’s name to Daniel 12:3 (“The enlightened will shine like the radiance [*zohar*] of the sky”); Matt’s apparatus and later Be-Reshit material develop the *zohar*/radiance complex, while the opening body itself centers other loci.

In the Zohar body (Pritzker I, Haqdamah ~1a):
- **Rose / Assembly of Israel:** Rabbi Ḥizkiyah opens Song of Songs 2:2: “Who is a rose? Assembly of Israel. For there is a rose, and then there is a rose!” Red and white mark judgment and compassion; “Just as a rose has thirteen petals, so Assembly of Israel has thirteen qualities of compassion surrounding Her on every side.” Five sturdy leaves are “Salvation” / five gates / the cup of blessing resting on five fingers.
- **Covenant seed / forty-two letters:** From the second *Elohim* of Genesis onward, light is “created, concealed, contained in the covenant, entering the rose, emitting seed into Her…. That seed endures in the actual sign of covenant…. so the engraved, explicit name is sown in forty-two letters of the act of Creation.”
- **Who / What / Concealed of all Concealed:** On Isaiah 40:26, “Who created these?” — *Mi* (Who) is “the concealed ancient one, susceptible to questioning,” End of Heaven above; “Beyond, there is no question.” *Mah* (What) is the lower end of contemplation’s limit. Elijah’s Academy-on-High disclosure: “When Concealed of all Concealed verged on being revealed, it produced at first a single point, which ascended to become thought…. called מי (Mi), Who…. Seeking to be revealed… it garbed itself… and created אלה (elleh), these…. culminating in the name אלהים (Elohim). Until it created אלה (elleh), it did not attain the name אלהים (Elohim).”
- **Theurgy of innovated Torah:** “Every word innovated in Torah by one engaged in Torah fashions one heaven”; an innovated word of wisdom “settles on the head of צדיק (Tsaddiq), Righteous One—Vitality of the Worlds,” flies to the Ancient of Days, and is “transformed into a heaven.” Partnership re-reading: “Do not read ‘You are עמי (ammi), My people,’ but rather ‘You are עמי (immi), with Me, becoming My partner!’” Unqualified innovation becomes a “distorted heaven” and a “woman of whoredom.”
- **Bridal night (Shavuot-type):** Rabbi Shim’on and Companions as bridal attendants adorn the Bride through Torah–Prophets–Writings, midrash, and “mysteries of wisdom”; they are “members of the canopy.” Heaven is the Groom; *mesapperim* sparkles with sapphire radiance; “masters of the covenant with the Bride” are partners via “the covenant sealed in a man’s flesh.”
- **Fourteen commandments from the Creation narrative:** Rabbi Shim’on sequences mitsvot “written in Torah in general terms” from Gen 1: awe of YHVH (gate of faith), consummate love, daily unification (*Shema*), knowing YHVH is Elohim, Torah study, be fruitful, circumcision (eighth day; Elijah’s chair), love of the convert, generosity to the poor, tefillin, tithe, first fruits, redeeming the son, and Sabbath (including Other Side retreat, Lilith/demon warnings for nude lamp-intercourse, havdalah).
- **Dumah / covenant:** “The one appointed over Hell is named Dumah… not permitted to approach any of those who guarded the holy covenant in this world.”

### Parashat Be-Reshit (Genesis 1:1–6:8)
The Zohar’s interpretation of the creation account is its most celebrated exegetical achievement. Key sections (Zohar body, Pritzker I; folios ~**15a** onward for the theogonic overture):

- **Genesis 1:1 — theogony, not cosmogony:** In Zohar **1:15a** (Pritzker I), “A spark of impenetrable darkness flashed within the concealed of the concealed, from the head of Infinity… concealed within the concealed of the mystery of Ein Sof…. a single, concealed, supernal point shone. Beyond that point, nothing is known, so it is called ראשית (Reshit), Beginning.” The point expands, builds a palace: “With this beginning, the unknown concealed one created the palace. This palace is called אלהים (Elohim), God. The secret is: … *Be-reshit bara Elohim*, **With beginning, created God** (Genesis 1:1).” Elohim is thus the **object** of the first verse (Binah/palace), not the subject; *Reshit* maps to Ḥokhmah. Related re-parsings in the same run include *bara shit* (“created six”) and the forty-two-letter name engraved at *bara*. See [[sefirot|Ten Sefirot]] and [[ein-sof|Ein Sof]].
- **Botsina de-qardinuta:** The same overture names the first impulse as a “spark of impenetrable darkness” (*botsina de-qardinuta* in Matt’s gloss of the Aramaic), “so intensely bright that it cannot be seen” (Matt, Translator’s Introduction, illustrating the oxymoron). “It split and did not split its aura” marks the transition from Ein Sof to the first point.
- **Genesis 1:2 — Tohu, bohu, darkness, wind:** Mapped to divine names: “Tohu—upon it rests the name שדי (Shaddai). Bohu—upon it rests the name צבאות (Tseva’ot)…. Darkness—upon it rests the name אלהים (Elohim). Wind—upon it rests the name יהוה (YHVH),” correlated with Elijah at Horeb (1 Kings 19: wind, earthquake, fire, “sound of sheer silence”).
- **Genesis 1:3–5 — Primordial light; Day/Night:** Light treasured away for the righteous; Day as central pillar extending **Yesod** (Foundation, Vitality of the Worlds); Night as female moon / Adonai / [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]]. “God made the two great lights” initially in a single bond; the moon “diminished herself, diminished her light”; *me’orot* spelled deficiently as *me’erat* (curse), embracing the evil serpent and shells (Lilith complex).
- **Genesis 1:6 — Firmament / second day:** “Here conflict was created through the left…. Out of that conflict… emerged Hell.” The third day (central pillar) mediates; “that it was good is not written of the second day.” Korah vs Aaron is read as left vs right in this key.
- **Human / male-female:** “A human is male and female, and is otherwise not called human.” Adam emerges from the site of the Temple; original Lilith (books of the ancients) removed as unfit helper; face-to-face array after rain/coupling is fitting.
- **Garden / trees:** Tree of Life (five-hundred-year span, middle of the garden, waters of Creation); Tree of Knowledge suckles two sides (sweet and bitter). Command not to take the tree of death “alone” / “separating saplings”; Adam “stretched his foreskin… separated the holy covenant from its site.”
- **Genesis 3 — Fall as cosmic divorce:** Rabbi Shim’on: “Indeed, all is one. It was Samael appearing on a serpent, and the form of the serpent is Satan; all is one.” “When Adam sinned by eating from the tree… he caused a defect, separating the Woman from Her Husband. The fault of this defect stood out in the moon, until Israel stood at Mount Sinai… Once Israel sinned with the calf, She relapsed… the evil serpent prevailed.” On Gen 3:24: “Who drove out *Et*? Adam. Adam actually drove out *Et!*” (Shekhinah). Garments of light (*kotnot or*) become garments of skin. Cain from the impure side / Samael’s slime; from Cain “all the haunts of maleficent species, spirits, demons, and sorcerers.”
- **Genesis 6:1–4 / Flood generation:** Uzza and Azael fall, engender *nefilim*; wasted seed and open sin empower the impure spirit called “evil” so that “Shekhinah withdraws from the world.” Noah at birth is “circumcised, inscribed with the sign of Shekhinah.”

### Parashat Noaḥ (Genesis 6:9–11:32)
- **Noah as Yesod below:** In the Zohar (Pritzker I), “Noah was a righteous man… corresponding above… *The righteous one is the foundation of the world* (Proverbs 10:25)… Righteous One, and Noah is called righteous below.” *Ish ha-adamah* = “husband of earth”; the ark is the Ark of the Covenant with which he must unite “to sustain the seed of all.” Born circumcised; “perfect in his generations” is dual-edged.
- **Ambivalence / failure to intercede:** Limited by “*in this generation*, precisely”; unable to abolish death because the serpent’s slime remained and “Torah, Tree of Life, had not yet been brought down.” Explicit contrast with Moses: Noah “did not plead for compassion for the world and it perished…. waters of the Flood are named after him… *like the waters of Noah to Me* (Isaiah 54:9)…. he just wanted to save himself, abandoning the entire world.”
- **Flood as sexual/covenant crime (measure-for-measure):** “They wasted their ways, so that the upper waters and lower waters failed to unite fittingly…. Who has ever ruined like this: male and female waters?” Punishment is boiling water “peeling off their skin”; *All the springs of the great abyss* = lower waters; *sluices of heaven* = upper waters. Nothing so provokes divine jealousy “as the sin of the covenant.”
- **Ark vs Destroyer (*Mashḥit*):** “End of all flesh” = Angel of Death / Serpent / left end; “there was a flood, the Destroyer surging through that flood.” Noah hidden twelve months so “as long as one’s face is not visible to the Destroyer, he has no power” (Passover-house typology). Ark entry requires Husband’s permission: “YHVH, husband of the ark.”
- **Post-Flood incompleteness:** World “remained unsettled and earth did not escape the slime of the serpent until Israel stood at Mount Sinai.” Noah’s offering eases the curse, but moon defect and full settlement remain suspended; seven Noahide commandments until Sinai.
- **Soul doctrine:** Tripartite structure—vitality (*nefesh*), spirit (*ruaḥ*), holy soul (*neshamah*); the unvirtuous have “two openings… but [are] devoid of the holy soul.” Three sons of Noah as three spirit-clusters / right–left–purple.
- **Gehinnom:** Seven abodes; Sheol (raiseable) vs Avadon (never raised); relief on new moons and Sabbaths; Kaddish response “Amen! May His great name be blessed!” signals Samriel to open gates for hour-and-a-half relief.
- **Vineyard / Ham:** Noah’s drunken exposure as failed probe of Adam’s sin, “exposing the breach of the world that had been closed”; Ham/Canaan as “scoria of gold… primordial serpent.”
- **Rainbow:** Covenant bow of the Righteous (Joseph/Noah); forbidden to gaze “lest shame be exposed in Shekhinah”; messianic reading of radiant colors.
- **Babel / holy tongue:** Journey from *qedem* = departure from Primordial faith; tower as “a name… a deity to worship.” “They were all speaking the holy tongue… powers above know and recognize only the holy tongue. Once language was scrambled… their power weakened.”

## Structure of Volume II (Patriarchal Genesis, Gen ~12–32)

*Scope of Pritzker Vol. II (Stanford, 2004; Matt):* **Lekh Lekha, Va-Yera, Ḥayyei Sarah, Toledot, Va-Yetse** (Gen 12:1–32:3). ### Parashat Lekh Lekha (Gen 12:1–17:27; ~1:77a–97a)
- **Call / self-refinement:** *Lekh lekha* read as “Go for yourself, to refine yourself, to perfect your rung” / “to know yourself” (Zohar body ~1:78a). Abraham tests “conduits of the inhabited world” and fails at the central point of Canaan until God reveals what he could not grasp.
- **Arousal below → above:** “An entity above is not aroused until there is first aroused below”; black light (Shekhinah) not joined to white until She is first aroused (~1:77b–78a).
- **Abraham as Ḥesed / South / right:** Journeying “toward the Negev—the South, share of Abraham”; four promises as right / left / middle / land; “throne of four legs, all comprised in Abraham” (~1:78b). Egypt as refinement of lower rungs (contrast Adam/Noah seduction); [[sarah|Sarah]] accompanied by [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]] (“my sister” / Prov 7:4).
- **[[melchizedek|Melchizedek]]** (Gen 14; ~1:87a–b): *melekh shalem* / two worlds as one; “Brought out bread and wine, for She contains both”; priest of God Most High = Right serving supernal world; tenth.
- **[[circumcision|Circumcision]] as Yesod gateway:** Before the covenant, only a low *maḥazeh* rung spoke; after, “all those rungs rested upon him.” Covenant = “cluster of all those supernal rungs”; enter share of “The righteous one is the foundation of the world” ([[tsaddiq|Tsaddiq]]/Yesod). *I am El Shaddai*: uncircumcised marked with lower *shin–dalet*; circumcision reveals *yod*, completes *Shaddai* (~1:89a–96b). Abram→Abraham / Sarai→Sarah (*he*); Isaac’s *yod*; [[ishmael|Ishmael]] while still *Abram* = engendering “below.”
- **Afterlife accents:** midnight Garden; Dumah/Sheol for sleepers; deathbed sealing of deeds; covenant protects from Dumah (~1:77b, 79a, 92a+).

### Parashat Va-Yera (Gen 18:1–22:24; ~1:97b–120b)
- **Post-circumcision theophany:** Song 2:12 midrash — “the time of pruning” = command to circumcise; then “YHVH appeared to him” (~1:97b–98a). Three visitors as patriarchal triad (Abraham–Isaac–Jacob) over the lower rung; *Adonai* = [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]] with three-color throne (~1:99a–101b).
- **Annunciation:** Sarah hears “the opening of the tent” as lower rung / opening of faith; first divine word she has heard; Abraham “sitting behind Shekhinah” (~1:103a–104a).
- **Sodom:** *I will go down and see* = compassion turning to judgment; *Va-YHVH* rain = Lower Court authorized from above; Abraham’s partial intercession (fifty→ten) vs Moses who does not budge; Lot saved by Abraham’s hospitality merit (~1:104a–108a).
- **[[ishmael|Ishmael]] expulsion:** *metsaḥeq* as idolatry; scoria vs gold once Isaac born; seed “through Isaac… not through Ishmael” (~1:118b–119a).
- **Akedah** (~1:119a–120b): *Elohim tested Abraham*—not Isaac—so water (Ḥesed) is embraced by fire (Gevurah); *et Abraham* = Isaac in low power until binding crowns him; “until [[jacob|Jacob]] appeared and everything harmonized—triad of patriarchs completed.” Isaac 37, walks on together as willing lamb; ram at twilight. See [[akedah-interpretation|Akedah Interpretation]].

### Parashat Ḥayyei Sarah (Gen 23:1–25:18; ~1:121b–134a)
- **Sarah’s years:** “One hundred… twenty… seven… all… supernal”; *shanah* (unity) vs *shanim* (judgment–compassion split) (~1:122a–123a). R. Ḥiyya: dies when Isaac is bound (*va-yihyu* = 37). Death “not caused by that sinuous serpent” — *be-Qiryat Arba* / City of Four / Shema / Hebron joining patriarchs.
- **Machpelah:** Opening to the Garden of Eden; Adam and Eve already there; only the destined owner (Abraham) sees light; field/cave “arose”; double *he* of the Name / Jerusalem above–below (~1:127a–129a). See [[zoharic-afterlife|Zoharic Afterlife]].
- **Abraham *ba ba-yamim*:** “coming into days—into those supernal days… mystery of faith”; soul courts/house; resurrection dew / Tree of Life (~1:129a–131b).
- **[[rebekah|Rebekah]] betrothal:** Angel of Covenant; spring with *he*; minḥah timing; entering Sarah’s tent rekindles lamp and [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]] (~1:131b–133b). **Keturah = Hagar** renamed/perfumed; gifts = impure rungs sent east (~1:133b–134a).

### Parashat Toledot (Gen 25:19–28:9; ~1:134b–146b)
- **Generations:** “Abraham engendered Isaac” as image stamped; Isaac not blessed by Abraham so Esau not inherit; well Lahai Roi = joining [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]] (~1:134b–136a).
- **Twins:** Jacob side of sacred throne vs [[esau|Esau]] side of the serpent; *saʿir* / Yom Kippur goat typology; red lentils break power of red; birthright sold (~1:138a–139b). Isaac loves Esau as “species loves its kind” (din above → din below); Rebekah mild Judgment balances severe Judgment.
- **Blessing plot:** Two goats (Yom Kippur typology); Adam’s garments; fragrance = beauty of Adam / loveliness of YHVH; blessings invert Eden curses; Hell enters with Esau (trembling worse than the Akedah knife); Samael confirms/wrestles foreshadow (~1:142a–146b). See [[sitra-ahra|Sitra Aḥra]].

### Parashat Va-Yetse (Gen 28:10–32:3; ~1:147a–165b)
- **Sun-Jacob / ladder:** Jacob as sun from Beer-sheba to Haran; ladder as rung on which rungs depend; angels of the nations ascend/descend by Israel’s deeds; *Anokhi* / wings of Shekhinah (~1:147a–150b).
- **Two wives = two worlds:** [[rachel|Rachel]] = revealed / Sabbatical / lower world / *Attah* (You) / essence of the house (*iqqara de-veita*); [[leah|Leah]] = concealed / Jubilee / higher world / *Hu* (He). “Through the revealed one reaches the concealed” (~1:152b–155b). Joseph–Benjamin as two *tsaddiqim* flanking the Sabbatical; twelve tribes adorn Assembly of Israel; when Benjamin is born Rachel dies and Shekhinah takes the house (~1:156a–159a).

## Structure of Volume III (Close of Genesis, Gen ~32–50)

### Parashat Va-Yishlaḥ (Gen 32:4–36:43; ~1:166a–179a)
- **Wrestle with the Prince of Esau:** the “man” is **Samael**, heavenly Prince of [[esau|Esau]] / champion of the [[sitra-ahra|Sitra Aḥra]]; Jacob is left alone so he can force confirmation of the blessings. Struggle in *avaq* (ash) of the Other Side; dawn ends night-exile dominion; thigh = support of body/Torah (*gid ha-nasheh*); name **Israel** forced at dawn; arrives *shalem* (complete above and below) (~1:166a–171a).

- **Dinah / Shechem:** assault from the impure side; Simeon and Levi vs Hamor; circumcision-then-slaughter typology (~1:174a–176a).
- **[[rachel|Rachel]]’s death** and house of twelve tribes; Reuben’s bed-disturbance as disgracing Shekhinah (not simple adultery) (~1:175b–176b).
- **Jacob as body of the patriarchs:** right Abraham / left Isaac / composite torso (Tif’eret); chariot (~1:166b–168a).
- **Edom kings / Sitra Aḥra:** impure rungs “before any king… Israel”; eschatological judgment of Edom (~1:177a–179a).

### Parashat Va-Yeshev (Gen 37:1–40:23; ~1:179b–193a)
- **[[joseph|Joseph]] the Righteous = [[tsaddiq|Yesod]]:** “These are the generations of Jacob: Joseph” read as Righteous One fruiting from Jacob; Yesod as foundation / Zion; covenant mystery of Joseph (~1:179b–182a).
- **Sale / coat / dreams:** hatred and exile of tribes with Shekhinah; 22-year delay; dotted *et* / Gabriel (~1:182a–186a).
- **Judah and [[tamar|Tamar]]** (Gen 38): Tamar acts out of wisdom; she and Ruth as the two women through whom the seed of Judah is established—**David, Solomon, Messiah**. Er/Onan wasted seed feeds the Other Side (~1:186a–189a).
- **Potiphar’s wife:** Other Side trial; image of Jacob; Joseph flees and guards the sign of the covenant (~1:189a–191a).
- **Prison dreams:** vine = Assembly of Israel; baker / darkened moon; spirit of falsehood vs Torah (~1:191a–193a).

### Parashat Mi-Qets (Gen 41:1–44:17; ~1:193b–205a)
- ***Mi-qets* = End of the Left / place of forgetting** after Joseph’s “remember me”; rise as river/Yesod; *Avrekh*; sun–moon second chariot (~1:193b–197a).
- **Famine theurgy:** seal the Yesod-spring so the Other Side does not father “alien children”; great substance of Gen 15 (~1:197a–200a).
- **Brothers’ descent:** harsh Judgment (Simeon/Levi) vs Joseph’s peace; schemes only for Benjamin; Judah as deep waters for Benjamin; Shekhinah follows the righteous into Egypt and had withdrawn from Jacob when Joseph was lost (~1:200a–205a).

### Parashat Va-Yiggash (Gen 44:18–47:27; ~1:205b–211b)
- **Judah approaches** Joseph = world–world / king + Tsaddiq; Benjamin surety of both worlds; self-offering (~1:206a–206b).
- **Reunion:** peace of tribes; Joseph alone with brothers / Assembly of Israel; weeping for Temples and ten tribes; *qol* without *vav* = Shekhinah (~1:208a–210a).
- **Wagons / eglah:** Jacob’s spirit revives → Israel rung; “Joseph my son is still alive” as Life of the Worlds / covenant (~1:210a–211b).
- **Goshen:** single convergence; Shekhinah goes down to Egypt; Jacob blesses Pharaoh; dual chariots (Other Side left vs holy right) (~1:211a–b).

### Parashat Va-Yḥi (Gen 47:28–50:26; ~1:216b–251a)
- **All Israel righteous / inherit the land:** via circumcision they enter the Righteous One and inherit the Matronita ([[shekhinah|Shekhinah]]); Jacob’s seventeen years in Egypt as life with Shekhinah (~1:216b–218a).
- **Soul dossier densification:** *tselem*, thirty days, rooster, Machpelah ladder to Garden, three garments, twelve months — see [[zoharic-afterlife|Zoharic Afterlife]] (~1:218b–225a zone).
- **Perfect deathbed:** Abraham right, Isaac left, Jacob center, Shekhinah in front; sons *Be gathered*; sun gathered to moon; **“Jacob did not die”** (gathered, not “died”) (~1:235b, 1:248b).
- **Tribal blessings:** Reuben reserved; Simeon–Levi left of Shekhinah; Judah moon/kingship/Messiah/Shiloh; other tribes sefirotic roots (~1:235a–248a).
- **Joseph’s double *aron*:** embalmed; *va-yiysem ba-aron* with two *yods* = coffin below + ark of the covenant above, because he preserved covenant below and Covenant above (~1:251a).

## Structure of Volume IV (Opening of Exodus)

- **Amalek:** left crowns → “dog”; first of nations to attack; Judgment vs Compassion; **Samael** assists above; Joshua as Youth vs Youth above; sword avenging the covenant; Moses’ hands (Aaron+Ḥur) array battle above (~64b–67a). See [[amalekites|Amalek]].

- **Torah founds the world:** *Were it not for My covenant day and night…* (Jer 33:25); world “fragrantly firm” only after Sinai (~94a). Moon begins to shine; Judah *ne’eman* for kingship (~84a).

## Structure of Volume V (Mishpatim, Tabernacle Beginnings, Sava, Sifra di-Tsni’uta)

**Parashat Mishpatim** (Exodus 21:1–24:18) and **Parashat Terumah** (Exodus 25:1–27:19), together with *Sava de-Mishpatim* and *Sifra di-Tsni’uta*.

**Standards reingest 2026-07-18** (source [[matt-zohar-pritzker-vol5|Matt Pritzker Vol V]]; cache body lines 329–4102; digests A–G). Working extract: `raw/commentaries/Zohar/_cache_vol5/zohar_v_full.txt`. Matt’s preface frames the volume: gilgul via Sava; *mishkan* = Shekhinah / **Kallat Moshe**; Sifra plain then annotated; Zohar as compilation of books that “became a book” in sixteenth-century print.

This volume shifts from liberation/theophany (Vol. IV) into covenant laws and Dwelling instructions. Theurgical construction draws Shekhinah into earthly union with Israel and especially with Moses.

### Parashat Mishpatim and Sava de-Mishpatim (~2:94b–114a Sava; ~2:121a–126a main body)

- **Sava de-Mishpatim** (“Old Man of Mishpatim”): independent stratum. R. Ḥiyya and R. Yose meet a donkey-driver at the Tower of Tyre; three riddles (serpent/ant; eagle; “beautiful maiden without eyes”); he is later revealed as **Yeiva Sava** (2:114a). Distinctive parabolic / troubadour style.
  - **Torah-as-maiden** (2:99a–b): palace, window-glimpse → derasha → haggadah → face-to-face secrets (“husband of Torah”) → peshat without adding/deleting a letter.
  - **Gilgul** (first extensive Jewish treatment in this corpus): scales of righteousness/deceit; soul as slave-girl (Exod 21:7–10); Lilith and infant souls; preexistent unwilling descent; levirate/redeemer/vessel; sling (1 Sam 25:29); Worlds of Male and Female via Exod 21:2–5 six years/seventh; *ba’al teshuvah*; resurrection of double-body transmigrants (Dan 12:2 *admat afar*). Full locus map on [[gilgul|Gilgul]].
  - Closing: Song 8:6; Companions report to R. Shim’on, who calls the Sava a “supernal lion” (2:114a).
- **Commentary on Mishpatim** (main body after Sava, from 2:121a): *People of holiness* (Exod 22:30) as climax of a holiness ladder; mishpatim after Decalogue to balance **Gevurah**-given Torah with peace; “Secret of Secrets” anatomical-sefirotic map of the King (head → Yesod/Female); Sinai covenant blood, sapphire vision, Nadab/Abihu deferred judgment (Exod 24).

### Parashat Terumah and the Dwelling (~2:126b–176b)

- Offering/*terumah* as taking Matronita from the Righteous One (*kol ish*) with divine permission (Exod 25:2 midrash); *Mi zot* as two worlds joined through Jacob.
- **Kallat Moshe**: Num 7:1 *kallot Mosheh* re-read *kallat Mosheh*, “Bride of Moses” — Shekhinah breaks through accusatory darkness and descends; *va-yhi* as pain of armies (2:140b–141a). Dwelling = body for Holy Spirit; shell/kernel model of Holy Land.
- Materials (gold/silver/bronze; blue/purple/crimson; taḥash) mapped to calendar and angelic/Other-Side colors; prayer theurgy (Redemption–Prayer, *nefilat appayim*, Shema 25+24 letters → 49 gates; Sabbath *Ke-Gavna* strand; bed/table/chair/lamp).
- Night unit: **Song of Songs** as Holy of Holies when Temple/moon full; dual Dwelling (Moses below / Metatron Youth above); cherubim face diagnostics; table/Bread of Presence; scapegoat/Other Side desert rock; Shema large *ayin*/*dalet* as *ed* (witness) with seven blessings (2:143b–161a zone).
- Late Terumah: ten curtains as expanses/body; mitsvot as limbs of *adam*; mountain-man wheat parable (general principles vs flour/bread/cake/pastry) leading into Sifra (2:176b approach).

### Sifra di-Tsni’uta (The Book of Concealment) (~2:176b–179a)

Matt prints **plain translation first**, then the **same text with commentary**. Five short chapters, condensed theosophy:

1. **Balance on scales**; primordial kings die until “garments of glory”; single **skull** with crystalline dew, fleece, armoire **breath**; Gen 1 numbers (six/seven/thirteen); serpent *tanninim*; male/female balance; “Woe to one who exposes their nakedness!”
2. **Beard of faith** (thirteen white strands); Short-Tempered One’s skull (two-color dew, three-color eyes, three nostril-flames); breath from Ancient One.
3. **Nine** of the lower (black) beard vs thirteen higher; *adam* male/female; kings re-established; Cain as nest of evil spirits.
4–5. Ancient vs Short-Tempered; *du*; firmament; Nephilim; kings/tree/serpent; Enoch as youth; **horn of Jubilee** → breath restored → complete Name YHVH Elohim.

Close: “Until here, sealed and crowned is concealment of the King. Happy is one who has emerged, knowing its paths and ways!” (2:179a). Later Kabbalah (Luria, Vilna Gaon, etc.) treats Sifra as a condensed “Mishnah” for the Idras.

The volume deepens Sod theurgy: laws and sanctuary as instruments of soul repair and Shekhinah’s indwelling, with Sifra as the upper concealed counterpart to the manifest *mishkan*.

## Structure of Volume VI (Tetsavveh, Golden Calf, Tabernacle Completion)

**Parashat Tetsavveh, Ki Tissa, Va-Yaqhel, and Pequdei** (Exodus 27:20–40:38) — completes the Zohar’s Exodus commentary (Matt preface).

**Standards reingest 2026-07-18** (source [[matt-zohar-pritzker-vol6|Matt Pritzker Vol VI]]; cache body lines 304–2995; digests A–G). Working extract: `raw/commentaries/Zohar/_cache_vol6/zohar_vi_full.txt`. Matt’s preface: *mishkan* = Shekhinah; Calf as rejection of Shekhinah; Moses immobilizes divine wrath; *Berikh shemeih* flagged as later (16th-c.) addition absent from many MSS.

### Parashat Tetsavveh (Exod 27:20–30:10; ~2:180a–187b)
- Opens on *ve-attah* / Moses–Shekhinah union; letter mysteries (*yod* / *he* / *vav*); priestly and festival theurgy (Passover/ḥamets, Omer, RH/YK/Sukkot via Nehorai Sava desert unit). Priestly-garment catalogues are not the sole sequential spine of this slice.

### Parashat Ki Tissa (Exod 30:11–34:35; ~2:188a–194b)
- **Census/ransom**: counting empties blessing so Other Side can harm; double blessing around ransom (2:188a).
- **Golden Calf = sin against Mother** (2:191a): *Rise up, make us Elohim* — Elohim = Glory of Israel / mother-hovering presence; *They exchanged their glory* (Ps 106:20) for *tavnit shor*, image of a bull. Mother (who usually grasps the King’s arm so the lash is suspended) was not there; Accuser’s first garment is the bull form.
- **Mixed multitude (*erev rav*)**: Egyptian sorcerers (incl. Yannes/Yambres) whom Moses accepted against the divine counsel; sought left-side Elohim; tore own ears; calf from sorcerous gold; ox+donkey spirits (2:191a–193a).
- **Moses pins three limbs of the King** (2:193b; Exod 32:10): *Now leave Me be* → right arm; *My wrath may blaze… consume them* → left arm; *I will make you a great nation* → embraces the body — “He could not move in any direction at all.” (Patriarchal arm-invocation is part of the same intercession economy.)
- Aftermath: ornaments stripped; Tent pitched **outside** the camp; *Tent of Mo’ed* double reading (festival vs allotted death-time); Joshua still dies because Dwelling/moon was tainted (2:193b–194b).

### Parashat Va-Yaqhel (Exod 35:1–38:20; ~2:195a–220a)
- Assembly after Calf: *from yourselves* (not every man of the mixed multitude); Sabbath re-gifted to Children of Israel alone; *terumah* theurgy (Shekhinah raised with Husband — *terei mi-me’ah*).
- Dense **Sabbath theurgy** (north star/seventy/pyre/colors; additional soul; liturgy including musaf); **Berikh shemeih** appears in square brackets in Pritzker — later stratum (not integral body).
- Curtains = heavens; Garden/Messiah Chamber of the Ill (Isa 53 theodicy); Bezalel’s ark vs Other-Side *aron*; incense as bridge; resurrection/Messiah in Galilee (Joseph’s portion) near close.

### Parashat Pequdei (Exod 38:21–40:38; ~2:220b–244b)
- **Reckonings (*piqqudei*)**: accounting fulfills the work; Holy Spirit counts; Bezalel (*be-tsel El*) right/Judah + Oholiab left/Dan; gold as remedy for calf; priestly garments / ephod / Urim–Thummim.
- **Moses alone erects** (2:235a): Bezalel knew letter permutations and fashioned all works but **could not erect** — intention of the letters of creation transmitted to Moses alone (*Moses erected, Moses placed, Moses set up*, Exod 40:18).
- **Glory fills** (Exod 40:35; 2:235b): cloud + glory; bride arraying for husband — husband must not enter mid-adornment; Dwelling brought to Moses first as bride to groom.
- **Dual/triple Dwelling**: below + above (*was erected*); *et* = sunken middle point restored; day of erection = **1 Nisan**; death/Samael **withdrawn** (not forever until Messiah, Isa 25:8); Samael bound, Other Side sockets fall; Other Side still receives a tiny share (tefillin hair, monthly goat) so as not to accuse.
- Volume body closes with Other Side seven-rung anatomy; *Heikhalot* not in this Pritzker cut.

Central drama: idolatry as rejection of the Mother/Shekhinah withdraws Her restraint; Moses’ theurgical intercession and erection restore presence **provisionally** — full abolition of death awaits the end.

## Structure of Volume VII (Leviticus: Sacrifices, Priesthood, Purity, and Holiness)

**Parashat Va-Yiqra, Tsav, Shemini, Tazri’a, Metsora, and Aḥarei Mot** (Leviticus 1:1–18:30). **Standards reingest 2026-07-18** — source page [[matt-zohar-pritzker-vol7|Matt Pritzker Vol VII]]; body folios roughly **3:3b–80a** (Pritzker opens at [3b]; Mantua letter-mysticism 3:2a–3b omitted as unattested in numerous MSS and Cremona — Matt apparatus note). Digests A–G under `raw/commentaries/Zohar/_cache_vol7/digests/`.

This volume shifts from the Tabernacle’s construction and dedication (Vols. V–VI) to the sacrificial system, priestly ordination, purity laws, and Yom Kippur / sexual holiness that allow the divine presence to remain among the people. The Zohar spiritualizes the rituals as sefirotic theurgy—especially the union (*zivvuga qaddisha*) of the masculine and feminine divine.

### Parashat Va-Yiqra (Lev 1–5; ~3:3b–27a)
- **Garden/bride banquet (Song 5:1; [3b]–[4a]).** R. Ḥiyya opens with the Song’s paradox of inviting after eating: on the day the Dwelling was erected, “another was erected with it… That day was joy of the blessed Holy One.” Garden of Eden above; *My sister, bride* = Assembly of Israel; couplings complete; companions/lovers (above/below six) blessed by the aroma of the offering. Moses outside (Exod 40:35) is invited as to a house-dedication meal.
- ***Qorban* as drawing near of crowns ([5a]).** Not *qeiruv* (nearness) but *qorban* — “drawing near of those holy crowns… until all become one… *to YHVH*, and not *to Elohim*” (Compassion, not Judgment). *Zivḥei Elohim* are slaughterings that assuage Gevurah on the north.
- ***Adam* = married ([5a]–[5b]).** Whoever is not male-and-female is not in the category of “human,” does not deserve blessing, and cannot offer: “When *adam* brings near—excluding whoever is not married… Shekhinah does not settle upon him.” **Nadab and Abihu** already exemplify: incense transcends offerings, yet they were unworthy as unmarried; Matronita prepared for the King, who saw them defective and withdrew — *Fire came out and consumed them*.
- **Ascent offering from Thought ([5b]–[6a]).** *Olah* entirely male, ascending “upon the heart”; consummation of Thought is *boqer* (morning) that sweetens the Female. Matt’s preface frames the *olah* as Shekhinah ascending to the blessed Holy One; the body densifies *qorban*/*olah* as crown-binding and Thought theurgy.
- **Later Va-Yiqra ([15a]–[26a]):** restitution / “waters of Noah” (Noah fails to intercede vs Moses); Ancient One / Short-Tempered / Mother and teshuvah as covering Mother’s nakedness; *ba‘alei teshuvah* higher than the completely righteous; soul sacrilege (*nefesh*/*ruaḥ*/*neshamah*); Other Side fire.

### Parashat Tsav (Lev 6–8; ~3:27b–34b)
- **Perpetual fire ([27b]–[28b]).** Fire is always Judgment; the priest is of the right, yet feeds wood so altar fire “eliminates impure spirit and evil species… fire consuming fire.” Arranged morning after morning to assuage Judgment.
- **Dual altars ([30b]).** Gold (inner incense / bond of faith / sheer silence) and bronze (outer / Michael); oil and incense rejoice the heart, not fat of Judgment.
- ***Zeh*/*zot* union ([31a]).** Wicked separate *zot* from *zeh* (peace of the house); Aaron and sons reunite King with Matronita so those above and below are blessed.
- **First Nadab–Abihu dossier in Tsav:** wrong time for incense, forced hour, unmarried/defective, wine.

### Parashat Shemini (Lev 9–11; ~3:35a–42a)
- **Eighth day = Assembly of Israel** after seven ordination days; calf/ram offerings linked to golden-calf atonement and Isaac’s ram.
- **Nadab and Abihu full dossier ([34a] and Shemini units):** *They offered alien fire*—“they surely bound something else with this bond and left Assembly of Israel outside.” Pinḥas refinement: not that they “left Her outside,” but “Assembly of Israel was not linked by them; for wherever male and female are not found, Assembly of Israel does not abide… a priest should not enter the sanctuary until he is married.” They aroused “disturbance between Matronita and the King.” Bodies burnt within; souls did not perish (later Aḥarei Mot, ~3:61b).
- **Food laws:** *zot ha-ḥayyah* as Name vs Other Side; ten Crowns of Faith vs ten impure crowns of sorcery; unclean food as permanent loathsomeness (*ve-nitmetem* without *alef*).

### Parashat Tazri’a and Metsora (Lev 12–15; ~3:42b–56b)
- ***Al mishkavi* ([42b]):** Assembly of Israel pleads “about my lying” in a foreign impure land; union only in His palace.
- **Childbirth / seed / *tselem*:** Male-first vs Female-first arousal order; image and Other Side deposits; face-to-face coupling ethics.
- ***Tsara’at*:** Linked to evil speech (*ha-motsi ra*), Serpent, “closing” (*segiru*); priest opens by Compassion; house affliction purges Canaanite impure buildings for Shekhinah.
- **Metsora purification:** Birds, cedar, crimson, hyssop mapped theurgically; Job 19:29 fear of the sword / judgment; flux and day of evil.

### Parashat Aḥarei Mot (Lev 16–18; ~3:56b–80a)
- **After the death:** double *YHVH* rungs; death of the righteous atones; Phinehas’s small *yod* reunites the half-bodies of Nadab/Abihu; *be-zot* only (not “all times”).
- **Yom Kippur goats ([62b]–[64a]):** Lots so the two Judgment-guardians do not join (world could not endure); one for YHVH, one for Azazel; Accuser becomes Advocate; Esau as *sa‘ir*; *ish itti* with physiognomic marks; goat limbs torn on the mountain; sins cast into depths of the sea (Mic 7:19).
- **Golden knotted cord (~3:67a):** Body states of the high priest before the Holy of Holies: “A golden knotted cord hangs from his leg.” Matt’s preface notes the motif is unattested in Bible/Talmud/Midrash before the Zohar and entered folklore/art from this (or oral) stream — **apparatus framing**, not body claim about prehistory.
- **High-priest theurgy:** Wash, three ranks blessing, three steps, incense, cherubim wings; balsam fragrance if worthy; time of favor (Isa 1:18).
- **Three rungs (3:73a):** “There are three rungs, interlinked—the blessed Holy One, Torah, and Israel. Each one is rung upon rung, concealed and revealed.” (Matt preface: folk “all one” form never appears; this is the body formulation. ## Major Themes

**1. Creation as divine biography (theogony)**
The Zohar reads the Torah not as the story of the world but as the story of God — specifically the story of how the infinite divine (Ein Sof) came to express itself in the ten sefirot, and how those sefirot relate to creation and to Israel. The first verse of Torah encapsulates this: Ein Sof (unknowable) → Ḥokhmah (first point, "beginning") → Binah (palace, womb, "God") → the seven lower sefirot → creation.

**2. The Divine Feminine (Shekhinah)**
The tenth sefirah, Malkhut/Shekhinah, is the divine Bride, the Moon, the Assembly of Israel, the channel through which divine blessing flows to the world. She is a full divine persona, distinct from the "blessed Holy One" (Tif'eret, the personal God of prayer). The Zohar's central theological drama is the *zivvuga qaddisha* (holy union) of Tif'eret and Shekhinah — a union that sustains cosmic order and whose disruption (by human sin) constitutes the catastrophe of exile. See [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]].

**3. Theurgy — commandments as cosmic maintenance**
Every mitsvah operates on the sefirotic structure. The Zohar supplies *ta'amei ha-mitsvot* (reasons for commandments) that are theurgical: circumcision binds the soul to the living being (Shekhinah) on the eighth day; Sabbath rest allows the Sitra Aḥra to hide while holiness rules; tefillin correspond letter-by-letter to the divine name and clothe the human in the image of Adam Qadmon (the Primordial Human). Religious practice is not merely covenantal obedience but metaphysical maintenance of the divine organism.

**4. The Other Side (Sitra Aḥra)**
Evil is not external to the divine but emerges from within it — specifically from the excess of Gevurah (divine judgment/left side) when it operates untempered by Ḥesed (love/right side). The demonic realm is a shadow-emanation of the sefirot, ontologically dependent on divine energy and human sin. Lilith, Samael, the serpent, and the Angel of Death all derive from this "other side." See [[sitra-ahra|Sitra Aḥra]].

**5. The Zohar as Torah commentary**
Despite its dense mystical symbolism, the Zohar remains rooted in the biblical text. Every teaching emerges from the opening of a specific verse. The Companions model the practice of *pithḥa* (opening a verse) and then exploring its inner meaning through verbal analogy, letter-combination, numerical equivalence, and sefirotic correspondence. This is the Sod (secret) level of the [[pardes|PaRDeS]] hermeneutical system practiced at its highest and most demanding form.

## Hermeneutical Method

The Zohar operates almost exclusively at the **Sod** (סוד, secret/mystical) level of biblical interpretation. Key moves:
- **Re-parsing the verse order**: "bereshit bara Elohim" → "With beginning, It created Elohim" (subject and object inverted)
- **Identifying divine names with sefirot**: YHVH = Tif'eret; Elohim = Binah (or Malkhut); El = Ḥesed; Shaddai = Yesod; Adonai = Malkhut; Tseva'ot = Netsaḥ/Hod
- **Letter mysticism**: individual letters and vowel-points carry sefirotic meaning; the *yod* = Ḥokhmah; the *he* = Binah
- **Verbal analogy** (*gezerah shavah*): shared vocabulary across widely separated verses implies sefirotic identity
- **Narrative expansion**: Torah scenes are re-enacted in the heavenly realm simultaneously
- **Apocryphal citation**: the Zohar appeals to *The Book of Enoch*, *The Book of King Solomon*, *The Book of Adam*, and *The Book of Mysteries* as sources of hidden tradition, almost certainly pseudepigraphical devices rather than actual texts

The Zohar's language is deliberately obscure — oxymorons ("a spark of impenetrable darkness"), neologisms, grammatical inversions — because language can only *hint* at the ineffable sefirotic reality. As the Zohar says: translation always betrays; the Aramaic itself is "suited to the soul" even when not understood.

## Interpretive Cruxes

**"A spark of impenetrable darkness" (botsina de-qardinuta)**: In Zohar ~**1:15a** (Pritzker I, Matt), “A spark of impenetrable darkness flashed within the concealed of the concealed, from the head of Infinity… of the mystery of Ein Sof,” yielding the single point called *Reshit*. Matt’s Translator’s Introduction glosses the Aramaic *botsina de-qardinuta* as “a spark of impenetrable darkness, so intensely bright that it cannot be seen.” The oxymoron is a signature Zoharic move: revelation by deeper concealment. Cross-link: [[ein-sof|Ein Sof]].

**"It split and did not split its aura"**: Same theogonic overture (Pritzker I): the transition from Ein Sof to the first point is stated and denied together — pure actuality before distinction. Related Haqdamah language: “touching yet not touching.”

**The diminution of the moon (Genesis 1:16)**: In the Zohar (Pritzker I), the two great lights are first “in a single bond”; the moon then “diminished herself, diminished her light,” thereafter receiving light only from the sun. Deficient *me’orot* is read as *me’erat* (curse) embracing the serpent’s slime and separation. Full moon / restored light = perfected divine marriage; waning / defect = exile (and Adam’s sin; Sinai healing; calf relapse).

**Adam's sin as cosmic divorce**: On Gen 3:24 the Zohar (Pritzker I) asks who divorced whom: “Who drove out *Et*? Adam. Adam actually drove out *Et!*” Parallel claim: Adam “caused a defect, separating the Woman from Her Husband,” the defect standing out in the moon until Sinai. The first sin, in this Sod reading, is fracturing of divine male–female integration, not mere disobedience.

**The generation of the Flood and sexual sin**: In the Zohar (Pritzker I, Noaḥ), wasting seed so that “upper waters and lower waters failed to unite fittingly” is punished measure-for-measure by boiling upper and lower waters; the Destroyer surges through the flood. Water punishes water-related / covenant-sexual crime.

## Manuscript and Publication History

As attributed in [[daniel-matt|Matt]]’s Translator’s Introduction (Pritzker Vol. I) and related apparatus:

- **No complete manuscript:** Matt states no complete reliable MS exists “anywhere in the world; in all likelihood it never did,” because “from the start the Zohar was circulated in sections or booklets.” “Probably no single complete Book of the Zohar existed until it was printed nearly three hundred years later in the sixteenth century, collated from various manuscripts.”
- **Surviving MSS:** 14th–16th century; originals in Oxford, Cambridge, London, Paris, Munich, Rome, the Vatican, Parma, Toronto, and JTS (Matt’s list for the corpus he used); microfilms at the Institute for Microfilmed Hebrew Manuscripts, JNUL / Hebrew University.
- **First print editions:** Mantua (1558–60) and Cremona (1559–60). Matt notes these editors also fashioned critical texts — Mantua from **ten** manuscripts, Cremona from **six**. Nearly all later standard printed editions derive from Mantua, sometimes supplemented by Cremona.
- **Older vs later lineages:** Matt concludes that older-lineage MSS reflect an earlier version later reworked; printed editions “obscured earlier versions.” Scribes and editors smoothed neologisms, “corrected” syntax, and restored creative Talmudic paraphrases to exact Talmudic form.
- **Pritzker critical text (Matt):** For Vols. 1–2, “approximately twenty reliable manuscripts” (provenance, age, lack of scribal errors, legibility). Eclectic critical text chosen after Academic Committee consultation (no single best MS per section). Starting base: Margaliot / Vilna / Mantua line; multi-witness superior readings substituted; aim to recover “primal texture and cryptic flavor,” while **not** claiming full restoration of a single original (piecemeal multi-year emergence). See [[matt-zohar-pritzker-vol1|Matt Pritzker Vol I]].
- **Standard pagination:** Mantua edition pagination (e.g., "1:34b") indicated in Pritzker running heads and used as canonical reference.
