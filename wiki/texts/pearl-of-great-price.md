The **Pearl of Great Price** is the fourth Latter-day Saint "standard work" — a short anthology of texts
produced by [[joseph-smith|Joseph Smith]]. It is canon for the **[[latter-day-saints|LDS Church]]** but,
unlike the [[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]] and [[doctrine-and-covenants|D&C]], was **not** canonized by
the Community of Christ — so its `canon_scope` differs slightly (Community of Christ moves into
`rejected_by`), a fine-grained example of intra-movement canon divergence.

## Contents

- **The Book of Moses** — a revelatory expansion of [[genesis|Genesis 1–6]] (part of Smith's revision of
  the Bible).
- **The Book of Abraham** — presented as a translation of an Egyptian papyrus; the source of distinctive
  cosmological doctrines (a council of gods, pre-mortal spirits, the plurality of worlds). Its relationship
  to the surviving papyri is a major historical controversy.
- **Joseph Smith—Matthew** and **Joseph Smith—History** — his revision of Matthew 24, and his first-person
  account of the First Vision and the coming-forth of the Book of Mormon.
- **The Articles of Faith** — Smith's 13-point summary of LDS belief.

## Significance

Small but doctrinally weighty: the Book of Abraham in particular underwrites Mormonism's distinctive
cosmology (exaltation, plurality of gods, pre-existence) that sets it apart from creedal
[[christianity|Christianity]] — see [[latter-day-saints|the sect page]].
