**Seder Moed** (Hebrew: סדר מועד, "Order of Appointed Times") is the second order of the [[mishna|Mishna]] and the [[talmud-bavli|Talmud]]. It comprehensively details the laws of the Jewish calendar, including the Sabbath, major festivals, and fast days.

Because these laws were universally applicable to Jews everywhere, the Babylonian Talmud features Gemara for almost all of its tractates.

## Tractates of Moed

1. **Shabbat (Sabbath):**
   - *Subject:* The laws of Sabbath observance, particularly the 39 categories of prohibited creative labor (*Melakha*) and laws concerning carrying in the public domain.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (157 folios).
2. **Eruvin (Mixtures):**
   - *Subject:* Legal mechanisms (*eruv*) that permit actions otherwise forbidden on the Sabbath, such as an *Eruv Chatzerot* allowing carrying within a shared courtyard, or an *Eruv Techumin* extending the distance one may travel.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (105 folios).
3. **Pesachim (Passover Festivals):**
   - *Subject:* The laws of Passover, including the prohibition and removal of *chametz* (leaven), the offering of the Paschal lamb, and the procedures for the Passover Seder.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (121 folios).
4. **Shekalim (Shekels):**
   - *Subject:* The annual half-shekel tax collected for the maintenance of the Temple and the purchase of public sacrifices.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No. (The standard Vilna Shas prints the Jerusalem Talmud's Gemara for this tractate).
5. **Yoma (The Day):**
   - *Subject:* The laws of Yom Kippur (the Day of Atonement), focusing extensively on the High Priest's intricate service in the Temple on that day, as well as the laws of fasting.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (88 folios).
6. **Sukkah (Booth):**
   - *Subject:* The festival of Sukkot (Tabernacles), detailing the construction of the *sukkah*, the laws of residing within it, and the taking of the Four Species (*Lulav* and *Etrog*).
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (56 folios).
7. **Beitza (Egg):**
   - *Subject:* Also called *Yom Tov*. It addresses the general laws applicable to all major festivals, particularly rules regarding food preparation, which is permitted on festivals but forbidden on the Sabbath.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (40 folios).
8. **Rosh Hashanah (New Year):**
   - *Subject:* The Jewish calendar, the sanctification of the new moon by the Rabbinic court, and the laws and prayers of Rosh Hashanah (including the blowing of the Shofar).
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (35 folios).
9. **Ta'anit (Fast):**
   - *Subject:* The laws of public fast days, particularly those instituted in times of drought or national distress, and the observances of Tisha B'Av.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (31 folios).
10. **Megillah (Scroll):**
    - *Subject:* The festival of Purim, the rules for writing and reading the Scroll of Esther, and the broader laws regarding synagogue practices and the public reading of the Torah.
    - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (32 folios).
11. **Moed Katan (Little Festival):**
    - *Subject:* The laws of *Chol HaMoed* (the intermediate days of Passover and Sukkot) outlining permitted and forbidden work, as well as the laws of mourning (*Aveilut*).
    - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (29 folios).
12. **Chagigah (Festival Offering):**
    - *Subject:* The duty of pilgrimage to Jerusalem on the three pilgrim festivals (Passover, Shavuot, Sukkot) and the special festival sacrifices. It also contains significant mystical and philosophical Aggadah.
    - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (27 folios).
