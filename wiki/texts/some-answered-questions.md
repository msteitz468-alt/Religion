# Some Answered Questions

*Populated from [[peter-smith|Smith, A Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith (2000)]]. All claims are from that source unless marked otherwise.*

## Overview

*Some Answered Questions* is a compilation of talks delivered by [[abdu-l-baha|'Abdu'l-Bahá]] in 'Akká between 1904 and 1906, in response to questions posed by Laura Clifford [[dreyfus-barney|Dreyfus-Barney]], an American Bahá'í (lines 29615–29619). A secretary recorded the talks in Persian; these transcripts were later checked by 'Abdu'l-Bahá himself. An English translation was published in London in 1908, revised in subsequent editions (lines 29620–29626).

## Content

The topics discussed include Christian subjects and a range of metaphysical and social issues (lines 29627–29629). The text is an important source for Bahá'í teachings on the [[soul-bahai|soul]] (SAQ 126, 151, 208–11, 224, 229, 239–40, 242, 265), on [[sin|sin]] (SAQ 92, 119, 119–26), on the afterlife (SAQ 224), on truthfulness (SAQ 215–16), on the nature of the Manifestations of God, and on a range of theological and philosophical questions.

## Canon Status

The text is not classed as divine revelation (that status is reserved for the writings of the Báb and Bahá'u'lláh), but is authoritative interpretation by 'Abdu'l-Bahá. It is canonical in the sense of having been checked and approved by 'Abdu'l-Bahá and is widely cited in Bahá'í doctrinal exposition.
