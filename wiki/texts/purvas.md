# The Purvas

The **Purvas** ("Prior [texts]") are the fourteen lost foundational scriptures of Jainism, held by
tradition to contain the most ancient and complete teaching, transmitted from before the surviving
[[jain-agamas|Agama]] and gradually forgotten.

## Canon and Reception (Dundas)

Both communities agree the Purvas are **lost**, but draw opposite conclusions:

- The **[[digambara|Digambaras]]** make the loss of the Purvas part of their argument that the *entire*
  original canon perished, justifying their rejection of the [[svetambara|Svetambara]] [[jain-agamas|
  Agama]] as inauthentic and their reliance on later systematic works.
- The **Svetambaras** hold that some Purva material was absorbed into surviving texts (e.g. the
  *Drstivada*, the lost twelfth *anga*) before the rest was lost, so their Agama remains the legitimate
  residue of the original teaching.

The last master traditionally said to have known the Purvas is **Bhadrabahu**.
