**Seder Tohorot** (Hebrew: סדר טהרות, "Order of Purities") is the sixth and final order of the [[mishna|Mishna]] and the [[talmud-bavli|Talmud]]. It delves into the highly complex system of ritual purity (*Tohorah*) and ritual impurity (*Tum'ah*). 

Because most of these laws became practically moot following the destruction of the Temple, only one tractate (*Niddah*, which relates to ongoing family purity laws) has a Babylonian Gemara.

## Tractates of Tohorot

1. **Kelim (Vessels):**
   - *Subject:* Details which types of utensils, vessels, and garments are susceptible to acquiring ritual impurity, based on their material, form, and function. (Longest tractate in the Mishna with 30 chapters).
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
2. **Oholot (Tents):**
   - *Subject:* The severe impurity generated by a human corpse (*Tumat Met*) and how that impurity spreads through "tents" or enclosed roofed structures.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
3. **Nega'im (Afflictions):**
   - *Subject:* The laws of *Tzaraath* (often mistranslated as leprosy), a spiritual affliction that affects human skin, clothing, and houses, requiring isolation and purification.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
4. **Parah (Cow):**
   - *Subject:* The intricate laws of the Red Heifer (*Parah Adumah*), whose ashes are required to purify a person who has contracted corpse impurity.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
5. **Tohorot (Purities):**
   - *Subject:* Euphemistically named; it primarily deals with minor sources of impurity that last only until evening, and impurities related to foods and liquids.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
6. **Mikva'ot (Ritual Baths):**
   - *Subject:* The specifications, dimensions, and required water sources for constructing a valid *Mikvah* used for ritual purification.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
7. **Niddah (Menstruant):**
   - *Subject:* The ritual impurity associated with menstruation and childbirth. This tractate is the basis for the modern laws of Family Purity (*Taharat HaMishpacha*).
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (73 folios).
8. **Makhshirin (Preparers):**
   - *Subject:* The rule that dry food is immune to impurity until it is intentionally wetted by one of seven specified liquids, making it "susceptible."
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
9. **Zavim (Discharges):**
   - *Subject:* The severe impurity of a person experiencing abnormal genital discharges.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
10. **Tevul Yom (Immersed of the Day):**
    - *Subject:* The intermediate status of a person who has immersed in a Mikvah but must wait until sunset to be completely pure.
    - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
11. **Yadayim (Hands):**
    - *Subject:* The Rabbinic decree of the ritual impurity of unwashed hands and the process of ritual hand-washing (*Netilat Yadayim*).
    - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
12. **Uktzin (Stalks):**
    - *Subject:* Whether the stalks, stems, or peels of a fruit can transmit or contract ritual impurity independently or as part of the fruit.
    - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
