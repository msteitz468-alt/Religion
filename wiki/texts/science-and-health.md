**Science and Health with Key to the Scriptures**, by **Mary Baker Eddy**, is the textbook of
[[christian-science|Christian Science]] — the work in which she set out her system of spiritual healing
and metaphysics. With the Bible, it is designated the church's **"pastor"**: passages from the two are
read aloud (without sermon) at Christian Science services.

## Status and Canon Scope

Science and Health is **not added scripture** in the Mormon sense; rather it functions as the **definitive
interpretive key** to the Bible — so authoritative as to be read alongside it in worship. In the wiki's
buckets it is `authoritative_for: [christian-science]` (a sub-canonical "key" elevated nearly to scripture)
rather than `canonical`. Its **"Key to the Scriptures"** section provides a metaphysical glossary that
re-defines biblical terms (God = Mind, Principle, Love; "Adam" = error; etc.).

## Teaching

The book argues that **reality is wholly spiritual** and that matter, sin, sickness, and death are
"errors" of mortal mind with no ultimate reality; God is infinite **Mind/Spirit**, and humanity is His
perfect spiritual reflection. Healing follows from **correct understanding** of this truth — the basis of
Christian Science "treatment." The famous "scientific statement of being" ("There is no life, truth,
intelligence, nor substance in matter…") encapsulates the system.

## Significance and Controversy

The book underwrites a [[christian-science|theology]] far outside historic Christianity and a practice of
spiritual healing that has generated its best-known controversies (reliance on prayer over medicine).
Recorded without adjudication (Contradiction Protocol).
