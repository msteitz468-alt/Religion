**Seder Kodashim** (Hebrew: סדר קדשים, "Order of Holy Things") is the fifth order of the [[mishna|Mishna]] and the [[talmud-bavli|Talmud]]. It deals extensively with the sacrificial rites of the Temple in Jerusalem, the duties of the Kohanim (priests), and dietary laws regarding animal slaughter (*Kashrut*).

## Tractates of Kodashim

1. **Zevachim (Sacrifices):**
   - *Subject:* The procedures for offering animal and bird sacrifices in the Temple, including the sprinkling of blood and burning of portions on the altar.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (120 folios).
2. **Menachot (Meal Offerings):**
   - *Subject:* Rules for grain and meal offerings, the Two Loaves of Shavuot, the Showbread, and laws concerning Tzitzit and Tefillin (which are logically appended here).
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (110 folios).
3. **Chullin (Ordinary/Non-Sacred):**
   - *Subject:* The laws of kosher slaughter (*Shechita*) for animals not meant for the altar, determining animal sicknesses that render meat non-kosher (*Treifot*), and the prohibition of mixing meat and milk.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (142 folios).
4. **Bekhorot (Firstborns):**
   - *Subject:* The sanctification and redemption of the firstborn of humans and kosher animals, and the physical blemishes (*Mumim*) that disqualify priests from service and animals from sacrifice.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (61 folios).
5. **Arakhin (Valuations):**
   - *Subject:* Pledging the financial "value" of a person or property to the Temple treasury as outlined in Leviticus 27, and the laws of the Jubilee year.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (34 folios).
6. **Temurah (Substitution):**
   - *Subject:* The prohibition against substituting an unconsecrated animal for one already designated for sacrifice, and the status of both animals if such a substitution is attempted.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (34 folios).
7. **Keritot (Exisions):**
   - *Subject:* Sins that carry the severe divine punishment of *Karet* (spiritual excision or premature death) if committed intentionally, and the sin offerings required if committed accidentally.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (28 folios).
8. **Me'ilah (Misappropriation):**
   - *Subject:* The laws against deriving personal benefit from property or items consecrated to the Temple, and the restitution required.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (22 folios).
9. **Tamid (Continual Offering):**
   - *Subject:* A descriptive narrative detailing the daily morning and evening continual offerings (*Korban Tamid*) and the daily routine of the priests in the Temple.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (partial, 8 folios).
10. **Middot (Measurements):**
    - *Subject:* The architectural layout, dimensions, and structural details of the Second Temple complex.
    - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
11. **Kinnim (Nests):**
    - *Subject:* The complex mathematical permutations involving bird offerings that have become confused or mixed up with one another.
    - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
