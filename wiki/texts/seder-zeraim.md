**Seder Zeraim** (Hebrew: סדר זרעים, "Order of Seeds") is the first of the six orders of the [[mishna|Mishna]] and the [[talmud-bavli|Talmud]]. It primarily addresses the agricultural laws applicable within the Land of Israel, including tithes, priestly portions, and charity for the poor. 

Because agricultural laws are heavily tied to the Land of Israel, only the first tractate (*Berakhot*, dealing with daily prayers) has a Babylonian Gemara. The Jerusalem Talmud, however, has Gemara on all tractates in Zeraim.

## Tractates of Zeraim

1. **Berakhot (Blessings):** 
   - *Subject:* The laws of prayer, specifically the recitation of the [[shema|Shema]], the daily *Amidah*, and blessings recited over food, fragrances, and natural phenomena.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* Yes (64 folios).
2. **Pe'ah (Corner):**
   - *Subject:* The agricultural gifts mandated for the poor, including the corners of the field (*Pe'ah*), gleanings (*Leket*), and forgotten sheaves (*Shikhehah*).
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
3. **Demai (Doubtful):**
   - *Subject:* Produce purchased from someone who might not have separated the required tithes (an *Am Ha'aretz*), and the procedure for tithe separation in such doubtful cases.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
4. **Kil'ayim (Mixtures):**
   - *Subject:* The prohibition against mixing different species, including planting mixed seeds, crossbreeding animals, and weaving wool and linen together (*Shatnez*).
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
5. **Shevi'it (Seventh):**
   - *Subject:* The laws of the Sabbatical year (*Shmita*), requiring the land to lie fallow every seventh year, and the cancellation of debts.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
6. **Terumot (Donations):**
   - *Subject:* The laws concerning the *Terumah*, the portion of the crop that must be given to the Kohen (priest), which becomes holy and can only be eaten by priests in a state of ritual purity.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
7. **Ma'aserot (Tithes):**
   - *Subject:* The "First Tithe" (*Ma'aser Rishon*), which is given to the Levites from the produce of the land.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
8. **Ma'aser Sheni (Second Tithe):**
   - *Subject:* The "Second Tithe," which must be taken to Jerusalem and eaten there in purity, or redeemed for money to be spent on food in Jerusalem.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
9. **Challah (Dough):**
   - *Subject:* The portion of dough that must be separated and given to the Kohen when baking bread.
   - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
10. **Orlah (Uncircumcised):**
    - *Subject:* The prohibition against eating the fruit of a tree during its first three years of growth, and the laws of the fourth-year fruit (*Neta Reva'i*).
    - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
11. **Bikkurim (First Fruits):**
    - *Subject:* The laws regarding bringing the first fruits of the Seven Species to the Temple in Jerusalem and the declaration recited at that time.
    - *Babylonian Gemara:* No.
