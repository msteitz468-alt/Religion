# G. Walter Hansen

American evangelical New Testament scholar, emeritus professor of New Testament at **Fuller Theological
Seminary**. His major commentary is *The Letter to the Philippians* in the **Pillar New Testament Commentary**
(PNTC; Eerdmans, 2009), the wiki's source for the [[philippians|Letter to the Philippians]]. He also wrote
extensively on **[[galatians|Galatians]]** (an earlier monograph on Abraham in Galatians and a Galatians
commentary), with a long-standing interest in Paul's rhetoric and the place of Abraham and faith in the
Pauline argument.

## Hermeneutical Method

Hansen reads grammatico-historically within a confessional evangelical frame, with particular attention to
**epistolary structure and rhetoric**, the situation of the Philippian church (its partnership in the gospel),
and the theology of the letter's great set-pieces. On Philippians his commentary highlights:

- The **Christ-hymn (Phil 2:6–11)** as the theological and ethical heart of the letter — Christ's self-
  emptying (*kenosis*) and exaltation as the pattern for the community's humility and unity.
- **Joy and partnership (*koinōnia*)** as the letter's keynotes, read against Paul's imprisonment.
- **Righteousness through faith vs. confidence in the flesh (Phil 3)**, engaging the Pauline soteriology
  debates.
