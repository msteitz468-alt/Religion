# Mark Julian Edwards

Mark Julian Edwards (b. 1962) is a British scholar of early Christianity at the University of
Oxford (Christ Church), Professor of Early Christian Studies, and a specialist in the
relationship between patristic theology and Greco-Roman philosophy. He is read in this wiki
chiefly for his revisionist account of [[origen|Origen]] and, more broadly, of how Christian
writers of the first six centuries used — and resisted — the philosophical idiom of their
day.

## Hermeneutical Method

Edwards is a **historical-critical and philosophical** reader whose signature move is
**suspicion of the inference from vocabulary to influence**. Against the long consensus that
the Fathers "Hellenized" the gospel, he insists that sharing a term or a problem with the
Platonists does not make a Christian writer a Platonist; what must be compared are the
*arguments*, set in their actual third- or fourth-century context and under the controlling
authority of Scripture and the rule of faith. The Ashgate series he co-edits programmatically
treats each father "not as a representative of a tradition... but as an individual immersed in
the intellectual culture of their day," concentrating on arguments rather than opinions.

## Characteristic Positions

1. **Origen against Plato.** In *[[edwards-origen-against-plato|Origen Against Plato]]* (2002),
   Edwards argues that [[origen|Origen]] built a Christian philosophy *to preserve* theology
   from pagan infiltration, not to dilute it, and that Origen's anthropology — the human as a
   created unity destined for bodily resurrection, not a fallen divine soul seeking escape — is
   "radically opposed to that of Platonism," with his hermeneutics, eschatology, cosmology, and
   Trinitarian theology all following from it.
2. **Scepticism about the Ammonius Saccas link.** He questions the over-read tradition that
   Origen studied under Ammonius (Plotinus's teacher) and that one "Origen" bridged Christian
   and Neoplatonist circles.
3. **Re-reading the "Gnostics" and Clement.** He advances fresh interpretations of the systems
   labelled [[gnosticism|Gnostic]] and of the Trinity in [[clement-of-alexandria|Clement of
   Alexandria]], pressing the same anti-"influence" caution.
4. **Method over genealogy.** Convergence or divergence between Christian and pagan thought is
   to be explained by argument and milieu, not by tracing a lineage of borrowed ideas.

## Place in the Wiki

Edwards supplies the principal **counter-thesis** to the "Christian Platonism" framing of
[[origen|Origen]] (recorded alongside it per the Contradiction Protocol) and bears on the
hermeneutics cluster, since Origen is the fountainhead of Christian
[[allegorical-reading|allegory]] and the [[fourfold-sense|spiritual senses]] that
[[henri-de-lubac|de Lubac]] traced. His work sits beside the wiki's other patristics
scholarship — [[wolfson-the-philosophy-of-the-church-fathers|Wolfson]] on the Fathers'
philosophical systems and [[holmes-apostolic-fathers|Holmes]] on the Apostolic Fathers.
