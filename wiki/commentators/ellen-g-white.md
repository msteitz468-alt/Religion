# Ellen G. White

**Ellen Gould White** (1827–1915) is the co-founder and prophetic voice of
[[seventh-day-adventism|Seventh-day Adventism]] — one of the most published authors in history (and the
most translated American author). Adventists regard her many visions and writings as a genuine
manifestation of the **"gift of prophecy."**

## The "Lesser Light" — her authority and its limits

White's status is the defining sub-canonical case among the modern sects: her writings are held to be
**inspired and authoritative, yet subordinate to the Bible** — a **"lesser light pointing to the greater
light."** They are not added to the canon (Adventism keeps the 66-book [[protestantism|Protestant]] Bible
as sole scripture); in `canon_scope` terms her corpus is `authoritative_for: [seventh-day-adventism]`, not
`canonical`. This is the cleanest example in the wiki of an inspired-but-sub-canonical body of writing —
distinct from the Mormon **open canon** and from the Christian Science **"pastor"** pairing.

## Major Works

- **The Conflict of the Ages** series — her magnum opus on salvation history (*Patriarchs and Prophets*,
  *Prophets and Kings*, *The Desire of Ages* [on the life of Christ], *The Acts of the Apostles*, and **The
  Great Controversy** [the cosmic conflict of Christ and Satan, and church history through the end]).
- **Steps to Christ** — her most widely circulated devotional.
- Extensive counsel on health, education, and the church.

## Hermeneutical Method

A **historicist** reading of biblical prophecy ([[daniel|Daniel]], [[revelation|Revelation]]) framed by
the "great controversy" metanarrative; the Bible read as sole canon, with her writings as confirming and
applying "lesser light."

## Influence

Foundational for the worldwide Seventh-day Adventist Church (~22 million); a major shaper of its Sabbath,
health, education, and eschatology, and of the broader conditional-immortality and historicist traditions.
