# George Thibaut

**George Thibaut** (1848–1914) was a German-born Indologist who spent his career in India (Benares,
then Allahabad) and produced the **standard English translations of the [[brahma-sutras|Brahma
Sutras]]** with their two great commentaries, in Max Müller's *Sacred Books of the East*. He is the
wiki's channel for Śaṅkara's and Rāmānuja's Vedanta in their own systematic words.

## Major Works

- ***The Vedānta-Sūtras with the Commentary of Śaṅkarācārya*** — SBE vols. **34 (1890)** and **38
  (1896)**.
- ***The Vedānta-Sūtras with the Commentary of Rāmānuja*** (the *Śrī-Bhāṣya*) — SBE vol. **48 (1904)**.
- Earlier work on the **Śulba-sūtras** (Vedic ritual geometry) and on Indian mathematics and astronomy.

## Hermeneutical Stance (and a Notable Scholarly Claim)

Thibaut translated as a **historical-critical Indologist**, not a confessional Vedantin, and his long
introductions weigh the commentators against the *sūtras* themselves. His most-cited (and contested)
thesis: on several points **[[ramanuja|Rāmānuja's]] theistic reading may stand closer to
[[brahma-sutras|Bādarāyaṇa's]] original intention than [[shankara|Śaṅkara's]] non-dualism** — i.e., the
Brahma Sutras may have been a theistic, not an Advaitic, text, with Advaita the later and more
philosophically powerful overlay. This is an early instance of the **modern critical** reading standing
apart from every traditional *bhāṣya*.

## Significance

By rendering both the Advaita and the Viśiṣṭādvaita commentaries on the *same* sutras, Thibaut let
readers see the wiki's central Hindu phenomenon directly: **one terse canon, irreconcilable systems**
(see [[brahman-atman-vedanta-schools|the comparison]]). [[swami-gambhirananda|Gambhirananda's]] later
Advaita Ashrama translations complement his older renderings.
