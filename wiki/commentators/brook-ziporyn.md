# Brook Ziporyn

**Brook Ziporyn** (b. 1964) is a philosopher and translator at the University of Chicago Divinity
School specializing in classical Chinese philosophy and Tiantai Buddhism. He is the author of
translations and studies of the [[daodejing|Daodejing]], the [[zhuangzi|Zhuangzi]], and Tiantai
Buddhist texts, as well as systematic philosophical work on the intersection of Chinese and Western
metaphysics.

## Major Works (ingested)

  See [[ziporyn-daodejing]].

## Hermeneutical Method

Ziporyn reads classical Daoist texts through what he calls the "minimally discernible position" —
the shared philosophical stance that can be abstracted from a corpus even if no single author wrote
it. He identifies the Daodejing's defining move as **ironic value inversion** (the A/B structure):
systematically valorizing the conventionally disvalued.

He operates with all major manuscript traditions simultaneously, treating variant readings not as
corruptions to adjudicate but as windows into a living, composite tradition.
