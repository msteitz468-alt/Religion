# Optatus of Milevus

Bishop of Milevus in North Africa, author of *De schismate Donatistarum* (366/367) against the Donatist leader Parmenianus — the chief Catholic ecclesiology before [[augustine|Augustine]] in the [[donatism|Donatist controversy]]. Drawn from [[kelly-early-christian-doctrines|J.N.D. Kelly]], ch. XV.

## Doctrine

- **Sacraments derive validity from God, not the minister:** in the Trinitarian formula "it is the Triune Godhead invoked… Who bestows the gift" — the Trinity is always present in the rite, regardless of the minister's worthiness. This anticipates Augustine's answer to [[donatism|Donatism]].
- **The Church's holiness lies in its endowments, not its members:** the Church is holy "not because of the character of those who belong to it, but because it possesses the symbol of the Trinity, the chair of Peter, the faith of believers, Christ's saving precepts, and… the sacraments." It is a mixed body (parable of the tares).
- **Catholicity and the *cathedra Petri*:** the true Church is marked by world-wide unity in communion with the see of Peter; the Donatists are "a fragment of Africa, a mere corner." He reckoned "the chair of Peter" among the Church's indispensable endowments — *claves… solus Petrus accepit* — the commission "first and uniquely conferred upon St. Peter," in which the other apostles and their successors "participate equally," ruling out rival chairs.
