# Averroes (Ibn Rushd)

Ibn Rushd (Latin **Averroes**, 1126–1198), the great Andalusian Aristotelian, defender of
**[[falsafa|falsafa]]** and "the Commentator" on Aristotle for the Latin West. He answered
**[[al-ghazali|al-Ghazali]]**'s *[[incoherence-of-the-philosophers|Tahafut al-Falasifa]]*
point by point in the **[[incoherence-of-the-incoherence|*Tahafut al-Tahafut*]]** ("The
Incoherence of the Incoherence").
