# Peter Smith

Academic scholar of the Bahá'í and Bábí faiths. Author of *A Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith* (Oneworld, 2000) and *A Short History of the Bahá'í Faith* (Oneworld, 1996). Smith writes from within the Bahá'í scholarly tradition; his works provide encyclopedic and historical overviews covering the full range of Bahá'í doctrine, history, administration, and biography.

## Major Works

- *A Short History of the Bahá'í Faith* (Oneworld, 1996) — narrative historical survey.
- *A Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith* (Oneworld, 2000) — alphabetically organized reference work covering figures, texts, concepts, locations, and administrative institutions of the Bábí and Bahá'í religions. Transliteration follows Bahá'í conventions.

## Hermeneutical Method

Smith writes in an academic register but within a sympathetic Bahá'í framework: he presents doctrinal positions as Bahá'í sources define them, while acknowledging historical complexity and variant accounts where they exist (e.g., on Subh-i-Azal's status; on the Báb's evolving claims).
