# Joel Hecker

Contemporary scholar of Jewish mysticism and Kabbalah; translator and annotator of **Volume 11** of the Pritzker Edition of the [[zohar|Zohar]] — *Midrash ha-Ne’lam and Zoharic Compositions* (Stanford University Press, 2016) — and of the majority of units in **Volume 12** (with [[nathan-wolski|Nathan Wolski]]). Standards reingest of Vol. XI: 2026-07-18 ([[hecker-zohar-pritzker-vol11|source page]]).

---

## Biographical and Scholarly Context

Hecker specializes in medieval Jewish mysticism, with particular expertise in literary and theosophic dimensions of the Zohar. His doctoral advisor was Elliot R. Wolfson (NYU). For the Pritzker project he constructed a critical Aramaic text with Jonatan Benarroch from manuscript and early-print variants. Unlike [[daniel-matt|Daniel C. Matt]] (main parashah commentary, I–IX) or [[nathan-wolski|Nathan Wolski]] (Torah *Midrash ha-Ne’lam*, Vol. X; Heikhalot/Piqqudin in XII), Hecker’s Vol. XI showcases **generic diversity** within the Zoharic library.

## Volume XI — verified contents (standards reingest 2026-07-18)

*Source: Hecker preface + body (cache lines 352–4768). Prior 2026-06-07 wiki summary incorrectly listed **Idra Zuta** among Vol. XI units; that unit is main-body Ha’azinu (Matt Vol. IX, ~3:287b–296b).*

Hecker’s preface framing (attributed):

> This eleventh volume in the series is the first to present a variety of Zoharic texts, reflecting the range of genres within the Zoharic library. Many of these sections first appeared in print in the publication that was titled *Zohar Ḥadash*, or *New Zohar*; this title is misleading, however, in that the volume comprises some of the earliest Zoharic material.

| Chapter | Character (Hecker + body) |
|---------|---------------------------|
| **MhN Song of Songs** | Short; kiss / four *ruḥot*; large *shin*; mostly Aramaic theosophy; non-sefirotic Ecclesiastes 12 close; likely intro to a larger unrealized work |
| **MhN Ruth** | Largest; soul origin/fate; Hell/Dumah; Shema, Grace after Meals, Qaddish for deceased; ten martyrs; threefold Torah method (later traditions altered toward PaRDeS); conversion/Gentile polemic; contested Zoharic status in early prints |
| **MhN Lamentations** | Israel vs Babylon eulogy contest; orphaned by Father/Mother; Hecker: subtle anti-Christian Holy Family reframe (Tif’eret–Shekhinah–Israel vs Father–Mary–Jesus) |
| **Zohar on Song of Songs** | Mature phase; Rashbi–Elijah on Song 1:1–11; ascent vs Other Side; letter mysticism; Cordovero ranks among most profound |
| **Matnitin** + **Tosefta** | First discrete collection in any language; oracular/hortatory; Hecker: **likely later** dense distillates, not early seeds |
| **Sitrei Torah** | Genesis-linked Other Side; later MhN-style ST print layers excluded from translation |

**Print history of MhN Ruth (Hecker preface):** largely absent from first Zohar prints (scattered Cremona 1558); Thiengen 1559 as *Yesod Shirim* / *Tappuḥei Zahav*; Venice 1565 as *Midrash ha-Ne’lam al Rut*; *Zohar Ḥadash* 1658.

**Hermeneutical contribution:** tracks Sod across **concealed midrash** (Megillot), **dialogue composition** (Rashbi–Elijah Song), **oracular “mystical Mishnah”** (Matnitin/Tosefta), and **anonymous ST** Other Side secrets — without collapsing them into main-body Companions theurgy.

## Contributions to Volume XII (Zoharic Compositions)

**Standards reingest 2026-07-18** densified Hecker units with coverage ledger and verbatim loci — source page [[wolski-hecker-zohar-pritzker-vol12|Wolski & Hecker Pritzker Vol XII]]. Hecker body units: Raza de-Razin (letter system + mutability quotes ~2:71a; ZḤ 35b–37c continuation); Sitrei Otiyyot (YHVH letter-chariots); Qav ha-Middah (Botsina de-Qardinuta); Merkevet Yeḥezqel (looking-glass prophecy); Zohar al ha-Parashot; seven Tosefta-style pieces.

Hecker translated and annotated the majority of units in Volume XII (*Zoharic Compositions*, with Wolski). Joint Introduction by Wolski and Hecker. Hecker handled Raza de-Razin, Sitrei Otiyyot, Qav ha-Middah, Merkevet Yeḥezqel, Zohar al ha-Parashot, and passages (mis)labeled as Tosefta (Wolski: Heikhalot Be-Reshit, Heikhalot Pequdei, Piqqudin).

These extend the composition register of Vol. XI’s Zohar on Song / Matnitin / Sitrei Torah — not the *Idra Zuta* (which is not a Vol. XI unit). See [[raza-de-razin|Raza de-Razin]], [[heikhalot|Heikhalot]], [[zohar|The Zohar]] Vol. XII section.

## Relation to the Broader Zohar and Wiki

Vol. XI deepens the portrait of the Zohar as a multi-genre library. MhN on Ruth/Lamentations/Song extends soul-, exile-, and glory-centered concealed midrash beyond Torah MhN (Vol. X). Zohar on Song of Songs supplies mature theosophic Song midrash and a body claim about words written **above** (Rashbi) vs **below** (Elijah by Rashbi’s hand) relevant to [[zohar-authorship|Zohar Authorship]]. Matnitin/Tosefta model oracular voice and Hell/liturgy densifications; Sitrei Torah models Other Side Genesis secrets.
