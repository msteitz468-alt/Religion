# Al-Farabi

Al-Farabi (Latin **Alpharabius**, c. 870–950), "the Second Teacher" (after Aristotle), was the
founding systematizer of Islamic **[[falsafa|falsafa]]** and of the **[[emanation|emanationist]]**
cosmology later developed by **[[avicenna|Avicenna]]**. In **[[al-ghazali|al-Ghazali]]**'s
*[[incoherence-of-the-philosophers|Tahafut]]* he is paired with Avicenna as the representative
of "the philosophers" whose metaphysics is refuted, Ghazali treating their Arabic Aristotelianism
as the most reliable transmission of the school.
