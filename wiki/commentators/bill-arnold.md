Bill T. Arnold is an evangelical Old Testament scholar at Asbury Theological Seminary and series editor of the New International Commentary on the Old Testament (from 2020). This wiki’s primary ingested work from him is *The Book of Deuteronomy, Chapters 1–11* (NICOT, 2022).

## Hermeneutical Method

Arnold combines **literary-rhetorical** reading of Deuteronomy’s discourse structure with **historical-critical** engagement (Wellhausen, Noth, Weinfeld, Lohfink, Otto, Römer, Sonnet, et al.) under confessional evangelical commitments. Text-critically he works from the Leningrad Codex and weighs LXX, SP, and DSS case-by-case, treating the LXX as an early witness to the history of interpretation and often as a divergent Hebrew *Vorlage*.

Relative to [[peter-craigie|Craigie]]’s 1976 NICOT Deuteronomy, Arnold is more explicit about multi-stage composition and manuscript pluriformity, and less reliant on second-millennium Hittite form as a decisive dating proof.

## Characteristic Positions (Deuteronomy 1–11 NICOT)

**Torah:** English “law” is inadequate; Torah is divine Will as grace, delivered primarily as **Moses’s speeches at Moab**, not only direct Sinai speech (5:6–21 is the major direct-YHWH block). Chs. 5–11 = paraenesis; 12–26 = detailed preached instructions (outside this volume).

**Composition / date:** Mosaic *authorization* (possibly farewell *ipsissima verba*) held open; *traditum/traditio* and scribal updating affirmed; *Urdeuteronomium* revising the Book of the Covenant; path through northern refugees and Hezekiah–Manasseh–Josiah; Josianic edition as early canonicity object. Rejects Noth’s isolation of Deut 1–3 as solely DtrH prologue; reads 1–4 as *relecture* introducing the Torah presentation at 4:44–49.

**Covenant / treaties:** ANE international treaties (Mendenhall six components; Neo-Hittite / EST transmission) form the background; Deuteronomy **transforms** political treaty into bilateral covenant relationship—not a mere treaty copy.

**Shema:** *ʾeḥad* as unified/stable divine self vs ANE fluidity of divine personhood; love with heart/soul/might as whole-person loyalty; love–fear as commandable.

**Ḥerem / election:** Election by YHWH’s love, not Israel’s merit; *ḥerem* and seven nations treated with ethical seriousness (including process/metaphorical readings Arnold argues in ch. 7).

**Education:** Deuteronomy devoted to the “life of the mind”; *lmd* learn/teach; parent-to-child recitation.
