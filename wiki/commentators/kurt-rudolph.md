# Kurt Rudolph

> **Commentator page** — Rudolph as a modern historian of religion. His single most
> influential work in this wiki's scope is summarized at
> [[rudolph-gnosis-the-nature-and-history-of-gnosticism|*Gnosis: The Nature and History of
> Gnosticism*]].

## Biographical Context

German historian of religion (b. 1929, Dresden; d. 2020), trained at Leipzig in the East German
*Religionswissenschaft* tradition and later professor at Marburg and UC Santa Barbara. A
leading 20th-century specialist in Gnosticism, Mandaeism, and the religions of late antiquity,
working in the wake of Hans Jonas and the history-of-religions ("Religionsgeschichtliche")
school.

## Hermeneutical Method

**Phenomenological + historical-critical.** Rudolph describes the ideology and mythology of a
religious phenomenon systematically first (its "nature"), then reconstructs its history. He
resists reducing [[gnosticism|Gnosis]] either to a Christian "heresy" (the Harnack line) or to
a single point of origin, emphasizing instead structural unity amid diversity and a
"syncretistic" convergence of Jewish apocalyptic, Hellenistic-Platonic, Iranian, and Christian
materials. He reads the [[nag-hammadi-library|Nag Hammadi]] texts as *primary gnostic voices*
that correct the polemical portraits of the heresiologists, and coined the wiki's working term
**"protest exegesis"** for the gnostic reading of scripture against its surface.

## Characteristic Positions

- Gnosis is a distinct *late-antique religion* (*spätantike Religion*), not merely a Christian
  offshoot.
- [[manichaeism|Manichaeism]] is Gnosis "in a new, world-historical form"; the Mandaeans are
  its only surviving community, a living relic.
- The origins question has no monocausal solution; the Sethian material in particular carries
  strong pre-/para-Christian Jewish coloring.

His account complements and partly challenges the patristic-heresiological and sociological
framings elsewhere in the wiki (see [[rodney-stark|Stark]], [[michael-kruger|Kruger]]) by
centering gnostic self-understanding. Recorded without adjudication (Contradiction Protocol).
