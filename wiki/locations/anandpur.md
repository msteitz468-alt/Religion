# Anandpur

The Shivalik-foothills town founded by [[guru-tegh-bahadur|Guru Tegh Bahadur]] that became the base of
[[guru-gobind-singh|Guru Gobind Singh]] and the birthplace of the [[khalsa|Khalsa]]. This page draws on
**Khushwant Singh, *A History of the Sikhs*, Vol. 1, Ch. 5**.

## In Sikh History (Khushwant Singh)

Tegh Bahadur "bought a hillock near the village of Makhowal, five miles north of Kiratpur… named it
*ānaṅdpur* (the haven of bliss)" (Singh, ch. 5). Under Gobind Singh it became an intellectual and military
centre, fortified with a chain of forts — **Anandgarh, Keshgarh, Lohgarh, Fatehgarh** — and home to
fifty-two court poets. It was here, at Baisakhi 1699, that Gobind Singh founded the Khalsa and administered
the first *amrit*. Besieged by the Mughal governor of [[sirhind|Sirhind]] and the allied hill rajas, the
Guru evacuated Anandpur on the night of 20–21 December 1704 under a safe-conduct that was promptly broken,
leading to the disaster at Chamkaur. Tegh Bahadur's severed head had earlier been cremated here at **Sīs
Gaṅj**. Singh notes that the [[nihang|Nihangs]] still gather at Anandpur in their thousands for the mock
battles of **Hola Mohalla**, the festival Gobind Singh ordained the day after Holi.
