# Jezreel

## Geographical and Historical Overview

"Jezreel" names two related things in the Hebrew Bible: a **broad agricultural valley** cutting
across northern Israel between the Galilee hills and the Samarian highlands, and a **town** at its
eastern end that functions in [[1-2-kings|1–2 Kings]] as the second residence of the Omride
kings. The valley is the country's principal east–west corridor and its most fought-over ground;
[[megiddo|Megiddo]] commands the pass at its western entrance. The town is where
[[ahab|Ahab]] keeps a palace beside the ancestral vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, where
[[jezebel|Jezebel]] is thrown from a window and eaten by dogs, and where [[jehu|Jehu]]'s purge of
the house of Omri begins and ends.

The name means "God sows," and the text plays on it: [[1-2-kings|Kings]] makes Jezreel the place
where the Omride dynasty's blood is spilled, and Hosea makes it the name of a child announcing that
the house of Jehu will itself be punished "for the blood of Jezreel."

## Primary Textual Appearances and Inhabitants

- **Josh 17:16; 19:18; Judg 6:33** — the valley/plain of Jezreel: Canaanite chariot country the
  tribes cannot clear, allotted to Issachar, and the mustering ground of the Midianite coalition
  against [[israelites|Israel]] in the Gideon narrative.
- **1 Sam 29:1, 11; 31:1-13** — the [[philistines|Philistines]] gather at Aphek and encamp by the
  spring in Jezreel before the battle in which [[saul|Saul]] and [[jonathan|Jonathan]] die on
  Mount Gilboa above the valley.
- **2 Sam 2:9; 1 Kgs 4:12** — Jezreel within Ish-bosheth's realm; later within Solomon's fifth
  administrative district. Ahinoam of Jezreel, one of [[david|David]]'s wives, is the mother of
  Amnon.
- **1 Kgs 18:45-46** — after the [[carmel|Carmel]] contest and the breaking of the drought,
  [[ahab|Ahab]] rides to Jezreel and [[elijah|Elijah]] girds up his loins and runs before him to
  the entrance of Jezreel.
- **1 Kgs 21** — **Naboth's vineyard.** Naboth the Jezreelite refuses to sell or exchange his
  ancestral inheritance, which lies beside Ahab's palace in Jezreel. Ahab sulks; [[jezebel|Jezebel]]
  writes letters in his name and seals them with his seal, proclaims a fast, seats Naboth at the
  head of the people, sets two worthless men to charge him with cursing God and the king, and has
  him stoned outside the city. Ahab goes down to take possession, and Elijah meets him there with
  the sentence: dogs will lick Ahab's blood in the place where they licked Naboth's, and **dogs
  will eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel**.
- **1 Kgs 21:27-29** — Ahab tears his clothes, puts on sackcloth and fasts, and the disaster is
  deferred to his son's days.
- **2 Kgs 8:29; 9:15** — Joram king of Israel, wounded by the [[arameans|Arameans]] at
  [[ramoth-gilead|Ramoth-gilead]], goes down to Jezreel to recover, and Ahaziah of Judah comes to
  visit him there. Both are in Jezreel when Jehu rides.
- **2 Kgs 9:14-37** — **Jehu's purge at Jezreel.** Anointed at Ramoth-gilead, Jehu drives
  furiously to Jezreel; the watchman on the tower reports the approach. Jehu shoots Joram between
  the shoulders so the arrow comes out at his heart and has the body thrown **on the plot of ground
  belonging to Naboth the Jezreelite**, in explicit fulfillment of the oracle. Ahaziah of Judah is
  shot as he flees. Jezebel hears, **paints her eyes, adorns her head, and looks out of the
  window**; at Jehu's word her eunuchs throw her down, her blood spatters the wall and the horses,
  and Jehu **tramples her**. When he sends to bury her, nothing is found but the skull, the feet,
  and the palms of her hands — the dogs having eaten the rest, as Elijah said.
- **2 Kgs 10:1-11** — the seventy sons of Ahab are beheaded in [[samaria|Samaria]] and their heads
  sent in baskets to Jehu at Jezreel, where he piles them in two heaps at the entrance of the gate
  and addresses the people over them; he then kills all who remained of Ahab's house in Jezreel.
- **Hos 1:4-5, 11; 2:22-23** — Hosea names a son **Jezreel**, "for in a little while I will punish
  the house of Jehu for the blood of Jezreel, and I will put an end to the kingdom of the house of
  Israel; and I will break the bow of Israel in the Valley of Jezreel." The name is later reversed
  in a restoration oracle, playing on "God sows."

## Modern Identification and Geography

The **valley** is secure: the Jezreel Valley (Greek *Esdraelon*) is the plain running northwest–
southeast between the Galilee hills and the Samarian highlands, opening to the Mediterranean
coastal plain past [[megiddo|Megiddo]] and descending eastward toward the Jordan. The biblical
notices fit it without strain — chariot country the highland tribes cannot take (Josh 17:16),
mustering ground for invading coalitions (Judg 6:33), and the approach to Mount Gilboa where Saul
falls (1 Sam 31).

The **town** is generally identified with **Tel Jezreel**, at the eastern end of the valley on the
northern slope below Mount Gilboa, adjacent to the site of the Arabic village **Zerʿin**, whose
name preserves the biblical name. The identification is name-based and topographical rather than
epigraphic, and it fits the narrative geography well: Jezreel lies a day's ride from
[[samaria|Samaria]], within sight of an approaching column across open ground (2 Kgs 9:17), below
the Gilboa ridge, and near a spring (1 Sam 29:1). The wiki records this as the **leading**
identification and does not declare the matter closed, since it holds no source arguing the case in
detail.

## Theological and Symbolic Significance

Jezreel is where the prophetic word in [[1-2-kings|Kings]] is shown to land on a map. Elijah's
sentence is pronounced *in* Naboth's vineyard and executed *on* Naboth's plot; the narrative
insists on the exact ground.

[[iain-provan|Provan]] (NIBC), as recorded on the [[1-2-kings|1–2 Kings]] and [[ahab|Ahab]] pages,
reads the Elijah–Elisha block as **salvation within judgment**, with "Naboth justice" named among
its load-bearing episodes, and reads [[jehu|Jehu]]'s purge as **incomplete** — Jehu executes the
oracle against Ahab's house and then keeps the calves of Bethel and Dan. Recorded as Provan's
position.

The canon does not speak with one voice about the blood shed here. Kings commends Jehu for doing
what was right in carrying out all that was in YHWH's heart against the house of Ahab; **Hos 1:4**
announces punishment on the house of Jehu **"for the blood of Jezreel."** The wiki records this as
a tension internal to the canon (as it is already recorded on [[jehu|Jehu]]) and does not
harmonize it. The name's own meaning, "God sows," carries the reversal: the valley of slaughter
becomes, in Hosea's restoration oracle, the ground of sowing.

## Key Events, Figures, and Controversies

- **Naboth's vineyard and the judicial murder** (1 Kgs 21) — [[naboth|Naboth]], [[ahab|Ahab]],
  [[jezebel|Jezebel]], [[elijah|Elijah]].
- **Elijah running before Ahab's chariot to Jezreel** after the [[carmel|Carmel]] contest
  (1 Kgs 18:45-46).
- **Joram's convalescence** after [[ramoth-gilead|Ramoth-gilead]] (2 Kgs 8:29).
- **Jehu's coup**: Joram shot and dumped on Naboth's plot; Ahaziah of Judah shot; **Jezebel thrown
  from the window, trampled, eaten by dogs**; the seventy heads heaped at the gate
  (2 Kgs 9–10) — [[jehu|Jehu]], [[omri|Omri]]'s house.
- **Saul's last campaign** in the valley and death on Gilboa (1 Sam 29; 31) — [[saul|Saul]],
  [[jonathan|Jonathan]], [[philistines|Philistines]].
- **Controversy — the evaluation of Jehu's purge.** Kings commends; Hos 1:4 condemns "the blood of
  Jezreel." Recorded as an unresolved canonical tension.
- **Controversy — site identification of the town.** Tel Jezreel / Zerʿin is the leading candidate
  on name-preservation and topographical grounds; the wiki does not declare it settled and holds no
  archaeological source on it.
