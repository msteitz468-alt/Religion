# Oriental Orthodoxy

> **Sect page.** The ancient non-Chalcedonian communion — distinct from [[eastern-orthodoxy|Eastern
> (Chalcedonian) Orthodoxy]]. *(Stub.)*

## Identity and Origins

The family of churches that **rejected the Council of Chalcedon (451)** and its "two natures" definition,
holding instead a **Miaphysite** Christology (Christ in *one* united nature, fully divine and fully human,
"without separation"). The communion comprises the **Coptic** (Egyptian), **Syriac**, **Armenian
Apostolic**, **Ethiopian** ([[ethiopian-orthodoxy|see its own page]]), **Eritrean**, and **Indian
(Malankara)** Orthodox churches.

## Relationship to the Parent Tradition

Among the oldest continuous Christian bodies, predating the Catholic/Eastern-Orthodox split. The breach
with the Chalcedonian churches was as much about the *language* of Christology (the legacy of **Cyril of
Alexandria** vs. the Chalcedonian formula) as about substance — modern dialogue has narrowed the gap, with
several joint declarations affirming a shared faith differently worded.

## Canon and Scripture (Canon Divergence)

Receives essentially the historic canon and the **first three ecumenical councils** (Nicaea,
Constantinople, Ephesus) — but **not** Chalcedon or the later councils the Chalcedonian churches accept.
Canonically, the [[ethiopian-orthodoxy|Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church]] is the outlier, with a notably
**broader Old Testament** (incl. [[1-enoch|1 Enoch]] and [[jubilees|Jubilees]]).

## Doctrinal Distinctives

**Miaphysitism** (one incarnate nature of the Word); veneration of **Cyril of Alexandria** and
Dioscorus; ancient liturgies (Coptic, West Syriac, Armenian, Ge'ez); rich monastic and ascetic traditions.

## Relationship to Orthodoxy and Other Sects

Long anathematized by, and anathematizing, the Chalcedonian churches as "Monophysite" / "Nestorianizing"
respectively — a 1,500-year Christological schism now substantially (if not fully) reconciled in dialogue.
Distinct again from the **Assyrian Church of the East** (the "Nestorian"/East Syriac church, which rejected
*Ephesus*). Recorded without adjudication (Contradiction Protocol).

## Influence

~60 million faithful; custodians of some of the oldest continuous Christian liturgy, art, and monasticism
(Egypt, Ethiopia, Armenia, Syria, Kerala).
