# Community of Christ

> **Sect page.** The second-largest denomination of the Latter Day Saint movement, distinct from the
> [[latter-day-saints|Utah LDS Church]]. This page resolves the `community-of-christ` slug used in the
> Mormon scriptures' `canon_scope`. *(Stub.)*

## Identity and Origins

When [[joseph-smith|Joseph Smith]] was killed in 1844, most Saints followed Brigham Young to Utah; a
remainder **rejected Young's leadership and the migration** and, in 1860, organized the **Reorganized
Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints (RLDS)** under Smith's son, **Joseph Smith III**.
Headquartered in **Independence, Missouri**, it renamed itself **Community of Christ** in 2001.

## Relationship to the Parent Tradition and to the LDS Church

Shares a founder with the [[latter-day-saints|LDS Church]] but diverges sharply: it **rejected plural
marriage** (long maintaining Joseph Smith never practiced it — historically contested), kept a different
line of prophetic succession, and over the 20th–21st centuries moved toward **mainline-Protestant,
Trinitarian, ecumenical** theology — ordaining women (1984), practicing open communion, and emphasizing a
**peace-and-justice mission** over distinctive Restoration doctrines.

## Canon and Scripture (Canon Divergence) — why the `canon_scope` differs from the LDS

This is the fine-grained intra-movement canon split the wiki's `canon_scope` captures:

- The **[[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]]** is scripture, but increasingly held as **secondary and
  non-obligatory** for belief (many members read it non-historically) — hence
  `deuterocanonical_for: [community-of-christ]` on its text page, not `canonical_for`.
- It maintains its **own [[doctrine-and-covenants|Doctrine and Covenants]]** — a *separate, still-growing*
  collection with different numbering, to which its prophet-presidents continue to add sections.
- It does **not** use the **[[pearl-of-great-price|Pearl of Great Price]]** at all — hence
  `rejected_by: [community-of-christ]` there.
- It prefers Joseph Smith's **"Inspired Version"** (the Joseph Smith Translation) of the Bible.

## Doctrinal Distinctives

Trinitarian (unlike the LDS Godhead); no temple-endowment or baptism-for-the-dead practice in the LDS
sense (its Independence Temple is dedicated to peace); a progressive social ethic; continuing revelation
through the prophet-president.

## Relationship to Orthodoxy and Other Sects

The principal "non-Brighamite" Restoration church; closer to mainline Protestantism than the LDS Church,
yet rooted in the same 1830 origin. Recorded without adjudication (Contradiction Protocol).

## Influence

~250,000 members worldwide; a notable example of a new-religious-movement **liberalizing** toward the
ecumenical mainstream — the mirror image of the LDS Church's distinctive trajectory.
