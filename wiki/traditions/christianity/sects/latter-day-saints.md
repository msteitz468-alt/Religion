# The Latter-day Saints (Mormonism)

> **Sect page.** The Restorationist movement founded by [[joseph-smith|Joseph Smith]]; the headline case for the wiki's `canon_scope` design. *Primary texts reingested 2026-07-17: the three additional standard works (public domain).*

## Identity and Origins

Founded in 1830 in upstate New York by **[[joseph-smith|Joseph Smith]]** (1805–1844), who reported the **First Vision** (JS-H), angelic visits from **Moroni**, and the translation of golden plates as the [[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]]. The movement holds that after a **[[great-apostasy|Great Apostasy]]** corrupted the early church, the true church and priesthood authority were **[[restoration-lds|restored]]** through Smith.

The largest body is the **Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints** (Salt Lake City; the "Brighamite" line under Brigham Young after 1844). Other branches — notably the **[[community-of-christ|Community of Christ]]** (formerly RLDS) and various fundamentalist groups — share the 1830 origin but diverge on succession, polygamy, and doctrine.

## Relationship to the Parent Tradition

Latter-day Saints **identify as Christian** (Jesus Christ as Savior; Articles of Faith 1, 3–4). [[catholicism|Catholic]], [[eastern-orthodoxy|Orthodox]], and most [[protestantism|Protestant]] bodies typically classify them as **outside historic Christianity** because of:
- added open-canon scripture;
- rejection of Nicene/Chalcedonian Trinitarian formulation in favor of three distinct beings ([[lds-godhead|LDS Godhead]]);
- [[exaltation-lds|exaltation]] / potential godhood of the faithful;
- temple ordinances unknown to the creedal churches.

Filed here as a Christianity sect (Restorationist) per self-understanding; the classification dispute is recorded on [[mormon-christian-identity]] (Contradiction Protocol).

## Canon and Scripture (Canon Divergence)

An **open canon**: the Bible (KJV preferred, "as far as it is translated correctly," AoF 8; with the **Joseph Smith Translation**) **plus** three additional standard works:

| Text | LDS standing | Community of Christ |
|------|--------------|---------------------|
| [[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]] | Full canon | Scripture, often graded secondary |
| [[doctrine-and-covenants|Doctrine and Covenants]] | Full canon (additive) | Own growing D&C |
| [[pearl-of-great-price|Pearl of Great Price]] | Full canon | Not used |

**[[continuing-revelation|Continuing revelation]]** through living prophets remains a canon principle (AoF 9; D&C growth). The Book of Mormon's claim to *continue* the biblical narrative is a reception fact on its text page, not a separate canon bucket.

## Doctrinal Distinctives (from the reingested standard works)

1. **[[lds-godhead|Godhead]]** — Father, Son, and Holy Ghost as three distinct personages; Father and Son with bodies of flesh and bones (D&C 130); First Vision of two Personages (JS-H).
2. **[[premortal-existence|Premortal existence]]** — eternal intelligences; noble and great ones; first/second estate (Abr 3; Moses 4 council).
3. **[[plan-of-salvation|Plan of salvation]]** — Fall as fortunate (2 Ne 2; Moses 5); probation; free agency; infinite atonement (Alma 34, 42); [[degrees-of-glory|three degrees of glory]] (D&C 76).
4. **[[exaltation-lds|Exaltation]]** — celestial fullness; "they are gods" (D&C 76:58; 132:20); comparative link to [[theosis-divine-nature|theosis]] without equating systems.
5. **Priesthood** — Aaronic and Melchizedek (D&C 13, 84, 107); living keys; sole earthly key-holder language in D&C 132.
6. **Temple** — baptism for the dead and welding link (D&C 128); eternal/celestial marriage (D&C 131–132); Kirtland keys of Moses, Elias, Elijah (D&C 110).
7. **[[zion-lds|Zion]]** — Independence center place (D&C 57); Enoch's city of one heart and no poor (Moses 7); New Jerusalem on the American continent (AoF 10; 3 Nephi).
8. **[[word-of-wisdom|Word of Wisdom]]** (D&C 89); **[[law-of-consecration|law of consecration]]** (D&C 42, 78, 82); tithing (D&C 119).
9. **Plural marriage** — taught in D&C 132; officially ended for the mainline LDS Church by Official Declaration 1 (1890; not in the PD edition ingested). See [[plural-marriage-lds]].

## Hermeneutical Method

- **Living prophet** and current apostolic teaching as authoritative alongside (and sometimes prioritizing over) past text.
- Bible subordinated where held mistranslated (AoF 8); supplemented by JST, BoM "plain and precious" restoration logic (1 Ne 13), and modern revelation.
- Book of Mormon methods: **likening**, **plainness**, typology, experimental faith (Alma 32), Moroni 10 spiritual confirmation.
- D&C methods: first-person revelatory speech; scripture expansion (John → D&C 76, 93).

## Relationship to Orthodoxy and Other Sects

Central boundary question: **are Mormons Christian?** — yes by LDS self-understanding and Christocentric scripture; no by creedal churches on canon, Trinity, and exaltation grounds. See [[mormon-christian-identity]].

Internal map: LDS (Brighamite) vs. [[community-of-christ|Community of Christ]] vs. fundamentalist (still-polygamous) offshoots. Community of Christ liberalized toward mainline Trinitarian Protestantism while retaining Restoration roots.

## Historical Development (sketch)

Palmyra/Manchester → Kirtland (temple, §76, §109–110) → Missouri Zion failures → Nauvoo (temple theology, §124–132) → martyrdom 1844 → succession crisis → Utah under Brigham Young → global church (~17 million self-reported in recent decades), missionary and temple culture. Official Declarations 1–2 mark major practice shifts (polygamy; 1978 priesthood).
