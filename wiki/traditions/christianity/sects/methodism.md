# Methodism

> **Sect page.** The Wesleyan evangelical movement out of 18th-century Anglicanism. The wiki's ingested
> Wesleyan-Arminian commentators (Oswalt, Green, Hartley, Cockerill) write in this stream. *(Stub.)*

## Identity and Origins

Began as a renewal movement within the [[anglicanism|Church of England]] led by **John Wesley** (and his
brother Charles), emphasizing disciplined holiness ("method"), open-air preaching, and the new birth;
it became a separate church after Wesley's death.

## Canon and Scripture

The **66-book Protestant canon**; no canon divergence. Its distinctive interpretive principle is the
**Wesleyan Quadrilateral** — Scripture as primary, illuminated by **tradition, reason, and experience**.

## Doctrinal Distinctives

**Arminian** soteriology against Calvinism — **prevenient grace** extended to all, grace that is
**resistible**, and the possibility of falling away (cf. the [[hebrews-warning-passages|warning passages]]
and the [[romans-7-identity|Romans 7]] debate); and Wesley's signature doctrine of **Christian perfection
/ entire sanctification** — that believers may be perfected in love in this life. Strong emphasis on
personal holiness and social reform.

## Relationship to Orthodoxy and Other Sects

The principal modern carrier of **Arminianism** against the Reformed [[presbyterianism|Calvinist]]
tradition; parent of the **Holiness** and (indirectly) the [[pentecostalism|Pentecostal]] movements.
Recorded without adjudication.

## Influence

A global family of ~80 million (United Methodist and many others); foundational for the Holiness movement,
the abolition and temperance movements, and the broader evangelical revival.
