# The Theban Cult of Amun

> **Sect page.** The imperial cult of **[[amun|Amun-Ra]]**, "king of the gods," centered on **Thebes** and
> the temple of **Karnak**. *(Stub.)*

## Amun-Ra, King of the Gods

**[[amun|Amun]]** ("the Hidden One"), originally a minor Theban and Hermopolitan god, rose with the Theban
dynasties to become the **supreme state god of New Kingdom Egypt** — merged with the sun-god [[ra|Ra]] as
**Amun-Ra**, "king of the gods." His vast temple complex at **Karnak** became the wealthiest and most
powerful religious institution in Egypt, and his **priesthood** grew so mighty that it eventually ruled
Upper Egypt directly.

## Hidden and Henotheistic

Amun's theology pushed toward a kind of **henotheism**: hymns exalt him as the one **hidden, transcendent**
power behind all the gods — "**one who made himself into millions**," whose true form and name are unknown
even to the other gods. This exaltation of a single supreme deity (while keeping the pantheon) is the
backdrop against which [[atenism|Akhenaten's]] radical reaction must be understood.
