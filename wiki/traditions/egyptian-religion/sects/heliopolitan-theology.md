# Heliopolitan Theology (the Ennead)

> **Sect page.** The creation theology of **Heliopolis** (Egyptian *Iunu*) — the most influential of the
> Egyptian systems. *(Stub.)*

## The Creation and the Ennead

At Heliopolis the cosmos begins with the **primeval waters of Nun**. From them the self-created sun-god
**[[ra|Atum-Ra]]** arises on the primeval mound and, alone, brings forth the first divine pair — **[[shu|Shu]]**
(air) and **[[tefnut|Tefnut]]** (moisture). Their children are **[[geb|Geb]]** (earth) and **[[nut|Nut]]**
(sky), and *their* children are the four great gods **[[osiris|Osiris]]**, **[[isis|Isis]]**, **[[set|Set]]**,
and **[[nephthys|Nephthys]]**. These nine — Atum and his eight descendants — are the **Ennead** (*Pesedjet*),
the foundational family of Egyptian myth and the cast of the [[osiris|Osiris myth]].

## Significance

The Heliopolitan system supplied the **genealogy** that underlies most Egyptian theology and the **solar
theology** of Ra that became central to kingship (the pharaoh as "son of Ra"). It is preserved in the
[[pyramid-texts|Pyramid Texts]], the oldest religious literature in the world.
