# Ancient Egyptian Religion

Top-level tradition page. **Ancient Egyptian religion** is one of the longest-lived religious systems in
human history — over **three thousand years** of polytheistic worship along the Nile. Its governing concern
was the maintenance of **[[maat|ma'at]]** (cosmic order, truth, justice, balance) against **isfet** (chaos
and disorder), upheld by the gods, the king, and proper cult — and, uniquely, an intense and elaborate
preoccupation with the **afterlife** and the survival of the dead.

## Character

Egyptian religion has **no creed and no single canon**. It is a religion of **cult, festival, kingship, and
funerary practice**, marked by:

- **Divine kingship** — the **pharaoh** is the living **[[horus|Horus]]**, son of **[[ra|Ra]]**, and after
  death an **[[osiris|Osiris]]** — the indispensable mediator who keeps ma'at and the gods' favor.
- **Local theologies and syncretism** — each great cult center had its own creation theology and chief god,
  and these were combined rather than rejected: gods merge (**[[amun|Amun-Ra]]**, **Ra-Horakhty**,
  **Ptah-Sokar-Osiris**) in a fluid **henotheism**.
- **The afterlife** — mummification, tombs, and a vast funerary literature (the [[book-of-the-dead|Book of
  the Dead]]) to guide the dead through the underworld (**Duat**) to the **[[maat|weighing of the heart]]**
  and eternal life.

## A Religion Without Scripture

There is no Egyptian "Bible," but a deep body of **authoritative funerary and ritual texts**:

- The **[[pyramid-texts|Pyramid Texts]]** (Old Kingdom; the oldest religious writings) → the **Coffin
  Texts** (Middle Kingdom) → the **[[book-of-the-dead|Book of the Dead]]** ("Book of Going Forth by Day,"
  New Kingdom).
- Hymns, the **Memphite Theology** (the Shabaka Stone), and the underworld "books" (Amduat, Book of Gates).

## The Great Myths and the Pantheon

- The **creation** — from the primeval waters (**Nun**), [[ra|Atum/Ra]] brings forth the first gods (the
  Heliopolitan **Ennead**).
- The **solar cycle** — [[ra|Ra]] sails the sky by day and the underworld by night, battling the chaos-serpent
  **[[apep|Apep]]** to be reborn each dawn.
- The **Osiris myth** — the heart of Egyptian religion: [[osiris|Osiris]] murdered by his brother
  [[set|Set]], revived by [[isis|Isis]], and avenged by their son [[horus|Horus]] — the charter of kingship,
  resurrection, and the afterlife.

See the deity pages and [[osiris|the Osiris myth]] for detailed narratives.

## Major Sects / Theological Systems

- [[heliopolitan-theology|Heliopolitan Theology]] — the **Ennead** of nine gods from [[ra|Atum-Ra]].
- [[hermopolitan-theology|Hermopolitan Theology]] — the **Ogdoad** and [[thoth|Thoth]].
- [[memphite-theology|Memphite Theology]] — creation by the word/heart of **[[ptah|Ptah]]**.
- [[theban-amun-cult|The Theban Cult of Amun]] — the imperial "king of the gods," [[amun|Amun-Ra]].
- [[osirian-cult|The Osirian / Afterlife Cult]] — [[osiris|Osiris]], the dead, and resurrection.
- [[atenism|Atenism]] — Akhenaten's revolutionary near-monotheism of the sun-disk **[[aten|Aten]]**.
