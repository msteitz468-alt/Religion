# Maya Religion

> **Sect page.** The religion of the **Maya**, preserved above all in the [[popol-vuh|Popol Vuh]].
> *(Ingested: the Popol Vuh.)*

## Character

The religion of the Classic Maya city-states and their descendants — a world of **divine kings** (who
performed **bloodletting** rites, drawing blood from their own bodies to feed and contact the gods),
sophisticated **astronomy and the Long Count calendar**, and a pantheon headed by the creator **[[itzamna|
Itzamna]]**, the rain-god **[[chaac|Chaac]]**, the moon-and-medicine goddess **[[ix-chel|Ix Chel]]**, the
**[[maize-god|Maize God]]**, and the death-gods of **[[xibalba|Xibalba]]**, the underworld.

## The Popol Vuh

The great surviving Maya sacred book, the **[[popol-vuh|Popol Vuh]]** of the K'iche' Maya, preserves the
**creation of the world**, the gods' repeated attempts to make humanity (failing with mud and wood before
succeeding with **maize**), and the epic of the **[[hero-twins|Hero Twins]]**, who descend into [[xibalba|
Xibalba]], defeat the lords of death, and become the sun and moon — the charter myth of Maya religion (see the
[[popol-vuh|Popol Vuh]] for the full narrative). Humanity, made of **maize dough**, exists to **nourish and
worship the gods**.
