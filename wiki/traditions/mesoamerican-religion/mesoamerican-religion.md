# Mesoamerican Religion

Top-level tradition page. **Mesoamerican religion** is the family of related polytheistic traditions of
pre-Columbian Mexico and Central America — the **Olmec, Maya, Aztec (Mexica), Zapotec, Mixtec, and Toltec**
peoples — sharing a common religious world over some three thousand years until the Spanish conquest (1521).
The two best-documented are the **[[maya-religion|Maya]]** and the **[[aztec-religion|Aztec]]**.

## Shared Worldview

- **Feeding the gods with sacrifice.** The cosmos is **fragile and in constant danger of collapse**; the
  gods sacrificed themselves to create the world and the sun, and humans must **repay them with sacrifice** —
  offerings, **autosacrifice** (drawing one's own blood), and, especially among the [[aztec-religion|Aztecs]],
  **human sacrifice** — to keep the **sun moving** and the world alive. Blood is the sacred energy that
  sustains the universe.
- **Cyclical time and world-ages.** History runs in **great cycles of creation and destruction**: the present
  world is one of several successive "**suns**" (ages), each destroyed in catastrophe, and destined to end —
  a deeply **cyclical** cosmology unlike the linear time of the Abrahamic faiths.
- **The sacred calendar.** A sophisticated interlocking calendar — the **260-day ritual count** (*tzolkin*/
  *tonalpohualli*) meshed with the **365-day solar year**, and (among the Maya) the **Long Count** — governed
  ritual, divination, fate, and the destiny of each person born on a given day. Mesoamerica's astronomy and
  calendrics were among the most advanced in the ancient world.
- **A layered cosmos**: thirteen heavens above, the earth, and nine underworlds below ([[xibalba|Xibalba]] for
  the Maya, Mictlan for the Aztecs), with a world-tree and four directional quarters, each with its color,
  god, and bird.

## Texts

Most pre-conquest books (codices) were **burned by the Spanish**; the religion survives through a few
surviving codices (the Dresden, Madrid, and Borgia codices), the colonial-era Aztec ethnography of **Bernardino
de Sahagún** (the Florentine Codex), and above all the **[[popol-vuh|Popol Vuh]]**, the great K'iche' Maya
book of creation and the Hero Twins.

## The Pantheons

- **Shared**: the **[[quetzalcoatl|Feathered Serpent]]** (Aztec Quetzalcoatl / Maya Kukulkan), the rain-god
  ([[tlaloc|Tlaloc]] / [[chaac|Chaac]]), the **maize god**, the sun, and the gods of death.
- **[[aztec-religion|Aztec]]**: the war-and-sun god **[[huitzilopochtli|Huitzilopochtli]]**, the fate-god
  **[[tezcatlipoca|Tezcatlipoca]]**, [[tlaloc|Tlaloc]], [[xipe-totec|Xipe Totec]], [[coatlicue|Coatlicue]],
  [[mictlantecuhtli|Mictlantecuhtli]].
- **[[maya-religion|Maya]]**: the creator **[[itzamna|Itzamna]]**, **[[chaac|Chaac]]**, **[[ix-chel|Ix
  Chel]]**, the **[[hero-twins|Hero Twins]]**, the **[[maize-god|Maize God]]**, and the death-god
  **[[ah-puch|Ah Puch]]**.
