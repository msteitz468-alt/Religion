# Orphism

> **Sect page.** The reforming, text-based theological current attributed to the mythic poet **Orpheus**.
> *(Ingested: the [[orphic-hymns|Orphic Hymns]].)*

## Identity and Origins

A distinctive, somewhat **ascetic and doctrinal** strand of Greek religion (from c. the 6th century BCE)
that — unusually — possessed **sacred writings** (Orphic theogonies, hymns, and the gold-leaf afterlife
tablets) attributed to **Orpheus**. It overlaps with the [[dionysian-mysteries|Dionysian]] cult.

## Distinctive Doctrine

Orphism is the Greek tradition that most resembles a **salvation religion of the soul**:

- The **soul is immortal and divine**, imprisoned in the body (*sōma/sēma*, "the body is a tomb") and
  subject to **transmigration** (*metempsychosis*) through many lives.
- A myth of **original sin**: humanity arose from the ashes of the **Titans** who devoured the infant
  **[[dionysus|Dionysus]]** — so each person is part Titanic (evil) and part Dionysian (divine), and must
  purify the divine part.
- **Salvation** through initiation, ascetic purity (vegetarianism, ritual rules), and right living, leading
  to release from the cycle and a blessed afterlife.

## Significance

Orphism deeply influenced **Pythagoras** and **Plato** (the immortal soul, judgment,
reincarnation) and is a striking pre-Christian parallel to ideas of original sin, the imprisoned soul, and
salvation — frequently compared with [[gnosticism|Gnostic]] and [[kabbalah|Kabbalist]] soul-doctrines.
