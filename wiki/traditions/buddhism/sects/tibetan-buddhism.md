# Tibetan Buddhism

> **Umbrella page.** The Indo-Tibetan form of [[mahayana|Mahayana]] and [[vajrayana|Vajrayana]]
> Buddhism, organized into several schools. Its major orders include the older **[[nyingma|Nyingma]]**
> (with **[[dzogchen|Dzogchen]]**) and the "new transmission" schools, among which the
> **[[gelug|Gelug]]** of **[[tsongkhapa|Tsong-kha-pa]]** became dominant. The framework of the
> **graded path** (lamrim) derives from **[[atisha|Atiśa]]** and the Kadam lineage.

## Overview

Tibetan Buddhism integrates the full Indian Mahayana scholastic inheritance ([[madhyamaka|Madhyamaka]],
[[yogacara|Yogācāra]], the [[bodhisattva|bodhisattva]] path of the [[six-perfections|six perfections]])
with Vajrayana tantra, transmitted through teacher–disciple lineages. Its canon comprises the
**Kangyur** (translated word of the Buddha) and the **Tengyur** (translated treatises).

## Major Schools

- **[[nyingma|Nyingma]]** — the "ancient" school of the first diffusion; home of [[dzogchen|Dzogchen]].
- **[[gelug|Gelug]]** — the reform school of Tsong-kha-pa; lineage of the Dalai and Panchen Lamas.
