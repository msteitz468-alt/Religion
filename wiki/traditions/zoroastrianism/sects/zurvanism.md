# Zurvanism

> **Sect page.** A heterodox Zoroastrian current resolving the dualism into a prior unity.
> *(Stub — known mainly through hostile/external reports.)*

## Identity and Origins

Zurvanism made **Zurvan** — infinite **Time** (and Space) — the **first principle**, the parent who
generated both **Ohrmazd** (Ahura Mazda) and **Ahriman** (Angra Mainyu). It appears to have been
influential in the Sasanian period, perhaps even a court orthodoxy at times, before fading.

## Relationship to the Parent Tradition

A reinterpretation of [[zoroastrianism|Zoroastrianism]]'s **cosmic dualism**: where orthodox (Mazdean)
theology keeps Ahura Mazda the uncreated good God opposed by a separate hostile spirit, Zurvanism places a
**single ultimate source** (Time) *behind* the two — a monism upstream of the dualism, often coupled with
**fatalism**.

## Canon and Scripture

No canon divergence — the same Avesta and [[gathas|Gathas]], read through the Zurvanite cosmogonic myth.

## Doctrinal Distinctives

Zurvan as the prior unity; the twin spirits as his offspring; a strong strain of **fatalism** (the rule of
Time over destiny).

## Relationship to Orthodoxy and Other Sects

Condemned as heterodox by later Mazdean orthodoxy, which reaffirmed the uncreated supremacy of Ahura
Mazda. Recorded without adjudication (Contradiction Protocol).

## Historical Development

Largely **extinct**; reconstructed from Zoroastrian polemics and outside (Armenian, Syriac, Arabic)
reports. A standing question in scholarship is how widespread it really was.
