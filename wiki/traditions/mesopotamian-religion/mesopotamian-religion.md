# Ancient Mesopotamian Religion

Top-level tradition page. **Mesopotamian religion** is the polytheistic system of ancient **Sumer, Akkad,
Babylonia, and Assyria** (c. 3500 BCE onward) — the religion of the world's first cities and first writing,
and a deep substratum beneath the later religions of the Near East (and behind several themes in the
[[genesis|Hebrew Bible]]; see [[genesis-ancient-near-east|the Genesis/ANE comparison]]).

## Worldview

- **Humanity made to serve the gods.** Humans were created (from **clay** mixed with the blood of a slain
  god) for one purpose: to **do the gods' labor** — to feed and house them through temple offerings, so the
  gods could rest. Religion is the **maintenance of the gods**, and the city's prosperity depends on it.
- **Gods as cosmic and civic powers.** The great gods are personified forces of nature and patrons of cities
  ([[enlil|Enlil]] of Nippur, [[marduk|Marduk]] of Babylon, [[ishtar|Inanna/Ishtar]] of Uruk), organized as
  a divine assembly under a supreme god — who **changes with the dominant power**: Sumerian [[enlil|Enlil]] →
  Babylonian [[marduk|Marduk]] → Assyrian [[ashur|Ashur]].
- **Divination.** The gods write their will into the world — in the **entrails of sacrificed animals
  (extispicy)**, the **stars (astrology)**, dreams, and omens of every kind. Mesopotamia **invented
  systematic divination** and astronomy, the central means of knowing the gods.
- **A bleak afterlife.** The dead descend to a dark, dusty **underworld** (Kur / Irkalla, the "Land of No
  Return"), where all alike eat dust — no judgment, no reward, no escape. There is **no hope of resurrection**
  (the sharpest contrast with [[egyptian-religion|Egypt]]).

## A Religion Without Scripture — but Great Myths

No canon, but a rich body of myth and ritual in cuneiform:

- The **[[enuma-elish|Enuma Elish]]** — the Babylonian **creation epic**: [[marduk|Marduk]] slays the
  chaos-dragon **[[tiamat|Tiamat]]** and forms the world from her body.
- The **[[epic-of-gilgamesh|Epic of Gilgamesh]]** — the great epic of mortality, friendship, and the futile
  quest for **immortality**, containing the **Flood** story (Utnapishtim).
- **The Descent of [[ishtar|Inanna/Ishtar]]** — the goddess's journey to the underworld and back.
- **Atrahasis** — creation of humanity and the Flood (see [[genesis-ancient-near-east|ANE parallels]]).

## Major Sects / Cultural-Historical Strata

- [[sumerian-religion|Sumerian Religion]] — the oldest stratum; the An–Enlil–Enki pantheon.
- [[babylonian-religion|Babylonian Religion]] — [[marduk|Marduk]] supreme; the Enuma Elish.
- [[assyrian-religion|Assyrian Religion]] — the national god [[ashur|Ashur]]; an imperial, militant cult.
