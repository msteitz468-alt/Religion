# The Church of Scientology

> **Sect page.** The official organization of [[scientology|Scientology]]. *(Stub.)*

## Identity and Origins

The corporate-religious organization founded by [[l-ron-hubbard|L. Ron Hubbard]] (first churches 1953–54)
and the steward of "the tech." Its structure includes **Church of Scientology International** (the
management church), the **Religious Technology Center (RTC)** — which **owns the Dianetics and Scientology
trademarks** and polices orthodoxy — and the **Sea Organization (Sea Org)**, a dedicated cadre whose
members sign symbolic **billion-year contracts**. Since Hubbard's death in 1986 it has been led by **David
Miscavige** (chairman of RTC).

## Relationship to the Parent Tradition

The **normative, trademark-holding** body that defines official Scientology — over against the
[[free-zone-scientology|Free Zone]] of independents it regards as unauthorized.

## Canon and Scripture

Holds the **entire Hubbard corpus ("Source")** as scripture, including the **confidential OT materials**,
access to which is gated by progress on the **Bridge** and by payment. Trademarks and "standard tech"
ensure central control over what counts as authentic.

## Doctrinal / Organizational Distinctives

Exclusive authority over the application of [[auditing|auditing]] and the upper levels; a tightly managed
organization (orgs, missions, "ethics," the Rehabilitation Project Force); the policy
**"Keeping Scientology Working"** mandating exact, unaltered application of the tech.

## Relationship to Orthodoxy and Other Sects

Regards [[free-zone-scientology|Free Zone]] practitioners as heretical "**squirrels**" (those who alter the
tech) and pursues trademark/copyright actions against them. Recorded without adjudication.

## Influence and Controversy

A globally visible, wealthy, and controversial organization — known for celebrity members, real-estate
holdings, aggressive litigation, and the controversies documented on the
[[scientology-religion-or-commercial-enterprise|status-controversy]] page.
