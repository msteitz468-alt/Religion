# The Free Zone (Independent Scientology)

> **Sect page.** Scientologists who practice "the tech" *outside* the official
> [[church-of-scientology|Church]]. *(Stub.)*

## Identity and Origins

A loose movement of **independent Scientologists** who left (or were expelled from) the official
[[church-of-scientology|Church of Scientology]] — major breakaways began in the early 1980s — but continue
to use [[l-ron-hubbard|Hubbard's]] methods. Notable strands include **Ron's Org** (founded by ex–Sea Org
officer Bill Robertson) and various independent auditors.

## Relationship to the Parent Tradition

Claims to preserve **authentic Scientology** against what it sees as the corruption of the tech under
**David Miscavige's** management — the mirror-image dispute to the Church's view of *them*.

## Canon and Scripture

Uses the **same Hubbard corpus** as the official Church; some Free Zone groups **alter or extend** the tech
(new upper levels), and some circulate the otherwise-confidential **OT materials** more freely — the very
thing the official Church litigates against.

## Relationship to Orthodoxy and Other Sects

The official Church brands Free Zoners **"squirrels"** (alterers of standard tech) and pursues
trademark/copyright enforcement; Free Zoners regard the Church as having betrayed Hubbard. A textbook
sect-vs-parent dispute over *who keeps the tradition* — recorded without adjudication (Contradiction
Protocol).

## Influence

Small and decentralized, but significant for the wiki's purposes: it shows that even a tightly
trademark-controlled new religion generates a **schismatic "independent" stream**, exactly as older
traditions do.
