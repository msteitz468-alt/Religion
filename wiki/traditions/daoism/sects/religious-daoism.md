# Religious Daoism

> **Sect page.** The organized Daoist religion (*daojiao*) — pantheon, priesthood, alchemy, and the
> quest for immortality. *(Stub — Heshang Gong's commentary is untranslated; see Outstanding Sources.)*

## Identity and Origins

*Daojiao*, the "teaching of the Dao" as an organized religion, begins with the **Celestial Masters**
(*Tianshi*) movement founded by **Zhang Daoling** (2nd c. CE), who reported a revelation from the
deified [[laozi|Laozi]] (Taishang Laojun). It develops a pantheon, an ordained priesthood, scriptures
(the vast **Daozang**, Daoist Canon), liturgy, and techniques for attaining **immortality** (*xian*).

## Relationship to the Parent Tradition

The devotional-ritual-soteriological pole of [[daoism|Daoism]]; it reads the same classics as the
[[philosophical-daoism|philosophical]] stream but for a different end — physical and spiritual
transformation, not (only) wisdom.

## Canon and Scripture (Canon Divergence)

Retains the [[daodejing|Daodejing]] and [[zhuangzi|Zhuangzi]] but adds an enormous canon — the
**Daozang** of revealed scriptures, ritual manuals, and alchemical treatises (Shangqing, Lingbao
revelations). It reads the Daodejing itself as a **manual for cultivating the body**: hence Heshang
Gong's longevity commentary.

## Doctrinal Distinctives

The pursuit of **immortality** through **internal alchemy** (*neidan* — refining *qi*, *jing*, and
*shen* within the body) and earlier **external alchemy** (*waidan* — elixirs); a celestial bureaucracy
of gods; talismans, liturgy, and meditation. Major later schools: **Shangqing** (Highest Clarity),
**Lingbao** (Numinous Treasure), and **Quanzhen** (Complete Perfection, 12th c.).

## Hermeneutical Method

**Commentary-as-transformation** in the strongest sense: **[[heshang-gong|Heshang Gong's]]** commentary
reads the Daodejing's "valley spirit" and "mysterious female" as the adept's own inner organs and
energies — the text as a physiological-spiritual technology, not metaphysics. The opposite pole from
[[wang-bi|Wang Bi]]; see [[wang-bi-vs-heshang-gong|the controversy]].

## Relationship to Orthodoxy and Other Sects

Shares the classics with philosophical Daoism; absorbed and competed with Buddhism; sponsored by various
imperial courts. Recorded without adjudication (Contradiction Protocol).

## Historical Development and Influence

From the Celestial Masters through the Shangqing and Lingbao revelations to the still-living **Quanzhen**
and **Zhengyi** lineages; the matrix of Chinese alchemy, medicine, and liturgical culture.
