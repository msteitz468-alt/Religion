# Daoism

Top-level tradition page. **Daoism** (Taoism) is the Chinese tradition oriented to the **Dao** ("the
Way") — the nameless, generative source and pattern of all things — and to living in spontaneous accord
with it. It is hermeneutically the most foreign tradition to the wiki's Abrahamic core: there is no
creator, no commandment, no covenant, and the founding text opens by declaring that **"the Dao that can
be spoken is not the eternal Dao."** This page is the parent node for the Daoist sects under
`traditions/daoism/sects/`.

## Two Streams (and the canon they share)

Scholars (and the Chinese terms) distinguish two streams that nonetheless share the classics:

- **[[philosophical-daoism|Philosophical Daoism]]** (*daojia*) — the Lao-Zhuang tradition of the
  [[daodejing|Daodejing]] and [[zhuangzi|Zhuangzi]], read as wisdom/metaphysics (later the *xuanxue*
  "Dark Learning" of Wang Bi).
- **[[religious-daoism|Religious Daoism]]** (*daojiao*) — the organized religion (from the 2nd-c.
  Celestial Masters onward): a pantheon, priesthood, liturgy, alchemy, and the quest for **immortality**
  through cultivation of *qi*, read into the same classics (Heshang Gong's commentary).

These two read the *same* [[daodejing|Daodejing]] in opposite registers — see
[[wang-bi-vs-heshang-gong|the Wang Bi vs. Heshang Gong controversy]] — a "one canon, two readings"
pattern like Hinduism's, but here philosophical-vs-soteriological rather than metaphysical-vs-metaphysical.

## Core Concepts (in place of theology)

- **[[dao|Dao]]** — the ineffable Way, source and course of all things.
- **[[wu-wei|Wu-wei]]** — "non-action"/effortless action; acting in accord with the grain of things.
- **[[ziran|Ziran]]** — "self-so," spontaneity/naturalness.
- **De** — virtue/power, the Dao as embodied in a thing; *yin-yang* and *qi* as the dynamics of the
  cosmos.

## Native Hermeneutical Framework

**Commentary-as-transformation** (CLAUDE.md): the great commentaries are not neutral glosses but
*practices* — Wang Bi's reading cultivates metaphysical insight; Heshang Gong's cultivates the adept's
body and longevity. Reading the text is meant to *change* the reader.
