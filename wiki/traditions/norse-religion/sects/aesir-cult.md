# The Æsir Cult

> **Cult-form page.** The worship of the **Æsir**, the principal Norse gods of sovereignty, war, and order —
> above all **[[odin|Odin]]** and **[[thor|Thor]]**. *(Ingested: the [[poetic-edda|Poetic]] and
> [[prose-edda|Prose Eddas]].)*

## The Gods of Sovereignty and War

The Æsir are the dominant divine family of [[norse-religion|Norse religion]]: the All-Father **[[odin|Odin]]**
(war, death, wisdom, poetry, magic, kingship), the thunder-god **[[thor|Thor]]** (protection, strength, the
common people's god), the sky/justice god **[[tyr|Týr]]**, the watchman **[[heimdall|Heimdall]]**, the radiant
**[[baldr|Baldr]]**, the queen **[[frigg|Frigg]]**, and the ambiguous trickster **[[loki|Loki]]**. Their
worship reflected a society's needs: **Odin** for kings, warriors, and poets; **Thor** for farmers and for
protection against chaos.

## Cult and Afterlife

Worship was **blót** — seasonal sacrifice and feasting (the great temple at **Uppsala** being the most famous
center). The cult promised the warrior a glorious afterlife: those who died bravely were taken by the
**Valkyries** to **[[valhalla|Valhalla]]**, Odin's hall, to feast and fight until **[[ragnarok|Ragnarök]]** —
sacralizing courage and a good death.
