# Ásatrú / Heathenry

> **Modern-revival page.** **Ásatrú** ("faith in the Æsir") is the modern reconstructed revival of
> [[norse-religion|Norse religion]] — a living new religious movement. *(Stub.)*

## Revival

Norse paganism died out with medieval Christianization, but was **revived in the 1970s** — formally recognized
in **Iceland (Ásatrúarfélagið, 1973)** and independently in the US, UK, and Scandinavia ("Heathenry,"
"Forn Siðr"). Practitioners **reconstruct** the religion from the surviving medieval sources — the
[[poetic-edda|Poetic]] and [[prose-edda|Prose Eddas]], the sagas, and skaldic poetry — and worship the
**Æsir and Vanir** ([[odin|Odin]], [[thor|Thor]], [[freyja|Freyja]], etc.) through revived rites: the **blót**
(offering) and the **sumbel** (ritual toasting).

## A Divided Movement

Heathenry is sharply **divided** between an **inclusive/universalist** majority (the gods and folkways are
open to all) and a **"folkish"** minority that ties the religion to Germanic ancestry or ethnicity — a split
that has produced ongoing controversy, since Norse symbols have also been appropriated by far-right and
white-nationalist groups (which most Heathen organizations explicitly repudiate). Recorded without
adjudication per the Contradiction Protocol.
