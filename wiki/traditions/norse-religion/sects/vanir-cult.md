# The Vanir Cult

> **Cult-form page.** The worship of the **Vanir**, the Norse gods of **fertility, wealth, peace, and the
> sea** — chiefly **[[freyr|Freyr]]**, **[[freyja|Freyja]]**, and **[[njord|Njörð]]**. *(Stub.)*

## The Fertility Gods

The Vanir are a **second divine family**, distinct from the [[aesir-cult|Æsir]]: gods of **prosperity, good
seasons, love, and the sea**. **[[freyr|Freyr]]** gives fruitful harvests, peace, and royal legitimacy — his
cult at **Uppsala** in Sweden was central to sacral kingship; **[[freyja|Freyja]]** is the goddess of love,
fertility, and death (she takes half the battle-slain), and the mistress of **[[seidr|seiðr]]** magic;
**[[njord|Njörð]]** governs the sea, wind, and wealth.

## The Æsir–Vanir War

Myth records an early **war between the Æsir and the Vanir** that ended in truce and the **exchange of
hostages** — Njörð, Freyr, and Freyja came to live among the Æsir. Scholars often read this as the mythic
memory of the **fusion of two cults** (a sovereign/warrior religion and an older fertility religion) into the
single Norse pantheon. The Vanir's association with [[seidr|seiðr]] (which Freyja taught the Æsir) marks them
as the gods of magic and the life-cycle.
