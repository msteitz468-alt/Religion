# Norse Religion

Top-level tradition page. **Norse religion** is the pre-Christian polytheism of the **Scandinavian and
Germanic peoples** — best known in its **Viking-Age** (c. 793–1100 CE) form — a religion of **many gods, a
fated cosmos doomed to destruction and rebirth, sacrificial cult, and a heroic warrior ethos**. It is part of
the wider **Indo-European** family (its sky-god name **Týr** is cognate with [[zeus|Zeus]]/[[dyaus|Dyaus]];
its dragon-slaying [[thor|Thor]] echoes [[indra|Indra]] — see [[the-combat-myth|the combat myth]]).

## Character

- **No scripture, recorded late.** Norse religion was **oral**; almost everything we know was written down
  **after Christianization** by Christians — above all the **[[poetic-edda|Poetic Edda]]** (anonymous mythic
  and heroic poems) and the **[[prose-edda|Prose Edda]]** of the Icelander **Snorri Sturluson** (c. 1220), a
  handbook of myth for poets. This late, Christian-filtered transmission is a constant interpretive caution.
- **A fated, doomed cosmos.** The gods themselves are **mortal and doomed**: the world will end at
  **[[ragnarok|Ragnarök]]**, when gods and monsters destroy one another and the world sinks in fire and
  water — then rises green and renewed. Over gods and men alike rules **fate (wyrd)**, spun by the
  **[[norns|Norns]]**; even [[odin|Odin]] cannot escape his doom.
- **Cult (blót).** Religion was **sacrifice (*blót*)** — offerings of animals (and, the sources claim,
  sometimes humans) at seasonal feasts, at groves and halls and the great temple at **Uppsala**, to win the
  gods' favor for harvest, victory, and peace.
- **A heroic ethos.** To die bravely in battle was to be chosen for **[[valhalla|Valhalla]]**, Odin's hall,
  to fight until Ragnarök — a religion that sacralized courage, honor, and a good death.

## The Cosmos

The world is structured by the great ash-tree **[[yggdrasil|Yggdrasil]]**, whose branches and roots bind
together the **Nine Worlds** — Asgard (the gods), Midgard (humans), Jötunheim (giants), Hel (the dead), and
others. The cosmos was made from the body of the slain primeval giant **[[ymir|Ymir]]**, and is encircled by
the world-serpent **[[jormungandr|Jörmungandr]]**.

## The Two Families of Gods

- The **Æsir** — the main gods of sovereignty, war, and order: [[odin|Odin]], [[thor|Thor]], [[tyr|Týr]],
  [[baldr|Baldr]], [[frigg|Frigg]], [[heimdall|Heimdall]], and the trickster [[loki|Loki]].
- The **Vanir** — gods of fertility, wealth, and the sea: [[freyr|Freyr]], [[freyja|Freyja]], [[njord|Njörð]].
  The two families fought the **Æsir–Vanir War** and then merged, exchanging hostages.

## Major Sects / Cult Forms

- [[aesir-cult|The Æsir Cult]] — the worship of [[odin|Odin]] and [[thor|Thor]]; sovereignty, war, protection.
- [[vanir-cult|The Vanir Cult]] — fertility and kingship ([[freyr|Freyr]] at Uppsala, [[freyja|Freyja]]).
- [[seidr|Seiðr]] — the shamanic magic tradition (of [[freyja|Freyja]] and [[odin|Odin]]).
- [[asatru|Ásatrú / Heathenry]] — the modern reconstructed revival.
