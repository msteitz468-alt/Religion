# Druidism (the druidic priesthood)

The **druids** were the **priestly-learned class** of [[celtic-religion|Celtic religion]] — the nearest thing
the Celts had to a clergy and a "church." Our knowledge is almost entirely **secondhand**: Greco-Roman writers
(Caesar's *Gallic War*, Posidonius, Strabo, Pliny, Lucan) and, for Ireland, the survival of the druids'
heirs — the **filid** (learned poets) — in early Irish law and saga.

## The learned orders

Classical sources distinguish three grades: **druids** (priests, judges, teachers, philosophers), **bards**
(poets and praise-singers), and **vates / *ovates*** (diviners, seers) — paralleled in Ireland by **druí**,
**fili**, and **fáith**. Training was **oral and immense** — Caesar reports up to **twenty years** of
memorization — because the druids **forbade committing their sacred lore to writing**, the single greatest
reason Celtic religion left no scripture of its own.

## Doctrine and practice

- **Immortality of the soul**: the doctrine the classical writers stress most — the soul does not perish but
  **passes on** (often read as transmigration). Caesar links Gaulish fearlessness in battle to it; others note
  debts repayable **in the next life**.
- **Sacrifice** at **groves (*nemeton*)**, springs, and rivers — including the human sacrifice luridly
  reported by Roman propaganda (the "wicker man" of Caesar; bog bodies in the archaeology).
- **Calendar and astronomy** — the **Coligny calendar**, a bronze luni-solar reckoning, and festival
  timekeeping (Samhain, Imbolc, Beltane, Lughnasadh).
- **Judgment and arbitration** — druids settled disputes and could **excommunicate** by barring offenders from
  sacrifice.

## Suppression and survival

Rome **outlawed** druidism in Gaul and Britain (Tiberius, Claudius), destroying the great druidic center on
**Anglesey (Môn)** in 60/61 CE. In **Ireland**, never Romanized, the order survived the longest, its poetic
and legal functions passing into the Christian-era **filid** who, ironically, preserved the very myths the
druids would not write.
