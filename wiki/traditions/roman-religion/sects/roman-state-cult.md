# The Roman State Cult

> **Sect page.** The official public religion of Rome, run by the priestly colleges. *(Stub.)*

## Identity

The **public *religio*** of the Roman state — the maintenance of right relations with the gods (the **pax
deorum**, "peace of the gods") on which Rome's success depended. It was administered not by a separate
clergy but by **magistrate-priests** and colleges: the **pontifices** (led by the *Pontifex Maximus*), the
**augures** (diviners of the gods' will from bird-signs), the **Vestal Virgins** (keepers of
[[vesta|Vesta's]] eternal flame), the **flamines** (priests of individual gods), and the **haruspices**
(Etruscan readers of entrails).

## Character — Orthopraxy

The defining feature is **ritual exactitude**: rites had to be performed **perfectly**; a single error
(*vitium*) voided the ceremony and required its complete repetition. The state read the gods' will through
**divination** (taking the auspices before any major public act) and tracked the sacred year through the
**calendar** of festivals (the subject of [[ovid-fasti|Ovid's *Fasti*]]).

## Significance

The most procedural, least creedal religion in the wiki — religion as **public law and correct procedure**
rather than belief — and the institutional backbone onto which the [[imperial-cult|imperial cult]] and the
imported [[mithraism|mystery]] cults were grafted. Recorded without adjudication.
