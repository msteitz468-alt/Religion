# Nihangs (Akalis)

A **martial order** of the [[khalsa|Khalsa]] who claim to be the truest Khalsa — a living remnant of
the 18th-century Sikh armies. Organized into **four "armies" (*dal*)**, each under a *jathedār*; most
are unmarried, living in *deras* and roaming the Punjab on horseback in dark-blue garments hung with
steel weapons, wearing a high turban (*damālā*) topped with a *pharahara* (standard). They converge at
Anandpur for the martial festival **Hola Mahalla**, and are noted for the ritual use of *bhang*
(cannabis) (McLeod, agent 5 ~L151–205).

*(Scaffolded 2026-06-27 from [[mcleod-sikhism|McLeod]] Ch. 9.)*

## Canon and Hermeneutics

Alone among Sikhs the Nihangs "give full honour" to the [[dasam-granth|Dasam Granth]], holding it
equal in standing to the [[guru-granth-sahib|Guru Granth Sahib]] — the chief point on which their
canon diverges from mainstream Sikh practice, which treats only the Adi Granth as Guru Granth Sahib.

## Note

After the 1984 assault on the [[amritsar|Golden Temple]], the section led by Santa Singh supervised the
government rebuilding of the **Akal Takhat**; the Panth subsequently demolished and rebuilt it "with
Khalsa hands unsullied by the attack" (agent 5 ~L198–205).
