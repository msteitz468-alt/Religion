# Are Latter-day Saints Christian?

## The Dispute

Do members of the [[latter-day-saints|Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints]] count as **Christians**?

## LDS Affirmative Case (from self-understanding and standard works)

- Title page of the [[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]]: convince Jew and Gentile that **Jesus is the Christ, the Eternal God**.
- 3 Nephi Christophany; AoF 1, 3–4 on Christ and ordinances.
- Church name and worship centered on Jesus Christ.
- Self-designation as Christian and Restoration of primitive Christianity.

## Creedal Negative Case (typical historic-church grounds)

- **Canon:** open canon with three additional standard works rejected by historic churches.
- **Godhead:** three distinct beings; corporeal Father (D&C 130); not Nicene consubstantial Trinity ([[lds-godhead]]).
- **Anthropology/soteriology:** [[premortal-existence]]; [[exaltation-lds|exaltation]] / becoming gods (D&C 76, 132).
- **Authority:** exclusive restored priesthood after [[great-apostasy|Great Apostasy]] claim (JS-H 1:19 on creeds).

## Graded Middle

[[community-of-christ|Community of Christ]] has moved toward mainline Trinitarian Protestant identity while retaining Restoration roots — illustrating that "Mormon/Christian" is not a single binary across the whole movement.

## Protocol

Contradiction Protocol: both families of judgment are recorded; the wiki files LDS as a Christianity *sect* by self-understanding without adjudicating the boundary.
