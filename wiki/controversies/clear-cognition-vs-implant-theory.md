# Clear Cognition vs. Implant Theory

A genuine internal theological tension within [[scientology|Scientology]]: the account of the reactive mind's origin at the **Clear** level contradicts the account given at the **OT 2** level. The same volume ([[scientology-ot-levels|Technical Bulletins Vol. XIV]]) presents both positions without explicit reconciliation.

## The Positions

### Position 1 — The Bank Is Self-Generated (Clear Cognition)

The **Clear Cognition** (HCOB 24 Sep 1978R, marked Confidential) is the pivotal realization that produces the state of Clear:

> "CLEAR OCCURS WHEN ONE STOPS MOCKING UP BANK, OR REALIZES THAT HE IS MOCKING IT UP OR HAS BEEN MOCKING IT UP. THAT IS THE CLEAR COGNITION: THE REALIZATION THAT HE IS OR HAS BEEN MOCKING UP THE MASS AND PICTURES, ETC., THAT MAKE UP THE BANK."

On this account, the reactive mind is a product of the thetan's own creative activity — the thetan generates (mocks up) the bank. The bank is endogenous, arising from the thetan's own compulsive picture-making. The same position appears in HCOB 22 Sep 1966 ("Clear occurs when he stops mocking up bank, or realizes he is doing it") and is reinforced by the Original OT 8 HCOB, which defines engrams as "a refusal to leave a place at which force was exerted to drive one away" — a self-generated mental persistence, not an external implant.

### Position 2 — The Bank Was Externally Implanted (OT 2 / GPM Theory)

The **OT 2 / Clearing Course materials** present an entirely different account. The bank addressed at OT 2 consists of **Implant GPMs** — goals that were electronically forced into thetans by external agents approximately 75 million years ago:

> "An Implant GPM [is] a GPM made up of goals (and their opposing forces) which are not the pc's own but which have been 'implanted' in him." — HCOB 30 Sep 1981, Glossary

> "The dates on the track table of Section II are asserted dates and are part of the Implant. The actual date is 75 million years ago." — Additional Data Re: Dates, 20 Apr 1968

On this account, the bank is exogenous — it was manufactured by external implanters and forced into thetans against their will. The thetans did not create it; it was done to them. This is the foundation of the cosmological account of spiritual bondage (see [[body-thetans|Body Thetans]], [[goals-problems-mass|Goals Problems Mass]]).

## The Tension

The two positions are not obviously compatible:

- If the bank is **self-generated**, the Clear Cognition ("I have been doing this to myself") is the therapeutic key — recognizing one's own creative authorship of one's suffering.
- If the bank is **externally implanted**, the therapeutic key is recognizing that alien goals were forced into one — the victim insight, not the authorship insight.

These are distinct realizations requiring opposite understandings of agency and causality. Within the OT corpus they appear as sequential stages rather than contradictions — one becomes Clear by recognizing self-authorship, then discovers at OT 2 that there is a deeper layer of the bank that was externally imposed. But this sequencing is assumed rather than argued within the materials, and the ontological relationship between the two layers is not explicitly reconciled.

A sympathetic reading: the thetan perpetuates the implanted bank through its own compulsive mocking-up; the implant created the initial pattern, but the thetan has since been autonomously regenerating it. The Clear Cognition addresses the self-perpetuating mechanism; OT 2 addresses the original implanted content. On this reading, both accounts are true at different levels of analysis.

An unsympathetic reading: the two accounts attribute fundamentally different causal origins to the bank and offer incompatible therapeutic frameworks. "The bank is my fault" and "the bank was done to me" cannot both be fully accurate.

## Hermeneutical Notes

- The Clear Cognition is simultaneously **confidential** (HCOB 24 Sep 1978R says feeding it to pcs is a disciplinable offense) and **openly stated** in the same volume (HCOB 22 Sep 1966, which predates the confidentiality policy). The volume contains both its disclosure and its secrecy classification.
- The OT 2 dates are described within the same documents as "part of the Implant" — meaning practitioners are told not to trust the chronology given to them by the very materials they are studying. The epistemological status of the implant theory is thus self-undermining at one level.
- The Clearing Course instruction booklet warns: "If you skip 4 items and do a light, you will surely become ill" — treating the implant contents as causally real and dangerous, which presupposes they are external impositions, not self-generated fictions.
