# Book of Abraham Translation Controversy

## The Dispute

The [[pearl-of-great-price|Book of Abraham]] is presented as Joseph Smith's translation of Egyptian papyri. Surviving papyri fragments, read by modern Egyptologists, are ordinary funerary texts (e.g. Book of Breathings), not an autobiography of Abraham. How then is the published book related to the papyri?

## Positions (not adjudicated)

1. **Missing source** — The Abraham text was on portions of the collection now lost.
2. **Catalyst** — Papyri prompted revelation without being a conventional translation.
3. **Literal ancient Abrahamic text** — somehow encoded or not captured by standard Egyptology.
4. **Critical** — 19th-century composition; translation claim fails Egyptological test.

**Facsimiles** (not present in the public-domain dump ingested) are a major sub-debate: Smith's explanations vs. Egyptological readings of the vignettes.
