# Book of Mormon Historicity

## The Dispute

Does the [[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]] describe real ancient peoples in the Americas (Nephites, Lamanites, Jaredites), or is it a product of the nineteenth century?

## Positions (not adjudicated)

1. **Faithful historical** — Divine translation of ancient plates; archaeology may be incomplete; limited-geography models; spiritual witness (Moroni 10) primary.
2. **Inspired non-historical** — Scripture that mediates God without requiring ancient American Israelites (common in some Community of Christ and liberal LDS academic circles).
3. **Critical composition** — 19th-century religious text engaging the KJV, frontier theology, and debates about Indian origins; no ancient referent.

## Evidence Domains Invoked

- Text-internal: witnesses, reformed Egyptian claim, Isaiah KJV dependence, anachronism debates (horses, steel, etc.).
- External: Mesoamerican/North American archaeology; DNA and peopling of the Americas; linguistics.
- Epistemology: Moroni 10 spiritual confirmation vs. public historical method.
