# Scientology and the 8th Dynamic: God as Apophatic Infinity vs. Auditable Past-Life Valence

A theological tension within [[scientology|Scientology]]'s advanced materials ([[scientology-ot-levels|Technical Bulletins Vol. XIV]]): the **Super Power Rundown** presents the 8th Dynamic (God/infinity) as apophatic — beyond all identity or MEST categorization — while the **L12 Character List** presents God, the Buddha, and the Devil as auditable past-life valences (case items to be cleared). Both positions appear in the same volume without reconciliation.

## The Positions

### Position 1 — God as Apophatic Infinity (Super Power Series 2)

The Super Power Rundown's 8th Dynamic series opens with an explicit apophatic declaration:

> "God is not an object. When one labels god with an identity one immediately degrades the 8th dynamic into MEST terms. The 8th dynamic cannot be defined in terms of MEST. It can only be defined in terms of infinity which the number 8 symbolizes."

On this account, the 8th Dynamic is irreducibly beyond categorization. Assigning God a name, identity, or attribute is itself a theological error — it reduces infinity to a MEST-level object. This is structurally similar to **apophatic** (negative) theology in Christian mysticism ([[beatific-vision|Beatific Vision]] tradition; Pseudo-Dionysius, Meister Eckhart) and to the Hindu concept of **nirguna brahman** (Brahman without qualities). The Super Power process "Get the idea that you are infinite" and its claimed EP (ecstatic laughter, exteriorization, floating TA) orient the practitioner toward this non-objectified infinity.

### Position 2 — God/Buddha/Devil as Past-Life Valences (L12 Character List)

The **L12 Character List** categorizes archetypal identities to be located, assessed on the E-meter, and cleared as past-life valences accumulated across the practitioner's whole track. Two categories are theologically significant:

**"THE ALMIGHTY"**: God, A Goddess, The Buddha, Pan, Diana, The Father of All, The Horned God.

**"THE EVIL ONE"**: The Antichrist, The Devil, His Satanic Majesty.

These are treated identically to other valences (warrior, ruler, slave, etc.) — as identities the practitioner has embodied in past lives that create accumulated case mass. On this account, the belief or identification "I am God" (or "I am the Buddha," or "I am the Devil") is not a theological truth or a delusion but a **case item**: a past-life experience of having occupied that identity/role, which now creates restimulatable charge.

The L10 **8th Dynamic Rundown** reinforces this by treating religious concepts — God, prayer, gospels, faith, sacred texts, hell, miracles — as items on a checklist of potential aberrations to be assessed for charge.

## The Tension

The two positions approach the divine from opposite directions:

- **Super Power's apophaticism** says: God cannot be named or identified; labeling God degrades the 8th Dynamic into MEST. Religious concepts and divine identities are therefore epistemically inappropriate.
- **L12's valence approach** says: Divine identities (God, Buddha, Devil) are real in the sense that thetans have actually *been* these beings at past points on the whole track; the problem is not the identity itself but the accumulated charge from having held it.

A sympathetic reconciliation: Super Power addresses the *current* 8th Dynamic properly (don't reify it as an object); L12 addresses *past-track* experiences of having occupied divine/demonic roles (which are case items regardless of the current reality of those roles). These are different operations on different time frames.

An unsympathetic reading: if God cannot be assigned an identity without degrading the 8th Dynamic, then the L12 procedure of auditing the identity "God" as a past-life valence is itself the act of degradation Super Power warns against. The L12 treats God as a role one can have been and cleared; Super Power says God is not that kind of entity at all.

## Comparison with Other Traditions

Scientology's L12 character list bears structural resemblance to **Tibetan Buddhist deity yoga** (visualizing oneself as a deity to embody its qualities) and to **Gnostic multiple-eon cosmology** (in which various divine figures are layers to be traversed), but inverts the valuation: in Scientology, having been God is a **problem** to be cleared, not an achievement.

The Super Power apophatic position aligns more closely with medieval Christian mysticism and Advaita Vedanta, but lacks their affirmative dimension: Scientology's 8th Dynamic processing moves toward infinite self-identification ("you are infinite") rather than toward relation to an infinite Other.
