# Dian Cécht

**Dian Cécht** is the **god of healing and medicine** among the **[[the-tuatha-de-danann|Tuatha Dé Danann]]**.
His two great deeds frame the wiki's [[celtic-religion|Celtic]] material on **sovereignty** and **the limits of
healing**:

- He fashions the **silver hand** for **[[nuada|Nuada]]** after the king loses his own at the First Battle of
  Mag Tuired — a working hand of metal that lets a maimed king function, though it is his son **Miach** who
  later surpasses him by **regrowing Nuada a hand of living flesh**. Jealous that his son out-healed him, Dian
  Cécht **kills Miach** — and from Miach's grave grow **365 healing herbs**, one for each sinew and joint,
  which his sister **Airmid** sorts upon her cloak, until Dian Cécht, still envious, **scatters them**, so that
  the perfect knowledge of every cure was **lost to humankind**.
- At the **Second Battle of Mag Tuired** he tends a **Well of Healing (Sláine)** into which the slain Tuatha Dé
  are cast and from which they rise **whole and alive** the next day — a regenerative spring that nearly turns
  the battle, until the Fomorians fill it with stones.

Dian Cécht is thus both the **healer of the gods** and a cautionary figure: the divine physician whose
**jealousy deprives the world of complete healing**.
