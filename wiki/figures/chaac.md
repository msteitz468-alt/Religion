# Chaac

**Chaac** is the [[maya-religion|Maya]] god of **rain, thunder, and lightning** — and so of the **maize and
fertility** that the rains bring, the single most vital god for the Maya farmer. The Aztec counterpart of
**[[tlaloc|Tlaloc]]**, he is shown with a long, downcurving snout, fanged, often wielding a lightning-axe with
which he **strikes the clouds to release the rain**. He was conceived as fourfold — a **Chaac at each of the
four directions**, each with its color — and was invoked in rituals at the sacred cenotes (sinkholes), into
which offerings (and, in times of drought, victims) were cast to win the rain. Goggle-eyed images of Chaac
cover Maya temples (the Puuc-style "Chaac masks"), a measure of how completely Maya survival depended on his
favor.
