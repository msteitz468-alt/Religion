# Apsu (Abzu)

**Apsu** (Sumerian **Abzu**) is the primordial god of the **sweet, fresh underground waters** — the abyss
beneath the earth from which springs and rivers flow. With his consort **[[tiamat|Tiamat]]** (the salt sea),
his waters mingled at the beginning to bear the first gods.

## In the Enuma Elish

When the noisy young gods disturbed his rest, **Apsu plotted to destroy them all** — but the wise
**[[enki|Ea]]** put him to sleep with a spell, **killed him**, and built his own dwelling (and fathered
[[marduk|Marduk]]) **upon the body of the slain Apsu**. Thereafter "the Apsu" became the name of the
freshwater deep that Ea/Enki rules, the cosmic source of wisdom and water beneath the temples (a temple
water-basin was even called an *abzu*). Apsu's death is the first act that sets the whole creation-drama in
motion.
