# Pan (Faunus)

**Pan** (Roman **Faunus**) is the goat-legged, horned god of the **wild, of shepherds and flocks, mountains,
and rustic music** — a creature of the untamed countryside rather than Olympus.

## Character and Cult

Associated with [[hermes|Hermes]] (often his father) and the nymphs, Pan haunts caves and mountainsides,
plays the **syrinx** (panpipes, made from a nymph he pursued), and inspires the sudden irrational terror of
lonely places — **"panic."** A god of fertility and unrestrained nature. The Roman **Faunus** was an old
Italic woodland and prophetic god (his festival the Lupercalia is tied to fertility and purification).

## A Famous Late Legend

Plutarch reports the cry "**Great Pan is dead**" heard at sea in the reign of Tiberius — a story later
Christians read (anachronistically) as marking the passing of the pagan gods at Christ's coming.
