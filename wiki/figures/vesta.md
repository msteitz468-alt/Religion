# Vesta

**Vesta** is the Roman goddess of the **hearth and the sacred flame** — the counterpart of Greek
**[[hestia|Hestia]]**, but vastly more central to Roman public religion. Her undying fire in the Forum was
held to guarantee the **safety and continuity of Rome itself**.

## The Vestal Virgins

Her cult was served by the **Vestal Virgins** — six priestesses chosen as girls and sworn to **30 years of
chastity**, charged with keeping the sacred flame perpetually alight. Their purity was equated with Rome's
security: letting the fire die, or breaking the vow of chastity, was a catastrophe expiated by **burying the
guilty Vestal alive**. They enjoyed unique honors and legal privileges. Vesta was also honored at every
domestic [[roman-household-religion|hearth]] and in [[ovid-fasti|Ovid's *Fasti*]] (the Vestalia, June).
