# Anu (An)

**An** (Sumerian; Akkadian **Anu**) is the **sky-god and primeval father of the gods** of
[[mesopotamian-religion|Mesopotamia]] — the most exalted but most **remote** of the great gods. He sits at
the top of the divine hierarchy as the ultimate source of **authority and kingship** ("Anu-ship" = supreme
rule), but rarely acts directly; the active governance of the cosmos falls to his son **[[enlil|Enlil]]**.
His patron city was **Uruk** (which he shared with [[ishtar|Inanna]]). To "hold the Anu-power" was to be king
of the gods — a status later claimed by [[marduk|Marduk]] and [[ashur|Ashur]]. He heads the great triad of
sky-gods: **Anu (heaven), [[enlil|Enlil]] (air/earth), and [[enki|Enki/Ea]] (the waters/wisdom)**.
