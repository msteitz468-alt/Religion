# Nuada

**Nuada** is the **first king of the [[the-tuatha-de-danann|Tuatha Dé Danann]]** and a god of **sovereignty**.
His myth is the great Celtic statement that **a blemished king cannot rule**: at the **First Battle of Mag
Tuired** against the Fir Bolg, Nuada **loses a hand**, and by the law that the king must be **bodily whole** (as
the land must be whole), he is forced to **abdicate**. The kingship passes to the half-Fomorian tyrant **Bres**,
whose meanness brings the gods low. The physician **[[dian-cecht|Dian Cécht]]** then fashions Nuada a working
**silver hand** — whence his title **Airgetlám, "Silver Hand"** — and Dian Cécht's son Miach later grows him
back a hand of flesh; restored, Nuada resumes the throne. Yet recognizing that **[[lugh|Lugh]]** is the
greater leader against the Fomorians, Nuada **yields his seat to Lugh** before the Second Battle of Mag Tuired,
where Nuada is slain by **[[balor|Balor]]**. He survives in Wales as **Nudd / Lludd Llaw Eraint** ("Silver
Hand") and in Roman Britain as **Nodens**, a healing-god with a temple at Lydney.
