# Anubis

**Anubis** (Egyptian *Anpu*), the **jackal-headed** god, presides over **embalming, mummification, the tomb,
and the guidance of the dead** — the original lord of the dead before [[osiris|Osiris]] took that role, then
his loyal attendant.

## Roles

Jackals haunted the desert cemeteries, so the Egyptians made the jackal-god the **protector of the dead and
the necropolis**. In the [[osiris|Osiris myth]], Anubis **invented embalming** to preserve the dismembered
Osiris (with [[isis|Isis]] and [[nephthys|Nephthys]]) — becoming the patron of mummification, his priests
wearing his jackal mask. He is the **guide who leads the dead** through the underworld to judgment, and it is
Anubis who **adjusts the scales** at the [[maat|weighing of the heart]], ensuring a true verdict. He "who is
upon his mountain" and "lord of the sacred land" watches over every tomb.
