# Hathor

**Hathor** ("House of [[horus|Horus]]") is one of Egypt's greatest and most beloved goddesses — deity of
**love, beauty, music, dance, joy, motherhood, and fertility**, but also of the **sky** and a protectress of
the **dead**. Shown as a **cow**, a woman with cow's horns and a sun-disk, or a woman with cow's ears.

## Roles

The goddess of all pleasures — the **"Lady of Drunkenness, Music, and Myrrh"** — who delights the gods and
nurses the king. She is the divine mother and consort associated with [[horus|Horus]] (and at Dendera the
mother of his form Ihy); as the **"Lady of the West"** she welcomes the setting sun and the dead into the
afterlife, offering them food and shade beneath her sycamore. Yet, like [[sekhmet|Sekhmet]], she is also a
form of the **Eye of [[ra|Ra]]** and can turn destructive (in the Destruction of Mankind she is the
slaughtering Eye before becoming Sekhmet). The Greeks identified her with **[[aphrodite|Aphrodite]]**. Her
great temple at **Dendera** is among the best-preserved in Egypt.
