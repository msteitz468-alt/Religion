# Nanna (Sin)

**Nanna** (Sumerian; Akkadian **Sin**) is the **moon-god** — a great and wise god of [[mesopotamian-religion|
Mesopotamia]], lord of **time and the calendar** (the lunar month) and of cattle and fertility. Father of the
sun-god **[[utu-shamash|Utu/Shamash]]** and the goddess **[[ishtar|Inanna/Ishtar]]** (Venus), so the chief
heavenly bodies are a single divine family. His great temple-cities were **Ur** (where the king's daughter
served as his high priestess — Enheduanna, the world's first named author, held this office) and **Harran**.
The waxing and waning moon, the measure of months and the basis of divination, made Sin a god of secret
knowledge and destiny; the last Babylonian king, Nabonidus, controversially promoted his worship above
[[marduk|Marduk]].
