# Kagutsuchi

**Kagutsuchi** (also **Homusubi**, "Fire-Producer") is the **fire-kami** of [[shinto|Shinto]] whose **birth
brings death into the divine world**. As the last of the kami born to the creator-couple
**[[izanagi-and-izanami|Izanagi and Izanami]]**, the fire-child **fatally burns his mother Izanami** in being
born; she sickens and dies — the **first death** — and departs to the underworld of **Yomi**, setting in motion
Izanagi's grief, his failed descent to retrieve her, and the origin of [[harae|pollution and purification]].

In rage and grief, **Izanagi draws his sword and beheads the fire-child Kagutsuchi** — and, as with so much in
the [[kojiki|Kojiki]], **violence is generative**: from Kagutsuchi's spurting **blood** and from the **pieces
of his severed body** spring whole sets of new kami — deities of **rocks, mountains, thunder, and the sword**
(including the mountain-kami and the fierce blade-deities). Kagutsuchi thus stands at a **double origin point**:
the entry of **death** (through the mother he kills) and a fresh **proliferation of nature-kami** (from the
body he loses). As fire-kami he is propitiated in **fire-pacification rites (*Ho-shizume*)** to guard against
the destructive power of fire — a serious concern in a land of wooden buildings.
