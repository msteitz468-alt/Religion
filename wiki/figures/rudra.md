# Rudra

**Rudra** ("the Howler") is the **fierce, ambivalent god** of [[vedic-religion|Vedic religion]] — lord of
**storms, the wild mountains and forests, wild beasts, disease, and healing**. Feared and propitiated rather
than freely praised, he is a god of the **dangerous margins**: his arrows bring sickness and death to people
and cattle, yet, propitiated, he is the great **physician** who heals (he holds "a thousand remedies"), and he
is addressed as **Shiva**, "the auspicious/kindly one" — a name meant to coax his benevolence. Over time this
fierce outsider-god grew into the great Hindu god **[[hinduism|Shiva]]**, one of the supreme deities of
classical Hinduism: the destroyer-and-transformer, the ascetic lord, who **rises from minor Vedic Rudra to
cosmic supremacy** — the mirror image of the *decline* of [[indra|Indra]] and [[varuna|Varuna]].
