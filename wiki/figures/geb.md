# Geb

**Geb** is the Egyptian **god of the earth** — son of [[shu|Shu]] (air) and [[tefnut|Tefnut]] (moisture),
husband and brother of the sky-goddess [[nut|Nut]], and father of [[osiris|Osiris]], [[isis|Isis]], [[set|
Set]], and [[nephthys|Nephthys]]. Notably, in Egypt the **earth is male and the sky female** — the reverse of
the Greek arrangement. He lies beneath Nut, who arches over him as the sky; their father [[shu|Shu]] holds
them apart. Geb's laughter is earthquakes, and the fertile land is his body; his "green" depictions show
plants growing from him. As an early king of the gods, the royal throne was called "the throne of Geb."
