# Nut

**Nut** is the Egyptian **sky-goddess** — daughter of [[shu|Shu]] and [[tefnut|Tefnut]], wife of the earth-god
[[geb|Geb]], and mother of [[osiris|Osiris]], [[isis|Isis]], [[set|Set]], and [[nephthys|Nephthys]]. She is
shown as a great woman **arched over the earth**, her body the star-spangled vault of heaven, her fingers and
toes touching the four horizons, held up by her father [[shu|Shu]]. Each evening she **swallows [[ra|Ra]] the
sun**, who travels through her body during the night to be **reborn from her at dawn** — so Nut is also a
goddess of **death and rebirth**, painted inside coffin lids to embrace and regenerate the dead as she does
the sun. The "Books of Nut" map the stars and the sun's journey across her.
