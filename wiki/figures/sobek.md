# Sobek

**Sobek** is the **crocodile-god** — a deity of the **Nile, fertility, raw power, and military might**, shown
as a crocodile or a crocodile-headed man. The crocodile's danger and strength made Sobek both feared and
revered: he embodies the fierce, fertile power of the river and its life-giving floods, and as a war-god he
lends the pharaoh his ferocity. Associated with [[ra|Ra]] (as Sobek-Ra) and a protector against the dangers
of the water and the dead, he was worshipped especially in the **Faiyum** (Crocodilopolis) and at Kom Ombo,
where sacred crocodiles were kept and mummified. He was said to have helped recover the scattered parts of
[[osiris|Osiris]] from the water.
