# Hades (Pluto)

**Hades** (Roman **Pluto** / Dis Pater) is the god of the **underworld and the dead** — brother of
[[zeus|Zeus]] and [[poseidon|Poseidon]], who received the **realm of the dead** when the three divided the
cosmos. His name also denotes the **place** (the underworld itself).

## Myths and Character

Stern but not evil — a just, implacable keeper of the dead, rarely leaving his realm. His great myth is the
**abduction of [[persephone|Persephone]]**, [[demeter|Demeter's]] daughter, whom he carries off to be his
queen (the origin of the seasons; see [[demeter|Demeter]]). He owns the **helm of invisibility** and guards
the dead with the hound **Cerberus**. Because the earth's wealth (crops, metals, the buried) is "his," he is
called **Plouton/Pluto**, "the rich one."

## Cult and Roman Identification

Rarely worshipped directly (people turned their faces away); honored with [[persephone|Persephone]] in
chthonic rites. As **Dis Pater / Pluto** he ruled the Roman underworld.
