# Hestia

**Hestia** is the Greek goddess of the **hearth, home, and the sacred fire** — the eldest child of
[[cronus|Cronus]] and Rhea, and a virgin goddess who renounced marriage. Her Roman equivalent is
**[[vesta|Vesta]]**, who became far more prominent in Rome than Hestia ever was in Greece.

## Character and Cult

The gentlest and least mythologized Olympian (she has almost no myths — she stays home on Olympus). She
presides over the **hearth of every household** and the **communal hearth of the city** (the prytaneion),
whose ever-burning fire symbolized the community's life. The **first portion** of every sacrifice was hers.
In some lists Hestia yields her Olympian seat to [[dionysus|Dionysus]], keeping to the hearth.
