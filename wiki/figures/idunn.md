# Iðunn

**Iðunn** is the [[norse-religion|Norse]] goddess of **youth and rejuvenation**, keeper of the magical
**apples** that the gods eat to **stay young and immortal** — making her quietly indispensable to the whole
pantheon. Wife of the poet-god **Bragi**.

## The Theft of Iðunn

Her central myth shows how vital she is: **[[loki|Loki]]**, coerced by the giant **Þjazi**, **lured Iðunn out
of Asgard** with her apples, and the giant (in eagle form) carried her off. Deprived of the apples, **the gods
swiftly began to age** — grey and withered. Discovering Loki's part, they forced him to **win her back**:
borrowing [[freyja|Freyja's]] falcon-cloak, Loki flew to Jötunheim, turned Iðunn into a nut, and flew home
with Þjazi in fiery pursuit (the giant was burned up at Asgard's walls). The myth makes vivid that even the
gods' **immortality is contingent** — sustained only by Iðunn's apples.
