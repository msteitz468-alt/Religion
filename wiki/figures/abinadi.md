# Abinadi

**Abinadi** is the prophetic martyr of Mosiah 11–17 in the [[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]], whose teaching converts Alma the Elder.

## Biographical Overview (textual)

- Calls King Noah and his priests to repentance; imprisoned.
- Recites the Decalogue; expounds Isaiah 53; teaches the Father and the Son, the resurrection, and the law as a type of Christ (Mosiah 12–16).
- Refuses to recall his words; burned to death (Mosiah 17); Alma flees and founds a church at the waters of Mormon.

## Theological Significance

- Bridge from Isaiah's servant song to explicit Nephite Christology.
- Martyr prototype; catalyst for Alma's church order and baptism narrative.
