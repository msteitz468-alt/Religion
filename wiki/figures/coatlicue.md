# Coatlicue

**Coatlicue** ("She of the Serpent Skirt") is the [[aztec-religion|Aztec]] **earth-mother goddess** — the
fearsome womb-and-tomb of all life, who **gives birth to everything and devours everything** back into herself.
Her colossal statue shows her with a skirt of woven serpents, a necklace of severed hands and hearts, and, in
place of a head, **two serpents** (the spurting blood of her decapitation) — an image of the terrible unity of
**creation and destruction**, fertility and death. She is the mother of the moon ([[huitzilopochtli|
Coyolxauhqui]]), the stars, and — by a ball of feathers that fell as she swept the temple — of the war-god
**[[huitzilopochtli|Huitzilopochtli]]**, who sprang from her armed to defend her (see [[huitzilopochtli|his
birth-myth]]). Coatlicue embodies the Mesoamerican sense of the **earth as a devouring, regenerating power**
that must be fed with blood to keep yielding life.
