# Khnum

**Khnum** is the **ram-headed potter-god** — a creator who **fashions living beings on his potter's wheel**.
Lord of the **source of the Nile** (he was thought to control the inundation from the caverns at the First
Cataract near Elephantine), Khnum shapes the **bodies and *kas* of children** (and even of the gods) out of
Nile clay on his wheel, then places them in their mothers' wombs — a vivid image of the **craftsman-creator**
(comparable to the molding of humanity from clay in [[genesis|Genesis 2]] and many traditions). His control
of the flood made him a god of fertility on whom Egypt's very survival depended (the subject of the "Famine
Stela").
