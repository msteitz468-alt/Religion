# Lehi

**Lehi** is the founding patriarch of the Lehite peoples in the [[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]] — the father of Laman, Lemuel, Sam, [[nephi|Nephi]], Jacob, and Joseph.

## Biographical Overview (textual)

- Prophesies destruction of Jerusalem in Zedekiah's first year; pillar-of-fire vision and a heavenly book; mocked and threatened (1 Ne 1).
- Commanded to flee into the wilderness; leaves wealth; encamps by the Red Sea (valley of Lemuel).
- Sends sons for the brass plates and for Ishmael's family; receives the **Liahona**.
- **Tree-of-life dream** (1 Ne 8): fears for Laman and Lemuel who will not partake.
- Prophesies the Messiah 600 years from departure; olive-tree scatter/gather of Israel (1 Ne 10).
- Final blessings in 2 Nephi 1–4: **choice land** covenant; doctrinal discourse to Jacob on opposition, Fall, and free agency (2 Ne 2); Joseph-of-Egypt / choice-seer prophecy to son Joseph (2 Ne 3); dies and is buried (2 Ne 4:12).

## Theological Significance

- Bearer of the **prosper / cut-off** land covenant that structures Nephite history.
- Author (via Nephi's record) of the book's classic Fall and agency theology (2 Ne 2:11, 25–27).
- Josephite lineage (brass-plate genealogy) ties Lehites to biblical Israel.
