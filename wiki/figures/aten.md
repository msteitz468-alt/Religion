# The Aten

The **Aten** is the visible **disk of the sun** — originally a minor aspect of [[ra|Ra]] — elevated by Pharaoh
**Akhenaten** into the **sole god** of the universe in the religious revolution of [[atenism|Atenism]]
(c. 1353 BCE).

## A God of Light, Not Myth

Unlike the other gods, the Aten had **no human or animal form and no mythology** — it was depicted only as the
**sun-disk with rays ending in hands** that reach down to bless the royal family with the *ankh* (life). The
Aten was worshipped as the **sole creator and sustainer**, known through its **life-giving light** in the
visible world, celebrated in the magnificent **Great Hymn to the Aten** (whose nature-imagery famously
parallels [[psalms|Psalm 104]]). Worship reached the Aten **only through Akhenaten and Nefertiti**, the sole
intermediaries. After Akhenaten's death the cult was abolished and the old gods restored.
