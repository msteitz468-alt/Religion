# Freyja

**Freyja** ("Lady") is the great goddess of the [[vanir-cult|Vanir]] — deity of **love, beauty, fertility,
and gold**, but also of **war, death, and magic**. Daughter of [[njord|Njörð]] and twin of [[freyr|Freyr]],
she is the most prominent Norse goddess.

## Roles

A goddess of paradoxes: she rides a chariot drawn by **cats**, weeps **tears of red gold** for her wandering
husband Óðr, and wears the famous necklace **Brísingamen**; yet she is also a **death-goddess and chooser of
the slain** who receives **half the battle-dead** in her hall **Fólkvangr** (the other half going to
[[odin|Odin's]] [[valhalla|Valhalla]]). She is the mistress of **[[seidr|seiðr]]** — the shamanic magic she
is said to have **taught the Æsir**, including Odin. The giants repeatedly demand her as the prize for their
services (for building Asgard's wall, for the return of Thor's hammer), and the gods must always trick their
way out of surrendering her.
