# Amun (Amun-Ra)

**Amun** ("the Hidden One") rose from a minor [[hermopolitan-theology|Hermopolitan]] and Theban god to
become the **supreme state god of New Kingdom Egypt** — merged with the sun-god [[ra|Ra]] as **Amun-Ra,
"King of the Gods."** Patron of [[theban-amun-cult|Thebes]] and its vast temple of **Karnak**, he was credited
with the empire's victories and accumulated immense wealth and a powerful priesthood.

## A Hidden, Transcendent God

Amun's name marks his theology: he is the **hidden, unseen power** behind all things, whose true form is
unknown even to the gods — "one who made himself into millions." This drive toward a single, transcendent
supreme deity (while keeping the pantheon, *henotheism*) is the high point of Egyptian theology and the
backdrop to [[atenism|Akhenaten's]] monotheistic revolt against Amun's priesthood. Shown as a man with a tall
double-plumed crown, often ram-headed.
