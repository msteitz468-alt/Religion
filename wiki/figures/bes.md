# Bes

**Bes** is one of the most beloved and ubiquitous **popular** gods of Egypt — a grotesque, grinning **dwarf**
with a lion's mane, protruding tongue, and feathered crown, who guards the **home, women in childbirth, and
young children** against demons, snakes, and misfortune. Unlike the great temple gods, Bes belonged to
**everyday domestic life**: his comic, frightening face appears on amulets, headrests, mirrors, cosmetic
items, and the walls of birth-houses, scaring away evil with his ugliness, noise, and dancing. A god of
**music, dance, sexuality, and joy** as well as protection, he was invoked by ordinary people far more often
than the lofty state gods — a reminder that lived religion centered on protection in daily life.
