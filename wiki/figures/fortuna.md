# Fortuna

**Fortuna** is the Roman goddess of **fortune, chance, and luck** — counterpart of Greek **[[tyche|Tyche]]**
but with a deep and ancient native Italian cult of her own.

## Cult and Significance

Far more than a literary personification, Fortuna had major temples and many specialized cults: **Fortuna
Primigenia** (the great oracular sanctuary at Praeneste), **Fortuna Virilis** and **Fortuna Muliebris**
(men's and women's fortune), **Fortuna Redux** (safe return), **Fors Fortuna** (a popular festival). She
carries the **rudder, wheel, and cornucopia** — steering events, turning the wheel, pouring out plenty (and
taking it away). Her instability made her the great image of **the precariousness of human success**, a theme
that runs from Roman moralists through Boethius (the **Wheel of Fortune**) into medieval and Renaissance
thought.
