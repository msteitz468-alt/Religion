# Prajapati

**Prajapati** ("Lord of Creatures") is the **creator-god** who rises to prominence in the **late
[[rig-veda|Rig Veda]] and the [[shrauta-brahmanism|Brahmanas]]** — a more abstract, unifying creator than the
older nature-gods, the source from whom all beings spring. He is identified with the cosmic **Purusha** of the
[[rig-veda|Purusha Sukta]] (RV 10.90), the primal Man whose **self-sacrifice and dismemberment** produces the
whole world, the gods, and the social order — so that Prajapati **creates the cosmos by an act of sacrifice**,
and **is himself** the sacrifice. This makes him the divine guarantor of [[yajna|the yajna]]: each ritual
sacrifice re-enacts Prajapati's creative self-offering and renews the cosmos. In later [[hinduism|Hinduism]]
Prajapati is largely absorbed into the creator-god **Brahma**.
