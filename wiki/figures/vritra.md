# Vritra

**Vritra** ("the Encloser") is the great **serpent-dragon of drought and chaos** in [[vedic-religion|Vedic
religion]] — the cosmic adversary whose defeat by **[[indra|Indra]]** is the central Vedic myth. A serpent
(*ahi*) of monstrous size, Vritra **coiled around the mountains and "enclosed" the cosmic waters**, holding
back the rivers and the rain so that the world lay parched, barren, and unborn — the embodiment of obstruction,
inertia, and the chaos that resists order ([[rita|rita]]).

## The Defeat

Strengthened by [[soma|soma]] and armed with the thunderbolt, **[[indra|Indra]] smashed Vritra and released
the waters**, which rushed forth to bring life to the world (see [[indra|Indra]] for the full narrative).
Vritra is thus the **chaos-monster of the primeval waters** in its Indo-European Vedic form — closely parallel
to [[tiamat|Tiamat]] (slain by [[marduk|Marduk]]), Typhon (slain by [[zeus|Zeus]]), and the biblical sea-dragon
Leviathan — the obstacle whose slaying is creation itself.
