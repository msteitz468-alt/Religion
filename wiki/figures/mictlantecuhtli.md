# Mictlantecuhtli

**Mictlantecuhtli** ("Lord of Mictlan") is the [[aztec-religion|Aztec]] god of **death and the underworld**,
ruling the lowest of the nine levels of **Mictlan** with his consort Mictecacihuatl. A skeletal, blood-
spattered figure with a necklace of eyeballs, he receives the **ordinary dead** — those who did not die in
war, sacrifice, childbirth, or by water (who went to brighter destinations). The newly dead faced a **four-
year journey** through the nine perilous levels of Mictlan, aided by grave-goods and an accompanying dog,
before reaching Mictlantecuhtli's realm and final rest (a quiet dissolution, neither reward nor punishment).
In myth, **[[quetzalcoatl|Quetzalcoatl]] descended to Mictlan** to win from Mictlantecuhtli the **bones of the
dead** of the past age, from which he created present humanity — the death-lord resisting and tricking him at
every turn.
