# Arawn

**Arawn** is a **king of [[annwn|Annwn]]**, the Welsh **[[the-otherworld|Otherworld]]**, in the **First
Branch** of the **[[mabinogion|Mabinogion]]**. When **[[pwyll|Pwyll]]** lets his hounds seize a stag already
brought down by Arawn's own pack — the spectral white, red-eared **Cŵn Annwn** — Arawn demands recompense, and
the two strike a remarkable bargain: they **exchange shapes and realms for a year and a day**, Pwyll ruling
Annwn in Arawn's likeness and defeating Arawn's rival **Hafgan** with a single blow, while Arawn rules Dyfed.
Pwyll's scrupulous honor during the year — chastity beside Arawn's queen, just rule of Annwn — earns Arawn's
deep and lasting **friendship and alliance** between the two kingdoms. Arawn is the gracious face of the
Otherworld: not a death-tyrant but a **king with a court, hounds, and rivalries** much like a mortal lord's,
exemplifying the Celtic sense of the Otherworld as a **neighboring kingdom** rather than a hell. (In later
Welsh tradition Annwn's kingship passes to **Gwyn ap Nudd**, son of [[nuada|Nudd]].)
