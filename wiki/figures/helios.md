# Helios (Sol)

**Helios** (Roman **Sol**) is the god who **is the Sun** — driving his fiery, four-horse chariot across the
sky each day from east to west and returning by night in a golden cup. A Titan-descended god (son of
Hyperion), brother of [[selene|Selene]] (Moon) and Eos (Dawn).

## Myths and Cult

The **all-seeing** witness of gods and men (he tells [[demeter|Demeter]] of [[persephone|Persephone's]]
abduction and exposes [[aphrodite|Aphrodite's]] affair). Father of **Phaethon**, who begged to drive the
sun-chariot and, losing control, was struck down by [[zeus|Zeus]] to save the scorched earth. His great cult
center was **Rhodes** (the Colossus). Often merged with [[apollo|Apollo]] as a sun-god in later antiquity.

## Roman Sol Invictus

At Rome, **Sol Invictus** ("the Unconquered Sun") became a major imperial cult in the 3rd century, his
festival on **December 25** — a date and solar symbolism that influenced the later Christian Christmas.
