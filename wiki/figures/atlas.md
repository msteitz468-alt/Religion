# Atlas

**Atlas** is the [[cronus|Titan]] condemned to **hold up the sky** for all eternity — a son of Iapetus and
brother of [[prometheus|Prometheus]] and Epimetheus.

## Myths

For siding with the Titans against [[zeus|Zeus]] in the **Titanomachy**, Atlas was sentenced to stand at the
western edge of the world and bear the **vault of heaven** on his shoulders forever (Greeks did not say he
held the *earth*; that image is later). He appears in two famous tales: he is the father of the **Hesperides**
(and of the Pleiades and Hyades), whose garden held the golden apples; and in the eleventh of the
**[[heracles|Twelve Labors]]**, Heracles briefly takes the sky onto his own shoulders so Atlas will fetch the
apples — then tricks the Titan into resuming his burden. Perseus, showing him the Gorgon's head, was said to
have turned him to stone — the **Atlas Mountains** of North Africa. His name is the source of "atlas" (a book
of maps).
