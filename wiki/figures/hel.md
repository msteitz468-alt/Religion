# Hel

**Hel** is the [[norse-religion|Norse]] goddess who **rules the realm of the dead** that bears her name —
the dim, cold underworld (in **Niflheim**) where go those who die of **sickness and old age**, as opposed to
the battle-slain who go to [[valhalla|Valhalla]] or Fólkvangr. A daughter of **[[loki|Loki]]** and the
giantess Angrboða (sister of the wolf [[fenrir|Fenrir]] and the serpent [[jormungandr|Jörmungandr]]), she was
cast down by [[odin|Odin]] to govern the dead. She is described as **half living flesh and half black/blue
corpse** — grim and implacable. It is **Hel** who holds the dead [[baldr|Baldr]] and sets the impossible
ransom (that *all* things weep for him), which [[loki|Loki]] foils. The English word **"hell"** derives from
her name, though her realm is a neutral abode of the dead, not (originally) a place of punishment.
