# Ushas

**Ushas** is the radiant **goddess of the dawn** — the subject of some of the **most beautiful poetry in the
[[rig-veda|Rig Veda]]**. A lovely young woman who throws open the doors of the sky each morning, she drives
away the darkness, awakens all living things to activity, and renews the world day after day — yet, ever
young herself, she **wears away the lives of mortals**, each dawn bringing them closer to death (a poignant
Vedic meditation on time). The daughter of [[dyaus|Dyaus]] (Sky) and sister of the night, she is the Vedic
cognate of the Greek **[[helios|Eos]]** and Roman **Aurora** and of the Lithuanian dawn-goddess — a clear
**Indo-European** inheritance (the very name *Ushas* = *Eos* = *Aurora*).
