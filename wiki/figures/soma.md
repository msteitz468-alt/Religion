# Soma

**Soma** is at once a **sacred plant, the intoxicating drink pressed from it, and the god of both** — one of
the most important and mysterious elements of [[vedic-religion|Vedic religion]]. The entire **ninth book
(Mandala 9) of the [[rig-veda|Rig Veda]]** is devoted to Soma.

## The Drink of the Gods

In the [[yajna|sacrifice]], the soma plant was pressed between stones, filtered through wool, mixed with milk,
and offered to the gods (and drunk by the priests). The resulting drink produced an **exhilarating, visionary,
even ecstatic effect** — it is praised as granting inspiration, vigor, and a taste of **immortality** and the
divine. **[[indra|Indra]]** drinks great draughts of soma to strengthen himself before slaying [[vritra|
Vritra]]; the gods crave it. The identity of the original plant (ephedra? a hallucinogenic mushroom?) is a
famous unsolved puzzle. Soma is the **Indo-Iranian** cousin of the Zoroastrian **[[zoroastrianism|Haoma]]**,
revealing a shared ritual heritage. In later [[hinduism|Hinduism]], when the soma cult faded, **Soma** became
identified with the **moon** (the cup of the gods, waxing and waning as the gods drink it).
