# Tefnut

**Tefnut** is the Egyptian **goddess of moisture** — dew, rain, and humidity — the first goddess, created by
[[ra|Atum]] together with her twin brother [[shu|Shu]] (air), with whom she forms the first pair of the
[[heliopolitan-theology|Ennead]]. Their union produced [[geb|Geb]] and [[nut|Nut]]. Often leonine, Tefnut is
one of the goddesses identified with the fierce **Eye of [[ra|Ra]]**: in a famous myth she quarreled with her
father and stalked off into Nubia as a raging lioness, and [[thoth|Thoth]] had to be sent to soothe her and
coax her back — her return bringing the life-giving moisture (and the Nile flood) with her.
