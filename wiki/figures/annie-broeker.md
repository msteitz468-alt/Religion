# Annie Broeker (Annie Tidman)

**Annie Broeker** (legal name **Annie Tidman**; also known by her married name **Annie Broeker-Tidman**) was one of [[l-ron-hubbard|L. Ron Hubbard]]'s most trusted personal aides during his final years in hiding and subsequently a senior [[sea-org|Sea Org]] officer at [[gold-base|Gold Base]].

## Biographical Overview

Annie Tidman was among the small circle of Sea Org members who directly attended Hubbard during his years of hiding in the California desert, alongside **Pat Broeker** — whom she married. Pat Broeker was Hubbard's last handler and declared himself **"Loyal Officer"** with access to upper OT materials at the time of Hubbard's death in January 1986.

After [[david-miscavige|David Miscavige]] moved against Pat Broeker and stripped him of authority (confirmed complete by approximately 1988), Annie remained in the Sea Org under Miscavige's direct control. Her marriage to Pat Broeker made her a suspected asset or liability — sources differ on how she was treated in the aftermath.

## Gold Base Confinement

**Marc Headley** (*Blown for Good*, 2009) identifies Annie Tidman as one of the permanent residents at Gold Base during the period of his time there (approximately 1990–2005). Headley places her in a specific restricted category:

- She served as **Commanding Officer, Commodore's Messenger Organization Gold (CO CMO Gold)** — a senior operational role.
- She lived at or near **Happy Valley** (a secluded area on or adjacent to the Gold Base property, used for confinement of specific individuals deemed security risks) and/or in a **small house near Old Gilman's** at the edge of the Gold Base property.
- Headley states she was **never allowed to leave the base** — a form of internal confinement that paralleled (though was distinct from) the Hole.

[[atack-a-piece-of-blue-sky|Atack]] (*A Piece of Blue Sky*, 1990) documents a **schism in February 1987** between Miscavige and "the Broekers" over custody of the "upper level Holy Scriptures" — the contest that Miscavige ultimately won, placing Pat Broeker under private investigator surveillance for over 25 years (per Tampa Bay Times reporting). Annie's position in that schism and its aftermath is not fully documented in the wiki's ingested sources.

## Atack's Account

Atack documents **Vicki Aznaran**'s testimony to the 1987 schism between Miscavige and Pat and Annie Broeker over the disposition of Hubbard's posthumous OT materials. Atack does not provide extended biographical information on Annie specifically, but the schism account is the earliest documentation of the Broeker/Miscavige power struggle in which she was a party.

## Headley's Account

Headley (*Blown for Good*) is the primary source in this wiki for Annie's situation at Gold Base during the 1990s–2000s. He notes:

- She was a known figure at the base — senior enough to be recognized by Gold Base staff
- Her confinement was open knowledge among those stationed there, though not discussed openly
- As of Headley's escape in January 2005, she remained at the base in restricted status

## Controversies and Current Status

Annie Broeker/Tidman's whereabouts and condition after Headley's departure are not addressed in the sources ingested to date. The Church has not publicly commented on her status. Former members (including Marty Rathbun in post-departure writings not yet ingested) have discussed her situation, but that material is not in this wiki's current source base.
