# Ashur

**Ashur** (Assur) is the **national god of [[assyrian-religion|Assyria]]** — originally the deified spirit of
the city of Ashur, elevated as Assyria grew into an empire to the position of **supreme king of the gods**,
displacing [[marduk|Marduk]] (the Assyrians even **rewrote the [[enuma-elish|Enuma Elish]]** to make Ashur,
not Marduk, the dragon-slaying creator). Uniquely among Mesopotamian high gods, Ashur began as the **god of a
single city that bore his name** and rose with its political fortunes.

## God of Empire

Ashur and the Assyrian state were inseparable: the king was **Ashur's earthly vicar and high priest**, and
**warfare was holy** — Assyrian conquest, deportation, and tribute were framed as **extending the rule of
Ashur** and punishing those who rebelled against him. To submit to Assyria was to submit to Ashur; to resist
was sacrilege. This **fusion of religion, kingship, and militarism** made Assyrian religion a powerful
ideological engine of the ancient world's first great empire.
