# Ra (Re / Atum)

**Ra** (Re) is the **sun-god and supreme creator** of [[egyptian-religion|Egyptian religion]] — the source
of life and order, king of the gods, and divine father of every pharaoh. He has many names for the sun's
phases: **Khepri** the scarab at dawn, **Ra** at noon, **[[ra|Atum]]** the old creator at dusk; and he merges
with other gods as **Ra-Horakhty** (with [[horus|Horus]]) and **[[amun|Amun-Ra]]**.

## Myths in Detail

**The creation.** In the [[heliopolitan-theology|Heliopolitan]] theology, before the world there was only
**Nun**, the dark primeval waters. From Nun the self-created sun-god **Atum-Ra** rose on the first mound (the
*benben*), and — alone — brought forth the first divine couple, **[[shu|Shu]]** (air) and **[[tefnut|Tefnut]]**
(moisture), by his own seed or breath. From them came **[[geb|Geb]]** (earth) and **[[nut|Nut]]** (sky), and
from them the four gods of the [[osiris|Osiris]] cycle — so the whole world unfolds from Ra's first act. He
created humankind from his tears.

**The Eye of Ra and the destruction of mankind.** Ra's fearsome power is his **Eye**, a goddess in her own
right (Hathor, Sekhmet, or Wadjet). When mankind plotted rebellion against the aging Ra, he sent his Eye as
the lioness **[[sekhmet|Sekhmet]]** to slaughter them — but her bloodlust grew so terrible she threatened to
destroy all humanity. To stop her, Ra had the fields flooded with beer dyed red like blood; Sekhmet drank
it, fell into a drunken sleep, and humanity was spared.

**Isis and the secret name.** Grown old, Ra drooled, and **[[isis|Isis]]** mixed his spittle with earth to
form a venomous serpent that bit him. In agony, and curable only by her magic, Ra was forced to reveal to
Isis his **secret true name** — the source of his power — giving her, and her son [[horus|Horus]], a share of
his supreme authority (told in Budge's *Legends of the Gods*).

**The solar journey.** Each day Ra sails across the sky in his **solar barque**; each night he descends into
the **Duat** (the underworld), where he must pass twelve hours of darkness and do battle with the great chaos-
serpent **[[apep|Apep/Apophis]]**, who tries to swallow the sun and end the world. Defended by the gods
(including [[set|Set]] at the prow), Ra defeats Apep each night, briefly unites with the body of [[osiris|
Osiris]], and is **reborn at dawn** — the daily victory of [[maat|ma'at]] over chaos, and the model of
resurrection.
