# Njörð

**Njörð** is the [[vanir-cult|Vanir]] god of the **sea, seafaring, wind, fishing, and wealth** — the father
of [[freyr|Freyr]] and [[freyja|Freyja]], and one of the Vanir hostages who came to live among the Æsir after
the Æsir–Vanir War. Invoked by sailors and fishermen for safe voyages and rich catches, and for **prosperity**
generally. His famously **mismatched marriage** to the giantess **Skaði** (a goddess of mountains, skiing, and
winter, who chose him by his beautiful feet thinking he was Baldr) failed because **neither could bear the
other's home** — she could not sleep by his loud sea, nor he by her cold mountains — a vivid little myth of
sea-versus-mountain incompatibility. An old, possibly originally female or paired, fertility deity (cf. the
Germanic goddess *Nerthus*).
