# Heimdall

**Heimdall** is the ever-vigilant **watchman of the [[norse-religion|Norse]] gods** — the guardian who stands
at the rainbow bridge **Bifröst**, the entrance to Asgard. Born of **nine mothers** (the waves), he needs less
sleep than a bird, can **see for a hundred leagues** by day or night, and can **hear grass grow on the earth
and wool on the sheep**. He keeps the great horn **Gjallarhorn**, which he will **blow to summon the gods to
[[ragnarok|Ragnarök]]** when he sees the enemies approaching. In the *Rígsþula* he wanders the world as
**Ríg** and fathers the three classes of human society (thrall, farmer, noble). His ancient enmity with
**[[loki|Loki]]** (once contested over Freyja's necklace Brísingamen) ends at Ragnarök, where the two
**slay each other**.
