# Alma the Younger

**Alma the Younger** (Alma₂), son of Alma the Elder, is the dominant prophetic voice of the Book of Alma in the [[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]].

## Biographical Overview (textual)

- With the sons of Mosiah, seeks to destroy the church; angelic rebuke; three days of paralysis; conversion (Mosiah 27; chiastic retelling Alma 36).
- First chief judge under the new system and high priest; later resigns the judgment seat to preach (Alma 4–5).
- Major sermons: spiritual rebirth (Alma 5); Christology (Alma 7); debate with Zeezrom; holy order / Melchizedek (Alma 12–13).
- Mission context for Ammon's Lamanite mission; confronts **Korihor** (Alma 30); mission to the Zoramites with the **faith-as-seed** discourse (Alma 32–33).
- Charges to sons Helaman, Shiblon, Corianton (Alma 36–42): plates, sexual sin, resurrection, justice and mercy, plan of happiness.
- Prophesies destruction; departs (Alma 45) — tradition of being taken up.

## Theological Significance

- Principal teacher of **infinite atonement**, intermediate state, and the justice/mercy structure of the plan of salvation in the middle BoM.
- Model of radical conversion from anti-church persecutor to prophet (Pauline resonance without explicit citation).
