# Bastet

**Bastet** (Bast) is the **cat-goddess** of [[egyptian-religion|Egypt]] — protector of the **home, women,
children, and fertility**, and goddess of joy, music, and dance. Originally a fierce lioness-goddess like
[[sekhmet|Sekhmet]], she was gentled over time into the domestic **cat**, the **benevolent counterpart** to
Sekhmet's rage (the two are the soothed and furious aspects of the Eye of [[ra|Ra]]). Cats were sacred to her,
keeping homes and granaries free of vermin and snakes, and were mummified by the thousands at her cult center
of **Bubastis**, whose joyous festival Herodotus described as the most riotous in Egypt. A loving, protective
goddess invoked for safety and fertility.
