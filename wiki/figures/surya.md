# Surya

**Surya** is the **sun-god** of [[vedic-religion|Vedic religion]] — the radiant deity who crosses the sky in
a chariot drawn by seven horses, the **"eye of [[mitra|Mitra]] and [[varuna|Varuna]],"** who sees all and
dispels darkness, disease, and evil dreams. As **Savitr** ("the Impeller"), the life-giving and stimulating
power of the sun, he is invoked in the most sacred and still-recited Vedic prayer, the **Gayatri mantra** (RV
3.62.10), a meditation on the sun's divine radiance. The all-seeing sun makes Surya a witness to truth and a
healer; he remained a worshipped deity throughout [[hinduism|Hinduism]] (with great temples like Konark).
