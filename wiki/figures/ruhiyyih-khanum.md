# Rúhiyyih Khánum (Amatu'l-Bahá Rúhiyyih Rabbání)

*Populated from Smith, A Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith (Oneworld, 2000). All claims from that source unless marked.*

## Biographical Overview

Rúhiyyih Khánum (born Mary Maxwell, 1910) was the American-Canadian wife of [[shoghi-effendi|Shoghi Effendi]] and a [[hands-of-the-cause|Hand of the Cause]]. Her parents were William Sutherland and [[may-maxwell|May Maxwell]] (lines 27206–27249).

Involved in Bahá'í youth activities in North America, she moved to Europe in 1935 to support Bahá'í activities there. She married Shoghi Effendi on 24 March 1937, seemingly without much in the way of courtship. She served also for many years as his personal secretary. Shoghi Effendi appointed her as a member of the [[international-bahai-council|International Bahá'í Council]] in 1951 to act as liaison between him and the Council (lines 27252–27263). She was appointed as a Hand of the Cause on 26 March 1952 (line 27264).

## Role After Shoghi Effendi's Death

Following the death of Shoghi Effendi in 1957, she acted as the rallying point for her fellow Hands and was one of the custodial Hands. Her travels have taken her to almost all parts of the Bahá'í world, including many remote rural areas (lines 27269–27278).

The honorific *Amatu'l-Bahá* means "maidservant" or "handmaiden of Bahá" (line 27277).
