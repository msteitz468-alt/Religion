# Susanoo

**Susanoo-no-Mikoto** — Chamberlain's "His Impetuous-Male-Augustness" — is the **storm- and sea-god** of
[[shinto|Shinto]], the violent, unruly brother of the sun-goddess **[[amaterasu|Amaterasu]]**. He is the most
**ambivalent** of the great kami: at once a destructive force of chaos and a culture-hero and dragon-slayer —
embodying the kami's double nature (*ara-mitama* and *nigi-mitama*).

## Myths in Detail

### Banishment and the offense against Amaterasu

Born from **[[izanagi-and-izanami|Izanagi]]'s nose** in the great [[harae|purification]], Susanoo is given rule
of the **sea**, but instead **weeps and wails** ceaselessly to go to his dead mother in **Yomi**, withering the
mountains; Izanagi **banishes him**. Going up to heaven to bid farewell to Amaterasu, his approach so shakes
the land that she suspects invasion and arms herself; they swear an **oath-ordeal**, producing children by
chewing and crushing each other's regalia, to prove his good faith. But Susanoo then runs riot — breaking the
rice-paddies, defiling the sacred halls, and **hurling a flayed horse into Amaterasu's weaving hall** — driving
her into the **rock-cave** (see [[amaterasu|Amaterasu]]). For this he is **fined, his beard and nails plucked,
and expelled** from heaven.

### The slaying of Yamata-no-Orochi

Descending to **Izumo**, Susanoo finds an old couple weeping: the **[[yamata-no-orochi|eight-forked serpent]]**
(Yamata-no-Orochi), a monstrous dragon with **eight heads and eight tails**, spanning eight valleys and eight
hills, its belly bloody and eyes like winter-cherries, has devoured seven of their daughters and comes now for
the last, **Kushinada-hime**. Susanoo agrees to kill it in exchange for the maiden. He brews **eight vats of
strong sake**, sets them behind eight gates; the serpent's **eight heads each drink and fall drunk asleep**,
and Susanoo **hacks it to pieces** with his sword. In the serpent's tail he finds a great sword — the
**Kusanagi ("Grass-Cutter")**, which he presents to Amaterasu, and which becomes one of the **imperial
regalia**. He marries Kushinada-hime, builds a palace at Suga (uttering Japan's "first poem"), and becomes the
**ancestor of [[okuninushi|Ōkuninushi]]**, lord of Izumo.

## Significance

Susanoo is the Shinto **combat-myth** hero — the storm-god who slays the chaos-**dragon**
([[yamata-no-orochi|Yamata-no-Orochi]]; cf. [[the-combat-myth|the combat myth]], [[tiamat|Tiamat]],
[[jormungandr|Jörmungandr]]) — and simultaneously the disruptive force whose violence drives the sun into
hiding. His worship centers on **Izumo**, the great rival pole to Amaterasu's **Ise**, and the
heaven/earth, sun/storm, Ise/Izumo tensions structure much of the [[kojiki|Kojiki]].
