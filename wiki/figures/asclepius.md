# Asclepius (Aesculapius)

**Asclepius** (Roman **Aesculapius**) is the god of **medicine and healing** — a son of [[apollo|Apollo]]
raised by the centaur Chiron, who became so skilled that he could **raise the dead**, for which [[zeus|Zeus]]
struck him with a thunderbolt, then set him among the stars (and the gods).

## Cult — Healing Sanctuaries

His [[greek-hero-cult|cult]] was among the most beloved and practical in antiquity: the sick traveled to his
sanctuaries (the great one at **Epidaurus**, and at Pergamon and Kos) to practice **incubation** — sleeping in
the temple to receive a healing dream or a remedy from the god, often with the help of his sacred
**non-venomous snakes**. His staff entwined with a serpent (the **Rod of Asclepius**) remains the symbol of
medicine. His daughters include **Hygieia** (Health) and **Panacea**. Brought to Rome in 293 BCE to end a
plague.
