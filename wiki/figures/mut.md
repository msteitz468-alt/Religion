# Mut

**Mut** ("Mother") is the great **mother-goddess** of [[theban-amun-cult|Thebes]] and **queen of the gods** —
the consort of **[[amun|Amun]]** and, with their son **[[khonsu|Khonsu]]** the moon-god, one of the **Theban
Triad** that headed the New Kingdom state religion. Shown as a woman wearing the double crown and a vulture
headdress, she is a divine mother and protectress, associated with the lioness-Eye goddesses ([[sekhmet|
Sekhmet]]) in her fierce aspect. Her temple precinct at Karnak, with its hundreds of statues of Sekhmet, lay
beside Amun's.
