# Eros (Cupid)

**Eros** (Roman **Cupid** / Amor) is the god of **love, desire, and attraction** — but the tradition holds
two very different Eros figures.

## Primordial vs. Olympian Eros

- **Primordial Eros** — in [[hesiod-theogony|Hesiod]] and the [[orphism|Orphic]] cosmogonies, Eros is one of
  the **first beings** (born of [[chaos|Chaos]]), the cosmic generative force of attraction that drives all
  creation and the union of the elements. A profound metaphysical principle (taken up by Plato's *Symposium*).
- **Later Eros / Cupid** — the familiar mischievous **winged boy**, son of [[aphrodite|Aphrodite]], who
  shoots golden arrows that make gods and mortals fall in love. The story of **Cupid and Psyche** (the soul's
  trials for love), told in Apuleius, is his most famous tale.
