# Khonsu

**Khonsu** ("the Traveller") is the **moon-god** of [[theban-amun-cult|Thebes]] — the divine child of
**[[amun|Amun]]** and **[[mut|Mut]]**, completing the **Theban Triad**. As the moon crossing the night sky he
is a god of **time and the calendar**, and — because the moon's light was thought to aid conception and the
moon-god [[thoth|Thoth's]] wisdom to heal — he became a powerful god of **healing and exorcism**, famous for
casting out demons (as in the Bentresh Stela, where his image cures a foreign princess). Often shown as a
youth with the side-lock of childhood and a crescent-and-disk crown.
