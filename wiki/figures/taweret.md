# Taweret

**Taweret** ("the Great One") is the **hippopotamus-goddess** who protects **pregnant women and women in
childbirth** — a fierce guardian shown as an upright, pregnant hippopotamus with the limbs of a lion, the
back of a crocodile, and human breasts, often holding the *sa* sign of protection or a torch to drive off
demons. Combining the most dangerous animals of the Nile, her terrifying form was turned to **protective**
ends: she frightened away the evil forces that threatened mothers and infants at the perilous moment of
birth. With **[[bes|Bes]]**, she was among the most popular **household deities**, her image on amulets,
beds, and the magical "birth bricks." (Her dangerous male counterpart was associated with [[set|Set]].)
