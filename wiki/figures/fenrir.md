# Fenrir

**Fenrir** is the monstrous **wolf** of [[norse-religion|Norse]] myth — a child of **[[loki|Loki]]** and the
giantess Angrboða — fated to **devour [[odin|Odin]] at [[ragnarok|Ragnarök]]**. As Fenrir grew with
terrifying speed, the gods, knowing the prophecy, resolved to **bind** it. Twice it broke ordinary fetters;
so the dwarves forged the silken-soft but unbreakable ribbon **Gleipnir** (made of impossible things — a
cat's footfall, a woman's beard, a mountain's roots…). Fenrir agreed to be bound only as a test of strength,
and only if a god laid a hand in its jaws as surety — and **[[tyr|Týr]] alone dared**, losing his hand when
the wolf was betrayed (see [[tyr|Týr]]). Bound until the end, Fenrir **breaks free at Ragnarök**, runs with
jaws gaping from earth to sky, and **swallows Odin** — then is killed by Odin's son **Víðarr**, who tears its
jaws apart. With its kin [[jormungandr|Jörmungandr]] and [[hel|Hel]], Fenrir embodies the **monstrous chaos**
the doomed gods can only postpone.
