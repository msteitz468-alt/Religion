# Itzamna

**Itzamna** is the **supreme creator-god of the [[maya-religion|Maya]]** — an aged, benevolent lord of the
**heavens, day and night**, and the great culture-bringer credited with giving humanity **writing, the
calendar, learning, agriculture, and medicine**. The wise high god who set the cosmos in order, he is
associated with the celestial **"sky-serpent"** and (in his many aspects) presides over the priestly knowledge
on which Maya civilization rested. Often paired with the moon-and-medicine goddess **[[ix-chel|Ix Chel]]** (as
her consort), he is the **first priest** and the patron of the scribes who kept the sacred books. Among the
most peaceable and least sacrificial of the great gods, Itzamna is the Maya counterpart to the supreme,
ordering creator-figures of other pantheons.
