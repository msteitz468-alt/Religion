# Ix Chel

**Ix Chel** ("Lady Rainbow") is the great [[maya-religion|Maya]] goddess of the **moon, childbirth, medicine,
weaving, and water** — patroness of women, midwives, and healers. A goddess of **two faces** like the moon
itself: a benevolent young goddess of fertility, weaving, and healing, and an aged, fearsome crone associated
with **floods, destruction, and the waters of death** (sometimes shown with serpents in her hair and clawed
hands, pouring out a destroying flood). Women made **pilgrimage to her shrine on the island of Cozumel** to
pray for fertility and safe childbirth. As consort of the creator **[[itzamna|Itzamna]]** and goddess of the
moon, she complements the solar and creator gods, governing the intimate cycles of women's lives, the tides,
and the healing arts.
