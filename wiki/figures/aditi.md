# Aditi

**Aditi** ("the Boundless," "the Unbound," from *a-diti*, "non-binding/freedom") is a great
**mother-goddess** of [[vedic-religion|Vedic religion]] — the personification of **infinity, freedom, and the
limitless expanse**, and the mother of the **Adityas**, the sovereign gods led by **[[varuna|Varuna]]** and
**[[mitra|Mitra]]** (and, in some lists, including [[surya|Surya]] and others). As the boundless source she is
associated with the sky, the earth, and all space; she **frees from sin, sickness, and bondage** (fitting her
name), and is invoked for liberation, protection, and the granting of children. A somewhat abstract,
all-encompassing maternal principle — the "mother of the gods" whose boundlessness contrasts with the bound,
ordered world the gods uphold.
