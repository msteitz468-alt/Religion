# Jacob (Book of Mormon)

**Jacob**, son of [[lehi|Lehi]] born in the wilderness, is consecrated a priest and teacher by [[nephi|Nephi]] and is the voice of 2 Nephi 6–10 and the Book of Jacob.

## Biographical Overview (textual)

- Blessed as firstborn of the wilderness (2 Ne 2); consecrated with Joseph (2 Ne 5:26).
- Temple sermon: Isaiah likened to Israel; infinite atonement; "O how great the plan of our God" (2 Ne 9); angel names the Messiah **Christ** (2 Ne 10:3).
- Own book: condemns pride and unauthorized polygyny (Jacob 2–3); delivers Zenos's **olive-tree allegory** (Jacob 5); confronts **Sherem** the anti-Christ (Jacob 7); turns plates to Enos.

## Theological Significance

- Principal early voice for **plan of salvation** vocabulary and atonement.
- Jacob 2 is a major locus for later debates about plural marriage vs. BoM monogamy teaching (see [[plural-marriage-lds]]).
