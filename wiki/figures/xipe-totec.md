# Xipe Totec

**Xipe Totec** ("Our Lord the Flayed One") is the [[aztec-religion|Aztec]] god of **spring, agricultural
renewal, and regeneration** — and the patron of goldsmiths. His grim symbolism made vivid the idea of **new
life out of death**: in his festival (Tlacaxipehualiztli), sacrificial victims were **flayed**, and priests
wore the **flayed skins** for twenty days until they rotted away and were cast off — an enactment of the seed
shedding its husk, the **earth putting on the new green growth** of spring over the dead skin of winter. He is
usually shown wearing a flayed human skin, the loose hands of it dangling at his wrists. Behind the
disturbing imagery is the universal Mesoamerican conviction that **life is renewed only through death and
sacrifice** (see [[mesoamerican-sacrifice|sacrifice]]).
