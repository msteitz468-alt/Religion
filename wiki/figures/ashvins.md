# The Ashvins

The **Ashvins** (Nasatyas) are the **divine twins** of [[vedic-religion|Vedic religion]] — radiant young
**horsemen** who ride a golden chariot across the sky just before the dawn ([[ushas|Ushas]]), bringing light,
and who are above all **gods of healing, youth, and rescue**. The **physicians of the gods**, they are
endlessly praised for their miraculous aid to humans in distress: restoring sight to the blind, youth to the
old, a leg to the maimed, and saving the shipwrecked and the lost. As **twin saviors and healers** they are a
clear instance of the widespread **Indo-European "Divine Twins"** (compare the Greek **Dioscuri**,
Castor and Pollux, and the Baltic "Sons of God") — a shared inheritance of horse-riding, dawn-associated
rescuer twins.
