# Apep (Apophis)

**Apep** (Greek **Apophis**) is the giant **serpent of chaos and destruction** — the eternal enemy of the
sun-god [[ra|Ra]] and of [[maat|ma'at]] itself, the embodiment of the darkness, dissolution, and non-existence
(*isfet*) that the ordered cosmos must forever resist.

## The Nightly Battle

Each night, as [[ra|Ra's]] solar barque travels through the underworld, **Apep lies in wait to swallow the
sun** and plunge the world into eternal darkness. He is repelled in a nightly battle by the gods of Ra's
crew — above all by **[[set|Set]]**, who spears him from the prow — and is cut, burned, and bound, only to
regenerate and threaten again the next night. Apep is never finally destroyed: chaos is permanent, and
**maintaining order is an unending struggle**, the cosmic counterpart of the human duty to uphold ma'at.
Priests performed rituals ("the Overthrowing of Apep," burning wax figures of him) to aid Ra's victory.
