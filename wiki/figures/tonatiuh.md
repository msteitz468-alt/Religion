# Tonatiuh

**Tonatiuh** is the [[aztec-religion|Aztec]] **sun-god** — the deity of the present age, the **Fifth Sun**,
whose face is thought to gaze out from the center of the great **Aztec Sun Stone (Calendar Stone)**. He
embodies the sun that was born by the gods' self-sacrifice at Teotihuacan (see the [[five-suns|Five Suns]]) and
that must be **fed with the blood of [[mesoamerican-sacrifice|human sacrifice]]** to keep rising and moving
across the sky — for without that nourishment the sun would halt and the world would end in darkness and
earthquake. He was also conceived as the destination of **fallen warriors and sacrificed captives**, who
became his radiant companions, escorting the sun across the morning sky (women who died in childbirth escorted
it in the afternoon) — a warrior's heaven, one of the few bright afterlives in Mesoamerican thought.
