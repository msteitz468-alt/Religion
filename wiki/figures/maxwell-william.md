# William Sutherland Maxwell (1874–1952)

*All content from Peter Smith, A Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith (Oneworld, 2000), lines 22078–22096.*

## Biographical Overview
Canadian [[hands-of-the-cause|Hand of the Cause]]. He was the husband of May [[maxwell-may|Maxwell]] (married 1902) and father of [[ruhiyyih-khanum|Rúhíyyih Khánum]]. He became a Bahá'í after meeting [[abdu-l-baha|'Abdu'l-Bahá]] in 1909. Following May's death in 1940 he moved to Haifa.

A well-known architect, he designed the superstructure for the [[shrine-of-the-bab|Shrine of the Báb]]. He was named a Hand of the Cause in December 1951, shortly before his death. [[shoghi-effendi|Shoghi Effendi]] also named one of the doors of the Shrine in his honour (lines 22078–22095).
