# Nergal

**Nergal** is the [[mesopotamian-religion|Mesopotamian]] god of **plague, war, death, and the destructive
heat of the noonday sun** — a fearsome bringer of mass death (war and pestilence), associated with the planet
**Mars**. His patron city was Kutha, a center of the cult of the dead. In the myth of **Nergal and
[[ereshkigal|Ereshkigal]]**, Nergal descends to the underworld, becomes the lover of its queen, and remains
to rule beside her as **king of the dead** — so he, with Ereshkigal, governs the realm of the departed. He
embodies the violent, sudden forms of death (the battlefield and the epidemic), the grim counterpart to the
life-giving powers.
