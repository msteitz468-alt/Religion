# Moroni (Book of Mormon)

**Moroni**, son of [[mormon-historian|Mormon]], completes the [[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]] record, seals and hides the plates, and — in Latter-day Saint narrative — returns as the **angel** who directs [[joseph-smith|Joseph Smith]] to them (JS-H; D&C 2, 27).

## In the Book of Mormon

- Survives the Nephite destruction; wanders alone; finishes his father's book (Mormon 8–9): latter-day churches, God of miracles, reformed Egyptian.
- Edits the **Book of Ether**; includes the brother of Jared theophany and Ether 12 on faith, hope, and charity; warns Gentiles against secret combinations (Ether 8).
- **Moroni 1–6** — ordinances: gift of the Holy Ghost by hands; ordination; sacrament prayers; baptismal discipline.
- **Moroni 7** — Mormon’s sermon on faith, hope, charity; Spirit of Christ to judge good from evil.
- **Moroni 8** — condemns infant baptism.
- **Moroni 10** — gifts of the Spirit; the **promise**: ask God with real intent whether the record is true; the Holy Ghost will manifest the truth of all things (10:3–5); "come unto Christ, and be perfected in him"; farewell at Jehovah’s bar.

## In the Restoration narrative

- Appears to Joseph Smith 21–22 September 1823 (and annually); identifies the plates as the record of former inhabitants of this continent; quotes Malachi, Isaiah, Acts, Joel with variation (JS-H 1:27–54).
- Listed in the keys chain of D&C 27; Elijah/priesthood themes of D&C 2.

## Theological Significance

- **Epistemological seal** of the Book of Mormon (Moroni 10) — spiritual confirmation as truth-test.
- Bridge figure between ancient narrative and modern restoration.
- Title-page sealer: "sealed by the hand of Moroni."
