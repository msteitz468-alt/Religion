# Nike (Victoria)

**Nike** (Roman **Victoria**) is the winged goddess who **personifies Victory** — in war, athletics, and
every contest. Daughter of the Titan Pallas and the river **Styx**, she and her siblings (Zelus/Rivalry,
Kratos/Strength, Bia/Force) sided with [[zeus|Zeus]] in the Titanomachy and thereafter attend him,
crowning victors.

## Cult and Significance

Often shown flying down to bestow a wreath; closely linked with [[athena|Athena]] (Athena Nike on the
Acropolis). As **Victoria**, she was hugely important to **Rome**: her image in the Senate house and the
**Altar of Victory** became a famous flashpoint in the 4th-century struggle between pagan senators and the
Christian emperors over the end of the old religion. The model for innumerable victory monuments (and the
modern brand name).
