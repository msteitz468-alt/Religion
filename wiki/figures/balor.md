# Balor

**Balor of the Evil Eye** is the great **Fomorian** champion of [[celtic-religion|Celtic religion]] — the
one-eyed power of **blight and chaos** opposed to the **[[the-tuatha-de-danann|Tuatha Dé Danann]]**. His
single enormous eye, kept shut beneath a heavy lid that **four men must haul open with a ring**, **withers and
destroys whatever it looks upon**, blasting whole armies; it gained its venom, the story says, from the fumes
of his father's sorcerers' brew. A prophecy warns Balor he will be killed by his own **grandson**, so he locks
his daughter **Ethniu** in a tower — but she bears **[[lugh|Lugh]]** nonetheless.

At the **Second Battle of Mag Tuired**, as Balor's deadly eye is being levered open and has already slain
**[[nuada|Nuada]]**, Lugh strikes it with a **sling-stone** that drives the eye **back through Balor's skull**,
so that its blighting gaze falls on the **Fomorian host behind him** and destroys them. Balor's death breaks
the Fomorian tyranny. He is the Irish chaos-power of the **[[the-combat-myth|combat myth]]** — the malign,
one-eyed force of barrenness whom the bright, skilled god must overthrow (cf. [[tiamat|Tiamat]],
[[jormungandr|the Midgard Serpent]]).
