# Tyche

**Tyche** is the Greek goddess of **fortune, chance, and prosperity** — and especially the **luck of a city**
(each city had its protecting Tyche, often crowned with city walls). Her Roman counterpart is
**[[fortuna|Fortuna]]**.

## Character and Significance

Holder of the **rudder** (she steers events), the **cornucopia** (she dispenses plenty), and sometimes a
**wheel** or **ball** (fortune's instability). Her prominence **grew enormously in the Hellenistic age**,
when the collapse of the old city-states and the rise of vast, unpredictable empires made **blind chance**
feel like the real ruler of human affairs — a mood the philosophers (Stoics, Epicureans) and later Christians
argued against (providence vs. Fortune; cf. Boethius's *Wheel of Fortune*).
