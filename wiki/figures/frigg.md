# Frigg

**Frigg** is the **queen of the Æsir** and wife of [[odin|Odin]] — goddess of **marriage, motherhood, the
household, and prophecy**. She gives her name to **"Friday"** (Anglo-Saxon Frīge). Foremost of the goddesses,
she alone shares Odin's high seat Hliðskjálf, from which all worlds can be seen.

## Foreknowledge and Grief

Frigg **knows the fate of all** but speaks it to none — a foreknowledge that makes her central to the wiki's
Norse tragedy: it is Frigg who, dreading the death of her radiant son **[[baldr|Baldr]]**, extracts the oath
from all things not to harm him, overlooking only the **mistletoe** — the gap through which [[loki|Loki]]
engineers Baldr's death (see [[baldr|Baldr]]). Her grief and her attempt to ransom Baldr from Hel drive the
myth that begins the slide to [[ragnarok|Ragnarök]]. A goddess of the loom and of fate, of wifely devotion and
a mother's loss. (She is sometimes confused or merged with [[freyja|Freyja]] in the sources.)
