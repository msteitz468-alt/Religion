# Manannán mac Lir

**Manannán mac Lir** ("Manannán son of the Sea") is the **sea-god and Otherworld lord** of
[[celtic-religion|Celtic religion]] — the ferryman between the human world and the
**[[the-otherworld|Otherworld]]**, whose realm lies across or beneath the waves. The **Isle of Man** bears his
name. He rides the sea in his self-steering boat **Wave-Sweeper (Scuabtuinne)** and across it in a chariot as
if over a plain; he owns the **horse Enbarr** of the flowing mane, a **cloak of invisibility / forgetfulness**,
and a sword, **Fragarach** ("the Answerer"), that no armor stops. He gives the **[[the-tuatha-de-danann|Tuatha
Dé Danann]]**, after their defeat by mortals, the **Féth Fíada** (the magic mist that hides them) and the
**Feast of Age** with its ale of immortality — so it is Manannán who settles the gods into the unaging
**síde**. In the Voyage of Bran and the tale of Cormac he meets travelers at sea and reveals that **what looks
like ocean to mortals is a flowery plain to him** — the Otherworld seen from within.

In Wales his counterpart **Manawydan fab Llŷr**, brother of **[[bran-the-blessed|Brân]]** and
**[[branwen|Branwen]]**, is the wise, patient hero of the **Third Branch** of the [[mabinogion|Mabinogion]],
who marries the widowed **[[rhiannon|Rhiannon]]** and breaks the enchantment that empties Dyfed by
out-thinking the sorcerer behind it.
