# Adad (Ishkur)

**Adad** (Akkadian; Sumerian **Ishkur**; West-Semitic **Hadad/Baal-Hadad**) is the **storm-god** of
[[mesopotamian-religion|Mesopotamia]] — lord of **rain, thunder, lightning, and the destructive storm**, a
profoundly **ambivalent** power. His rains bring the **fertility** on which dry-farming depends, but his
storms and the flood he unleashes bring **devastation**: in the flood myths it is Adad's thunder and tempest
that drown the world. Wielding the **lightning-bolt** and riding the storm, he is a god both prayed to for
rain and feared for ruin, and a great god of **divination** (the diviners' patron with [[utu-shamash|
Shamash]]). His West-Semitic form **Hadad/Baal** became the great storm-and-fertility god of Syria-Palestine,
the rival of YHWH in the Hebrew Bible.
