# Ymir

**Ymir** is the **first being** of the [[norse-religion|Norse]] cosmos — the primeval **frost-giant** from
whose slain body the world was made, the Norse instance of the widespread **creation-from-a-cosmic-body**
motif.

## The Creation from Ymir's Body

In the beginning there was only the void **Ginnungagap**, between the icy mist of **Niflheim** and the fire of
**Muspelheim**. Where the ice met the fire's heat, drops quickened into life: the giant **Ymir** and the
primeval cow **Auðumla** (who licked the first god, Búri, out of the salty ice). From Ymir's body more giants
were born. Then Búri's grandsons — **[[odin|Odin]]** and his brothers Vili and Vé — **slew Ymir** and from his
vast corpse **fashioned the world**: his **flesh became the earth**, his **blood the seas and lakes**, his
**bones the mountains**, his **teeth the rocks**, his **skull the dome of the sky** (held up at four corners
by dwarves), his **brains the clouds**, and his **eyebrows the wall of Midgard**. So the ordered world is
literally **made from the body of slain primeval chaos** — a close parallel to [[tiamat|Tiamat]] (Babylon)
and the Vedic [[prajapati|Purusha]]. The gods then made the **first humans, Ask and Embla**, from two
tree-trunks on the shore.
