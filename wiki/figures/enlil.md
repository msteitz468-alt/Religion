# Enlil

**Enlil** ("Lord Wind") is the **active king of the gods** in the older Sumerian and Babylonian pantheon —
god of **wind, air, storm, and the earth**, patron of the holy city of **Nippur**, and the one who actually
**governs the cosmos** and bestows kingship on earthly rulers (where his father [[anu|Anu]] is remote). To
hold the "Enlil-power" was to rule.

## Character

Enlil is powerful and necessary but **dangerous and often hostile to humanity**: it is Enlil who, disturbed
by the **noise of the teeming human race**, repeatedly sends plague, drought, and finally the **Great Flood**
to wipe them out (in the Atrahasis epic and the [[epic-of-gilgamesh|Gilgamesh]] flood) — only to be thwarted
by the human-friendly **[[enki|Enki/Ea]]**. He decrees destinies and can curse as well as bless. As Babylon
and Assyria rose, his supreme role was transferred to **[[marduk|Marduk]]** and **[[ashur|Ashur]]**.
