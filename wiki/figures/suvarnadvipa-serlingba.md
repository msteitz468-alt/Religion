# Suvarṇadvīpa (Ser-ling-ba)

**Dharmakīrti of Suvarṇadvīpa** (Tibetan **gSer-gling-pa**, "the man from the Golden Isle" — the
Srivijaya realm, traditionally Sumatra), the teacher from whom **[[atisha|Atiśa]]** received the
**[[bodhicitta|spirit of enlightenment]]** lineage and "a general presentation of the stages of the
Mahāyāna path."

In the **[[lamrim-chenmo|Lamrim Chenmo]]** (Vol. I, ch. 1), [[tsongkhapa|Tsong-kha-pa]] makes him a
key example: although Ser-ling-ba held the (in Atiśa's judgment inferior) "true-aspectarian"
Cittamātra view while Atiśa held [[madhyamaka|Madhyamaka]], Atiśa "upheld Ser-ling-ba as the guru who
was unrivaled amongst his gurus" — because the guru who transmits bodhicitta is the kindest, whatever
his philosophical view. The "six preparatory practices" of the meditation session are likewise called
"the activities of Ser-ling-ba." Per D. Seyfort Ruegg, Atiśa's *Bodhipathapradīpa* was itself based
in part on instruction received from him.
