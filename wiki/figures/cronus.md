# Cronus (Saturn)

**Cronus** (Roman **Saturn**) is the youngest [[cronus|Titan]] and their **king** — the son of
[[uranus|Uranus]] and [[gaia|Gaia]] who **castrated his father** to seize power, and was in turn overthrown
by his own son **[[zeus|Zeus]]**.

## Myths

Warned that a son would depose him, Cronus **swallowed each of his children** at birth ([[hestia|Hestia]],
[[demeter|Demeter]], [[hera|Hera]], [[hades|Hades]], [[poseidon|Poseidon]]) — until Rhea hid the infant Zeus
and gave Cronus a stone instead. Zeus grew up, forced him to disgorge the others, and led the **Titanomachy**,
imprisoning the Titans in Tartarus. (Often punningly linked with *Chronos*, "Time.")

## Roman Saturn and the Golden Age

The Romans identified him with **Saturn**, an old Italic god of **agriculture and the harvest**, said to
have ruled Italy in a lost **Golden Age** of peace and plenty. His festival, the **Saturnalia** (December),
was Rome's most joyous — role-reversals, gift-giving, and license — and a notable influence on the timing and
spirit of later midwinter (Christmas) customs.
