# Thoth

**Thoth** (Egyptian *Djehuty*), shown as an **ibis** or ibis-headed man (or a baboon), is the god of
**wisdom, writing, the moon, magic, time, and reckoning** — the divine **scribe and intellect** of
[[egyptian-religion|Egypt]] and patron of [[hermopolitan-theology|Hermopolis]].

## Roles

The **inventor of writing** (hieroglyphs, "the words of the gods") and of mathematics, astronomy, and the
calendar; the **scribe of the gods** who records all things; the **arbiter** who settles their disputes (he
mediates the Contendings of [[horus|Horus]] and [[set|Set]] and restores Horus's torn-out eye). At the
[[maat|weighing of the heart]] it is Thoth who **records the verdict** of the judgment. He gave [[isis|Isis]]
the spells to heal the infant Horus, and his magic and wisdom uphold [[maat|ma'at]]. As the lord of secret
knowledge he was identified by the Greeks with **Hermes Trismegistus**, founder of the Hermetic and alchemical
tradition.
