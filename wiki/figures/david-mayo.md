# David Mayo

**David Mayo** was the **Senior Case Supervisor International** and a **Class 12 Auditor** — the highest auditing classification in [[scientology|Scientology]], available only to [[sea-org|Sea Organization]] members trained at Flag. He was the movement's foremost technical expert in the late 1970s and early 1980s, and Hubbard's designated successor for Scientology's auditing technology. His removal and subsequent founding of a breakaway organization makes him a significant figure in Scientology's internal schisms.

## Development of NOTs

In **September 1978**, Mayo worked intensively with [[l-ron-hubbard|L. Ron Hubbard]] to develop **New Era Dianetics for Operating Thetans (NOTs)** — the advanced auditing procedure designed to address "body thetans" (spiritual entities clinging to an individual, whose presence is said to cause physical and psychological problems). NOTs became a central element of the upper Bridge and is widely regarded as one of Scientology's most commercially significant courses.

The collaborative nature of NOTs' development has been disputed: the Church credits the work entirely to Hubbard; Mayo claims significant co-development. Atack's account credits both but notes Mayo's central practical role.

## "Tech Hats" — Designated Heir

In **April 1982**, Hubbard reportedly gave Mayo the **"tech hats"** — responsibility for maintaining and developing Scientology's auditing technology — for a period of 20–25 years. This made Mayo, in effect, Hubbard's designated technical successor. This delegation is central to Mayo's later claim that his subsequent removal was illegitimate.

## Removal and RPF

In **August 1982**, Mayo was removed from his position. He was sent to **Happy Valley**, the Sea Org detention facility on an Indian reservation near the California desert, where (per Atack and other ex-member testimony) executives and senior staff were required to run around poles in the desert for extended periods daily as punitive conditioning. Mayo was one of the most senior people subjected to this treatment.

## RTC Incorporation Dispute

Mayo reportedly **signed the incorporation documents for the Religious Technology Center (RTC)** on **January 1, 1982**. He subsequently claimed that the terms of the incorporation were **altered after his signature** — a claim that, if accurate, would mean RTC was built partly on a fraudulently altered document. The Church disputes this. Atack records it without adjudication.

## Advanced Ability Center (AAC)

After leaving the Church, Mayo founded the **Advanced Ability Center (AAC)** in Santa Barbara, California, in **July 1983** — one of the earliest and most professionally credentialed organizations in the **"Free Zone"** (independent Scientology practice outside Church control). The AAC was subject to immediate legal attack from the Church over trademark and copyright claims. It eventually closed under legal pressure.

## Significance

Mayo represents the "technical lineage" strand of Scientology schism: unlike critics who reject the tech entirely, he claimed the tech was valid but that the Church had deviated from it (and from Hubbard's legitimate designations). This mirrors a recurring pattern in NRM schisms — the legitimate heir who is displaced and then treated as heretical. The Church has consistently portrayed him as a "squirrel" (Scientology terminology for one who misapplies standard tech).
