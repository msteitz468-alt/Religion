# Nyx (Nox)

**Nyx** (Roman **Nox**) is the primordial goddess of the **Night** — one of the first beings, born of
[[chaos|Chaos]], and a power so ancient and dread that even [[zeus|Zeus]] feared to offend her.

## In the Theogony

From Nyx (with Erebus, Darkness) come Aether and Day; and from Nyx alone springs a brood of dark powers —
**Death (Thanatos), Sleep (Hypnos), the [[fate-moirai|Fates (Moirai)]], Nemesis, Strife (Eris), Doom, Old
Age**, and the rest. She is thus the mother of much of the cosmos's shadow side. In **[[orphism|Orphic]]**
theogony Nyx is even more exalted — a primal creative principle and oracular power at the beginning of all
things.
