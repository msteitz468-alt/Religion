# Shu

**Shu** is the Egyptian **god of air, light, and the atmosphere** — the first being created by [[ra|Atum]]
(with his twin [[tefnut|Tefnut]]), and father of [[geb|Geb]] (earth) and [[nut|Nut]] (sky). His great cosmic
role is to **hold the sky-goddess Nut aloft above the earth-god Geb**, standing between them with arms
upraised — the **separation of sky and earth** that makes a habitable world possible (a creation motif
paralleled in many traditions). As the god of the cool air and the light that fills the space of the world,
he also wards off the powers of chaos.
