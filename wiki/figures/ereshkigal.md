# Ereshkigal

**Ereshkigal** ("Queen of the Great Below") is the dread goddess who rules the [[mesopotamian-underworld|
Mesopotamian underworld]] — the dark "Land of No Return" where all the dead descend. Sister (and dark
counterpart) of [[ishtar|Inanna]], she presides over the gloomy realm of dust with absolute, joyless
authority.

## Myths

In the **Descent of [[ishtar|Inanna]]**, it is Ereshkigal who strips her sister of all power at the seven
gates, fixes her with the eye of death, and hangs her corpse on a hook — the implacable law of the dead. In
the myth of **Nergal and Ereshkigal**, the war-god **[[nergal|Nergal]]** comes to the underworld, becomes her
lover, and stays to rule beside her as king of the dead. Ereshkigal embodies the **finality of death** in a
tradition that offered no resurrection and no reward — death's queen, whom even the great goddess could not
overcome without a substitute.
