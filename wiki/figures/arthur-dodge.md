# Arthur Pillsbury Dodge

*Populated from [[peter-smith|Smith, Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith (2000)]]. Claims grounded in lines 10939–10951.*

## Biographical Overview

Prominent early American Bahá'í. A lawyer, magazine publisher, and inventor, he became a Bahá'í in 1897. His home in New York City became the meeting place for the newly established Bahá'í community, and he was chosen as its first president (1898) (lines 10941–10947).

[[shoghi-effendi|Shoghi Effendi]] named him as one of the [[disciples-of-abdu-l-baha|Disciples of 'Abdu'l-Bahá]] (lines 10947–10950).
