# Tezcatlipoca

**Tezcatlipoca** ("Smoking Mirror") is the great **fate-god** of the [[aztec-religion|Aztecs]] — an
all-powerful, **capricious, and inscrutable** deity of **destiny, the night sky, sorcery, kingship, war, and
strife**, who sees all things in his obsidian **smoking mirror**. The most feared and revered of the gods,
"the Enemy on Both Sides," he gives and withdraws fortune at will, raising up kings and casting them down,
rewarding and ruining without explanation — a god before whom humans are utterly at the mercy of an
unaccountable destiny.

## Myths

Tezcatlipoca is the eternal **rival of [[quetzalcoatl|Quetzalcoatl]]**: in the cosmology of the [[five-suns|
Five Suns]] the two alternately serve as the sun and overthrow one another, and Tezcatlipoca, by his sorcery,
**shames Quetzalcoatl into exile**. In the creation, Tezcatlipoca offered his own **foot** as bait to the
earth-monster Cipactli (whence his missing foot), helping tear her apart to make the earth and sky. A famous
rite dramatized his lordship over fate: each year a flawless young man was chosen to **impersonate
Tezcatlipoca**, honored as a living god for a year in every pleasure — and then **sacrificed**, a vivid image
of fortune's reversal and the fragility of all earthly glory.
