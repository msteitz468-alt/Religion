# Nephthys

**Nephthys** (Egyptian *Nebet-Het*) is the goddess of **mourning, the night, and the protection of the dead**
— youngest of the four children of [[geb|Geb]] and [[nut|Nut]], sister of [[isis|Isis]], [[osiris|Osiris]],
and [[set|Set]] (whose wife she is). Despite her marriage to Set, she sided with Isis: together the two
sisters are the great **mourners** who lament and protect the dead [[osiris|Osiris]] (and, by extension,
every deceased person), and she helped [[isis|Isis]] and [[anubis|Anubis]] gather and embalm his body. (By
Osiris she was, in some traditions, the mother of [[anubis|Anubis]].) The paired figures of Isis and Nephthys
at the head and foot of the bier are a constant of Egyptian funerary art.
