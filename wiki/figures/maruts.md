# The Maruts

The **Maruts** are the youthful **storm-gods** of [[vedic-religion|Vedic religion]] — a fierce, glittering
**troop of brothers** (sons of [[rudra|Rudra]], hence the *Rudras*) who ride the winds and thunderclouds in
golden armor, brandishing lightning and bringing the rains. As a **warrior-band** they are the loyal
companions and allies of **[[indra|Indra]]**, roaring across the sky at his side in the battle against
[[vritra|Vritra]] (though one hymn dramatizes a quarrel when Indra claims the victory for himself alone).
They embody the **violent splendor of the monsoon storm** — terrifying yet life-giving — and the ideal of the
youthful, united warrior brotherhood.
