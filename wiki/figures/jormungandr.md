# Jörmungandr

**Jörmungandr**, the **Midgard Serpent** or **World-Serpent**, is the colossal sea-serpent of
[[norse-religion|Norse]] myth — a child of **[[loki|Loki]]** — so vast that it **encircles the entire world
(Midgard), biting its own tail** beneath the ocean. [[odin|Odin]] cast it into the sea, where it grew to ring
the earth (an *ouroboros* whose loosing helps signal [[ragnarok|Ragnarök]]).

Its great enemy is **[[thor|Thor]]**: once Thor nearly **hauled it from the sea on a fishing line** and raised
his hammer to kill it (a favorite scene of Norse art) before the line was cut. At **[[ragnarok|Ragnarök]]**
the two meet for the last time — **Thor kills the serpent, but staggers back nine paces and dies of its
venom**, the storm-god and the chaos-dragon destroying each other. With its siblings [[fenrir|Fenrir]] and
[[hel|Hel]], Jörmungandr is the wiki's Norse **chaos-serpent** ([[the-combat-myth|combat myth]];
cf. [[tiamat|Tiamat]], [[vritra|Vritra]], Leviathan).
