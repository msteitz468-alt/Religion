# Chaos

**Chaos** is the **first thing that came into being** in [[hesiod-theogony|Hesiod's]] cosmogony — not
"disorder" in the modern sense but a **primordial gap or void** ("Chaos" from *chainein*, "to gape/yawn"),
the formless beginning out of which the ordered cosmos emerges.

## In the Theogony

"First of all **Chaos** came to be, and then broad-breasted **[[gaia|Gaia]]** (Earth)… and dim **Tartarus**…
and **[[eros|Eros]]** (Desire)." From Chaos come **[[nyx|Nyx]]** (Night) and **Erebus** (Darkness), and from
their union Aether (Bright Air) and Hemera (Day). Chaos is thus the **root of the dark, primal line** of the
cosmos, parallel to Gaia's generative line. Later writers (Ovid) reinterpreted Chaos as the **unformed
chaotic mass** of elements before creation — closer to the modern meaning.
