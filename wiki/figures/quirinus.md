# Quirinus

**Quirinus** is one of the oldest and most distinctively **Roman** gods — with **no Greek equivalent** — a
member of the **Archaic Triad** (with [[zeus|Jupiter]] and [[ares|Mars]]) that headed the earliest Roman
state religion before the Capitoline Triad. His own priest (the *flamen Quirinalis*) was one of the three
major flamines.

## Identity

His original nature is debated — apparently a **war-and-civic god** of the Sabine/early-Roman community
(perhaps "the god of the *Quirites*," the Roman citizenry in their civil, peacetime aspect, as Mars governed
them in war). By the late Republic he was firmly identified with the **deified Romulus**: Rome's founder,
taken up to heaven after death and worshipped as the god Quirinus — an early model of Roman **apotheosis**
that prefigured the later [[imperial-cult|imperial cult]].
