# Freyr

**Freyr** ("Lord") is the great god of the [[vanir-cult|Vanir]] — deity of **fertility, prosperity, peace,
fair weather, and bountiful harvests**, and of **sacral kingship**. Son of [[njord|Njörð]] and twin of
[[freyja|Freyja]], he was worshipped especially in **Sweden** (the great temple at **Uppsala**), where the
royal Yngling line claimed descent from him (Yngvi-Freyr).

## Roles and Myths

Freyr brings **peace and plenty** — good seasons, prosperity, and male potency (his images were notably
ithyphallic). He owns the magical ship **Skíðblaðnir** and the golden boar **Gullinbursti**. His central myth
(*Skírnismál*) is his lovesickness for the giantess **Gerðr**: pining for her, Freyr **gave away his magic
sword** (which fights on its own) to win her — a fateful loss, for at [[ragnarok|Ragnarök]] he must face the
fire-giant **Surtr without it**, and falls. His giving up the sword for love is one of the poignant
"fated-loss" motifs of Norse myth.
