# Uranus (Caelus)

**Uranus** (Roman **Caelus**) is the personified **Sky** — son and husband of [[gaia|Gaia]] (Earth) and the
**first ruler of the cosmos** in the generations before the Olympians.

## The Castration Myth

Uranus and Gaia produce the **[[cronus|Titans]]**, Cyclopes, and Hundred-Handers, but Uranus, hating his
children, imprisons them within Gaia. In agony she forges a sickle and incites her son **[[cronus|Cronus]]**,
who **castrates his father** and casts the genitals into the sea — from the blood spring the Furies, Giants,
and nymphs; from the sea-foam rises **[[aphrodite|Aphrodite]]**. This **succession myth** (Uranus → Cronus →
[[zeus|Zeus]], each son overthrowing the father) is the spine of [[hesiod-theogony|Hesiod's]] theogony and a
famous parallel to Near Eastern succession myths (the Hurrian-Hittite *Kumarbi*).
