# Ptah

**Ptah** is the **creator-god of [[memphite-theology|Memphis]]** and the patron of **craftsmen, architects,
and artisans** — shown as a mummiform man holding a staff, with a skullcap.

## Roles

In the [[memphite-theology|Memphite Theology]], Ptah is the supreme creator who brings the world into being
**by the thought of his heart and the word of his tongue** — conceiving and *speaking* creation (a striking
ancient "logos" cosmogony; cf. [[genesis|Genesis 1]]). As the divine **craftsman** he is patron of all
making, and the Greeks identified him with **[[hephaestus|Hephaestus]]**. His high priest bore the title
"Greatest of Craftsmen." In funerary contexts he merged with Sokar and [[osiris|Osiris]] as
**Ptah-Sokar-Osiris**. The very word "**Egypt**" derives (via Greek *Aigyptos*) from one of his temple
names, *Hwt-ka-Ptah*.
