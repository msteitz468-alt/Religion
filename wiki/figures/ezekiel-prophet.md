## Biographical Overview
A priest who was called to be a prophet while in exile in Babylon by the Chebar canal, contemporary with the fall of Jerusalem.

## Primary Source Appearances
The primary human figure, narrator, and authorial voice of the Book of Ezekiel.

## Theological and Narrative Significance
Ezekiel functions as a "watchman" for Israel. His visions deeply emphasize the holiness and glory of God. He is central to articulating the theological development of individual responsibility and the promise of Israel's inner transformation via a new heart and new spirit under a New Covenant.
