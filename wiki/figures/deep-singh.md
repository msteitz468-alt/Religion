# Baba Deep Singh

Leader of the **Shaheed misl** and one of the most venerated martyrs of Sikh history, killed defending the
[[amritsar|Harimandir]] after its desecration by [[ahmad-shah-abdali|Ahmad Shah Abdali]]. This page draws on
**Khushwant Singh, *A History of the Sikhs*, Vol. 1, Ch. 8**.

## Account (Khushwant Singh)

Deep Singh was in scholarly retirement "making copies of the Granth" when Abdali's fourth invasion (1757)
blew up the Harimandir and fouled its sacred pool. He "proclaimed his intention of rebuilding the temple" and
gathered over five thousand peasants who "dressed themselves in gaudy clothes like bridegrooms going to their
nuptials." Intercepted at Goharwal, he was severely wounded in the neck and "was barely able to get to the
Harimandir to keep his tryst with death" (Singh, ch. 8). Singh notes his standing in Sikh devotion: "One of
the most revered heroes of Sikh history. Colour prints show him running with his head on his left palm, still
wielding his sword with his right" (fn.21). His misl took the name *śahīd* (martyr) after his death.
