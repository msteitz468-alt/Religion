# Ninurta

**Ninurta** (also **Ningirsu**) is a great **warrior-god** of [[mesopotamian-religion|Mesopotamia]] — a
champion who defends the gods against chaos, and also a god of **agriculture, healing, and the spring floods**
(the farmer-warrior). Son of [[enlil|Enlil]]. His chief myths cast him as the **dragon-slaying hero**: he
defeats the monstrous bird **Anzu**, who had stolen the **Tablet of Destinies**, recovering it for the gods;
and in *Lugal-e* he battles the demon Asag and a stone army, afterward organizing the rivers and irrigation
for Sumer's farmland. So Ninurta unites the **storm-warrior** and the **provider of fertility** — the divine
power that both fights chaos and brings the harvest. A forerunner of the champion-creator pattern fulfilled by
[[marduk|Marduk]].
