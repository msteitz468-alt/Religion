# Branwen

**Branwen daughter of Llŷr** gives her name to the **Second Branch** of the **[[mabinogion|Mabinogion]]** and
is one of its most tragic figures — sister of the giant-king **[[bran-the-blessed|Brân the Blessed]]** and of
**Manawydan** ([[manannan|Manannán]]'s Welsh reflex), a daughter of the **family of Llŷr**. Married to
**Matholwch, king of Ireland**, to seal an alliance between Britain and Ireland, she is at first honored — but
after her half-brother **Efnisien** insults Matholwch by mutilating his horses, the Irish turn on her:
Branwen is **driven from the king's bed, set to drudgery in the kitchen, and struck on the ear daily by the
butcher**. In her isolation she **tames a starling**, teaches it to speak, and **sends it across the Irish Sea
with a message** bound to its wing, so that her brother Brân raises all Britain to avenge her. The war that
follows is ruinous — fought over the **Cauldron of Rebirth** that keeps reviving the Irish dead — and leaves
only **seven British survivors**, Branwen herself among them. Brought home, she looks back on Britain and
Ireland both laid waste **for her sake**, cries *"Woe is me that I was ever born; two good islands have been
destroyed because of me,"* and **dies of a broken heart**, buried in a four-sided grave on the bank of the
Alaw. She is the Welsh archetype of the **bride whose mistreatment unmakes kingdoms**.
