# Mitra

**Mitra** ("Contract," "Friend") is the Vedic god of **covenants, oaths, friendship, and the binding
agreements that hold society together** — the close partner of **[[varuna|Varuna]]**, with whom he forms the
sovereign pair (the *Adityas*) upholding **[[rita|rita]]**. Where Varuna is the awesome, punishing guardian of
cosmic order, Mitra is the **benevolent god of human concord** — he watches over the keeping of promises,
presides over the daytime, and "brings people together," holding the social and moral world in order through
the **sanctity of the contract**. His Iranian cousin **Mithra** (a great god of covenant, truth, and the sun)
gave rise, by a long and indirect route, to the Roman mystery god of [[mithraism|Mithraism]] — a striking
Indo-Iranian-to-Roman thread.
