# Ninigi

**Ninigi-no-Mikoto** is the **grandson of the sun-goddess [[amaterasu|Amaterasu]]** and the pivotal figure of
the **heavenly descent (*tenson kōrin*)** in [[shinto|Shinto]] — the kami who comes down from heaven to rule
the earthly land, founding the line that leads to the **imperial house**.

After **[[okuninushi|Ōkuninushi]]** cedes the Central Land of Reed-Plains, **Amaterasu** (with the creative
kami Takami-musubi) sends her grandson Ninigi to govern it. She entrusts him with the **Three Sacred
Treasures** — the **mirror Yata-no-Kagami** (in which she had seen herself at the rock-cave), the sword
**Kusanagi** (won by [[susanoo|Susanoo]] from the [[yamata-no-orochi|serpent]]'s tail), and the jewel
**Yasakani-no-Magatama** — the regalia that **remain the symbols of imperial authority to this day**. Ninigi
**descends to the peak of Mount Takachiho in Himuka (Kyūshū)**, accompanied by the gods of the rock-cave
episode (including [[ame-no-uzume|Ame-no-Uzume]]), and the earthly kami **Sarutahiko** guides his way. On earth
he weds **Konohanasakuya-hime** ("Princess-Blossoming-Brilliantly-like-the-Flowers-of-the-Trees"), daughter of
the mountain-kami — but **rejects her elder sister Iwanaga-hime ("Rock-Long Princess")**, sent with her; for
this slight the mountain-god declares that, had he taken the rock-princess, his descendants would have lived
**eternal as stone**, but having chosen only the blossom-princess, they will be **beautiful but mortal as the
blossoms** — the [[kojiki|Kojiki]]'s explanation of why **even the emperors die**. Ninigi's son and grandson
(the "Luck of the Sea / Luck of the Mountain" brothers) continue the line to **[[jimmu|Jimmu]]**, the first
emperor.
