# Ninhursag

**Ninhursag** ("Lady of the Mountain"; also **Ninmah**, **Nintu**, "Lady of Birth") is the great
**mother-goddess** of [[mesopotamian-religion|Mesopotamia]] — goddess of the **earth, fertility, and
childbirth**, and one of the chief deities who **created humanity**. With **[[enki|Enki]]** she shaped the
first humans from **clay** (in Atrahasis, the birth-goddess mixes clay with the blood of a slain god and pinches
off the first people) so that they could do the gods' labor. In the myth *Enki and Ninhursag* (set in the
paradise-land Dilmun) she heals the sick Enki by giving birth to deities for each of his ailing body parts —
including, from his rib, a goddess whose name puns on "lady of the rib / lady who makes live," a detail often
compared with the creation of Eve from Adam's rib ([[genesis|Genesis 2]]). The nurturing, life-giving power
that balances the destructive gods.
