# Sekhmet

**Sekhmet** ("the Powerful One"), the **lioness-headed** goddess, is the fearsome deity of **war,
destruction, and plague** — and, by reversal, of **healing**. She is a form of the violent **Eye of [[ra|
Ra]]**, the burning, vengeful power of the sun unleashed against the enemies of order.

## The Destruction of Mankind

Her great myth (in Budge's *Legends of the Gods*): when humanity plotted against the aging [[ra|Ra]], he sent
his Eye as Sekhmet to **slaughter the rebels** — but her bloodlust grew so uncontrollable that she would have
**annihilated all humanity**. To save them, Ra flooded the fields with **beer dyed red like blood**; Sekhmet,
mistaking it for gore, drank until she fell into a drunken stupor, and the slaughter ceased. So Sekhmet is
both the **bringer of plague** ("the arrows of Sekhmet") and, propitiated, the **great healer** — her
priests were physicians. She is the wild counterpart of the gentle cat-goddess [[bastet|Bastet]].
