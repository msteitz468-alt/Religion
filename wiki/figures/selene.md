# Selene (Luna)

**Selene** (Roman **Luna**) is the goddess who **is the Moon** — sister of [[helios|Helios]] (Sun) and Eos
(Dawn), crossing the night sky in her chariot, crowned with a crescent.

## Myths and Associations

Best known for her love of the beautiful shepherd **Endymion**, granted eternal sleep so she could visit him
nightly. She forms a **triple moon-goddess** with [[artemis|Artemis]] (the new/waxing moon) and
[[hecate|Hecate]] (the dark moon) — a grouping important in magic and later esotericism. As **Luna** she had
a temple on the Aventine.
