# Utu (Shamash)

**Utu** (Sumerian; Akkadian **Shamash**) is the **sun-god** and, because the all-seeing sun observes
everything, the great god of **justice, truth, and law**. Son of the moon-god [[nanna-sin|Nanna/Sin]] and
brother of [[ishtar|Inanna]], he crosses the sky by day and the underworld by night, illuminating all and
judging the deeds of gods and men. As the patron of **justice** he protects the wronged and punishes the
oppressor — it is **Shamash** who is shown handing **Hammurabi** the rod and ring of authority atop the famous
law-code stele (cf. [[hammurapi|Hammurabi]]), framing Babylonian law as divinely sanctioned righteousness. He
is also a great god of **divination** (the diviners' patron), since the sun sees the truth of all things, and
he aids heroes (he protects [[gilgamesh|Gilgamesh]] and Enkidu against Humbaba).
