# Claire Headley

**Claire Headley** (née **Claire Edwards**) is a former [[sea-org|Sea Org]] member who served at [[gold-base|Gold Base]] from approximately the late 1980s through January 2005. She is the wife of memoir author **[[marc-headley|Marc Headley]]** (*Blown for Good*) and a significant whistleblower on Sea Org conditions, particularly coerced abortion practices at Gold Base.

## Biographical Overview

Claire Edwards joined the Sea Org as a minor. She is described by [[marc-headley|Marc Headley]] as having **red hair** and working in the **Qualifications Division** (Qual) at Golden Era Productions as a **supervisor**. The Qualifications Division handles training verification and correction of technical errors — a significant administrative role.

**Marriage**: Claire and Marc Headley married in **August 1992 in Las Vegas, Nevada**. Marc notes the Las Vegas location was specifically chosen because California required a psychological evaluation for marriages involving anyone under 18 — Claire was under 18 at the time. The marriage was performed without such an evaluation.

**Neil Gaiman connection**: Headley identifies Claire (as Claire Edwards at the time) as the **sister of author Neil Gaiman**. Headley notes that Neil Gaiman was himself at some point declared a Suppressive Person, though this was apparently later resolved. The Church has not publicly addressed this.

## Service at Gold Base

Claire worked at Gold Base throughout the period of Marc's service there (approximately 1990–2005). She is mentioned in [[wright-going-clear|Wright]] (*Going Clear*) as having testified about coerced abortions — one of the most significant testimonial records on that subject from Gold Base.

**Coerced abortions**: Wright documents that Sea Org policy at Gold Base effectively prohibited children; Claire Headley's testimony is among the most detailed on the practice of pressure to terminate pregnancies within the Sea Org. Wright estimates 60–80% of women at Gold Base terminated pregnancies. Claire testified about her personal experience with this policy.

## Escape (January 2005)

Claire's escape from Gold Base followed Marc's by approximately a day or two:

- She departed Gold Base via **Greyhound bus** — a different route and method than Marc's motorcycle escape
- In **Las Vegas**, she was intercepted by **RTC representative Greg Wilhere** and an associate named **Sharon Johnston**, who attempted to persuade her to return
- Claire **refused** to go back and continued to Kansas City, where Marc had already arrived
- Both were formally declared **Suppressive Persons** by the Church

At the time of Marc's book's publication in 2009, they had not spoken to their Sea Org family members for over four years.

## Post-Departure

Claire Headley became an active witness and litigant against the Church. She filed a civil lawsuit, making her testimony about coerced abortions and labor conditions a matter of public record. Her case is among those cited by critics as evidence of systematic abuse within the Sea Org.
