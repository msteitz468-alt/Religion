# Hecate

**Hecate** is the goddess of **magic, witchcraft, the crossroads, ghosts, and the night** — a liminal,
chthonic power honored at boundaries and gateways. [[hesiod-theogony|Hesiod]] gives her unusual honor as a
Titaness whom [[zeus|Zeus]] allows to keep wide powers over earth, sea, and sky.

## Character and Cult

A torch-bearing goddess often shown **three-formed** (facing three ways at the crossroads), accompanied by
hounds. She helped [[demeter|Demeter]] search for [[persephone|Persephone]] and so became associated with the
underworld and the restless dead. Offerings ("Hecate's suppers") were left at crossroads at the new moon.
Closely linked with the moon and with [[artemis|Artemis]] and [[selene|Selene]] in a **triple goddess** of
the moon, and invoked in magic throughout antiquity. As Roman **Trivia** ("of the three ways").
