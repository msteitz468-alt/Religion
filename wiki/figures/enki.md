# Enki (Ea)

**Enki** (Sumerian; Akkadian **Ea**) is the god of **wisdom, magic, crafts, and the fresh waters** of the
underground ocean (the **Apsu**) — the cleverest of the gods and, crucially, the **great friend and protector
of humanity**. Third of the supreme triad with [[anu|Anu]] and [[enlil|Enlil]], patron of **Eridu**.

## The Wise Helper of Mankind

Where [[enlil|Enlil]] is hostile to noisy humanity, **Enki repeatedly saves it**: he helped **create
humankind** (shaping the first people from clay, with the mother-goddess Ninhursag), and when the gods sent
the **Flood**, it was Enki who secretly **warned Utnapishtim** to build the boat (subverting the gods' decree
by telling it to a reed wall while the man "happened" to overhear) — the act that spared the human race (see
[[epic-of-gilgamesh|Gilgamesh]] and the [[genesis-ancient-near-east|flood parallels]]). In the [[enuma-elish|
Enuma Elish]] it is Ea who **kills [[apsu|Apsu]]**, fathers [[marduk|Marduk]], and devises humanity from
Kingu's blood. The trickster-sage who bends the rules for mercy and cunning, he holds and dispenses the
**me** (the divine decrees of civilization).
