# Ah Puch

**Ah Puch** (also **Yum Cimil**, "Lord of Death," or **Kisin**) is the [[maya-religion|Maya]] god of **death,
decay, and the underworld** — the lord (or chief lord) of **[[xibalba|Xibalba]]**, the realm of the dead. A
skeletal, putrefying figure adorned with bells, often shown with the swollen belly of a corpse and accompanied
by the **owl** and the dog (omens of death), he presides over disease, the rot of the body, and the terrors of
the underworld. In the [[popol-vuh|Popol Vuh]] the death-lords of Xibalba (One Death and Seven Death) — of whom
Ah Puch is the type — are the great adversaries **defeated by the [[hero-twins|Hero Twins]]**, whose victory
breaks death's absolute dominion over the world. The Maya feared and propitiated the death-god, for whom the
ever-present reality of death loomed large in their religion.
