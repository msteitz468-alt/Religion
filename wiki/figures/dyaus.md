# Dyaus (Dyaus Pitar)

**Dyaus Pitar** ("Sky Father") is the personified **Sky** of [[vedic-religion|Vedic religion]] — paired with
**Prithvi** (Earth Mother), the two together (**Dyavaprithvi**) the parents of the gods. By Vedic times Dyaus
is already an **archaic, fading figure**, rarely worshipped on his own, the old sky-father overshadowed by his
more dynamic son [[indra|Indra]]. His chief importance is **comparative**: the name **Dyaus Pitar** is the
exact linguistic cognate of Greek **[[zeus|Zeus]] (pater)** and Latin **Jupiter** (*Diu-piter*) — and of Norse
Týr — preserving the name of the **Proto-Indo-European sky-father** (*Dyeus Phter*), the single most secure
reconstruction of Indo-European religion. Where the Greeks and Romans kept their *Dyeus* as the supreme king,
the Vedic Indians demoted theirs in favor of Indra — a revealing divergence from a common origin.
