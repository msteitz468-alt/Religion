# Gaia (Terra / Tellus)

**Gaia** (Roman **Terra** or **Tellus Mater**) is the personified **Earth** and the great **primordial
mother** from whom nearly all the gods descend — one of the first beings to emerge after [[chaos|Chaos]].

## In the Theogony

Gaia bears, without a mate, the **Sky ([[uranus|Uranus]])**, the **mountains**, and the **Sea (Pontus)**;
then, with Uranus, the **[[cronus|Titans]]**, the **Cyclopes**, and the Hundred-Handers. When Uranus
imprisons her children, Gaia incites **[[cronus|Cronus]]** to castrate him; later she helps [[zeus|Zeus]]
overthrow Cronus, then opposes Zeus by mothering the **Giants** and the monster **Typhon**. She is the
deep, fertile, prophetic ground of the cosmos (the original owner of the Delphic oracle).

## Cult and Significance

Honored as the nourishing Earth, source of crops and the dead alike (a chthonic power); the Roman **Tellus**
received agricultural and boundary rites. The archetypal **Mother Earth** of Western tradition.
