# Amaterasu

**Amaterasu-Ōmikami**, "the Great August Heaven-Shining Deity," is the **sun-goddess** and the **highest kami**
of [[shinto|Shinto]] — ruler of **Takamagahara**, the Plain of High Heaven, and the **divine ancestor of the
Japanese imperial line**. She is the radiant center of the [[kojiki|Kojiki]]'s age of the gods and the
linchpin of the imperial myth.

## Myths in Detail

### Her birth from purification

Amaterasu is born not by sexual generation but by **[[harae|purification]]**. When **[[izanagi-and-izanami|Izanagi]]**
returns from the polluting underworld of **Yomi** and **bathes to cleanse himself**, the greatest kami emerge
from the act: as he **washes his left eye, Amaterasu (the sun) is born**; from his right eye,
**[[tsukuyomi|Tsukuyomi]] (the moon)**; from his nose, **[[susanoo|Susanoo]] (the storm)**. Izanagi rejoices
and gives Amaterasu rule over the **High Plain of Heaven**, hanging his necklace of jewels upon her.

### The heavenly rock-cave (Ama-no-Iwato)

The central Amaterasu myth is her **withdrawal into the rock-cave**. Her brother **[[susanoo|Susanoo]]**, raging
through heaven, breaks down the rice-paddy divisions, defiles her halls, and finally **flings a flayed
piebald horse through the roof of her weaving hall**, killing one of her weaving-maidens. Terrified and
offended, **Amaterasu shuts herself into the heavenly rock-cave and seals the door — and the world goes
dark**, both heaven and earth plunged into night, and a host of calamities breaks out. The **eight hundred
myriad kami** assemble to lure her out: they gather long-singing cocks, forge a great **mirror** and string a
jewel-necklace, hang them with cloth offerings on a sacred *sakaki* tree, and the goddess **[[ame-no-uzume|Ame-no-Uzume]]**
performs a wild, comic, half-naked **dance** on an overturned tub until the assembled gods **roar with
laughter**. Amaterasu, puzzled that they should rejoice in her absence, **peers out** — and Uzume tells her a
deity still more illustrious has appeared, showing her **her own dazzling reflection in the mirror**. As
Amaterasu edges out in wonder, a strong god seizes her hand and draws her forth, a rope is stretched across the
cave-mouth, and **light returns to the world**. (The mirror becomes one of the imperial regalia; the episode is
read as a sun-return/dawn myth and re-enacted in *kagura*.)

### The imperial descent

Amaterasu sends her grandson **[[ninigi|Ninigi]]** down to rule the earth (the "Central Land of Reed-Plains"),
giving him the **Three Sacred Treasures** — the **mirror, the sword [[susanoo|Kusanagi]], and the jewel** — and
commanding that her descendants reign forever. Ninigi's great-grandson **[[jimmu|Jimmu]]** becomes the **first
emperor**, so that every Japanese emperor is held to descend in unbroken line from the sun-goddess — the
doctrine enshrined at **Ise**, her grand shrine, and made state ideology by **[[state-shinto|State Shinto]]**.

## Significance

Amaterasu fuses **cosmic sun-deity**, **supreme kami**, and **imperial ancestress**. Unusually, the chief
solar deity and head of the pantheon is **female**, and her authority is **bright, ordering, and life-giving**
against her brother Susanoo's storm-chaos. She is the religious heart of Ise and of the throne's legitimacy.
