# Vayu

**Vayu** (also **Vata**) is the **wind-god** of [[vedic-religion|Vedic religion]] — swift, invisible, and
powerful, the **breath of the cosmos**. Closely allied with [[indra|Indra]] (with whom he often rides), Vayu
is honored as the **first to receive the soma** at the sacrifice, for the wind is everywhere and swift to
come. As the moving air he is linked to **breath and life** (*prana*) — the wind in the world and the breath
in the body being the same vital force — an association that becomes important in later [[upanishads|
Upanishadic]] and [[hinduism|Hindu]] thought (and in [[yoga-sutras|yogic]] breath-discipline). The unseen
power that animates all things.
