# The Hero Twins (Hunahpu and Xbalanque)

**Hunahpu and Xbalanque**, the **Hero Twins**, are the central heroes of the [[popol-vuh|Popol Vuh]] and of
[[maya-religion|Maya]] religion — clever **trickster-heroes** and master ballplayers who **descend into the
underworld, defeat the Lords of Death, and rise to become the Sun and the Moon**.

## Their Story

Conceived by the maiden Xquic from the spittle of their murdered father's skull (their father and uncle had
been killed by the death-lords of [[xibalba|Xibalba]] over the **ballgame**), the Twins grew into prodigious
heroes. Summoned to Xibalba in turn, they **survived its deadly test-houses** (Gloom, Cold, Jaguars, Fire, and
Bats — a bat beheaded Hunahpu, but Xbalanque restored his head with a carved squash) and, by a brilliant
trick, **outwitted the Lords of Death**: disguised as wandering performers, they amazed the lords by
**sacrificing and then resurrecting each other**; when the eager lords demanded to be sacrificed and revived
too, the Twins **killed them and refused to bring them back** — breaking the power of death over the world.
Their victory accomplished, the Twins **ascended into the sky as the Sun and the Moon**. (See the
[[popol-vuh|Popol Vuh]] for the full narrative.)

## Significance

The Twins embody the Mesoamerican themes of the **ballgame as cosmic struggle**, the **conquest of death**,
and **transformation into the heavenly bodies** — a dying-and-rising, death-defeating heroism comparable to
underworld-descent myths worldwide (recorded without asserting dependence).
