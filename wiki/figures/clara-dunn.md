# Clara Dunn

*Populated from [[peter-smith|Smith, Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith (2000)]]. Claims grounded in lines 11194–11203.*

## Biographical Overview

Australian [[hands-of-the-cause|Hand of the Cause]] of British background. She became a Bahá'í in Washington around 1907, and accompanied her husband [[hyde-dunn|J.H. Hyde Dunn]] to Australia in 1920. She was appointed a Hand of the Cause by [[shoghi-effendi|Shoghi Effendi]] in February 1952 (lines 11194–11203).
