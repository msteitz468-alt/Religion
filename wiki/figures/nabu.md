# Nabu

**Nabu** (biblical **Nebo**) is the [[babylonian-religion|Babylonian]] god of **writing, scribes, wisdom, and
destiny** — son of **[[marduk|Marduk]]**, patron of the scribal arts on which Mesopotamian civilization and
divination depended. As keeper of the **Tablet of Destinies** (in which fates are inscribed), he had power
over the future, and his cult (centered at Borsippa) grew enormously in the Neo-Babylonian period, when his
name was given to kings (Nebuchadnezzar, Nabonidus). Holding the reed stylus, he is the divine **scribe** who
records and decrees — the Babylonian counterpart of the Egyptian [[thoth|Thoth]]. His name survives in the
Hebrew Bible (Isaiah 46:1, "Bel bows down, Nebo stoops") and in the place-name where Moses died, Mount Nebo.
