# Pwyll

**Pwyll, Prince of Dyfed**, is the hero of the **First Branch** of the **[[mabinogion|Mabinogion]]** and the
husband of **[[rhiannon|Rhiannon]]**. While hunting he offends **[[arawn|Arawn]]**, king of
**[[annwn|Annwn]]** (the [[the-otherworld|Otherworld]]), by letting his hounds drive Arawn's from a stag; in
recompense he agrees to an extraordinary exchange — **the two trade shapes and kingdoms for a year**, Pwyll
ruling Annwn in Arawn's form and, at the year's end, **slaying Arawn's rival Hafgan** with the single permitted
blow. Through the whole year Pwyll **sleeps chastely each night beside Arawn's wife**, never touching her — an
act of honor that wins Arawn's lasting friendship and the title **Pwyll Pen Annwn, "Head of Annwn."** Back in
Dyfed he wins **[[rhiannon|Rhiannon]]**, the uncatchable otherworldly rider, outwitting her rival suitor
**Gwawl** in the trick of "badger-in-the-bag," and fathers **Pryderi**. Pwyll is the Welsh model of the
**mortal who proves worthy of the Otherworld** through restraint and good faith.
