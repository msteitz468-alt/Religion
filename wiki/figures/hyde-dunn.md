# J.H. Hyde Dunn

*Populated from [[peter-smith|Smith, Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith (2000)]]. Claims grounded in lines 11204–11231.*

## Biographical Overview

English-American Bahá'í who, with his wife [[clara-dunn|Clara]], was responsible for the introduction of the Faith to Australia (lines 11206–11208). He became a Bahá'í around 1905 in Seattle (lines 11209, 11222).

Already in his sixties, Hyde became a travelling salesman, using his mobility as a means of presenting the Faith throughout Australia (lines 11213–11216). He married Clara in 1917, and the couple moved to Australia in 1920 in response to [[abdu-l-baha|'Abdu'l-Bahá]]'s call in the [[tablets-of-the-divine-plan|Tablets of the Divine Plan]] (lines 11223–11232).

## Honours

[[shoghi-effendi|Shoghi Effendi]] named him as Australia's 'spiritual conqueror', and in 1952 posthumously designated him a [[hands-of-the-cause|Hand of the Cause]] (lines 11223–11228).
