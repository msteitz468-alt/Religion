# Saadh Sangat (Company of the Holy)

**Saadh Sangat** (also *Sat Sangat*, *Saadh Sangat*; literally "the congregation of the holy / the saints") is the [[sikhism|Sikh]] concept of the holy community as a transformative vehicle for liberation. In the [[guru-granth-sahib|Guru Granth Sahib]], the Saadh Sangat ranks alongside [[nam-simaran|Naam Simaran]] as the primary means through which [[nadar|Nadar]] (divine grace) reaches the seeker. No fewer than a third of the compositions in Part 1 reference it as indispensable.

## Role in the Guru Granth Sahib

**Sodar section (Angs 8–12)**: Mehl 4 in Goojaree Raag makes the Sat Sangat the place where the Lord's Essence is obtained: "Blessed, blessed is the Sat Sangat, the True Congregation, where the Lord's Essence is obtained." (L2192, Ang 10).

**Siree Raag (Angs 14–93)**: Mehl 5 elevates Saadh Sangat above the 68 traditional pilgrimage sites: "Let the Saadh Sangat, the Company of the Holy, be your cleansing baths at the sixty-eight sacred shrines of pilgrimage." (L10573, Ang 48).

**Raag Maajh (Angs 94–150)**: Mehl 4 frames every hymn around the longing for the Sangat: "In that Congregation, the Lord God abides." (L20959, Ang 95). "Come, O Saints — let's meet together and chant the Naam." (L38914, Ang 173).

**Sukhmani Sahib (Angs 262–296)**: Ashtapadī 7 is entirely devoted to Saadh Sangat as the source of every blessing — illuminated face, removed ego, revealed wisdom, obtained Naam jewel. "There is no difference between the Holy people and God." The alchemy metaphor appears in the Var with Shaloks by Mehl 4 (Ang 304): "Just as iron is transmuted into gold by the touch of the Philosopher's Stone, so are people transformed by joining the Sangat, the Holy Congregation."

## Relation to Other Concepts

- **Saadh Sangat and [[naam|Naam]]**: The Sangat is the setting in which the Naam is most readily encountered; the two are not identical but mutually constitutive.
- **Saadh Sangat and [[nadar|Nadar]]**: Grace flows through the congregation — joining the Saadh Sangat is both a result of divine grace and the vehicle through which further grace is extended.
- **Saadh Sangat and [[langar|Langar]]**: The Langar (free kitchen) is the concrete, institutional expression of Saadh Sangat — the community in which caste distinctions are dissolved.
- **Saadh Sangat and [[gurdwara|Gurdwara]]**: The Gurdwara is the spatial home of the Sangat.
