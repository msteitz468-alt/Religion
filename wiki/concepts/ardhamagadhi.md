# Ardhamagadhi

Ardhamagadhi ("half-Magadhi") is the Middle Indo-Aryan **Prakrit** in which the [[svetambara|Svetambara]]
[[jain-agamas|Agama]] is composed — the canonical scriptural language of that community.

## Significance (Dundas)

[[dundas-the-jains|Dundas]] (ch. 3) notes that Ardhamagadhi is traditionally held to be the very language
[[mahavira|Mahavira]] preached in — indeed the language into which his speech was understood to be
miraculously transformed so that all beings could comprehend it. Its use marks the Svetambara canon as
distinct from later Jain works in Sanskrit (e.g. Umasvati's [[tattvartha-sutra|Tattvartha Sutra]]) and the
Sauraseni Prakrit of [[kundakunda|Kundakunda]]'s [[digambara|Digambara]] writings. The choice of a Prakrit
("the language of the people") over Brahminical Sanskrit was itself an [[sramana|sramana]] statement.
