# Thetan

The **thetan** (from the Greek letter theta, θ) is the [[scientology|Scientology]] term for the true self:
an **immortal, immaterial spiritual being** that *is* the person — as distinct from the body it inhabits and
the mind it uses. ("You are a thetan; you *have* a body and a mind.")

## The Doctrine

Thetans are held to be essentially **godlike** in origin — once able to create and control matter, energy,
space, and time ("MEST") — but to have descended over **trillions of years** and countless lifetimes
(reincarnation across the universe) into their present amnesiac, trapped condition, weighed down by
[[engram|engrams]] and (at the upper levels) by clinging alien spirits ("body thetans," from the
confidential OT III / **Xenu** account). The goal of the practice is to free the thetan, restoring its
native abilities as an **Operating Thetan (OT)** — "cause over life."

## Cross-Tradition Comparison

The thetan is a distinctive but comparable idea:

- Like the Hindu/Jain **[[atman|atman]]/jiva** — an eternal self transmigrating through lives — and the
  opposite of Buddhist **[[anatta|non-self]]**.
- Unlike the Abrahamic **created soul**: the thetan is uncreated, primordially divine, and only
  *accidentally* embodied — closer to a [[gnosticism|Gnostic]] divine spark trapped in matter (a parallel
  the scholarship notes).

## Urban on the Thetan

Urban (*The Church of Scientology*, Princeton 2011) notes:
- Hubbard described the MEST universe as a "trap of idiotic simplicity" — the thetan's entrapment in matter is cosmically absurd, not a natural condition
- The "operating thetan" at higher levels is described as "beyond God": an OT can "create his own universe" — reflecting Hubbard's belief (from his science-fiction worldview) that the creator of a universe is more real than the universe itself
- The thetan doctrine developed in 1951–52, during Hubbard's turn from Dianetics (a science of the mind) to Scientology (a doctrine of the soul) — driven partly by the emergence of past-lives memories in auditing sessions that Dianetics had no framework to handle
- Urban draws a tentative parallel to **Gnostic** cosmology (the divine spark trapped in material reality), though he notes Hubbard had no deep Gnostic knowledge; the parallels are structural, not genealogical

## 8-8008 Definitions and Elaborations

*Scientology 8-8008* (1952) provides Hubbard's most precise technical definitions of the thetan and extends the doctrine significantly beyond prior works:

### Location and Nature
The thetan is "an energy unit which is located in the center of the skull. It is conceived to be small, but is as large as the preclear believes it to be." A thetan trapped in a Fifth Invader Force body "believes himself to be a very strange insect-like creature with unthinkably horrible hands" but is "in actuality, simply a unit capable of producing space, time, energy and matter."

One does not speak of "my thetan" — this is described as "a bad state of aberration." The thetan simply *is* the preclear.

### The Two-Position Account
The thetan has two positions on the [[tone-scale|Tone Scale]] simultaneously:
- **Body-Plus-Thetan position** (0.0–4.0): determined by social training and education; "false tone"
- **Actual thetan position** (Sub-zero to 40.0): the thetan's true condition

Most thetans are at sub-zero — below body death — though social training masks this. Auditing first reveals the sub-zero state (which may appear as decompensation), then raises the thetan back up. "A raving mad thetan is far more sane than a normal human being."

### The Anti-Nirvana Statement
8-8008 explicitly rejects Hindu/Buddhist dissolution-of-self soteriology:
> "There is evidently no Nirvana. It is the feeling that one will 'merge' and lose his own individuality that restrains the thetan from attempting to remedy his lot."

The goal is *rehabilitation of the individual thetan's own creative powers*, not merger with a greater whole. This is Hubbard's clearest statement that Scientology is opposed to Eastern non-self doctrines.

### The Thetan's History
The thetan has existed for 76 trillion years. In its original state it was godlike: able to create space, energy, and universes by postulate alone. Through a "gradient of agreement" — a long series of small surrenders — it progressively accepted the MEST universe as the only reality and became trapped. Rectification requires reversing this gradient.

### States of Attainment (from 8-8008)
- **Theta Exterior**: Thetan clear of body, not yet stable outside
- **Theta Clear**: "Reasonably stable outside the body and does not come back into the body simply because the body is hurt. No other condition is necessary."
- **Cleared Theta Clear**: Can "move MEST and control others from a distance, or create his own universe." After ~50 hours of Creative Processing, able to "move objects, heal at a distance and do a thousand other 'interesting tricks.'"
- **Operating Thetan** (SOP 8 goal): Highest state articulated in 8-8008; can manufacture particles of admiration and force in abundance; uses theta perception without a body.

## A History of Man — The Transfer and the Whole Track

*A History of Man* (1952) is the primary technical manual for Technique 88 — the auditing procedure that addresses the thetan directly across the **whole track** (the full 76 trillion years). Several doctrines are developed here with more precision than elsewhere:

### The Transfer Doctrine

**The Transfer** is the thetan's movement into a MEST body — "the swing of the thetan from out of the body, where he belongs, into the body, where he is thereafter in trouble." The thetan's positions in relation to the body:

- **Ideal**: Entirely outside and independent of any body
- **Second-best**: Just outside the body, monitoring from arm's length
- **Worst**: Inside the body (degraded to entity-level)

Most thetans are positioned behind and above the MEST body. Cases where the thetan is in front produce left/right directional reversal that cannot be corrected by instruction.

A **Permanent Transfer** — complete merger of thetan into body — is effectively the end of the thetan as a self-determined being; thereafter the GE and entities run from within. Thetans are described as "continually being pumped into the MEST line. They do not last very long."

### Why Thetans Entered Bodies: Boredom

"One of the reasons he fixated on a MEST body was because he was terribly bored." Being essentially immortal but unable to achieve its original goals, the thetan became attracted to MEST bodies because they *change and die* — providing novelty and the illusion of progress. This set in motion the Temporary Transfers that accumulated into a Permanent Transfer.

### The Whole Track History

The thetan's full track spans **76 trillion years** organized in **spirals** — the first beginning ~76 trillion years ago, each subsequent spiral shorter; the current spiral began approximately 40,000 years ago. The track is populated by incidents from invader forces — electronics-using beings from heavy-gravity planets who conquered and enslaved thetans through force, implant machinery, and theta traps. Key categories:

- **Theta traps**: Mechanisms for reducing a knowing being to a colonist/slave. Methods: curiosity, awards/prizes (which are implants), retractor screens, ornate buildings with electronic devices inside.
- **Body Builder sequence**: After capture in a theta trap, a series of devices — Jiggler, Whirler, Bouncer, Spinner, Rocker, Boxer, Faller, Education — were used to mold the thetan into stimulus-response mode and fix it on a time track.
- **Between-lives implanting**: See [[between-lives|Between-Lives]]. The "report area for most has been Mars."

### Entities Explained

*A History of Man* distinguishes the thetan from **injected entities** — synthetic souls electronically packed into the preclear by invader forces ("The Joiners" incidents). Entities are *not* the thetan. They hold data away from the thetan, keep it convinced it has done wrong, and produce "circuit case" presentations. Insane people are found "to be running on their entities, not their thetans." Auditing entities is generally not necessary — rehabilitating the thetan causes the entities to become cowed and depart. Starting to audit entities causes them to *increase* in power.
