# Thetan

The **thetan** (from the Greek letter theta, θ) is the [[scientology|Scientology]] term for the true self:
an **immortal, immaterial spiritual being** that *is* the person — as distinct from the body it inhabits and
the mind it uses. ("You are a thetan; you *have* a body and a mind.")

## The Doctrine

Thetans are held to be essentially **godlike** in origin — once able to create and control matter, energy,
space, and time ("MEST") — but to have descended over **trillions of years** and countless lifetimes
(reincarnation across the universe) into their present amnesiac, trapped condition, weighed down by
[[engram|engrams]] and (at the upper levels) by clinging alien spirits ("body thetans," from the
confidential OT III / **Xenu** account). The goal of the practice is to free the thetan, restoring its
native abilities as an **Operating Thetan (OT)** — "cause over life."

## Cross-Tradition Comparison

The thetan is a distinctive but comparable idea:

- Like the Hindu/Jain **[[atman|atman]]/jiva** — an eternal self transmigrating through lives — and the
  opposite of Buddhist **[[anatta|non-self]]**.
- Unlike the Abrahamic **created soul**: the thetan is uncreated, primordially divine, and only
  *accidentally* embodied — closer to a [[gnosticism|Gnostic]] divine spark trapped in matter (a parallel
  the scholarship notes).
