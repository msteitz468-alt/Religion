# Goals Problems Mass (GPMs)

In [[scientology|Scientology]] cosmology, **Goals Problems Masses** (GPMs) are the deep structure of the reactive mind, operating at a level below individual [[engram|engrams]]. They constitute the subject matter of **OT 2** (the Clearing Course) on the [[operating-thetan|Bridge to Total Freedom]].

## Definition and Structure

A GPM is an interlocking set of **opposed goals** that create irresolvable internal conflict: a being holds a primary goal (e.g., "to help") which generates an automatic opposition goal ("not to help"), and further sub-goals in dialectical sequence — together producing a "mass" (an area of mental/spiritual weight or "charge") that suppresses the being's causativeness.

Hubbard's key claim is that these goals-patterns are not organically produced but were **artificially implanted** at an enormous scale. The Clearing Course materials (reproduced in full in [[scientology-ot-levels|Technical Bulletins Vol. XIV]], OT 2 section) present the specific **GPM sequences** that Hubbard claimed to have "discovered" as universal implants common to all beings in this sector of the galaxy.

## Cosmological Origin

GPMs originate in the **R6 implant** — a massive galactic implantation event approximately 75 million years ago, associated with Incident II (see [[operating-thetan|Operating Thetan]]) (the Xenu/volcano event). "All CC and OTII materials are in R6 75 million years ago." (HCOB 2 Oct 1968). This is the same event that produced the [[body-thetans|body thetans]] handled in OT 3, situating GPMs and BTs as two aspects of the same primordial catastrophe.

The specific GPMs in the Clearing Course materials are presented as **universal** — the same patterns shared by all thetans who passed through this region of the galaxy, not the products of individual lives. This distinguishes OT 2/CC materials from lower-Bridge engram work, which addresses individual life experiences.

## Auditing Procedure

GPMs are audited in the **Clearing Course** (CC) and **OT 2** using the E-meter. The practitioner reads through the published GPM sequences and "runs" each pattern — identifying charged areas, running them to a floating needle (F/N), and thereby erasing the mass. The specific GPM lists in this volume run to hundreds of pages, covering sequences named: **Electrical GPM**, **Tocky GPM**, **Big Being GPM**, **House GPM**, **Psycho GPM**, **Banky GPM**, **Forerunner GPM**, **Basic Basic GPM**, **Basic GPM**, **LP GPM**, **Body GPM**, and others.

## Theological Significance

The GPM doctrine represents Scientology's claim that **spiritual bondage has a specific, reproducible cosmic structure** — not merely the result of individual karma or sin but of a deliberate galactic conspiracy to imprison thetans in confusion. The practitioner who clears these patterns achieves the state of **Clear** (free of the reactive mind's domination) and is thereafter able to address the body thetan layer of the case (OT 3 and above).

The GPM materials are among the most technically detailed in the OT corpus — hundreds of pages of specific "item" sequences, each to be audited in exact order. This reflects Scientology's deeply technical character: salvation is not achieved through faith or ritual but through a precise, reproducible **engineering of consciousness**.
