# The Soul and the Afterlife (Kabbalah)

The [[zohar|Zohar]]'s account of the human soul and its fate after death — developed most fully in **Parashat Va-Yeḥi** (the death of [[jacob|Jacob]]) — is one of its most distinctive contributions, and the wiki's first sustained treatment of Jewish mystical **eschatology and anthropology**. It comprises three linked doctrines: the **tripartite soul** (nefesh / ruaḥ / neshamah), the **soul's journey after death** (through the cave of Machpelah into the Garden of Eden), and the mystery that **"the righteous do not die"** but are "gathered" to the [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]].

---

## The Tripartite Soul: Nefesh, Ruaḥ, Neshamah

The Zohar teaches that the soul "comprises three aspects and therefore has three names, corresponding to supernal mystery":

| Name | Rung | Sefirotic root | Acquired |
|------|------|----------------|----------|
| **Nefesh** (נֶפֶשׁ) | lowest | [[shekhinah|Malkhut/Shekhinah]] | from birth; the vital/animal soul, "by which a person is refined" |
| **Ruaḥ** (רוּחַ) | middle | Tif'eret | awakened through moral-religious effort; "hovers over nefesh" |
| **Neshamah** (נְשָׁמָה) | highest | Binah | attained by the devoted; the "holy rung transcending all" |

> "At first one possesses **nefesh**… When one begins to purify himself, he is ready to be crowned with **ruaḥ**… Once elevated by nefesh and ruaḥ… then **neshamah** alights upon him — supernal, holy rung prevailing over all… Then he is consummate, perfected on all sides, worthy of the world that is coming." (Zohar 1:206a)

The three are not given equally to all: nefesh is universal, but ruaḥ and especially neshamah must be **cultivated through devotion and Torah**. Only the one who attains the neshamah is fully "beloved of the blessed Holy One" and ready for the world to come. (The two higher levels, *ḥayyah* and *yeḥidah*, are developed more fully in later Kabbalah; the Zohar's core scheme is the threefold one, already noted on the [[kabbalah|Kabbalah]] page.)

---

## The Journey of the Soul After Death

The Zohar narrates a graded post-mortem ascent, keyed to the **cave of Machpelah** (the patriarchs' burial site, Gen 23) as the gateway between worlds:

- For the first **seven days** the soul lingers, grieving, moving between the grave and the home; "the body becomes what it becomes, and the soul enters her place."
- The worthy soul then "**enters the cave of Machpelah**… until reaching the Garden of Eden, encountering the cherubim and the flame of the sword in the **lower Garden of Eden**. If she is worthy of entering, she enters." (Zohar 1:218b)
- The **lower (earthly) Garden of Eden** is distinguished from the **higher Garden of Eden**, which the Zohar identifies with the [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]] herself. The soul ascends by rungs, "infused by the spices of the Garden," clothed in a luminous garment.
- **Dew of resurrection**: three times a day a spirit "enters the cave of Machpelah and wafts upon the graves of the patriarchs; bones heal and stand vitally erect. That spirit draws **dew from above — from the head of the King**," and the patriarchs "awaken… and plead for mercy for their children." This dew is the agent of the future bodily **resurrection** (*teḥiyyat ha-metim*).

The cave of Machpelah functions, in this scheme, as the cosmic hinge: rabbinic tradition held that Adam and Eve as well as the patriarchs were buried there, so the Zohar makes it the **portal from the grave to Eden** and the conduit of resurrection dew.

## "The Righteous Do Not Die": Gathering to the Shekhinah

Of [[jacob|Jacob]], Scripture says not "he died" but "**he expired and was gathered to his people**" (Gen 49:33). The Zohar reads this precisely:

> "**Jacob did not die** — for regarding him, death is not mentioned; rather, *he expired and was gathered to his people*… he was **gathered to the moon**. The **sun** ([[sefirot|Tif'eret]]) **does not die**, but is gathered from the world and goes toward the **moon** ([[shekhinah|Shekhinah]])." (Zohar 1:235b)

For the perfected righteous, "death" is not annihilation but **reunion**: the masculine sun (Tif'eret, embodied in Jacob) is gathered to the feminine moon (the Shekhinah). At that moment "the moon was illumined, attaining fullness," and Jacob — beholding "a complete realm, as happened to no one else" — blesses each of his sons according to his sefirotic root. This is the eschatological counterpart of the *zivvuga qaddisha* (the holy union): the righteous one's death consummates, rather than severs, his bond with the divine. The doctrine connects to the cosmic role of the [[tsaddiq|Tsaddiq]], whose death also shields the generation (Isa 57:1).

## Rav Metivta densification (Pritzker Vol. IX — standards reingest 2026-07-18)

*Source: [[matt-zohar-pritzker-vol9|Matt Pritzker Vol IX]]. Body ~3:161b–174a zone (embedded in Shelaḥ Lekha). Opens mid-sentence in the manuscript/printed tradition (Matt note).*

**Visionary frame:** Rabbi Shim’on and Companions tour the Garden; academies (Love/Aaron → Light/Moses → Heavenly / Supreme); Head of Academy (Rav Metivta); Generation of the Desert as denizens of the World that is Coming, **revived first**.

**Soul anatomy (body terms):** *nafsha* naked until flesh (Other Side / Satan’s base) is gone; *ruḥa* as Holy Spirit; *nishmeta* from Tree of Life; bones endure; garment of light / *Malkhut*; filthy garments first.

**Women’s afterlife palaces:** Bithiah, Serah, Jochebed, Deborah, and the matriarchs — soul–soul joy; path for *ger tsedeq* souls.

**Machpelah / dew / Messiahs:** couples in the cave; two Messiahs materials; resurrection dew themes connect to Va-Yeḥi Machpelah portal above.

## Significance and Cross-Tradition Context

This material gives Kabbalah a **developed eschatology** that classical rabbinic Judaism left comparatively diffuse: a graded soul, a mapped post-mortem geography, a mechanism of resurrection, and a mystical reinterpretation of death itself. Points of comparison:

- **vs. Greek tripartite soul**: the nefesh/ruaḥ/neshamah scheme superficially resembles Plato's tripartite soul (appetite/spirit/reason) and the body–soul–spirit anthropology of Hellenistic Judaism and Paul (1 Thess 5:23), but the Zohar's levels are **rungs of attainment rooted in the sefirot**, not faculties or parts.
- **vs. Christian intermediate state and resurrection**: the soul's ascent through paradisical levels and its wait for bodily resurrection parallels Christian discussions of the intermediate state and the resurrection of the dead, though the sefirotic framing and the "dew" mechanism are distinctively Kabbalistic.
- **vs. the Garden of Eden in [[genesis|Genesis]]**: the Zohar transforms the lost earthly Eden of Genesis 3 into a **present, layered post-mortem realm** — the "lower Garden of Eden" — while reserving the "higher Eden" for the Shekhinah.

## Grounding from Pritzker Zohar Volume I (standards reingest 2026-07-18)

Attributed to the Zohar body as rendered in Matt, *The Zohar: Pritzker Edition*, Volume I — especially **Parashat Noaḥ** (tripartite soul, Hell geography, Kaddish, garments) and related Be-Reshit Fall notes. Earlier wiki material from Va-Yeḥi (Jacob) is retained above; this section anchors Vol. I loci.

### Nefesh / ruaḥ / neshamah (Noaḥ range)

Three sons as "three clusters of spirit roaming, embraced by three worlds." Descent: "The soul emerges, navigating the mountains of separation, and spirit is joined to soul. As it descends, vitality joins spirit…."

> "Vitality and spirit intertwine, while soul abides in one's ways—a hidden abode… If one strives to purify himself… assisted by a holy soul… called 'holy.' If… unvirtuous… two openings—vitality and spirit—but is devoid of the holy soul." (Zohar Noaḥ; Pritzker I)

Those who cling only to the two lower openings, without the "artisan of artisans," are "erased from the Book of Life" (Tosefta insert). Haqdamah commandment 5 already links daily Torah study to adornment with "another holy soul" and becoming "like the holy angels" (1:12b–13a). Supernal three → Shem (right) / Ham (left) / Japheth (purple blending both) (Noaḥ).

### Hell: seven abodes; Sheol vs Avadon; twelve months

Handed to Dumah; burned in blazing fire **except on new moons and Sabbaths** (*From new moon to new moon and from Sabbath to Sabbath*); afterward *Let the wicked return to Sheol!*; four directions of fire in Valley of Ben Hinnom, thrice daily (Noaḥ Tosefta).

> "In Hell there are abodes upon abodes: second, third, until seven… Two abodes adjacent… **Sheol and Avadon**. One who descends to Sheol is judged… raised… level after level… But one who descends to Avadon is never raised… totally *avid*, lost." (Zohar Noaḥ; Pritzker I)

"The punishment of the wicked in Hell lasts for twelve months" — Flood hide typology: Noah sees Angel of Death, enters ark for twelve months = "that is the punishment of the wicked" (Noaḥ). Flood generation: "Fire and snow, water and fire—one freezing, one boiling"; dual Hell in the Flood (water above; boiling-fire water below) (Noaḥ). Haqdamah: Dumah at Hell's door; covenant-keepers beyond his reach (1:8a–b); premature Sabbath lamp kindles Hell-fire (1:14b–15a).

### Kaddish theurgy — temporary relief

> "When Israel responds aloud: 'Amen! May His great name be blessed!' the blessed Holy One is filled with compassion… signals to the angel… **Samriel**… Three keys… opens three gates on the side of the wilderness… they see the light of this world… **relief for an hour-and-a-half**… three times a day and whenever Israel recites [that response]." (Zohar Noaḥ; Pritzker I)

### Garments of radiance; Adam at Eden's gate; childlessness

Departing soul meets "Adam, sitting by the gate of the Garden of Eden," delighting in commandment-keepers and *baʿalei teshuvah* ("inhabitants of *ḥadel*, cessation"). Souls ascending to the "bundle of life… bask in radiance of the resplendent speculum" need "a **garment of supernal radiance**" (as Moses entered the cloud). Companions call it the "'shirt' worn in that world."

> "If the soul did not garb herself in the radiance of another garment, then she could not draw near… anyone who does not generate children… is banished… not allowed to view that radiant light." (Zohar Noaḥ; Pritzker I)

Fall material: first garments were *kotnot or* (אור, light); after sin *kotnot or* (עור, skin) — "soothing the skin, not the soul" (Be-Reshit Fall). Contrast: post-mortem radiant garment vs post-sin skin garment.

### Resurrection disputes (in-range, not adjudicated)

- R. Yose: Flood wicked "will not rise from the dead to stand at Judgment… *They were obliterated*" (Noaḥ).
- R. Shimʿon: "They certainly have no share in the world to come… **However, they will rise for Judgment**… *some to everlasting life, some to shame and everlasting contempt* (Daniel 12:2)" (Noaḥ).
- Seed-wasters / obliterated: tradition of no soul-return in the Land — "although the ancients disputed this" (Noaḥ).

Primordial light "preserved for the righteous… *Light is sown for the righteous* (Ps 97:11)" until the world-to-come (1:32a). Eschaton: death swallowed; moon like sun (1:34a; Noaḥ).

## Grounding from Pritzker Zohar Volume II (fidelity reingest 2026-07-18)

Attributed to the Zohar body as rendered in Matt, *The Zohar: Pritzker Edition*, Volume II. Densifies Dumah, Machpelah↔Garden, soul courts, resurrection dew, and midnight Garden with folio loci from Lekh Lekha through Ḥayyei Sarah (and related Toledot notes).

### Midnight Garden of Eden (Lekh Lekha; Toledot)

At midnight the blessed Holy One enters the Garden of Eden with the righteous; trees sing (Zohar 1:77a–b; Pritzker II). Souls ascend at midnight, clothe in supernal light; God delights with dead and living virtuous (1:82b–83a zone). Midnight stream waters Garden plants and the head of the Torah-student (1:92a–b). Toledot: those who rise for Torah “stand by night in the house of YHVH” while God delights with the righteous in Eden (1:136b). Ḥayyei Sarah links Jacob’s evening prayer to midnight delight with the righteous in the Garden (1:132b–133a).

### Dumah; covenant protection

Sleepers who neglect Torah: body defiled; soul in noontime sun; sling stones; “delivered into the hands of **Dumah**… *As a cloud fades and vanishes, so whoever goes down to Sheol does not come up*” (Job 7:9) (Zohar 1:77b; Pritzker II). Death day: three messengers enumerate days/sins; seal by hand (Job 37:7) (1:79a). Bearer of the covenant sign is “not to be delivered into the hands of Dumah”; demons scatter at the sign of holiness (1:94b–95a). Side of impurity at death → Dumah → Hell; twelve months then appropriate site; worthy ascend (Ḥayyei Sarah 1:130a–b zone).

### Nefesh / ruaḥ / neshamah and black–white–concealed light

Lower black light (wick) → white-light throne → concealed light = grades of soul and of divine light; Abraham receives nefesh, then ruaḥ, then neshamah via altars and journeys (Zohar 1:83a–84a; Pritzker II). Pure night-soul → Countenance of Days / share in world that is coming; impure clings to hooded demons and gets true or false knowledge (same range; 1:130a–b sleep-souls).

### Machpelah ↔ Garden of Eden (Ḥayyei Sarah)

Abraham recognizes the cave: image of Adam + **opening to the Garden of Eden**; light and lamp; only the destined owner sees light while Ephron sees darkness (Zohar 1:127a–128b; Pritzker II). Adam buried by God, hidden until Abraham; “because of you—there is enduring ascension for me and the world.” Field and cave “arose—rising, literally”; Adam and Eve re-established through Abraham (*be-hibbare’am* / *be-Avraham*). **Name Machpelah**: not only double cave but “double” — land of Israel enfolded in Jerusalem above/below; **double *he* of the holy Name** (1:128b–129a). Ordinary person dies on seeing Adam at death; Abraham gazes and endures.

### Soul courts; graded abodes

Many places prepared after death: “courts” = outer rungs / access among standing ones / messengers like angels if sanctified; defiled → impure abode → demons of the world → Hell (Zohar 1:129b–130a; Pritzker II). Court vs house (“goodness of Your house”); Enclosure seating for Davidic kings; further within for supernal devotees “encamping… to the east”; divergent lights, each ashamed by companion’s light. Inmost for those who know the mystery of the Lord daily (Isa 64:3). Soul’s path “as one draws down upon himself in this world”: holiness → attendant among angels (Zech 3:7); defilement → Hell with wounders (Va-Yera 1:99a–101a). Hell: water and fire; twelve-month purge (Flood and Sodom as dual Hell typology) (1:107a–108a).

### Resurrection dew

Same body and soul restored; **dew from God’s head** = “dew of lights” from supernal lights; Tree of Life pours incessant life; now life fails because the evil serpent prevails and the moon is obscured; future: serpent eliminated, moon’s light like the sun’s (Isa 30:26) (Zohar 1:130b–131a; Pritzker II). Multiple bodies for one soul: non-virtuous prior bodies as never existed; final flourishing body arises; or other spirits poured — unworthy ashen under feet of the righteous (Dan 12:2). Dead of the land of Israel first (“will live”); outside “arise,” roll underground to Israel for soul. Toledot: *invigorate bones* = resurrection — perfect body + intensified soul light in the resplendent speculum (1:141a–b); supernal dew from Ancient of Days via heaven to Holy Apple Orchard (1:143b).

### Nightly soul court; death agent

Every night the soul leaves and is judged; if deserving, restored; not judged for future wrongs but can be saved by future good deeds (Ḥayyei Sarah openers, Gen 21:17 midrash). When days no longer abide on supernal rungs, the death-agent obtains authorization, plucks the soul, defiles the body; the righteous remain undefiled; bands of demons from the primordial serpent requite deeds (1:125a–b zone). Torah-engaged are not overpowered/defiled by the evil serpent though they still die; they cleave to Shekhinah — “Benayahu… son of a living man” (1:131b–132a).

- [[matt-zohar-pritzker-vol2|Pritzker Zohar Volume II]] (Matt) — Lekh Lekha 1:77a–84a, 1:92a–95b; Va-Yera 1:99a–108a; Ḥayyei Sarah 1:121a–134a; Toledot 1:136b, 1:141a–143b

## Grounding from Pritzker Zohar Volume III (fidelity reingest 2026-07-18)

Attributed to the Zohar body as rendered in Matt, *The Zohar: Pritzker Edition*, Volume III — especially **Parashat Va-Yeḥi** (the death of Jacob), with supporting units in Va-Yishlaḥ–Va-Yiggash. This is the volume’s densest afterlife block.

### "Jacob did not die": sun gathered to the moon

Va-Yeḥi (two statements, same doctrine):

> After *Yours O YHVH are gedullah…* (1 Chr 29:11), sun gathered to moon, east to west (*He gathered his feet into the bed*); moon illumined to fullness; "Then surely, we have learned, **Jacob did not die**." (Zohar 1:235b; Pritzker III)

Full midrash reprise: death is not mentioned; *gathered his feet into the bed* = gathered to the moon; "the **sun does not die** but is gathered from the world toward the moon"; when Jacob was gathered, the moon shone and desire of the supernal sun was aroused (1:248b). Fruit of Jacob every generation: gathered to the moon generating fruit within her for the world (1:249a). Perfect deathbed: Abraham right, Isaac left, Jacob center, Shekhinah front; sons arrayed; many supernal chariots (1:235b).

### *Tselem*; thirty days; fourfold death theater; black rooster

Before death: herald in 250 worlds; two birds / snare; 30-day proclamation (Garden if virtuous); soul departs nightly and views its place (1:217b–218a). *Tselem* darkens; image on the ground withheld; "Only with a *tselem* does a human walk about" (Ps 39:7) (1:218a). Four aspects / four clusters at death; 270 worlds; flame from north → **River of Fire** → **black rooster** thrice (Judgment side; midnight north wind) (1:218b–219a). Supernal spirit increases at death → sees what never attained; "No human can see Me and live" — in death they can (1:218b). Relatives greet or cry "Woe"; same-rung companions accompany the soul. Unvirtuous stays till burial then wardens → **Dumah** → Hell (1:218b–219a).

### Seven days; Machpelah densified; Zion ladder; dew

Seven days: soul house ↔ grave mourning (Job 14:22) (1:219a). After seven days: **cave of Machpelah** → lower Garden of Eden / cherubim / flaming sword (1:219a). Four pillars hold body-image; three-colored pillar "foundation of Mount Zion" (Isa 4:5) → opening of Righteousness / Zion and Jerusalem; further: cleave to body of King; further still: delight *above YHVH* (Isa 58:14) = upper ḥesed vs lower *ḥasdei david* (1:219a). Thrice daily a spirit enters Machpelah and wafts the graves; **dew from the head of the King** / supernal patriarchs awakens the patriarchs below; they plead for their children (1:224b–225b). Machpelah "Double" = two and one; Leah with Jacob (six tribes); Rachel *iqqara* essence but not in the cave — revealed vs concealed (1:222b–223a). When Jacob entered the cave: aromas of Garden of Eden; lamp returned; cave complete; never receives another human; souls of the virtuous pass before the patriarchs (1:250b–251a). City of Four: Adam+Eve, Abraham+Sarah, Isaac+Rebekah, Jacob+Leah (1:248b–249a).

### Twelve months; three garments; days as glory

After **twelve months**: body dust; nefesh bound to ruaḥ in a garment; ruaḥ in the Garden; neshamah in the bundle of delight; bond at times (1:226b). Three soul garments (El’azar): days of virtue woven into garments of glory; sin diminishes garment; two days Hell per spoiled day; resurrection: "They will stand like a garment" (Job 38:14) (1:224a–b). Mitsvot fashion garments above, below, and this world — woven from actual days (1:226b). Hell twelve months: half heat half snow; Israel’s household not afraid — clothed in *shenayim* (paired) precepts (1:238a zone). Dumah: Ps 121:7 — this world from accusers; that world soul adornment vs Dumah + deputies; seven abodes; Sabbath open then *wicked return to Sheol*; righteous not delivered to Dumah (1:238a).

### Matronita escorts; double *aron*; cistern → well

King sends son (soul) to village (world); when mature, sends **Matronita to escort** to the palace; righteous should rejoice at death day; God delights only in souls of the righteous (1:245a–b). Perfected soul makes the **cistern a bubbling well**; female desire; union by soul of the righteous (1:235b). **Double *aron*** (Gen 50:26 *va-yiysem ba-aron* with two *yod*s): coffin below **and** ark of the covenant above; Joseph preserved covenant below and Covenant above → two caskets; soul joined Shekhinah though departed in a foreign domain (1:251a). Embalming of Jacob/Joseph: royal oil preservation (not ordinary); body of the patriarchs perdured body and soul (1:250b–251a). Soul linked at departure in Egypt because of the perfect bed — not an alien domain (1:250b).

### Supporting units (Va-Yishlaḥ–Va-Yiggash)

Torah for its own sake protects in the grave and speaks at resurrection; first to everlasting life (Dan 12:2) (Va-Yishlaḥ 1:175b–176b; Va-Yeshev). Worm/silkworm/glass: Israel dies and is remade; rise from dust (1:177b–179a). Hand under thigh / Righteous One appointed to revive dwellers of the dust (1:181b). Dead of the Land of Israel first at resurrection (1:181b). Righteous soul passes the river of fire fearlessly; charity enables passage (Mi-Qets 1:200b–201b zone).

## Midrash ha-Ne’lam stratum (Pritzker Vol. X, Wolski)

**Standards reingest 2026-07-18** — [[wolski-zohar-pritzker-vol10|Wolski Pritzker Vol X]]. Earliest Zoharic voice on soul and afterlife; often more philosophical-allegorical and midrashic than the sefirotic Va-Yeḥi scheme above. Body samples:

- **Storehouses / intellectual soul (Be-Reshit)**: Body of Souls (form before embodiment) vs Treasuries of Eternal Life (fulfilled Torah); *nefesh ha-sikhlit* acquired by contemplation; *neshamah* as luminous spark vs creaturely soul “like breath.”
- **Seven openings of ascent (Noaḥ)**: Machpelah → Eden → Zevul … → Aravot/*menuḥah* (= Noah in one reading); Shem/Ham/Yephet = soul / evil impulse / good impulse.
- **Patriarchal soul allegory (Lekh Lekha–Ḥayyei Sarah)**: Abram=soul, Sarai=body, Lot=evil impulse; Michael as Melchizedek vs Hell’s prince; **Bookkeeper** complex — Dumah/Ephron registers of dust-dwellers; Michael welcomes at Jerusalem Above; Metatron leavens bone / tests body as Rebekah; Isaac/Rebekah = soul + holy body at resurrection; Laban = residual/refined evil impulse.
- **Sabbath / Hell rest (Be-Reshit)**: Sabbath as paradigm of Olam ha-Ba / seventh millennium; Hell rests on Sabbath.
- **Ki Tetse + Addenda**: reincarnation two or three times then cut off; levirate as name/seed preservation; nightly soul raised as witness of deeds; **Shema by its letters cools Hell**; **dog attribute** / sling to Hell vs Israel spared; **path of life** (three days darkness then light); Metatron raises souls for the sun’s first song-degree.

Complements main-body Vol. I Noaḥ Hell/garments, Vol. III Va-Yeḥi Machpelah geography, Vol. V *gilgul*, Vol. IX *Rav Metivta* academies — without collapsing registers. See [[allegorical-reading|Allegorical Reading]], [[gilgul|Gilgul]], [[nathan-wolski|Nathan Wolski]].

## Midrash ha-Ne’lam on Ruth + Matnitin/Tosefta densification (Pritzker Vol. XI, Hecker)

**Standards reingest 2026-07-18** — [[hecker-zohar-pritzker-vol11|Hecker Pritzker Vol XI]]. Megillot MhN and oracular compositions extend Vol. X’s soul/afterlife apparatus into Ruth’s conversion narrative and Hell liturgy.

- **Fourfold soul map on YHVH letters (MhN Ruth):** *nefesh / ruaḥ / neshamah / nishmeta le-nishmeta* mapped to letters of the Name; left-side animal soul from serpent; Elimelech–Naomi–Ruth–Orpah as household soul array.
- **Night path / empty return:** worthy leave with Ps 91 protection; unworthy asked “Could this be Naomi?” and answer Mara / empty (Ruth 1:19–21 as death journey).
- **Hell apparatus:** seven Geihinnom compartments under **Dumah**; Temple Mount subterranean fire; weekday respite when Israel recites *Yet He is compassionate*; **Tosefta** densifies **Samri’el** opening three Hell gates when Israel answers Amen May His great name be blessed.
- **Near-death:** R. Kruspedai — Angel of Death denied by Shekhinah; Sandalfon; beit din; waive retribution; substitute death of R. Kruspedai the Elder.
- **Intergenerational rescue:** Upper Galilee butcher’s son’s Shema/Torah frees father from Hell crevice (Naḥum ha-Pekuli strand).
- **Soul as pledge / procreation:** *shekheinot*; soul built as *name* into the child’s body; preexistence of soul before embodiment.
- **R. Peraḥiah** afterlife tour: Garden firmament; Shema windows.
- **Sitrei Torah:** *nefesh–ru’aḥ–neshamah* itinerary; Machpelah; Michael offerings; spirit perfected by successive righteous until Dwelling (Secret of Secrets).

Complements Vol. X Dumah/Bookkeeper and Vol. IX *Rav Metivta* without equating MhN Ruth’s hybrid midrash with main-body sefirotic death theology. See [[book-of-ruth|Ruth]], [[joel-hecker|Joel Hecker]], [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]].
