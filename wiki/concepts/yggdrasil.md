# Yggdrasil and the Nine Worlds

**Yggdrasil** is the immense **world-tree** at the center of the [[norse-religion|Norse]] cosmos — a great
**ash** whose trunk, branches, and three roots bind together the **Nine Worlds** and hold the universe in
order. Its name ("Odin's steed/gallows") recalls [[odin|Odin's]] self-sacrifice hanging upon it.

## The World-Tree

Yggdrasil's three roots reach to three wells, including the **Well of Urð** (fate), where the [[norns|Norns]]
tend the tree and the gods hold their council. The tree teems with creatures: an **eagle** at the top, the
dragon **Níðhöggr** gnawing the roots, a squirrel (**Ratatoskr**) carrying insults between them, and stags
browsing its leaves. Though eternally **gnawed and decaying, it endures** — and shelters the two humans who
survive [[ragnarok|Ragnarök]]. The tree is the **axis** that makes the many worlds one cosmos.

## The Nine Worlds

Norse cosmology arranges existence in **Nine Worlds** on three rough levels, including:

- **Asgard** — the realm of the [[aesir-cult|Æsir]] gods (containing [[valhalla|Valhalla]]); linked to Midgard
  by the rainbow bridge **Bifröst**.
- **Vanaheim** — home of the [[vanir-cult|Vanir]].
- **Midgard** — the world of **humans**, encircled by the serpent [[jormungandr|Jörmungandr]].
- **Jötunheim** — the land of the **giants** (the gods' chaotic enemies).
- **Niflheim / Muspelheim** — primordial **ice** and **fire**, from whose meeting the cosmos arose.
- **Hel** — the realm of the ordinary dead, ruled by the goddess **[[hel|Hel]]**.
- (and Álfheim, Svartálfheim/Niðavellir — elves and dwarves).
