# Degrees of Glory

**Degrees of glory** is the Latter-day Saint doctrine of a graded afterlife revealed in **D&C 76** (16 February 1832 vision to [[joseph-smith|Joseph Smith]] and Sidney Rigdon while translating John 5:29), refined by **D&C 131** (three degrees *within* the celestial kingdom).

## The Three Kingdoms (D&C 76)

| Kingdom | Glory image | Who (per the vision) |
|---------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Celestial** | Sun | Received testimony of Jesus, baptized, washed by commandments, sealed by Holy Spirit of promise; church of the Firstborn; "**they are gods**, even the sons of God"; dwell in presence of Father and Son forever |
| **Terrestrial** | Moon | Died without law; or received not the testimony in the flesh but afterwards; honorable but blinded by craftiness; **not valiant** in the testimony of Jesus; presence of the Son but not fulness of the Father |
| **Telestial** | Stars | Received not the gospel nor testimony of Jesus; deny not the Holy Spirit; suffer until the last resurrection; glory surpasses understanding yet **cannot come where God and Christ dwell**, worlds without end |

**Sons of perdition** alone are not redeemed after the sufferings of wrath: they knew the power, denied the truth, and crucified the Son unto themselves.

## Celestial Degrees (D&C 131)

In the celestial glory are **three heavens or degrees**; the highest requires entering the **new and everlasting covenant of marriage**; without it one "cannot have an increase."
