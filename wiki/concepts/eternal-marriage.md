# Eternal Marriage

**Eternal marriage** (celestial marriage) is the LDS doctrine that marriage sealed by priesthood authority under the **new and everlasting covenant** endures beyond death and is required for the highest exaltation (D&C 131–132).

## Textual Teaching

- **D&C 131:1–4** — three degrees in celestial glory; highest requires this order of the priesthood (new and everlasting covenant of marriage); without it, no "increase."
- **D&C 132** — covenants not sealed by the Holy Spirit of promise under the anointed key-holder end at death (angels, ministering servants, no exaltation); sealed marriage → thrones, continuation of seeds forever, "shall they be gods."
- The same section also presents **plural marriage** as part of the Abrahamic law of the covenant (see [[plural-marriage-lds]]) — historically practiced, later discontinued in the mainline church by Official Declaration 1.
