# Super Power

**Super Power** is an advanced [[scientology|Scientology]] rundown written by [[l-ron-hubbard|L. Ron Hubbard]] in the **late 1970s**, designed to rehabilitate **57 "perceptics"** — Hubbard's term for the full range of human sensory and perceptual channels, including standard five senses plus proprioception, spatial orientation, balance, and others not recognized by conventional science.

## Origins

Hubbard wrote the Super Power rundown relatively late in his active period. Its intended recipients were originally **Sea Org staff**, not public Scientologists — it was conceived as a way to enhance the perceptual and operational capabilities of the organization's core members.

The material was largely unpublished and not widely delivered during Hubbard's lifetime.

## The 57 Perceptics

Super Power works through a series of processes targeting each of the 57 perceptics identified by Hubbard. For some perceptics, the rundown requires specialized **physical devices** designed to stimulate or challenge the targeted sense. The major devices described by **Marc Headley** (*Blown for Good*, 2009):

- **Orbitron**: a device that rotates the person through space — targeting spatial orientation and vestibular perceptics
- **Zero Gravity Rig**: simulates weightlessness or altered gravity — targeting proprioceptive perceptics
- **Smell vials**: bottles containing specific scents, used for olfactory perceptic processing
- **Balance platform**: a platform designed to challenge and rehabilitate balance-related perceptics

These devices were developed or commissioned by the **Church of Spiritual Technology (CST)** — the archival preservation arm of the Church — according to Headley. CST's involvement signals the level at which this project was treated internally.

## The Pilot and the Building

**Marc Headley** (*Blown for Good*) piloted the Super Power rundown himself at the **Int Base (Gold Base)** in **1991–92** — a three-month pilot program. Headley's description:

- The pilot was intensive and extended; he underwent the full sequence of perceptic processes
- The devices were functional during the pilot period at Gold Base
- Miscavige subsequently directed the construction of a dedicated **Super Power building** in Clearwater, Florida — to be attached to the Flag Land Base at the Fort Harrison Hotel complex
- A **$4 million donation** from the **Feshbach family** (a wealthy Scientology family known for short-selling hedge fund activities) was among the major contributions to the project
- Total fundraising exceeded **$100 million** over the years
- The building was **not yet open** as of 2009 (the date of Headley's publication), despite the fundraising

## Controversy — Scope Creep and Fundraising

Headley's account makes clear that the Super Power building and project became a **major fundraising vehicle** far exceeding its original institutional function:

- Originally a staff rundown, it was repositioned for **public Scientologists** as a premium offering
- The associated building became one of the most prominent dedicated Scientology construction projects in history
- The gap between fundraising ($100M+) and delivery (not yet open as of 2009) attracted criticism even from within the broader ex-member community

The Super Power building in Clearwater (known as the "Flag Building" or "Superpower Building") eventually opened in **2013** — approximately 15 years after major fundraising began. This fact is not in the Headley source (which was published in 2009); it is outside-source context.

## Connection to Golden Age of Tech

Headley places Super Power development in the same institutional moment as the [[golden-age-of-tech|Golden Age of Tech]] pilot (1995 at Flag). Both represent Miscavige's direct personal involvement in Scientology's "tech" at a moment when he was consolidating his authority over the delivery apparatus.

## Theological Significance

Super Power represents a distinctive node in Scientology's soteriology:

- The 57-perceptics framework extends Hubbard's conception of human capacity well beyond conventional science — consistent with the broader Scientology claim that auditing rehabilitates abilities "suppressed" in this lifetime and across past lives
- The physical devices represent an unusual intersection of technology and spiritual practice, somewhat unique within new religious movements
- The original staff-only framing connects to Hubbard's vision of the Sea Org as an elite corps whose capabilities would be enhanced beyond those of ordinary humanity
