# The ARC Triangle

The **ARC triangle** is one of [[scientology|Scientology's]] central conceptual tools, set out in
[[scientology-fundamentals-of-thought|*The Fundamentals of Thought*]]. Its three corners —
**Affinity, Reality, and Communication** — are held to be the interdependent components of
**understanding**.

## The Three Corners

- **Affinity** — emotional closeness, liking, the degree of "love" or attraction between people (and
  related to the [[tone-scale|Tone Scale]]).
- **Reality** — agreement; "reality is agreement" on what is.
- **Communication** — the interchange of ideas; held to be the **most important** corner, the one that
  most readily raises the other two.

The doctrine: the three rise and fall **together** — raise one and you raise the others, and their sum is
**understanding (U)**. Conversely, a break in communication lowers affinity and reality (an "ARC break" is
Scientology's term for an upset).

## Function in the System

ARC is foundational to [[auditing|auditing]] (the auditor maintains high ARC with the preclear) and to
Scientology's whole theory of relationships and persuasion; raising ARC raises a person on the
[[tone-scale|Tone Scale]]. It is among the most-used everyday concepts in Scientology practice.
