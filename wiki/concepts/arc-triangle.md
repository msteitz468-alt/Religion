# The ARC Triangle

The **ARC triangle** is one of [[scientology|Scientology's]] central conceptual tools, set out in
[[scientology-fundamentals-of-thought|*The Fundamentals of Thought*]]. Its three corners —
**Affinity, Reality, and Communication** — are held to be the interdependent components of
**understanding**.

## The Three Corners

- **Affinity** — emotional closeness, liking, the degree of "love" or attraction between people (and
  related to the [[tone-scale|Tone Scale]]).
- **Reality** — agreement; "reality is agreement" on what is.
- **Communication** — the interchange of ideas; held to be the **most important** corner, the one that
  most readily raises the other two.

The doctrine: the three rise and fall **together** — raise one and you raise the others, and their sum is
**understanding (U)**. Conversely, a break in communication lowers affinity and reality (an "ARC break" is
Scientology's term for an upset).

## Function in the System

ARC is foundational to [[auditing|auditing]] (the auditor maintains high ARC with the preclear) and to
Scientology's whole theory of relationships and persuasion; raising ARC raises a person on the
[[tone-scale|Tone Scale]]. It is among the most-used everyday concepts in Scientology practice.

## 8-8008 Elaborations

*Scientology 8-8008* (1952) contains the most emphatic statement of Communication's primacy in any of Hubbard's works:

> "Communication is not only the modus operandi, it is the heart of life and is by thousands of percent the senior in importance to affinity and reality."

**Communication as "heart of life"**: Affinity is a form of energy. Reality is agreement. But Communication is the process by which both are established or degraded. The ARC triangle is therefore not an equilateral triangle in practice — it is Communication-dominant.

**Communication and perception**: The link between Communication and perception is stated as an axiom: "A thetan can be what he can see. He can see what he can be." The thetan's perceptual range is its communicative range. Restrictions in communication produce restrictions in what the thetan can be.

**The sole accurate test of case improvement**: "The only accurate test of case improvement is improvement in communication" — including perceptual changes (the ability to see color, hear clearly, feel again). All other metrics (changed emotional state, changed beliefs) are secondary to whether communication has expanded.

**ARC breaks**: A lowering of any one corner automatically lowers the other two. An "ARC break" (*arcx*) is an upset triggered by a perceived drop in affinity, reality, or — most frequently — communication. The 8-8008 processing methodology for ARC breaks always goes to Communication first.
