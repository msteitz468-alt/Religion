# Continuing Revelation

**Continuing revelation** is the principle that God still reveals doctrine and commandment through living prophets — the structural reason the LDS canon is **open and additive**.

## Textual Anchors

- **Articles of Faith 9** — believe all God has revealed, does now reveal, and will yet reveal.
- **Doctrine and Covenants** as a growing corpus of modern revelation (§§1–138 in the ingested edition; Official Declarations in modern printings).
- **D&C 1, 68** — when moved by the Holy Ghost, the servants' speech is the will/mind/word of the Lord.
- **2 Nephi 28–29** — condemnation of those who say "A Bible! … there cannot be any more Bible"; line upon line; God not finished.
- **3 Nephi 29** — wo to those who deny revelation and miracles when the book comes forth.

## Canon Implication

Unlike closed-canon traditions, new sections (or, in Community of Christ, new D&C sections from the prophet-president) can enter scripture. This is the load-bearing difference tracked by `canon_scope` on LDS text pages.
