# Bardo (the intermediate state)

**Bardo** (Tibetan *bar-do*, Sanskrit *antarābhava*) is the **intermediate state** — a
transitional phase of consciousness. In its original abhidharma usage the term named only the gap
**between death and rebirth**; the [[nyingma|Nyingma]] (and Kagyu) tradition extends it into a
scheme of **six intermediate states** spanning the whole of life and death, the organising frame
of the [[bardo-thodol|Tibetan Book of the Dead]].

## The Six Intermediate States

1. **Living** (*rang-bzhin bar-do*) — from birth to death; the time for study, reflection, and
   meditation.
2. **Dreams** (*rmi-lam bar-do*) — from falling asleep to waking; the domain of dream yoga.
3. **Meditative concentration** (*bsam-gtan bar-do*) — meditative equipoise (samādhi).
4. **The time of death** (*'chi-kha'i bar-do*) — from the onset of dying to the arising of the
   [[inner-radiance|inner radiance of the ground]]; structured by the sequential dissolution of
   the five elements.
5. **Reality** (*chos-nyid / chönyi bar-do*) — after death: sounds, lights, and rays arise, and
   the [[peaceful-and-wrathful-deities|peaceful and wrathful deities]] manifest, each paired with
   a [[five-pristine-cognitions|pristine cognition]].
6. **Rebirth** (*srid-pa'i / sipa bar-do*) — the consciousness in a **mental body**, driven by
   past actions toward a new birth among the [[six-realms|six classes of beings]], until
   conception (up to forty-nine days).

## Significance

The bardo doctrine reframes death as a **graded series of opportunities for liberation**: to
recognise the inner radiance at death yields the [[trikaya|dharmakāya]]; to recognise the deities
in the bardo of reality yields the sambhogakāya; to recognise one's situation in the bardo of
rebirth yields the nirmāṇakāya. Throughout, the cardinal instruction is that every appearance —
radiant or terrifying — is "the natural expressive power of your own awareness," not an external
reality. Heightened lucidity after death is what makes "[[bardo-thodol|liberation by hearing]]"
possible.
