# Exaltation (LDS)

**Exaltation** in Latter-day Saint usage is the highest soteriological goal: full participation in the life and glory of God in the celestial kingdom — including, in Nauvoo-era texts, becoming **gods**, having eternal increase, and sitting on thrones (D&C 76:58; 132:19–20).

## Textual Anchors (reingested standard works)

- **D&C 76** — celestial heirs "are gods, even the sons of God"; fulness and glory of the Father.
- **D&C 132** — sealed new and everlasting covenant of marriage → pass by angels and gods to exaltation; "then shall they be gods"; continuation of the seeds forever; eternal lives = knowing the true God and Jesus Christ.
- **D&C 131** — highest celestial degree requires eternal marriage.
- **Moses 1:39** — God's work and glory: immortality and eternal life of man.
- **Abr 3** — keep second estate → glory forever; noble and great ones.

## Comparative Note

Exaltation is often compared to Eastern Christian [[theosis-divine-nature|theosis]] / divinization, but the systems differ: LDS exaltation is tied to embodied Godhead, eternal marriage, and graded kingdoms; Orthodox theosis is participation in divine energies within a creedal Trinity without becoming a god of the same kind. **Do not collapse** the two (Contradiction Protocol / comparative care).
