# Raza de-Razin (Secret of Secrets)

*Raza de-Razin* (“Secret of Secrets”) is the Zoharic treatise on **physiognomy** (temperament, character, and fate from physical appearance), **metoposcopy** (forehead lines and marks), and **chiromancy** (palm and finger marks). The human body is read as a Sod-level text. Full contiguous English treatment: Pritzker Vol. XII (Hecker).

## Title, Lemmas, Speaker (body)

Full title in the unit: *Raza de-Razei de-Sitrei de-Oraita Qadishta Shelimta* — “Secret of Secrets of the Mysteries of the Holy, Perfect Torah.”

Primary lemmas:
- Exod 18:21 — *And you, you shall behold from all the people*
- Gen 5:1 — *sefer toledot ha-adam* read as **Book of Human Features** (“one of the concealed and profound books”)

Speaker: **Rabbi Shim’on son of Yoḥai**. Shekhinah instructs Moses when he is perplexed; she “viewed and selected all the men with distinctive visages.” Six characteristics: **hair, eyes, nose, lips, face, and hands** (markings of the hands) — all under *And you, you shall behold* (**~2:70b**).

Lore is said to have been transmitted to **Primal Adam**, bequeathed to **King Solomon** (hidden book). Examinations apply from **age thirteen** upward (holy spirit separated from impure spirit).

## Letter System (body)

Features are “inscribed” in permutations of letters drawn from *zeh sefer* (Gen 5:1 context):

> **zayin, resh, he, pe, samekh, final tsadi**

Hair lore centers on **zayin** (mnemonic: Samson’s weaponry in the hair); eyes on **resh** / **pe**; nose often on **he**. Close of unit: “In this book of the righteous are engraved all features of the world, through permutations of” these letters (**ZḤ ~37c**).

## Mutability — Core Distinctive Claim (body)

Unlike systematic medieval physiognomy manuals, the Zoharic unit stresses that markings **change with deeds**:

> “Just as the appearance of the stars and constellations shift in the expanse of the sky, as a result of worldly affairs, so too the appearance of markings and lines in the skin of a human being change, depending on one’s deeds from moment to moment.”  
> “From moment to moment according to one’s deeds—thus they are engendered, inscribed, and transmuted.” (**~2:71a**)

Holy Spirit dwelling within generates external inscriptions; when it withdraws, an impure spirit “clatters within” and manifests recognizable features — even when hair/forehead positions remain stable. *Teshuvah* can switch letter dominance (e.g. samekh victorious when one “returns to his Lord”) and rewrite markings (nine-day fantasy case; youth vs old-age repentance changing death mode). Adultery case: sin hangs on the neck of a twisted serpent; timing of *teshuvah* (vigorous vs elderly) changes the navel marking and whether sickness departs (**ZḤ ~35d**).

**Introduction framing (Hecker, attributed):** what distinguishes Zoharic physiognomy from predecessors is “the stress on the mutability of people’s physical features as a direct result of virtuous or sinful behavior” — the Zohar’s “perennial pietism” in physical form. Medieval *Secretum secretorum* / *Sod ha-Sodot* is the likely title source (apparatus).

## Structure of the Unit (body map)

| Block | Folios (as marked) | Content |
|-------|--------------------|---------|
| Opening theory | ~2:70a–71a | Lemmas; six traits; skin/sky analogy; letter system |
| Hair | ~2:71b–72b | Clinical types; letter switches; *teshuvah* fortune |
| Eyes | ~2:73a–75a → **ZḤ 35b–36b** | Seed of David / seven golds; evil eye; witchcraft; Metatron academies; graphic sin cases (sparse) |
| Nose | **ZḤ 36c–37b** | Form cases; Saturn / Qurqafnei–Shamriron melancholy dreams; Sabbath vs holy people |
| Close | **ZḤ 37b–37c** | Primal Adam book; David’s days; Jacob = Adam beauty and lines |

Pagination note: standard print ends abruptly and continues in *Zohar Ḥadash* 35b–37c (also Zohar 2:272b–276a Tosafot).

## Relation to Broader Zoharic Themes

- **Body as text** — parallel to letter mysticism (*Sitrei Otiyyot*) and the claim that Torah is not “mere tales.”
- **Ethics / theurgy** — appearance is diagnostic and exhortative; *teshuvah* rewrites the body.
- **Other Side** — sexual and covenant infractions leave traces that feed or reveal impure spirits (see [[sitra-ahra|Sitra Aḥra]]).
- Complements [[heikhalot|Heikhalot]] prayer theurgy in the same volume’s composition block.
