# Phowa (Consciousness Transference)

**Phowa** (Tibetan *'pho-ba*, Sanskrit *saṃkrānti*) is the tantric practice of **transferring
consciousness at the moment of death** — ideally into the [[trikaya|dharmakāya]] realisation, or
else into a pure buddha-field. It is Chapter 10 of the [[bardo-thodol|Tibetan Book of the Dead]].

## Method and Timing

Phowa is "a powerful method… which does not necessarily require meditation," held especially
valuable for ordinary people who have had no time to train. It is begun **between the cessation of
the outer and inner breath**, "only at the exact moment of death" — performed prematurely it would
incur "the slaying of the deities" and the crime of suicide.

The training visualises the central channel (*avadhūtī*), a brilliant white **seminal point**
(*thig-le*, "the essence of awareness") within it, and the [[bardo-thodol|spiritual teacher]] as
Vajradhara above the crown; guttural **HI-KA** breaths drive the seminal point up the channel to
exit through the **crown fontanelle** (*brahmarandhra*) — the supreme of nine possible exits (each
of the others leading to a lower rebirth). HI-KA "reduces the lifespan" and is used only until the
signs of success appear (warmth, serous fluid at the crown).

## The Four Modes

Transference into the (1) Buddha-body of Reality (for those who know inner radiance), (2) Buddha-
body of Perfect Resource (generation-stage practitioners), (3) Buddha-body of Emanation (using an
image of Śākyamuni, the Medicine Buddha, Maitreya, or [[padmasambhava|Padmasambhava]]), and (4)
the consciousness transference **of ordinary beings**, performed for them by a teacher who calls
out the name of [[bardo-thodol|Mahākāruṇika]] and recites buddha-names and the six-syllable mantra.
