# Plural Marriage (LDS)

**Plural marriage** (polygyny) was taught as a divine commandment in **D&C 132** and practiced by [[joseph-smith|Joseph Smith]] and later Utah leaders, then officially discontinued for the LDS Church by **Official Declaration 1** (1890 — not in the public-domain D&C edition ingested).

## Textual Claims (D&C 132 only — not adjudicated)

- New and everlasting covenant; sole earthly key-holder.
- Abraham commanded regarding Hagar; patriarchs with plural wives justified when commanded; David fell only in the Uriah case.
- Instructions concerning Emma Smith and the "law of Sarah" (first-wife consent).
- Ten virgins justification under the law of the priesthood.

## Tension with the Book of Mormon

**Jacob 2–3** condemns unauthorized polygyny of David and Solomon and commands monogamy, with a narrow "raise up seed" exception (Jacob 2:30). LDS harmonizations treat D&C 132 as a commanded exception; critics treat the tension as contradiction. Recorded on both sides — see also the controversy page [[plural-marriage-lds]] if promoted; for now this concept page holds the tension.
