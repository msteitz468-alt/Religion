# Annwn

**Annwn** (also *Annwfn*) is the **Welsh [[the-otherworld|Otherworld]]** — the realm of the Brythonic
[[celtic-religion|Celtic]] gods and dead. Unlike a remote heaven, Annwn in the **[[mabinogion|Mabinogion]]** is
a **neighboring kingdom** with its own kings, courts, hunts, and wars, overlapping the human world of Dyfed.

In the **First Branch**, **[[pwyll|Pwyll]]** meets **[[arawn|Arawn]]**, a king of Annwn, while hunting — their
two packs of hounds quarreling over a stag. To make amends Pwyll **trades places and shapes with Arawn for a
year**, ruling Annwn and slaying Arawn's rival **Hafgan**, while sleeping chastely beside Arawn's queen; for
this honor he earns the title **Pwyll Pen Annwn** ("Pwyll, Head of Annwn"). Annwn is also the source of
**treasures** — its **cauldron** (which will not boil a coward's food) and its **otherworldly white animals**,
including the pigs that **Gwydion** steals, and the prize of the great spoil-raid in the early poem *Preiddeu
Annwn* ("The Spoils of Annwn"), an ancestor of the **Grail quest**.

Annwn's hounds, the **Cŵn Annwn** — white with red ears — survive in folklore as the spectral hounds of the
**Wild Hunt**. Later Christian usage flattened *Annwn* into a word for "hell," but in the myths it is a
**kingdom**, not a place of torment.
