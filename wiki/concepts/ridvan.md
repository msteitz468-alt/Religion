# Riḍván (PA.: "Paradise")

*Populated from Smith, A Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith (Oneworld, 2000). All claims from that source unless marked.*

## Overview

The name given by [[bahaullah|Bahá'u'lláh]] to two gardens: the Naẓíbíyyih Garden in [[baghdad|Baghdád]], in which Bahá'u'lláh first announced his prophetic claims to some of his close companions in 1863, and a garden on the Na'mayn river near [[akka|'Akká]], which Bahá'u'lláh often visited during the closing years of his exile. The Na'mayn Garden is a recognized Bahá'í holy place, but the Naẓíbíyyih Garden has never been in Bahá'í ownership; the garden has long since been built over, and is now the site of a large modern teaching hospital (lines 26991–27016).

## Significance

Although the details of Bahá'u'lláh's declaration in the Naẓíbíyyih Garden are obscure, the period of his stay in the garden is regarded by Bahá'ís as being of immense significance. Bahá'u'lláh himself refers to the first day of Riḍván as being the day on which the splendours of the divine names and attributes were shed upon the whole of creation, and all things were purified (lines 27017–27026).

## The Riḍván Festival

The whole period of Bahá'u'lláh's sojourn in the garden (21 April–2 May) is now commemorated as a sacred festival. Three particular days are marked off as holy days on which Bahá'ís should suspend work:
- **First day** (21 April): commemorates the arrival of Bahá'u'lláh at the Garden; celebration should be in the late afternoon
- **Ninth day** (29 April): commemorates the arrival of his family
- **Twelfth day** (2 May): commemorates his final departure

(Lines 27027–27040.)

## Riḍván and Bahá'í Elections

Apart from celebratory meetings, the Riḍván period is also the time of most Bahá'í elections, a practice begun during the ministry of 'Abdu'l-Bahá. Local spiritual [[assemblies|Assemblies]] are normally elected on the first day of Riḍván. Elections for national assemblies and the [[universal-house-of-justice|Universal House of Justice]] are also normally held during the Riḍván period. Both [[shoghi-effendi|Shoghi Effendi]] and the Universal House of Justice have frequently sent special Riḍván messages to the Bahá'ís at this time (lines 27041–27052).
