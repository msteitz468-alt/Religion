# The Five Pristine Cognitions (Five Wisdoms)

The **five pristine cognitions** (Tibetan *ye-shes lnga*, Sanskrit *pañcajñāna*; often "five
wisdoms") are the modality of awakened mind in [[vajrayana|Vajrayana]] Buddhism: the **natural
purity of the five [[bardo-thodol|psycho-physical aggregates]]**, each embodied by one of the five
male buddhas of the [[peaceful-and-wrathful-deities|maṇḍala]] and counteracting one afflictive
emotion. They are the doctrinal engine of the [[bardo|bardo of reality]] visions.

| Pristine cognition | Buddha | Aggregate purified | Affliction freed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reality's expanse (*dharmadhātujñāna*) | Vairocana | consciousness | delusion |
| Mirror-like (*ādarśajñāna*) | Akṣobhya-Vajrasattva | form | aversion |
| Sameness (*samatājñāna*) | Ratnasambhava | feeling | pride |
| Discernment (*pratyavekṣaṇajñāna*) | Amitābha | perception | attachment |
| Accomplishment (*kṛtyupasthānajñāna*) | Amoghasiddhi | motivational tendencies | envy |

(Correspondences of colour/light and family vary slightly across the cycle's chapters and
commentaries; the source records minor internal variation, e.g. whether white belongs to
reality's-expanse/Vairocana or to the mirror-like/Akṣobhya cognition.)

## In the Bardo

In the [[bardo|bardo of reality]] each pristine cognition arises as a brilliant, dazzling
coloured light, set against a **dull competing light** of one of the [[six-realms|six rebirth
realms]]. Recognising the brilliant light as one's own awareness is liberation; following the dull
light is rebirth. The fifth (accomplishment/Amoghasiddhi, green) is said not to arise until full
enlightenment, so only "four pristine cognitions combined" appear at one stage.
