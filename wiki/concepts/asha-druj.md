# Asha vs. Druj (Truth/Order vs. the Lie)

**Asha** (Avestan; Vedic **rta**) and **druj** (Avestan, "the Lie"; Vedic *druh*) are the two poles
of [[zoroastrianism|Zoroastrian]] cosmic and moral order. **Asha** is at once the **right order of
the physical world** and **truth/righteousness** in the moral world; **druj** is falsehood,
distortion, and disorder. The whole of existence — cosmic and ethical — is the contest between them.

## From Indo-Iranian *rta* to Zoroastrian *asha*

Per [[boyce-zoroastrians-beliefs-and-practices|Boyce]], the concept is **pre-Zoroastrian**, shared
with the Vedic Indians as **rta**: the natural order by which the sun moves, the seasons turn, and
sacrifice sustains the world. It already carried **ethical** force — truth, honesty, loyalty, and
courage belonged to the natural order, and vice was its betrayal. Old Iranian moralists divided
humanity into the **ashavan** (the righteous, who uphold asha) and the **drugvant** (the wicked,
who serve the Lie). The sacredness of a person's **given word** — guarded by the oath-lords
**Mithra** and **[[varuna|Varuna]]** through fire- and water-ordeals — was a central case of asha.
(*Asha* is genuinely hard to translate: "order" for the physical sphere, "truth" or
"righteousness" for the moral one.)

## In Zoroaster's Reform

[[zarathustra|Zoroaster]] made this polarity the **moral spine** of his revelation:

- **Asha is allied to [[ahura-mazda|Ahura Mazda]]**; druj to [[angra-mainyu|Angra Mainyu]] and the
  Daevas. The cosmic dualism of the two primal spirits is, on the human side, the choice between
  asha and druj.
- **Asha Vahishta** ("Best Truth/Righteousness") becomes one of the six [[amesha-spentas|Amesha
  Spentas]] — the hypostasis of asha, associated with **fire** and the noontime watch.
- The central Zoroastrian ethic, **"good thoughts, good words, good deeds,"** is the practical
  service of asha; the sacred **Ashem Vohu** prayer ("Asha is good, it is best…") concentrates the
  worshipper's mind on it.
- Upholding asha is **cosmically efficacious**: right worship and right conduct strengthen the good
  creation in its war against the Lie, hastening [[frashokereti|the final renovation]].

## Significance

The fusion of **physical order** and **moral truth** in a single word, set against a single
principle of falsehood, gives Zoroastrian ethics its distinctive shape: morality is not arbitrary
divine command but **alignment with the structure of reality itself**. The asha/druj antithesis is
a leading candidate antecedent for the sharp light/darkness, truth/falsehood dualisms of later
[[apocalyptic-literature|apocalyptic]] and Gnostic thought — see
[[zoroastrian-influence-on-abrahamic-eschatology|the influence page]].
