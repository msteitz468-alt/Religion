# Great Apostasy

The **Great Apostasy** is the Latter-day Saint claim that the early Christian church fell into general corruption after the apostles — losing priesthood authority, plain gospel truths, and right ordination — necessitating a **[[restoration-lds|Restoration]]** through [[joseph-smith|Joseph Smith]].

## Anchors in the Reingested Texts

- **1 Nephi 13** — "great and abominable church" removes **plain and precious** parts from the gospel record; many stumble.
- **2 Nephi 26–28** — latter-day churches built up for gain; deny miracles; "All is well in Zion"; precepts of men.
- **JS-H 1:19** — First Vision: join none of the sects; creeds an abomination; form of godliness, deny the power.
- **AoF 5–6** — authority by laying on of hands; need for primitive church organization restored.
- **D&C** narrative of restored Aaronic/Melchizedek priesthood (§13, §27, §84, §107, §110).

## Contested Reception

Historic Christian churches deny a total apostasy of authority; they may admit corruption/reform needs without accepting LDS restoration claims. Central to [[mormon-christian-identity]].
