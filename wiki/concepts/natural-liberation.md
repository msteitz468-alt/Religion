# Natural Liberation (rang-grol)

**Natural liberation** (Tibetan *rang-grol*) is the signature soteriological concept of
[[dzogchen|Dzogchen]] and the recurring phrase in the titles of the [[bardo-thodol|Tibetan Book of
the Dead]] cycle ("Natural Liberation through Recognition of Enlightened Intention," "Natural
Liberation of Habitual Tendencies," etc.). In Nyingma terms it is "recognition of the actual
nature of the object, which is free from any form of renunciation or antidote."

## The Distinctive Move

Where **causal** vehicles work by applying antidotes and renouncing the afflictions, the
**resultant** vehicle of [[dzogchen|Atiyoga]] holds that a dissonant state, *recognised* for what
it actually is, **self-liberates on the spot** — "right where it is." Hence the cycle's repeated
formula of "natural liberation **without renunciation** of the three poisons": ignorance, desire,
and aversion are not suppressed but seen as primordially pure ([[rigpa|intrinsic awareness]]).
Chapter 6 applies this pervasively, identifying each affliction and each philosophical error
(eternalism, nihilism, egotism, substantialism) as the "natural purity" of a corresponding deity.

This is also the hermeneutical key to the [[peaceful-and-wrathful-deities|peaceful and wrathful
deities]] and the [[bardo|bardo]] visions: terror and beauty alike are self-liberated the instant
they are recognised as one's own awareness.
