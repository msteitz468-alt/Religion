# The Tone Scale

The **Tone Scale** is a graded scale of **emotional/affective tones** in [[scientology|Scientology]],
introduced by [[l-ron-hubbard|Hubbard]] in [[science-of-survival|Science of Survival]] (1951) and
elaborated in the **Hubbard Chart of Human Evaluation**. It claims to map a person's chronic emotional
level — and from it to **predict** their behavior, health, ethics, and reliability.

## The Scale

Tones are ranked from low to high (with numeric values), e.g.: **death/apathy (0.0)**, **grief (0.5)**,
**fear (1.0)**, **anger (1.5)**, **antagonism (2.0)**, **boredom (2.5)**, **conservatism (3.0)**,
**cheerfulness (3.5)**, and **enthusiasm (4.0)**, with still-higher tones in the later Scientology
extension of the scale (up to "serenity of beingness"). A person's **chronic tone** is held to determine,
across many "columns" of life, how they think, behave, and respond — so that locating someone on the scale
lets one predict and handle them. The scale also underwrites controversial social judgments (e.g. the
low-toned person as untrustworthy).

## Function in the System

The Tone Scale is a core **practical instrument** of Scientology: in [[auditing|auditing]] and in daily
"ethics," moving a person **up the tone scale** is a measure of progress. It pairs with the
[[arc-triangle|ARC triangle]] (raising affinity-reality-communication raises tone).

## Reception

Not accepted by mainstream psychology as a validated construct; the wiki's secondary sources treat it
critically. Recorded without adjudication.

## 8-8008 Elaborations

*Scientology 8-8008* (1952) extends the Tone Scale in two significant ways not present in *Science of Survival*:

### Extended Range
The scale now runs from **-8.0 to 40.0**. The positive range (0.0 to 4.0) covers the Body-Plus-Thetan composite and ordinary human experience. The range above 4.0 to 40.0 covers the thetan in operation. **40.0 = Full Responsibility/will, no randomity; 20.0 = optimal thetan tone, "in command"; 4.0 = Homo sapiens (Enthusiasm).**

The negative (Sub-zero) range applies to the *thetan itself*, not the body:

- Body death: the threshold
- Sub-zero states (in descending order): Being a body → Being other bodies → Punishing other bodies → Responsibility as blame → Controlling bodies → Protecting bodies → Owning bodies → Approval from bodies → Needing bodies → Hiding

These sub-zero states represent the thetan's progressive identification with bodies rather than with its own beingness.

### The Two-Position Account
Every person has *two* positions on the scale simultaneously:
- **Body-Plus-Thetan (0.0–4.0)**: the composite being's social tone, maintained by education and habit
- **Actual thetan tone (Sub-zero to 40.0)**: the thetan's real state beneath

In most human beings the thetan is operating sub-zero. Auditing first drops the preclear from the false social tone to the actual thetan tone, then raises it. A preclear who becomes "irrational" during auditing is not regressing — the thetan's actual low state is simply being revealed. When the thetan rises above its sub-zero false floor, "it quickly rises upscale through the entire range, as a thetan, and generally settles at 20.0."

### Responsibility Gradient
8-8008 develops a detailed Tone Scale of responsibility:
- **40.0**: Full Responsibility; pure will; no randomity
- **20.0**: 50% responsibility; some sphere selected for randomity
- **4.0**: Homo sapiens; narrow disagreement with existing states; responsibility is low
- **2.0**: Blame enters the scale; the first appearance of fault-assignment
- **1.5**: Blame is almost the sole activity
- **1.1**: Pretends responsibility to demonstrate others are at fault
- **0.9/Fear**: Accepts all blame to escape punishment
- **0.5/Grief**: Blames himself
- **0.05/Apathy**: No question of blame; "one has become MEST"

"Slaves are made by giving them freedom from responsibility."
