# Xibalba

**Xibalba** ("Place of Fear") is the **underworld of the [[maya-religion|Maya]]** — a dreaded realm beneath
the earth, ruled by **twelve Lords of Death** and reached by a treacherous descent through rivers of blood,
pus, and scorpions.

## The Place of Fear

Xibalba was a place of **trial and terror**, full of deadly "test houses" (the Houses of Gloom, Cold,
Jaguars, Fire, and Bats). Its chief lords — **One Death** and **Seven Death** — spread sickness, decay, and
death among the living and demanded the lives of the dead. It is the great stage of the **[[popol-vuh|Popol
Vuh]]**: the [[hero-twins|Hero Twins]] descend into Xibalba, survive its houses of torment, and **defeat the
Lords of Death** by trickery — a mythic **conquest of death itself**, after which the Twins rise to become the
sun and moon. Maya kings and the dead were thought to journey through Xibalba, and (as elsewhere in
[[mesoamerican-religion|Mesoamerica]]) the **ballgame** was bound up with the cosmic struggle against the
underworld powers.

## Cross-Tradition Note

A **fearsome, trial-filled underworld conquered by a dying-and-rising hero** — comparable to other underworld
descents and "harrowings" (Inanna's [[mesopotamian-underworld|Descent]], [[heracles|Heracles]] and Cerberus,
the Christian Harrowing of Hell), recorded without asserting dependence.
