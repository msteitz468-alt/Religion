# Genetic Entity (GE)

The **Genetic Entity** (abbreviated **GE**) is the [[scientology|Scientology]] term for what Hubbard calls "the somatic mind" — the entity that carries the evolutionary history of the physical body across all its biological ancestors, from the first cellular life to the present human form. It is categorically distinct from the [[thetan|thetan]] (the true spiritual self) and from the injected entities (synthetic souls imposed by invader forces).

## Nature and Origin

The GE is a **composite** — not a person, not a self, but an accumulation of cellular experience across the entire genetic line. It is "the 'I' of the body," not the "I" of the person. Its characteristics:

- **Cowardly** and purely stimulus-response in character; without higher goals
- **Without will** beyond the imperative to grow and maintain a body
- Not capable of the kind of self-determination the thetan possesses

The GE enters protoplasm approximately **two days before conception** and remains with the dying body to its very end — unlike the thetan, which leaves the body at death and proceeds to the [[between-lives|Between-Lives]] area.

## The Genetic Line

The GE's history is the evolutionary history of life on Earth, but rendered by Hubbard as a sequence of **traumatic incidents** — each auditable on the E-Meter. These incidents are:

### Pre-cellular
- **Atom** — earliest cellular memory unit; basic atomic-level existence

### Single-Celled Life
- **Cosmic Impact** — one atom impacting another; earliest cellular incident
- **Photon Converter** — approximately 1 billion years ago; the organism converts light to food; explains the "good feeling" of sunlight on skin
- **Helper** — the mitosis incident; one cell divides, the two daughter cells momentarily try to occupy the same space; a fundamental confusion incident

### Bivalve and Marine Life
- **Clam** — the double-hinge bivalve; considered "a DEADLY INCIDENT." The two hinges operate in contrary motion. If described to someone unaware, it can restimulate immediately and cause severe jaw pain, ear problems, or other symptoms. Hubbard issues explicit warning: "Do not describe this incident to any preclear who isn't already running it."
- **Weeper** — a pumping creature that extracted salt water through itself; eyes evolved from the pumping tubes, which is why humans cry salt water; source of crying; lungs are derived from the breathing-pumping mechanism of this era

### Land Life and Vertebrates
- **Volcanoes** — early land creatures experiencing volcanic catastrophe; basis for terror responses
- **Birds** — flying creatures; fear of falling; aerial incidents
- **Being Eaten** — prey-animal incidents; being consumed by a predator

### Pre-Human Mammals
- **Sloth** — arboreal hanging; slow-motion incidents
- **Ape** — closest pre-human ancestor; fights, territorial incidents

### Pre-Human Hominids
- **Piltdown Man** — fought with others by biting; teeth marks on skull; murder and cannibalism incidents; explains some chronic oral/dental problems
- **Caveman** — past-death incidents; "Assumption" and "Departure" incidents (taking over and leaving bodies)

## The GE vs. the Thetan

The distinction is fundamental to Technique 88: Hubbard argues that most prior auditing (including early Dianetics) was inadvertently auditing the **GE's bank**, not the thetan's bank. This produced physical improvement (the body improved) but not spiritual advancement (the thetan did not improve). Only Technique 88, by explicitly targeting the theta being, addresses the true self.

Key statement from *A History of Man*: "People who keep running incidents without any recourse to an E-Meter will achieve much with the body, very little with the thetan."

The GE is also distinguished from the **Injected Entities** (synthetic souls electronically implanted by invader forces). The GE is a natural product of biological evolution; the entities are artificial intrusions.

## Auditing the GE

The GE's incidents are audited using **Technique 80** (the body-mind technique), not Technique 88 (the theta-being technique). Properly identifying *which* entity's bank one is auditing — the GE's, the thetan's, or an entity's — is the central diagnostic challenge of Technique 88, requiring an E-Meter.
