# The Norns (Fate / Wyrd)

The **Norns** are the [[norse-religion|Norse]] goddesses of **fate** — and **fate (Old Norse *urðr*; Old
English *wyrd*) is the deepest power in the Norse cosmos**, ruling **gods and humans alike**. Even [[odin|
Odin]] cannot escape what is fated; the gods go to [[ragnarok|their doom]] because it is **woven**.

## The Weavers of Fate

The three great Norns — **Urð** ("what has become / the past"), **Verðandi** ("what is becoming / the
present"), and **Skuld** ("what shall be / debt, the future") — dwell at the **Well of Urð** beneath the
world-tree [[yggdrasil|Yggdrasil]], which they tend and water. They **shape the lives of all beings**,
allotting each person their destiny at birth (lesser norns attend each birth). Their decrees are
**irreversible** — to know one's fate is not to escape it but to meet it well.

## The Weight of Wyrd

This sovereignty of **fate** gives Norse religion its characteristic tone: a world where courage matters
precisely *because* the outcome is fixed — one cannot avoid death, only choose how to face it. **Seiðr** magic
(see [[seidr|seiðr]]) sought to *glimpse* and slightly *bend* this fated order, and the great prophecy of the
*Völuspá* is a seeress reciting fate from creation to Ragnarök. Comparable to the Greek [[fate-moirai|Moirai]]
and the broader Indo-European idea of woven destiny.
