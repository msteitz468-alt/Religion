# Aaronic Priesthood

The **Aaronic Priesthood** is the lesser or preparatory priesthood in LDS order, restored by **John the Baptist** to Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery on **15 May 1829** (JS-H 1:68–72; D&C 13).

## Keys Named in the Texts

- Ministering of angels.
- Gospel of repentance.
- Baptism by immersion for the remission of sins.
- Held until the sons of Levi offer again an offering unto the Lord in righteousness (D&C 13).

Contrasted with the [[melchizedek-priesthood|Melchizedek Priesthood]] (higher; Holy Ghost by laying on of hands; temple keys; D&C 84, 107).
