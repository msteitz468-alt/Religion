# MEST

**MEST** is the [[scientology|Scientology]] acronym for the **physical universe**: **Matter, Energy, Space, and Time**. It refers to the entirety of the material world as distinct from **theta** (the spiritual principle, the life energy of thetans).

## The Theta-MEST Polarity

The fundamental cosmological axis in Scientology is the opposition between theta and MEST. In the original (pre-aberration) state, the [[thetan|thetan]] was pure theta — a creative, self-determined spiritual being capable of generating space, energy, and matter by postulate. MEST was the product of theta's creative activity.

The problem: over trillions of years, through a "gradient of agreement" — small, incremental surrenders to the authority of the physical universe — thetans progressively transferred their sense of self from theta to MEST. They forgot they created the MEST universe, began experiencing it as real and independently authoritative, and became trapped inside bodies (themselves MEST). The MEST universe is thus both a product of theta and the principal mechanism of its entrapment.

## Properties of the MEST Universe (*Scientology 8-8008*)

**Agreed-upon illusion**: "In Scientology, one is studying the lowest common denominators of agreement which bring about an acceptance of the MEST universe." The MEST universe is not independently real but is sustained by the collective agreement of thetans. Its "natural laws" are the laws of agreement, not absolute laws.

**A "police universe"**: "The MEST universe is essentially a police universe — for it operates upon force and intolerance and demands with pain that its laws be accepted." It punishes non-compliance. Even when one has agreed entirely with it, the MEST universe continues to punish.

**A "major expanding trap of finite dimensions and rather idiotic simplicity"**: designed to capture thetans indefinitely. The MEST universe "demands with enormous thoroughness that one shall agree with it or be destroyed."

**Not ultimately real**: "The MEST universe, in this light, is an agreed-upon illusion — not independently real, but sustained by the collective agreement of thetans." A hypnotist can produce experiences indistinguishable from MEST perception in a subject; this demonstrates the illusory character of MEST "reality."

**Jealous of theta**: "The MEST universe is a jealous universe. Those who are thoroughly imbued with its principles have, even at their best efforts, the goal of eradicating one's own universe. A control operation begins early in the life of almost every man, whereby his imagination is condemned."

**Goal of processing**: to reduce the apparent infinity of the MEST universe to zero *for the individual* while expanding the individual's own universe from zero to infinity — the meaning of the title *Scientology 8-8008*.

## MEST as the Lowest Tone

MEST is the goal of aberration — complete identification with the physical universe. "At 0.05 Apathy, there is no question of either blame or responsibility. At this level one has become MEST." The fully aberrated thetan has no space of its own, no self-determined action, and is subject to any force acting upon it. "MEST has no space of its own. It causes no action except when acted upon. And it owns nothing, but is itself owned."

## The Human Body as MEST

"The human body: a carbon-oxygen engine built of complex electronic ridges around the genetic entity which animates it." The body is wholly MEST. The genetic entity that animates it is a non-thetan entity — "cowardly, a thing of stimulus-response, without further will or goals than to grow a body." The thetan occupies the body but is not the body.
