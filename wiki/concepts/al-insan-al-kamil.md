# Al-Insan al-Kamil (the Perfect Human)

**Al-insan al-kamil** — "the Perfect" or "Complete Human" — is the [[sufism|Sufi]] doctrine,
formulated by **[[ibn-arabi|Ibn Arabi]]** in the opening (Adam) bezel of the
[[fusus-al-hikam|Fusus al-Hikam]] and named as such by his school, that the fully realized human being
is the **all-comprehensive image of God**: the single locus in which *all* the divine Names are
manifested together, and therefore the purpose and the sustaining principle of the entire cosmos.

## The Doctrine

- **The polished mirror**: God willed to see his own Names reflected in a comprehensive object. The
  cosmos was an unpolished mirror; **[[adam|Adam]]** — humankind — is the "polishing" of that mirror,
  the place where the Real contemplates itself. This is the inner meaning Ibn Arabi gives to Adam's
  creation "in the image" and his appointment as God's **vicegerent** (*khalifa*, Q 2:30).
- **The microcosm / the *barzakh***: the Perfect Man "sums up all the realities of the universe"; he
  is the **isthmus** (*barzakh*) joining the divine and created orders, partaking of both, belonging
  wholly to neither. The angels, knowing only their own facets, could not grasp the names Adam knew —
  the Qur'anic scene of their prostration read as the superiority of the comprehensive human.
- **The preserving seal**: in a striking image of the *Fusus*, the world is "guarded" only so long as
  the Perfect Man is in it — he is the seal on the treasury of existence; were he removed, the cosmos
  would dissolve. Humanity is thus not incidental to creation but its very point.
- **The Muhammadan Reality**: the doctrine culminates in the **bezel of Muhammad** — the Perfect Man
  realized supremely is the **Muhammadan Reality** (*al-haqiqa al-Muhammadiyya*), the comprehensive
  Name through which all the others are integrated. Realized human beings (prophets, and the saints
  who inherit from them) participate in this perfection.

## Relation to Other Doctrines

The Perfect Man is the anthropological face of **[[wahdat-al-wujud|wahdat al-wujud]]**: if the cosmos
is the Real's self-disclosure, the Perfect Man is the **complete and conscious** disclosure, the
mirror in which the unity becomes self-aware. He is also where Ibn Arabi's balance of
**[[tanzih-tashbih|tanzih and tashbih]]** is enacted — the one being who knows God as both
incomparable and manifest.

## Cross-Tradition Comparison (flagged)

The structure — a primordial/archetypal Human who is the image of the divine and the microcosm of the
cosmos — parallels the [[kabbalah|Kabbalist]] **Adam Qadmon** (the supernal anthropos configured of
the [[sefirot|sefirot]]) and the Gnostic/Philonic heavenly Man, and stands behind the Christian
"second Adam" typology. The shared **Adam-as-microcosm** motif is a strong candidate for a comparison
page; see the Kabbalistic reading on the [[adam|Adam]] page.
