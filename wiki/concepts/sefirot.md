# The Ten Sefirot (עֲשֶׂר סְפִירוֹת)

The ten dynamic emanations or divine potencies that together constitute the knowable inner life of God in Kabbalistic theology. The sefirot are not separate beings or parts of God but aspects of a single divine organism — what the [[zohar|Zohar]] calls the "mystery of faith" (*raza de-meheimanuta*). They emerge from and remain rooted in [[ein-sof|Ein Sof]] (the Infinite), and their dynamic interplay is the secret subject of the entire Torah.

The term *sefirot* (singular: *sefirah*) derives from the ancient *[[sefer-yetsirah|Sefer Yetsirah]]* ("Book of Formation"), where it means roughly "enumeration" or "number." By the 13th century the term had been fully theosophized to mean "divine potency."

## The Supernal Triad (Keter, Ḥokhmah, Binah)

This highest triad constitutes the most hidden and recondite level of the divine. Ordinary human religious life rarely reaches here; it is the realm of deepest contemplation.

- **Keter** — the first stirring of divine will, barely distinguishable from Ein Sof. Often called "Nothing" (*Ayin*) because it has no determinate content. The crown at the top of the divine body.
- **Ḥokhmah** — the first "real" point of existence. All being exists here in infinitely concentrated form. This is the primordial Torah, existing before letters; the *yod* (smallest letter), tip pointing toward Keter. Kabbalists read Job 28:12 ("Wisdom comes from *ayin*, nothing") as Ḥokhmah emerging from Keter. Identified with the patriarch's relation to God as "beginning."
- **Binah** — Ḥokhmah's light enters Binah's mirrored palace and generates all the lower sefirot from within her womb. She is the divine Mother (*Imma Ila'ah*, supernal Mother), the great sea, the jubilee (all returns to source in her), the object of repentance (*teshuvah*). The divine name *Elohim* is associated with her in many Zoharic readings.

The Ḥokhmah-Binah pair is the primordial Father-Mother (*Abba* and *Imma*), the primal Male-Female, whose union is eternal and uninterrupted (unlike the union of Tif'eret and Shekhinah, which can be disrupted).

---

## The Second Triad (Ḥesed, Gevurah, Tif'eret)

The key dialectical triad and the locus of the Zohar's most sustained theological reflection.

- **Ḥesed** — the grace of God, the right arm, the "south." Abraham is its human embodiment ("Abraham My lover," Isa 41:8). Unlimited love; the original flow of divine blessing.
- **Gevurah/Din** — the power and judgment of God, the left arm, the "north." Isaac is its human embodiment ("the Fear of Isaac," Gen 31:42). Isaac bound on the altar faces the God of overwhelming power — this is Gevurah's face. **Critical**: when Gevurah operates untempered by Ḥesed, the left "blazing" side tips into demonic fire. This is the birthplace of the [[sitra-ahra|Sitra Aḥra]].
- **Tif'eret/Raḥamim** — the central pillar; Jacob/Israel is its embodiment, integrating Abraham's love and Isaac's fear. The "blessed Holy One" who sits enthroned, to whom prayer is primarily addressed. Identified with the Tetragrammaton YHVH. The divine Groom whose union with Shekhinah is the central event of Kabbalistic piety.

---

## The Third Triad and Yesod (Netsaḥ, Hod, Yesod)

- **Netsaḥ and Hod** — largely channels for the higher energies to flow down to Yesod. Together they are the source of prophecy for prophets below the level of Moses (who reaches Tif'eret directly). Moses' prophetic supremacy is precisely his access to Tif'eret — the "clear mirror" — while other prophets receive refracted images from Netsaḥ/Hod.
- **Yesod** — the Foundation, the channel that gathers all ten energies and directs them to Malkhut. Represented as the cosmic phallus; Joseph (*Tsaddiq*, the one who resisted Potiphar's wife) is its patriarchal embodiment, the model of sexual holiness. Called *Kol* ("All") because it encompasses all the sefirot above it. The covenant of circumcision is specifically tied to Yesod — the mark of the covenant is inscribed on the physical representative of this sefirah.

## Malkhut / Shekhinah

See [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]] for full treatment.

The tenth sefirah is simultaneously:
- The divine Bride and Queen (*Malkhut*, Kingdom)
- The divine Presence (*Shekhinah*, "dwelling")
- The Moon (waxing and waning with the flow of divine blessing)
- The Assembly of Israel (the corporate body of the Jewish people in its divine dimension)
- The gate through which all souls enter and exit
- The divine name *Adonai* (Lord)

Her relationship to Tif'eret (as Bride to Groom) is the governing erotic metaphor of the entire Zohar. Their union — the *zivvuga qaddisha* — is promoted by human religious practice and disrupted by sin.

## The Sefirot and the Human Body

The sefirot collectively form the *Adam Qadmon* (Primordial Human), the divine body:
- Keter = crown of the head
- Ḥokhmah = right brain
- Binah = left brain
- Ḥesed = right arm
- Gevurah = left arm
- Tif'eret = torso/heart
- Netsaḥ = right leg
- Hod = left leg
- Yesod = phallus (the covenant organ)
- Malkhut = the female body or the feet

Because the human being was created *b'tselem Elohim* ("in the image of God"), the human body is a map of the divine structure. Every human being is a microcosm of the sefirotic universe. Kabbalah's ethics flow from this: to harm another human is to damage the divine image; to perform *mitsvot* with proper intention is to channel sefirotic energies through the human vessel.

## Cross-Tradition Comparison

**vs. the Christian Trinity**: Both are articulations of inner divine differentiation within a single divine reality. The Cappadocians' three *hypostases* in one *ousia* and the Zohar's ten *sefirot* from a single *Ein Sof* share a concern to preserve divine unity while affirming real inner plurality. The differences: the Trinity is three (fixed), the sefirot are ten (dynamic and gendered); the Trinity involves permanent co-equality, the sefirot involve hierarchy and flow; the Trinity is not theurgically manipulable, the sefirot respond to human religious action. See [[cappadocian-fathers|Cappadocian Fathers]], [[logos-christology|Logos Christology]].

**vs. Philo's Powers**: Philo identifies divine *dynameis* (powers) as intermediaries — primarily the Logos and Sophia — bridging the transcendent God and creation. These are structurally similar to the upper sefirot (Ḥokhmah/Wisdom is a direct parallel), but Philo's powers are a dyad or triad, not a ten-fold organism. See [[philo-of-alexandria|Philo of Alexandria]].

## Grounding from Pritzker Zohar Volume I (standards reingest 2026-07-18)

Attributed to the Zohar body as rendered in Matt, *The Zohar: Pritzker Edition*, Volume I (Haqdamah; Parashat Be-Reshit; Parashat Noaḥ). Sefirotic names are often implicit as rungs/symbols rather than technical labels.

### Gen 1:1 — Beginning, palace, Elohim as object

In the Be-Reshit theogony, a concealed point called *Reshit* (Beginning) expands and builds a palace; the palace is *Elohim*, and *Elohim* is the *object* of creation:

> "With this beginning, the unknown concealed one created the palace. This palace is called אלהים (Elohim), God. The secret is: … (Be-reshit bara Elohim), With beginning, created God (Genesis 1:1)." (Zohar 1:15a; Pritzker I)

Beginning is identified with Wisdom: "*Be-reshit*… *bet reshit*, two, beginning, beginning of wisdom" (1:29b). The point sows seed into the palace to "give birth, availing worlds" (1:15a–b).

### Keter not counted; Ḥokhmah is "beginning"

Rabbi Shimʿon on the six days:

> "For the supernal, concealed Crown is first, but since it is not included in the count, the second is beginning. So: *Bet, reshit*, 'two, beginning.'" (Zohar 1:31b; Pritzker I)

### Who / What; Jacob as middle

Haqdamah (Isa 40:26 frame): the concealed ancient one susceptible to questioning is *Mi* (Who), "End of Heaven above"; "Beyond, there is no question." Below is *Mah* (What) — the limit of inquiry: "What do you know? … All is concealed, as before." Jacob "inherited this, running from end to end… from first end, Who, to last end, What, for he stands in the middle" (Zohar 1:1b–2a).

Elijah's revelation: Concealed of all Concealed → single point → thought → *Mi* (origin of structure) → garb → *elleh* (these) → *Elohim* (1:2a).

### Yesod as Day / Kol / Vitality of Worlds / covenant

From the central pillar's perfect light:

> "From that perfect light, the central pillar, extended יסודא (Yesoda), Foundation, Vitality of the Worlds, Day, from the right side." (Zohar 1:16b–17a; Pritzker I)

Central pillar + three vowel-points yield the foundation called *Kol* ("All") (1:17a). The world stands by covenant of day and night together (Jer 33:25 reading; 1:32a–b). Gathering of waters to "one place" maps to the Righteous One / Seas (1:33a). Fruit-tree / bearing fruit = Righteous One; covenant of holiness and peace (1:33a–b).

### Malkhut as Night / moon / Adonai

> "He called forth… from the side of darkness a female, the moon ruling by night, called Night, mystery of אדני (Adonai)." (Zohar 1:17a; Pritzker I)

Moon initially shared one bond with the sun, then "diminished herself, diminished her light"; "Go, diminish yourself!"; thereafter light only from the sun (1:20a–b). Dry land = lower *he* / revealed world; Night left wanting until filled through "Increase" (Yesod) (1:17a; 1:18a–b).

### Right–left–center; six directions

- **Right** first (Ḥesed/light); **left** arouses conflict on the second day (expanse); **central pillar** (third day) mediates and pacifies (1:17a–b).
- *Bara shit* = "He created six… six directions extending from the supernal mystery" (1:15b); six words of *Shema* + six of *Barukh shem* as upper/lower unifications (Haqdamah commandments; Be-Reshit gathering of waters).
- Adam: six directions (E/S/N/W + above/below) hedge the garden; human "in our image" resembling four directions, above and below (1:34b–35a).
- 1 Chr 29:11 (Gedullah, Gevurah, Tifʾeret, Netsaḥ, Hod…) read as six days of Creation (1:31b).

### Noah = Yesod below

In Parashat Noaḥ, Noah is the lower double of the Righteous One:

> "Noah was a righteous man… corresponding above… *The righteous one is the foundation of the world* (Proverbs 10:25)… Righteous One, and Noah is called righteous below." (Zohar 1:59b–60a; Pritzker I)

He is *ish ha-adamah*, "husband of earth"; the ark is Shekhinah / Ark of the Covenant; world unfit "until they joined as one" (1:59b–60b). Three sons (Shem / Ham / Japheth) correspond to right / left / purple blending both (1:73a).

## Grounding from Pritzker Zohar Volume II (fidelity reingest 2026-07-18)

Attributed to the Zohar body as rendered in Matt, *The Zohar: Pritzker Edition*, Volume II (Lekh Lekha–Va-Yetse). Sefirotic names often appear as rungs, sides, and patriarchal symbols rather than a fixed ten-name list.

### Patriarchs as Ḥesed / Gevurah / Tif’eret

- **Abraham = Ḥesed / South / right / light Day.** “Continually journeying toward the Negev—the South, share of Abraham” (Zohar 1:79b–80a); “in the heat of the day… right was radiant, rung to which Abraham cleaved” (1:98a–b); fourfold promise as “throne of four legs, all comprised in Abraham” (1:78b).
- **Isaac = Gevurah / Din / North / fire / darkness / Night.** “Fire appropriates water, for water flows on the side of Gevurah… Isaac is joy… transforming water into fire, fire into water” (1:104a). “When Isaac was old, his eyes were too dim to see… He had to grow dark, cleaving to darkness, his rung, fittingly” (1:142a). Rebekah’s mild Judgment balances Isaac’s severity so the world can endure (1:137a).
- **Jacob = Tif’eret / center / sun / consummation of patriarchs.** “Consummate man” (*ish tam* = *gevar shelim*) comprises ḥesed with the kind and harsh judgment with the crooked; dwells in tents = embraces Abraham and Isaac (1:139b, 1:146a). Voice of Jacob / hands of Esau typology; *sarita with Elohim* = couple sun and moon (1:144b).

Prayer times map the triad: Abraham morning (Ḥesed endures all day); Isaac afternoon (Ḥesed fades, severe Judgment arouses); Jacob evening arrays the moon / *vav* adorns *he* (Ḥayyei Sarah 1:132a–b).

### Water / fire at the Akedah (completion of the triad)

The binding stages mutual completion of Ḥesed and Gevurah before Tif’eret can harmonize them:

> "Elohim tested Abraham—not Isaac… previously Abraham had contained no judgment at all. Now **water was embraced by fire**… Then division became apparent: **water versus fire**… until Jacob appeared and everything harmonized—**triad of patriarchs completed**, above and below arrayed." (Zohar 1:119b–120a; Pritzker II)

Later Abraham’s gift of “all” to Isaac reverses the blend (“fire completed by water… water embraced by fire”) for the “mystery of supernal faith” (1:133b–134a). Shofar on Rosh Hashanah is “blended of fire, water, and air… the world harmonizes, compassion prevails” (1:114a–b zone).

### Throne legs; David as fourth

Three visitors (Gen 18) = Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob standing over the lower rung, “from whom it sucks nourishment” (1:99a). *Adonai* (alef-dalet-nun-yod) = Shekhinah arriving with three colors as Her support and throne (1:101b). “The blessed Holy One made King David one leg of the supernal throne, just like the patriarchs… kingship in Hebron for seven years—in order to join them” (1:99a). In Va-Yetse, Judah completes four supports of the Throne; until then the Holy Name is incomplete while Assembly of Israel is in exile (1:155a).

### Ladder (Va-Yetse)

> "What does *ladder* imply? A rung on which other rungs depend… *angels of God ascending and descending on it*—deputies of all nations… When Israel sins, this ladder is lowered and they ascend; when Israel rectifies its actions, this ladder ascends, they all descend… All depends on this ladder." (Zohar 1:149b; Pritzker II)

“Its head reaching to heaven” = head of the bed / **consummation of the body** (covenant standing between thighs and torso) — Yesod as head of the ladder (1:149b–150a). Sun-Jacob pants toward the west / site of Shekhinah (1:147a–b).

### Two worlds (Jubilee / Sabbatical; Hu / Attah)

Jacob serves seven years so that the moon’s years alight on him — Heaven and Earth; first seven = concealed Jubilee (Leah), second = revealed Sabbatical (Rachel); “he was crowned with two worlds… **through revealed, one reaches concealed**” (1:153b–154a). Blessing *from world to world*; higher *Hu* (He) / lower *Attah* (You); *YHVH, YHVH* two times yet one (1:158b). Stork/*ḥasidah* (Leah / higher world): Ḥesed issues from Her (1:163a).

## Grounding from Pritzker Zohar Volume IV (fidelity reingest 2026-07-18)

Attributed to the Zohar body as rendered in Matt, *The Zohar: Pritzker Edition*, Volume IV (Shemot–Yitro). Source: [[matt-zohar-pritzker-vol4|Pritzker Zohar Volume IV]]. Technical sefirot names often appear as rungs, sides, names, colors, and patriarchal symbols.

### Names, two specula, voice/speech (Va-Era)

Exod 6:2: *Elohim spoke* = decree of **Judgment**; *He said, “I am YHVH”* = rung of **Compassion** — Judgment and Compassion linked as one (2:22b). *I appeared… as El Shaddai* = colors that are **seen** / **speculum that does not shine** (patriarchs); *by My name YHVH I was not known* = colors **not seen** / **speculum that shines** (Moses alone) (2:23a–23b). Four lights: three concealed + one revealed that receives like crystal facing the sun (2:23b). Contemplative limit at *ad* / *Yah YHVH* — place from which worlds emerged; no human permitted beyond (2:22a–22b).

Moses = **voice**; speech/word (Shekhinah) in exile until Sinai: “voice united with speech… *Elohim spoke all these words*” (2:25b; Exod 20:1). Covenant unites with the Female so existence is sustained by unification; *I remembered My covenant* = *zakhor* remembering couples desire (2:26a). Moses–Aaron = air with water / water with air; *It is the same* singular (2:26b).

### Plagues as graded force on dominion-rungs (Va-Era–Bo)

Plagues strike Egyptian dominion from **lowest rung first** to **firstborn rung** last; all ten signs issue from the **mighty hand**, muddling minds of their rungs (2:29a–29b). Blood: lower world suckles from the **left**; moon filled from left; sword of YHVH full of blood; Nile as false god struck first (2:28b–29a). Aaron strikes waters because waters are in Aaron’s site; left draws them to blood (2:29a). *Arov*: confounds powers **Egypt against Egypt** above and below (2:30b). Finger vs full **hand** of five fingers (pestilence species); *dever* letters reverse to *barad* hail (2:31b). Firstborn spectrum as low crowns of Kingship / millstones / lowest female (Bo 2:37b–38a).

### Red Sea: 72-letter Name; Ancient One; *yam suf* (Be-Shallaḥ)

Pillar cloud/fire = Ḥesed/Abraham vs Gevurah/Isaac within Shekhinah; angel moves from Ḥesed to Gevurah when Judgment’s time arrives (2:51b). Moon full, three arrays of seventy:

> "From the side of Ḥesed, seventy and two witnesses. From the side of Gevurah, seventy and two scribes. From the side of Tif’eret, seventy and two colors… the Holy Name culminates—mystery of the Chariot." (Zohar 2:51b; Pritzker IV)

Letter structure: first letters **straight** (Ḥesed); second **backward** (Gevurah, judgments/weapons); third display **colors** crowning the King (2:51b–52a). Holy Ancient One (*Attiqa*) illumines the King → patriarchs crowned → Matronita journeys with that consummation (2:52a). At the sea: “*Why are you crying out to Me?* … **To Me, precisely! All depends upon the Ancient One**… all is as difficult… as the splitting of the Red Sea… because the splitting… depends on the Ancient One” (2:52b; Book of Concealment cited in body). *Be-yam suf* = *sofa*, **end of rungs** — Judgment/sea/Kingdom (2:50a). Matnitin: seventy-two letters return clogged stones; after seventy-two rungs stones split; waters half-congeal, half-sink (2:50b).

Song theurgy: *az* = *alef* (Ancient One’s sparkle) joins *zayin* (Sword of YHVH full of blood) (2:54a); right hand present and future smashes enemy; Torah as that right hand (Song unit).

### Rock–Gevurah–Mother; manna crown of Ḥesed (Be-Shallaḥ manna)

Rock = **Gevurah**; from a facet of **Supernal Rock** / side of **Supernal Mother** Gevurah emerges though She is not Judgment; water normally from **Gedullah** — miracle turns rock into pool (2:64a–64b). Manna: holy dew from Concealed Ancient One fills head of Short-Tempered One (**Heaven**); scions of faith marked with **insignia of the crown of Ḥesed** (2:62a–62b). Evening Judgment / morning Love mappings of manna (2:63b).

### Sinai: Raḥamei then Gevurah; Decalogue body; five-within-five (Yitro)

Core theophany order (attributed):

> "I will reveal Myself to them in **Raḥamei** (Compassion)… first extending them Raḥamei and afterward giving them the Torah from the side of **Gevurah**… On the third day—precisely, which is Raḥamei!… Torah was given from the side of Gevurah… It returned to the right: *From His right hand, a fiery law*." (Zohar 2:81a; Pritzker IV)

*Qolot* deficient = two voices into one; voices **seen** as body in darkness/cloud (2:81a–82a). Moses: *ispaqlarya* that shines; other prophets and Ezekiel: does not shine / behind many walls; Sinai = head and body of the King (2:82a–82b). Tablets: five utterances included in five so all on the right; left readable from right (pairing *I am* with *You shall not murder*, etc.) (2:84a–84b). *Anokhi* = Shekhinah / upper-world YHV mysteries; 613 hang on **body of the King**; sin against a commandment = against that body (2:85a–85b). *Zakhor* = male/supernal Covenant; *Et* Sabbath includes Entrance of Sabbath (female night) (2:92b–93a). Ten utterances = totality of above/below and of Creation’s ten sayings; world not enduring until Israel receives Torah (Jer 33:25 reading) (2:93b–94a).
