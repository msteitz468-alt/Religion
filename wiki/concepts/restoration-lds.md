# Restoration (LDS)

The **Restoration** is the LDS claim that God restored the true church, priesthood keys, and fulness of the gospel through [[joseph-smith|Joseph Smith]] after the [[great-apostasy|Great Apostasy]].

## Components (from standard works)

1. **First Vision** and call (JS-H).
2. **Book of Mormon** as recovered scripture and witness of Christ.
3. **Priesthood** — Aaronic (John the Baptist, 1829); Melchizedek (Peter, James, John); temple keys of Moses, Elias, Elijah (D&C 110).
4. **Church organization** 6 April 1830 (D&C 20–21) with apostles, prophets, etc. (AoF 6).
5. **Continuing revelation** and open canon (AoF 9; D&C).
6. **Gathering and Zion** — New Jerusalem on the American continent (AoF 10; D&C 57; Moses 7).

Self-understood as restoration of *primitive* Christianity plus restored ancient keys — not merely Reformation of an existing church.
