# Zion (LDS)

**Zion** in LDS scripture is both a **place** and a **people/quality of society**.

## Place

- **Independence, Jackson County, Missouri** — "center place" of the city of Zion; temple lot west of the courthouse (D&C 57).
- **New Jerusalem** on the American continent (AoF 10; 3 Nephi 20–21; Ether 13; Moses 7).
- Stakes of Zion; Kirtland as a stake (D&C 82).

## People / quality

- **Moses 7:18** — Enoch's Zion: "of one heart and one mind… dwelt in righteousness; and there was no poor among them"; city taken up ("ZION IS FLED").
- **4 Nephi** — multi-generation common life without "-ites" as a Zion-like interim.
- **1 Nephi 13:37** — blessed who seek to bring forth Zion.
