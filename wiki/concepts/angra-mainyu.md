# Angra Mainyu (Ahriman, "the Hostile Spirit")

**Angra Mainyu** (Avestan, "the Hostile/Destructive Spirit"; Middle Persian **Ahriman**) is the
adversary of [[ahura-mazda|Ahura Mazda]] in [[zoroastrianism|Zoroastrian]] dualism — the
**uncreated, ignorant, and wholly malign** principle who opposes the good creation and all
[[asha-druj|asha]] (Truth/Order) with **druj** (the Lie/disorder).

## The Two Primal Spirits

Per [[boyce-zoroastrians-beliefs-and-practices|Boyce]], [[zarathustra|Zoroaster]] beheld in vision
the two great Beings "at their original, far-off encountering," and the Gathas describe them
(Yasna 30) as **twins** who chose: "Truly there are two primal Spirits, twins, renowned to be in
conflict. In thought and word and act they are two, the good and the bad." Angra Mainyu is **not a
creation of Ahura Mazda** but co-exists with him from the beginning — the defining claim of
Zoroastrian dualism, and what distinguishes it sharply from the Abrahamic [[satan|Satan]], a
*created* and subordinate rebel.

## Role in the Cosmic Drama

- When Ahura Mazda gives his perfect creation **material (*getig*) form**, it becomes vulnerable,
  and Angra Mainyu **straightway attacks**: in the Pahlavi myth ([[bundahishn|Bundahishn]]) he
  breaks through the stone sky, turns the waters salt, makes deserts, withers the primal plant,
  and slays the Uniquely-created Bull and the First Man, fouling even fire with smoke.
- He brings into being the **Daevas** (the false gods of the old religion, rejected by Zoroaster
  as demons), Wrath, and every moral and physical evil — **death** above all.
- His assault inaugurates the middle age of world-history, the time of **Mixture** (see
  [[the-three-times|the Three Times]]), in which good and evil are entangled.
- He is **doomed**: at [[frashokereti|Frashokereti]] the river of molten metal flows down into
  hell, the Daevas are annihilated, and Angra Mainyu is destroyed forever.

## Druj, the Daevas, and Renunciation

The believer's formal creed (the *Fravarane*) is a Mazda-worshipper's explicit **renunciation of
the Daevas** and "the company of all that belongs to the Drug." In daily prayer the Zoroastrian
**execrates Angra Mainyu**, flicking the ends of the sacred cord (*kusti*) contemptuously. Evil is
thus not merely avoided but actively repudiated — moral life is **enlistment in Ahura Mazda's war**.

## Zurvanite Reinterpretation

The heterodox [[zurvanism|Zurvanism]] made Ahriman the **twin son of Zurvan (Time)** alongside
Ohrmazd, born of Zurvan's moment of doubt — a genealogy that subordinates both spirits to a prior
principle and was condemned by orthodox tradition for compromising Ahura Mazda's supremacy.
