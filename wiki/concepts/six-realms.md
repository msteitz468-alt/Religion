# The Six Realms (Six Classes of Beings)

The **six classes of sentient beings** (Tibetan *'gro-ba rigs-drug*, Sanskrit *ṣaḍgati*) are the
six destinies of rebirth within [[samsara|cyclic existence]], each driven by a dominant
afflictive emotion. They structure the [[bardo|bardo of rebirth]] in the [[bardo-thodol|Tibetan
Book of the Dead]], where each realm is signalled by a **dull coloured light** the deceased must
resist, and is "obstructed" by an emanational sage of the deity maṇḍala.

| Realm | Driving affliction | Dull light (bardo) | Emanational sage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gods (*deva*) | pride | white | Devendra Śakra |
| Antigods (*asura*) | envy / jealousy | red | Vemacitra |
| Humans | desire (or all five) | blue | Śākyamuni |
| Animals | delusion | green | Sthirasiṃha |
| Anguished spirits (*preta*) | miserliness | yellow | Jvālamukha |
| Hell-beings | hatred | smoky | Dharmarāja ([[yama|Yama]]) |

## In the Bardo of Rebirth

The wandering consciousness, in its **mental body**, is drawn toward one realm by the momentum of
[[karma|past actions]]. The Book of the Dead gives signs by which the deceased can recognise the
realm looming (celestial palaces → gods; rocky caverns in mist → animals; black pits → hells,
etc.) and **methods for obstructing the womb-entrances** so as to avert a bad rebirth or, better,
transfer to a pure buddha-field. The whole human-realm rebirth is uniquely valued as the one
affording the opportunity for liberation.
