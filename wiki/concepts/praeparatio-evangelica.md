# Praeparatio Evangelica

> **Concept page.** The patristic claim that everything before Christ — Israel's Law, Greek
> philosophy, even pagan oracle and longing — was a divinely-ordered **preparation for the
> gospel**, *fulfilled* and *superseded* by his coming. The organizing category of Pelikan's
> first chapter in *[[pelikan-emergence-catholic-tradition|The Christian Tradition]]*; the title is
> Eusebius of Caesarea's apologetic work.

## The Threefold Schema

[[jaroslav-pelikan|Pelikan]] shows the fathers applying one structure to three streams, each
preparing for and then yielding to Christ:

1. **Israel and the Law** → the church as the **"true Israel."**
2. **Greek philosophy** → completed in Christ, "the revealer of true philosophy, ancient and yet
   new."
3. **Pagan longing** (oracles, Sibyl, Vergil) → answered in Christ as "the expectation of the
   nations."

The hinge proof text is **Genesis 49:10** (LXX: "he shall be the **expectation of the nations**"),
read as a three-point summary: Israel's mission → its completion in Christ → Christ as "the divine
answer to the aspirations of all the nations." The schema secures "the finality of God's
revelation in Christ while acknowledging the partial validity of earlier revelations" — no stream
is granted equality with the New Testament.

## Stream 1 — Israel and the "True Israel"

The church claimed to *be* Israel: Justin to Trypho — "we... are the true Israelitic race," and
the messianic passages "are contained in your Scriptures, **or rather not yours, but ours**." This
**supersessionist** appropriation Pelikan calls a **"doctrine of correction-and-fulfillment,"**
and treats it as the *structural template* for every later argument over tradition (Athanasius;
Augustine vs. the Pelagians; the Greek–Latin disputes; the Reformers). The Old Testament was made
a Christian book through **typology and allegory** (Origen: "the writings of Moses are the words
of Christ"; Irenaeus: "the treasure hidden in the [Old Testament] Scriptures is Christ").

Pelikan's sharpest theses on this stream:
- **Victory by default**: Christianity's triumph over Judaism "was achieved largely by default...
  not the superior force of Christian exegesis... but the movement of Jewish history" after 70 CE.
- **De-Judaization deformed doctrine**: the loss of Hebrew and of living dialogue made
  **immutability** "a primarily ontological category" rather than covenantal, distorting later
  Trinitarian, christological, and grace debates. "Most of Christian doctrine developed in a church
  uninformed by any knowledge of the original text of the Hebrew Bible."
- **Re-Judaization is not recovery**: the church's borrowing of priestly/sacrificial language back
  from Judaism showed its *independence* from living Judaism, not renewed engagement.

(See [[israelites|Israel/Israelites]] and [[hellenized-diaspora-jews|diaspora Judaism]]; the
*adversus Judaeos* genre became "a literary conceit" addressed to Christian readers. The
appropriation side of this is filed at [[christianity-apostasy-from-judaism|Christianity's
appropriation of / parting from Judaism]].)

## Stream 2 — Greek Philosophy

Three devices explained truth among pagans:
- **The seminal Logos (*logos spermatikos*, Justin)**: "the seed of reason in man" let Socrates see
  dimly what was revealed clearly in Christ — "Whatever things were rightly said among all men are
  the property of us Christians." (See [[logos-christology]].)
- **Natural law (Tertullian, Origen)** — universal moral knowledge from creation; Origen "the first
  to justify the right to resist tyranny by appealing to natural law" (see [[natural-law]]).
- **Theft from Scripture** — the widespread claim that Plato and the poets plagiarized Moses (the
  [[spoils-of-egypt|"spoils of Egypt"]] motif), with Pelikan's caveat that "many ideas... were being
  read *into* the Old Testament rather than being found there".

This whole stream is the patristic engine of the [[hellenization-of-christianity|hellenization
question]] — but Pelikan's verdict is dialectical: dogma was as much a *dehellenizing* "wall
against an alien metaphysic" as a capitulation (see [[development-of-doctrine]]).

**Clement's** classic image: "philosophy was a preparation, paving the way for him who is perfected
in Christ" — schoolmaster to the Greeks as the Law was to the Hebrews (Gal 3:24). This dispute
generated doctrinal development: **[[creation-ex-nihilo|creation *ex nihilo*]]** against Platonic
coeternal matter, and the absorption of **[[divine-impassibility|divine impassibility]]** and the
**immortality of the soul** as Greek inheritances. Pelikan's "metaphysics of Exodus": **Exodus
3:14** ("I Am Who I Am") became the Old Testament proof text for Christian ontology, from Clement
to Aquinas.

## Stream 3 — The Expectation of the Nations

Pagan **oracles** were enlisted: **Vergil's Fourth Eclogue** (the "messianic eclogue") and the
**Sibylline Oracles** (Lactantius even drawing Trinitarian proof from the Sibyl; the medieval
*Dies irae* invoking "David and the Sibyl"). These expressed a "longing for peace... that prepared
the way for the expansion of Christianity." Gregory I's missionary instruction — adapt pagan
temples and feasts to Christian use — is the practical corollary.

## The Pyrrhic Victory

Pelikan's closing irony: the triumph was real but **Pyrrhic** — "the theology that triumphed over
Greek philosophy has continued to be shaped ever since by the language and the thought of classical
metaphysics" (transubstantiation as test case; Boethius's *Consolation* as dramatization). The
unresolved tensions "were to take their revenge" in modernity. This pairs with his
**[[development-of-doctrine#hellenization-vs-dehellenization|dehellenization]]** counter-thesis to
Harnack.
