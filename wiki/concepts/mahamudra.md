# Mahāmudrā (the Great Seal)

**Mahāmudrā** (Sanskrit, "Great Seal"; Tibetan *phyag-rgya chen-po*) is a system of meditation on
the nature of mind, the contemplative summit of the **Kagyu** and **Gelug** schools — the New
Translation analogue to the [[nyingma|Nyingma]]'s [[dzogchen|Dzogchen]].

In the sūtra tradition it denotes the comprehension of [[sunyata|emptiness]] as the all-encompassing
ultimate nature; in tantra, advanced meditations integrating calm abiding and penetrative insight
into emptiness and pure appearance; in the Nyingma/Mahāyoga usage, the "great seal of buddha-body"
that secures the ground-of-all consciousness as mirror-like [[five-pristine-cognitions|pristine
cognition]]. The [[bardo-thodol|Tibetan Book of the Dead]] lists "Great Seal" among the synonyms
for [[rigpa|intrinsic awareness]] and names it (with the Great Perfection) as a path by which a
realised practitioner recognises [[inner-radiance|inner radiance]] directly, without needing the
Liberation by Hearing.
