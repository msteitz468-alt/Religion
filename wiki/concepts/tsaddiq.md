# Tsaddiq (צַדִּיק) — the Righteous One, Foundation of the World

*Tsaddiq* means "righteous one." In [[kabbalah|Kabbalah]] the term acquires a precise theosophical meaning: it is the title of **Yesod**, the ninth [[sefirot|sefirah]] (Foundation), and of the human being who embodies it. The governing proof-text is **Proverbs 10:25** — *"the righteous one is the **foundation of the world**"* (*ve-tsaddiq yesod olam*) — which the [[zohar|Zohar]] reads as a literal statement of cosmic architecture: the world stands upon the Tsaddiq. Yesod is the channel that gathers all the divine energies flowing down through the sefirot and transmits them to **Malkhut** / the [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]], and the earthly Tsaddiq is the righteous person — paradigmatically [[joseph|Joseph]] — who keeps that channel open by guarding the covenant.

---

## Yesod as the Cosmic Foundation

The Zohar (Parashat Va-Yeshev) builds the concept from a chain of verses:

> "When the blessed Holy One created the world, He arranged for it **seven pillars**… and all those pillars stand upon **one single pillar**… called **Righteous**, as is written: *the righteous one is the foundation of the world*. When the world was created, it was from that place — **foundation of the world and its perfection, single point of the world, center of all**. Who is that? **Zion**." (Zohar 1:186a)

Yesod is therefore:
- The **all-gathering channel** (*Kol*, "All") that unites the upper sefirot and pours their influx into Malkhut;
- The **point on which the world rests** — identified with **Zion**, the foundation-stone, the center from which "the whole world is nourished";
- Called **"Vitality of the Worlds"** (*Ḥai ha-Olamim*) and **"the Living One, the Righteous One, the Foundation of the World."**

Because Yesod is symbolized by the **covenant of circumcision** and the generative organ (the *berit*, the "sign of the covenant inscribed on the flesh"), the Tsaddiq is the sefirah of generativity and of **sexual holiness** — the right ordering of the creative/sexual power that channels blessing rather than dissipating it.

---

## The Human Tsaddiq: Joseph and the Guarding of the Covenant

The supreme human embodiment of the Tsaddiq is **[[joseph|Joseph the Righteous]]** (*Yosef ha-Tsaddiq*), who earns the title precisely by **guarding the sign of the covenant** — resisting Potiphar's wife. The Zohar reads "there was no man of the men of the house" (Gen 39:11) as including the **image of his father [[jacob|Jacob]]** (Tif'eret), at whose appearance Joseph "returned to his stability and withdrew." The covenant Joseph guards "is the **mystery of Joseph**," and because he guarded it, "Joseph's coffin accompanied [the Shekhinah]."

The negative pole is **Er and Onan** (Gen 38), who "wasted seed" — the misuse of the same generative power that empowers the [[sitra-ahra|Sitra Aḥra]] (see that page). The Tsaddiq concept is thus inseparable from the Zohar's ethics of sexuality: the same sefirah that, rightly guarded, is the foundation of the world is, when defiled, the chief feeder of the demonic.

---

## The Death of the Righteous

A second major theme attaching to the Tsaddiq is the cosmic function of the **death of the righteous**. Reading Isaiah 57:1 ("*the righteous one perishes, and no one takes it to heart*"), the Zohar teaches:

> "When the blessed Holy One gazes upon the world and finds it unfit, and judgment is poised to prevail, He **removes the righteous one** from among them, so that judgment will befall the others… For as long as a righteous person dwells in the world, **judgment cannot dominate the generation**." (Zohar 1:180a)

The Tsaddiq is the generation's shield: while he lives, his presence holds back judgment (the model is [[moses|Moses]] "standing in the breach," Ps 106:23); his death exposes the generation to the judgment he had been absorbing. The death of the righteous thus has an **atoning, protective** dimension — a theme that connects to the broader Zoharic account of the soul's afterlife (see [[zoharic-afterlife|The Soul and the Afterlife]]).

---

## Later Development: the Ḥasidic Tsaddiq

The Kabbalistic Tsaddiq becomes, in 18th-century **Ḥasidism**, the *rebbe* or *tsaddiq* — the charismatic holy leader who, as the living embodiment of Yesod, channels divine blessing (*shefa*) to his community and binds heaven and earth. The doctrine "the righteous one is the foundation of the world" is the theological charter of the Ḥasidic court. See [[kabbalah|Kabbalah]] (Ḥasidism section).

---

## Cross-Tradition Comparison

- **vs. the Christian "righteous one" / the cross**: the idea that a single righteous figure sustains or shields the world, and that the death of the righteous protects others, has obvious structural resonance with Christian atonement theology (Christ as the one righteous man whose death saves the many; cf. the "cornerstone," "the stone the builders rejected," and Zion/foundation imagery in 1 Peter 2:6–7). The Zohar's Tsaddiq is a *sefirah* and its many human embodiments, not a single incarnate redeemer, and the mechanism is theurgic rather than substitutionary in the Christian sense — but the comparison is illuminating and has been explored in scholarship.
- **vs. the "thirty-six hidden righteous" (Lamed-Vav)**: the later Jewish legend that the world is sustained by thirty-six hidden Tsaddiqim in every generation is a popular extension of the same Proverbs 10:25 principle.

## Grounding from Pritzker Zohar Volume I (standards reingest 2026-07-18)

Attributed to the Zohar body as rendered in Matt, *The Zohar: Pritzker Edition*, Volume I (Haqdamah; Be-Reshit; Noaḥ).

### Tsaddiq / Vitality of the Worlds / Foundation (Yesod)

Haqdamah letter drama: letter *tsadi* petitions; the righteous are sealed by *tsadi*; God is called *Tsaddiq* (Ps 11:7). Innovated Torah of wisdom:

> "an innovated word of wisdom ascends and settles on the head of צדיק (Tsaddiq), Righteous One—**Vitality of the Worlds**. From there, it flies… ascending to the Ancient of Days…. transformed into a heaven…. He calls them new heavens…" (Zohar 1:4b–5a; Pritzker I)

Benayahu "Son of a living man" = same "Tsaddiq, Righteous One—Vitality of the Worlds" (Haqdamah). *Bara* transposed to *ever* (organ) = "Holy Foundation on which the world stands" (1:3b). Be-Reshit:

> "From that perfect light, the central pillar, extended יסודא (Yesoda), Foundation, Vitality of the Worlds, Day, from the right side." (Zohar 1:16b–17a)

Called *Kol* ("All"); gathering of waters to one place = Righteous One / covenant of peace; fruit-tree "bearing fruit—the Righteous One… Covenant of holiness, covenant of peace" (1:33a–b). World established by covenant of day and night (1:32a–b).

### Noah as righteous foundation below

> "Noah was a righteous man… corresponding above… *The righteous one is the foundation of the world* (Proverbs 10:25)… Upon this, earth is established… Righteous One, and Noah is called righteous below." (Zohar Noaḥ; Pritzker I)

Further: "A righteous man, foundation of the world, covenant of peace… husband of earth, indeed!"; "*Perfect in his generations*… Perfect, for he was born circumcised"; destined to unite with the ark — "Until they joined as one, the world was unfit" (Noaḥ). Birth: "circumcised, inscribed with the sign of Shekhinah; he saw Shekhinah cleaving to him"; *ish ha-adamah* = husband of the soil / Righteous; offering liberates earth from curse (Fall prep / Noaḥ). Rainbow: "Since Noah was righteous, his covenant was a bow" // Joseph "His bow remained firm" / Joseph called Righteous (Noaḥ).

**Ambivalence retained (do not flatten):** "in this generation, precisely" can limit Noah to self-protection; he failed to plead for the world (Flood waters named for him, Isa 54:9) unlike Moses; could not abolish death; later vineyard episode reopens Adam's breach and Ham removes his covenant (Noaḥ). Exaltation and deficiency coexist in the same parashah.

### Innovated Torah settles on the Tsaddiq; partnership

Every innovated word of Torah "fashions one heaven"; ordinary innovations → "new earth"; God "makes them continually out of those innovations and mysteries of Torah" (Isa 66:22 *I am making*). Re-read *ammi* as *immi*: "with Me, becoming My partner! Just as I made heaven and earth by speaking… so do you" (1:4b–5a). Unqualified innovation → distorted heaven / woman of whoredom / damages holy covenant → Hell / Dumah (1:5a; 1:8a–b). "The righteous one rules the awe of God" (2 Sam 23:3) — righteous abolish decrees (Haqdamah).

### Converts of Righteousness

Commandment 8: love the convert who comes to circumcise / enter under Shekhinah's wings; "converts of Righteousness" dwell uniting under the wings (Israel from trunk; converts from wings) (Zohar 1:13a; Pritzker I).

## Grounding from Pritzker Zohar Volume II (fidelity reingest 2026-07-18)

Attributed to the Zohar body as rendered in Matt, *The Zohar: Pritzker Edition*, Volume II.

### Yesod / covenant as foundation (Lekh Lekha)

> "The world stands upon a single righteous one… *The righteous one is the foundation of the world*… all those others endure through the seventh." (Zohar 1:82b; Pritzker II)

World created only through Covenant (*berit* / Jer 33:25 day and night); after circumcision one is “crowned with the sacred corona” and enters the covenant on which the world stands (1:91b). Eighth-day offering of sons: Israel enters “this fine share… *The righteous one is the foundation of the world*” (1:93a–b zone). Bearer of the sign “does not descend to Hell if he preserves it properly—not inserting it into a foreign domain”; covenant mark scatters bands of demons and keeps one from Dumah (1:94a–95b). “From my flesh I will see God” = flesh of covenant; when married, the sign “enters its site” (*vav–he* male–female) (1:94b–95a).

### Joseph–Benjamin: two righteous (Va-Yetse)

> "Rachel gave birth to two righteous ones… Sabbatical dwells constantly between two righteous ones… **Joseph merited becoming Righteous One above because he guarded the sign of the covenant. Benjamin is righteous one below**… *When Rachel had given birth to Joseph, Jacob said… ‘Send me on my way’*… because the body has truly been completed." (Zohar 1:153b–154a; Pritzker II)

Female (Sabbatical / lower world) dwells between two tsaddiqim as male between two females. Righteous One **enters** the lower world in the mystery of Joseph and **emerges** in Benjamin (*Ben Oni* → *Ben Yamin*, Son of the Right, ascending to the higher world) (1:155b). Head of the ladder = consummation of the body / covenant between thighs and torso (1:149b–150a).

### Wreath of prayers on the head of the Tsaddiq

In Ḥayyei Sarah the feminine *Shim’ah* receives all prayers and weaves them as a **wreath on the head of Tsaddiq, Vitality of the Worlds** (Prov 10:6) (Zohar 1:132a; Pritzker II). Va-Yetse intensifies:

> "*Blessings upon the head of the righteous one*… Head of the righteous one is Holy Corona… head of the righteous one is Jacob, who receives blessings and channels them to Righteous One… site of head of Covenant, from which bubbling springs gush… flinging springs to female." (Zohar 1:162a; Pritzker II)

Whoever guards the covenant is called Righteous head to feet; *Ḥai* / *Ḥei ha-Olamim* names both Righteous One above and righteous below (1:163a–b zone).

### Well of the Living One; midnight delight

Well Lahai Roi = joining Shekhinah to the Living One / Righteous One / Vitality of Worlds — “not to be separated from the well” (Toledot 1:135b–136a). Blessed Holy One delights with the righteous in the Garden of Eden at midnight; those who rise for Torah “stand by night in the house of YHVH” (1:136b). God tests the righteous to elevate them (banner / *nes* from *nissah*), focusing delight on soul not body (1:140a–b).

## Grounding from Pritzker Zohar Volume III (fidelity reingest 2026-07-18)

Attributed to the Zohar body as rendered in Matt, *The Zohar: Pritzker Edition*, Volume III (Va-Yishlaḥ through Va-Yeḥi; standard pagination roughly Zohar 1:166a–251a).

### Joseph as Righteous One / foundation of the world / Zion

In Va-Yeshev the Zohar restates the Prov 10:25 architecture with Zion as the cosmic point:

> "All of these stand upon one rung… called Righteous… *The righteous one is the foundation of the world*… **Zion**, potency and point of the entire world… Zion and Jerusalem—one, compassion; one, judgment." (Zohar 1:186a; Pritzker III)

*"These are the generations of Jacob: Joseph"* is read as the sun–moon coupling that generates offspring only through the rung called Righteous One: "only that rung called Righteous One bears fruit; Joseph is the rung of Jacob bearing fruit" (1:180a). Joseph’s image matches Jacob’s (*vav* single mystery); through all Joseph’s days judgment does not befall; at his death exile begins (1:180a). After guarding the sealed covenant (Potiphar), he is called Righteous and crowned with Holy Covenant; elevated "from pit to well of living waters" (1:194b). Every river pertains to Joseph the Righteous; Pharaoh’s Nile dream is "a dream of Joseph" (1:193b–194a).

### Life of the Worlds; single pillar; sun nourishing moon

Mi-Qets equates the Living One with Yesod:

> "*YHVH lives*—Living One, Righteous One, Foundation of the World, called **Vitality of the Worlds**." (Zohar 1:194a; Pritzker III)

Va-Yiggash: world established on a **single pillar named Righteous** who waters the garden (Gen 2:10 river from Eden); Joseph cannot restrain himself before those poised to be nourished by him (1:208a). *Joseph harnessed his chariot* = Righteous One meets Israel in Goshen in single convergence; Jacob’s *"you are still alive"* = Joseph abided by "mystery of holy Covenant, called **Life of the Worlds**" (1:211a–b). As governor, Joseph is "sun governing moon, illumining her, nourishing her" (1:198a); *Avrekh* is the link linking sun with moon (1:196b–197a). Va-Yishlaḥ: Joseph "Righteous" illumines the moon more than all; equals all for David’s seventy years (1:168b); prayer-crown of the congregation settles on the head of Righteous One, Vitality of the Worlds (1:167b).

### Souls make the cistern a well; inherit the Matronita

Va-Yeḥi densifies the theurgic function of the perfected soul:

> When the soul is perfected and ascends, desire of the female toward the male; **cistern becomes a bubbling well**; by the soul of the righteous that place is perfected; love/passion above join as one. (Zohar 1:235b zone; Pritzker III)

Opening of Va-Yeḥi (Isa 60:21): the supernal heritage of the land is possessed only by one called righteous, "because **the Matronita cleaves to him, becoming fragrant, and the righteous one inherits the Matronita**, surely!" Circumcised and guarding the covenant, one "enters this **Righteous One**… inherit the land forever" (1:216b). *He blessed et Joseph*: *et* = sign of covenant Joseph guarded → called Righteous; Living Elohim flows Jacob → place/covenant (1:227b–228a). Head of the bed = Foundation of the World / Righteous One; bed = Shekhinah / Assembly of Israel (1:226a–b). Blessings of the father surpass → all to Joseph/Yesod: Righteous One absorbs all blessings, becomes river from Eden (1:247a–b). Double *aron*: Joseph preserved covenant below and Covenant above → coffin + ark of the covenant (1:251a).

### Covenant guarding; shield of the generation

Va-Yeshev restates Isa 57:1: while a righteous person dwells, judgment cannot dominate the generation; through Joseph (image of his father) judgment delayed (1:180a). Guarding the holy covenant = rain of favor into Assembly of Israel; heaven and earth stand on foundational covenant (Jer 33:25) (1:189b). Joseph as "supernal covenant": when he vanishes, covenant, Shekhinah, and Israel plunge into exile (1:184a). Righteous One and Righteousness never part (1:182b). Hand under thigh = "Righteous One, mystery of circumcision, sustaining pillar of worlds… to revive dwellers of the dust" (1:181b).

## Grounding from Pritzker Zohar Volume IV (fidelity reingest 2026-07-18)

Attributed to the Zohar body as rendered in Matt, *The Zohar: Pritzker Edition*, Volume IV (Exodus cycle). Source: [[matt-zohar-pritzker-vol4|Pritzker Zohar Volume IV]].

### Joseph the Righteous; Pillar loses flow in exile (Shemot–Va-Era)

New king “did not know Joseph” — issued from place of separation; Joseph = “place where all union abides, called **Righteous**” (2:10a). Isa 57:1 / Eccles 7:15: when the moon reigned, the **righteous one** = Pillar of the World *oved/avad* in exile because **She lies in the dust** — righteousness accompanies Israel in exile, blessings do not reach Him (2:11a). Land inheritance: only the circumcised inherit; “only a **righteous one** inherits… *Your people, all of them righteous*… Joseph… was not called righteous until he guarded that covenant… **Joseph the Righteous**” (2:23a). Covenant named **peace of the home** conducts paths into the Great Sea when agitated (2:31b).

### Righteous perish; Bundle of Life; midnight Garden (Shemot, Bo)

Early death of the righteous: sins of generation; or prevent future decay (treated as wicked) (2:10b–11a). Souls of the righteous fragrance in the garden; spirit from female as seal; soul from Tree of Life in bundle of life (2:11a–11b). Bo: God delights with the righteous in the Garden of Eden at midnight; Bundle of Life; do not separate from the multitude lest the designated virtuous be seized first (2:33b–34a, 2:37a). Righteous One as gardener sowing concealed light as seed in the Garden (2:35a).

### Manna: scions of faith; crown of Ḥesed; future food of the righteous (Be-Shallaḥ)

Hierarchy of food after Israel cleaves by **holy insignia** (circumcision): *matstsah* → bread **from heaven really** → Companions from Wisdom (2:62a). Scions of faith request food daily even if they have it so the tree of nourishment is blessed; daily requester = **son of faith** (2:62b). Before Torah, manna distinguished scions of faith (marked with **insignia of the crown of Ḥesed**) from non-scions deprived of that crown (2:62b). *A righteous one eats to satisfy his soul* (Prov 13:25) (2:62b). Scions gather manna with Garden fragrance, gaze supernal Wisdom → **Generation of Knowledge** to whom Torah is given (2:63a). Future: “From this manna the righteous are destined to eat in the world that is coming… *to gaze upon the beauty of YHVH*” (2:63b). Poor closer to the King (soul over body); guardian formed above for one who enlightens the weak (2:61b).

### Amalek war: righteous sons; Youth; Moses’ holy hands (Be-Shallaḥ)

Joshua to choose “**righteous sons of righteous ones**” for battle below (2:66a). Moses’ hands *kevedim* = glorious, venerable, holy, **never defiled** — worthy of war above (2:66a). Altar *YHVH Nissi*: vengeance for the holy insignia / covenant (2:66b).

### Physiognomy frame: *zeh sefer* and Righteous One (Yitro)

Gen 5:1 *zeh sefer* = two rungs in one, totality of male and female; Book of Remembrance; **Righteous One** (*zeh*) (2:70a–70b). Converts of Righteousness (*ger tsedeq*) under wings of Shekhinah at the mountain of Elohim (2:70a).
