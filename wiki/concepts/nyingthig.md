# Nyingthig (Heart-Essence)

**Nyingthig** (Tibetan *snying-thig*, "Innermost Spirituality" / "Heart-Essence") is the most
essential body of pith-instructions of the **Esoteric Instructional Class** of
[[dzogchen|Atiyoga]] — the [[nyingma|Nyingma]] tradition's contemplative core, containing the
khregs-chod and thod-rgal teachings.

Two streams descend from [[padmasambhava|Padmasambhava]] and Vimalamitra; they were integrated by
**Longchen Rabjampa** (1308–63), the great systematiser who also read the Guhyagarbha (and hence
the [[peaceful-and-wrathful-deities|deity maṇḍala]] of the [[bardo-thodol|Tibetan Book of the
Dead]]) from the Dzogchen standpoint. The best-known later recension is the *Klong-chen
snying-thig* ("Innermost Spirituality of Longchenpa"), revealed as [[terma|treasure]] by Rigdzin
Jigme Lingpa in the 18th century.
