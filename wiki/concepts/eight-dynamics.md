# The Eight Dynamics

The **Eight Dynamics** are [[scientology|Scientology's]] model of existence as a set of expanding "urges
toward survival," laid out in [[scientology-fundamentals-of-thought|*The Fundamentals of Thought*]]. All
life, Hubbard taught, is driven by the single command **"Survive!"**, and that drive expresses itself
across **eight widening spheres**:

1. **Self** — survival as an individual.
2. **Sex / Family** — procreation, children, the family.
3. **Group** — survival through community, society, organizations.
4. **Mankind** — the survival of the human species as a whole.
5. **Life Forms** — all living things (animals, plants).
6. **Physical Universe** — matter, energy, space, and time ("MEST").
7. **Spiritual** — survival as [[thetan|spirit]], the [[thetan|thetan]] itself.
8. **Infinity / Supreme Being** — the urge toward infinity, often equated with God or the "Allness of
   all."

## Function in the System

The dynamics are Scientology's framework for **ethics** and **well-being**: the optimum solution to any
problem is the one that brings the **greatest survival across the most dynamics**, and a healthy person is
one "in" on all eight. The eighth dynamic is Scientology's main reference to the divine — notably **left
largely open** ("the Supreme Being," the urge toward infinity), which is part of why Scientology presents
itself as compatible with belief in God while saying little doctrinally about one.

## Cross-Tradition Note

The minimal, open **eighth dynamic** is a striking contrast with the theistic traditions in the wiki: where
the Abrahamic faiths *begin* from God, Scientology places "the Supreme Being / infinity" at the **far end**
of a survival hierarchy and leaves its content to the individual — a near-**non-theistic** structure (like
[[buddhism|Buddhism]]) dressed in optionally theistic language.
