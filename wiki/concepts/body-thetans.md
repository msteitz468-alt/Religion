# Body Thetans (BTs)

In [[scientology|Scientology]] theology, a **body thetan** (BT) is "a thetan who is stuck to another thetan or body but is not in control" (L. Ron Hubbard, HCOB 5 Feb 1970, "Definitions, Sect. III," in [[scientology-ot-levels|Technical Bulletins Vol. XIV]]). A **cluster** is "a group of body thetans crushed or held together by some mutual bad experience" (ibid.).

## Theological Role

Body thetans constitute the central problem addressed by **OT 3** and above on the [[operating-thetan|Bridge to Total Freedom]]. Every human being is understood, at this level of Scientology doctrine, to be not merely a single [[thetan|thetan]] but a **composite**: one's own thetan plus large numbers of accumulated BTs and clusters attached to the body and mind. These accumulated entities contribute to illness, irrational behavior, and spiritual suppression.

"Body thetans just hold one back. You will continue to be you. You, inside, can of course separate out body thetans and so solo auditing is the answer." (HCOB 4 May 1968, "Character of Body Thetans")

BTs themselves are described as ordinary thetans in a degraded, confused state: "When you get rid of one he goes off and possibly squares around, picks up a body or admires daisies. He is in fact a sort of cleared being." They are not intrinsically evil entities but thetans that have been asleep, disoriented, or stuck for "the last 75 million years" — since the events of Incident II (see [[operating-thetan|Operating Thetan]]).

## Origin

Body thetans derive from the **Xenu/Incident II event** (~75 million years ago): billions of beings were brought to Earth (Teegeeack), destroyed with hydrogen bombs near volcanoes, and their disembodied spirits were gathered and implanted with false R6 memories before being scattered. Some became stuck to bodies or other thetans, accumulating over intervening millennia. See [[operating-thetan|Operating Thetan]] for the full cosmological account and Incident II parameters.

## Auditing Procedure

BTs are addressed via **solo auditing**: the OT 3 practitioner identifies BTs (using an **E-meter**) and runs them through two incidents:
- **Incident II**: ~75 million years ago (the R6 volcano implant)
- **Incident I**: ~4 quadrillion years ago (a universal incident shared by all thetans)

When successfully "blown," a BT departs from the practitioner's aura/body. The OT practitioner must avoid the "free wheel" error (running Incident II on a BT without completing Incident I on the same BT), which Hubbard says can cause the practitioner to run Incident II continuously until the body dies.

**NOTs (New Era Dianetics for OTs)** — OT 5–7 in the current Bridge — addresses a distinct population of BTs: dormant, confused, or wrongly-identified BTs that OT 3 procedures did not handle. NOTs constitutes the bulk of advanced Scientology auditing content.

## Soteriological Significance

The BT doctrine represents Scientology's **esoteric anthropology**: what appears to be one self is actually a multitude of confused spirits. The OT practitioner's goal is to progressively separate from these accumulated entities to recover full thetan sovereignty. This has been compared to:
- **Gnostic pneumatology**: the spark of light imprisoned in matter, requiring liberation
- **Spiritism/Theosophy**: astral shells or attached entities draining the living
- **Buddhist aggregates** (skhandas): the self as composite rather than unitary

Scholars (Harvey Urban, Hugh Urban) have noted that the BT doctrine represents a radical inversion of mainstream Western religion: rather than a single soul interacting with one God, the practitioner discovers he is a "composite being" whose "case" includes thousands of other beings.

## Extended BT Doctrine (from NOTs Series)

The NOTs HCOBs significantly expand the BT doctrine:

- **Auras**: "An aura actually is a collection of BTs and clusters mocking up the aura — and sticking in it — as it is a highly electronic mock up." Auras have no independent spiritual status (NOTs Series 33).
- **Attraction of attention**: BTs generate somatics as a mechanism to attract attention. "The reason why acknowledgement works is because an acknowledgement is attention. Pictures are a trick to get attention." This explains the therapeutic mechanism of the E-meter/auditor interaction (NOTs Series 33).
- **Universal fear**: "There are two fundamental things that all beings have in common... they are afraid they will become cells and molecules" — a fear of downward-spiral into unconscious matter (NOTs Series 38).
- **Collective identity**: BTs can hold collective identities ("I'm the army," "I'm us") that prevent blowing. The "We are all one" / monist worldview is explicitly framed as "an implant on the track" — a deliberately instilled aberration rather than a spiritual truth (NOTs Series 37).
- **Language barriers**: BTs can have misunderstood words and may not speak English; auditors must communicate with them "conceptually" (NOTs Series 46).
- **Generational transfer**: "BTs can be PTS to successive family members across generations: this transfer of BTs to successive family members causes a genetic misidentification — it convinces BTs that they are genetic." Apparent genetic inheritance of traits is attributed to this mechanism (NOTs Series 35).
- **Physical illness**: The NOTs Series explicitly attributes deafness, blindness, catatonia, cancer, chronic somatics, and body shape distortions to BT/cluster activity. "Illness is attributed to BTs 'being' disease states."
- **"False body"**: "The body's perceived solidity is BTs/clusters in body-shape; actual biological body is 'transparent' to an OT" (NOTs Series 15).

## Controversy

The BT doctrine was the center of legal battles when OT materials were publicly disclosed in the 1990s (see [[operating-thetan|Operating Thetan]] for legal history). Critics argue it creates an endless, monetized therapeutic demand: as BTs are blown, new ones are discovered, extending the therapeutic process indefinitely. Former members have described the OT levels as a "revolving door" that generates continuous fee revenue.
