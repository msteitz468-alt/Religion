# Engram (and the Reactive Mind)

An **engram** is, in [[dianetics|Dianetics]] and [[scientology|Scientology]], a complete mental recording of
a moment of **physical pain and (partial or full) unconsciousness** — together with everything perceived at
that moment. Engrams are stored not in the rational **analytical mind** but in the **reactive mind**, a
stimulus-response store that operates below awareness.

## The Doctrine

When something in the present resembles the content of an engram (a word, a sound, a smell present during
the original injury), the reactive mind **replays** it, imposing irrational fears, compulsions, pains, and
psychosomatic illness. [[dianetics|Dianetics]] claims engrams begin even **prenatally** and accumulate
across a being's [[thetan|many lifetimes]]. The reactive mind is thus the hidden source of human suffering
and aberration.

## Function in the System

The whole purpose of **[[auditing|auditing]]** is to locate and **discharge ("erase") engrams**, freeing the
person from the reactive mind. One who has fully cleared it is a **"Clear."** The engram/reactive-mind model
is the engine of the entire Scientology path: it defines the problem that auditing and the
[[auditing|Bridge]] exist to solve.

## Reception

Mainstream psychology rejects the engram model as unfounded (the term "engram" is borrowed from earlier
memory theory but used idiosyncratically). The wiki's secondary sources treat it critically. Recorded
without adjudication.
