# Infinite Atonement (LDS)

The **infinite atonement** is the Book of Mormon and D&C teaching that Christ's suffering and resurrection answer the Fall and sin without limit of time or person (except sons of perdition in D&C 76).

## Key Loci

- **2 Nephi 9** — infinite atonement or flesh cannot put on incorruption; monster of death and hell; "O how great the plan of our God."
- **Alma 34** — Amulek: atonement must be infinite and eternal; not a human sacrifice; mercy cannot rob justice.
- **Alma 42** — justice and mercy; God would cease to be God if justice destroyed; plan of mercy through the atonement of the Only Begotten.
- **Mosiah 3–4** (Benjamin) — blood atones for those who die not knowing; natural man; apply the atoning blood.
- **D&C 19** — suffering caused the greatest of all to tremble and bleed at every pore.
- **3 Nephi 11, 27** — Christ crucified to draw all to judgment; wash garments in his blood.
