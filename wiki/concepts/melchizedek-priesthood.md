# Melchizedek Priesthood

The **Melchizedek Priesthood** is the higher priesthood in LDS order — "after the Order of the Son of God," named for Melchizedek to avoid too frequent use of the divine name (D&C 107).

## Textual Anchors

- Promised in JS-H via Peter, James, and John holding the keys.
- **D&C 84** — oath and covenant of the priesthood; receive the Father's fulness; church under condemnation regarding the new covenant (Book of Mormon).
- **D&C 107** — structure of quorums; First Presidency; equality of quorum authority in their spheres; Adamic lineage; Adam-ondi-Ahman.
- **Alma 13** — holy order, foreordination, Melchizedek as type (BoM language later read through D&C categories).
