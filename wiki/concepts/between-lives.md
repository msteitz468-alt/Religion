# Between-Lives

In [[scientology|Scientology]] doctrine, **Between-Lives** (also written "between-lives") refers to the period between the death of one MEST body and the [[thetan|thetan]]'s entry into a new body. It is not a rest state or a transition of neutral character; it is described in *A History of Man* (1952) as an **active control and forgetting operation** run by invader forces.

## The Mechanics

At death, the thetan leaves the MEST body and travels to a **Between-Lives area**. Here, according to Hubbard, the following sequence occurs:

1. The thetan **"reports in"** (compelled, not voluntary)
2. It is seated before a **spinning wheel** of pictures — generalized still images of vacant lots, houses, backyards — which pass before it
3. A **force screen** hits it through the pictures
4. A **mirror arrangement** shows the thetan that it is still seated before the pictures
5. The combined effect is: (a) erasing the sense of past-life identity; (b) flatteneing the thetan's vitality; (c) installing a deep **forgetter**

The forgetter is not simply information removal — it operates by force alone, using the mirror/screen apparatus to convince the thetan that it has no past life, is no longer the same identity, and has had its memory erased.

## Mars and Other Report Stations

Hubbard's account is specific about location:

> "The report area for most has been Mars. Some women report to stations elsewhere in the solar system. There are occasional incidents about Earth report stations. The last Martian report station on Earth was established in the Pyrenees."

This is among the most specific of Hubbard's cosmological claims and has been widely cited by critics.

## Why Humans Cannot Recall Past Lives

The between-lives implant is Hubbard's explanation for why humans have no conscious access to prior incarnations. Without it, the thetan would naturally recall its past track. The implant does not permanently erase memory — the memories are still in the facsimile bank — but installs a forceful inhibition that prevents access. [[auditing|Auditing]] through the Between-Lives incidents restores past-life recall.

As the implant keys in gradually through a lifetime, it begins to engulf childhood memories, then progressively later and later periods. Eventually, in old age, the preclear begins to "cycle through it automatically and go into a 'second childhood'" — anticipating the next death and the implant cycle.

## Between-Lives and Multiple Invader Crews

*A History of Man* notes that Earth's between-lives control structure may reflect a **layered** system of competing invader operations: an older invader crew already operating a between-lives station, and newer invader crews "planting" beings into the same area (e.g., via the [[history-of-man#theta-traps|Ice Cube]]). The newer crew's carefully transported beings then fall into the older crew's between-lives routine, unbeknownst to anyone.

## The Permanent Forgetter Mechanism

Hubbard identifies a secondary mechanism: humans' tendency to accept the forgetter is not purely due to the implant. There are "natural causes" that the implant magnifies:

- The preclear **identifies itself as a MEST body** and others as MEST bodies
- It would prefer, having many unresolved overt acts, to start fresh with a clean slate
- The MEST-body orientation itself predisposes acceptance of the next body's life as primary reality

## Contrast with Other Traditions

The Scientology between-lives doctrine is sharply opposed to both **Buddhist** and **Hindu** accounts of rebirth:

- In Buddhist tradition, rebirth is governed by karma and the three marks of existence; there is no invader force administering a forgetter
- In Hindu tradition (particularly Vedantic thought), past lives are in principle accessible through spiritual practice (yoga, meditation)
- Hubbard explicitly rejects the Hindu/yogic approach: "India and 'join Nirvana' have given us 'techniques' WHICH ARE GUARANTEED TO GLUE A THETAN TO A BODY AS THOUGH RIVETED AND TIED WITH IRON BANDS."

The between-lives doctrine is uniquely Hubbard's: past-life amnesia is not a natural feature of consciousness but an artificial imposition, correctable only through E-Meter auditing.
