# Plan of Salvation

The **plan of salvation** (also plan of redemption, plan of happiness, plan of mercy) is the LDS systematic story of God's purposes for humanity from premortal life through exaltation.

## Arc (as synthesized from the reingested texts)

1. **Premortal** — intelligences / spirit children; council; agency; first estate (Abr 3; Moses 4).
2. **Creation and Fall** — earth as proving ground; Fall enables children, joy, knowledge of good and evil (2 Ne 2; Moses 5).
3. **Mortal probation** — free to choose liberty through the Mediator or captivity through the devil (2 Ne 2:27); commandments and ordinances.
4. **Atonement** — infinite and eternal (Alma 34; 2 Ne 9); Christ suffers for all (2 Ne 9:21); justice and mercy meet (Alma 42).
5. **Death and intermediate state** — paradise / spirit prison (Alma 40; D&C 138).
6. **Resurrection and judgment** — all resurrected; graded inheritances ([[degrees-of-glory|degrees of glory]], D&C 76).
7. **Exaltation** — celestial fullness, eternal marriage, eternal increase ([[exaltation-lds|exaltation]]).

Moses 1:39 states the purpose: "to bring to pass the immortality and eternal life of man."
