# Temple-Dwelling Monks (Caityavasis)

The *caityavasis* ("temple-dwellers") were medieval [[svetambara|Svetambara]] monks who, in departure
from the wandering-mendicant ideal, took up **permanent residence in temples** and managed their wealth,
endowments, and ritual life.

## The Reform Reaction (Dundas)

[[dundas-the-jains|Dundas]] (ch. 5) presents the temple-dwellers as the foil against which the great
Svetambara ascetic-lineage (*gaccha*) reforms defined themselves. The main objection was that settled,
property-administering monks violated the [[mahavrata|Great Vow]] of non-possession and the discipline of
homeless wandering. The reform [[kharatara-gaccha|Kharatara Gaccha]] in particular built its identity on
opposition to the *caityavasis*, asserting a return to strict mendicant rules. The [[digambara|Digambara]]
analogue is the institution of the clothed cleric-administrator, the [[bhattaraka|bhattaraka]].
