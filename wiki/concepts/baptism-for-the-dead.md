# Baptism for the Dead

**Baptism for the dead** is the LDS temple ordinance of vicarious immersion baptism on behalf of deceased persons, systematically taught in **D&C 124, 127–128** and illustrated in **D&C 138**.

## Textual Teaching

- Introduced in Nauvoo instructions (D&C 124); expanded in Joseph's 1842 letters (§127–128).
- Recorder system: eyewitness minutes; general church book; earthly records answer to heavenly books of judgment (Rev 20:12 midrash).
- Font as similitude of the grave; immersion likeness of death and resurrection.
- **Welding link** (Malachi 4 / Elijah): "their salvation is necessary and essential to our salvation… neither can we without our dead be made perfect" (D&C 128:15, 18).
- 1 Corinthians 15:29 cited as warrant (128:16).
- D&C 138 — spirit-world preaching and ordinance work for the dead.

## Significance

Extends the necessity of baptism (AoF 4; 3 Ne 11) beyond mortality; structures modern LDS temple and family-history practice.
