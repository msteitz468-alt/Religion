# The Hole

**"The Hole"** is the informal name for a set of double-wide trailers at [[gold-base|Gold Base]] (Gilman Hot Springs, California) used by [[david-miscavige|David Miscavige]] to confine senior [[church-of-scientology|Church of Scientology]] executives — members of the **Watchdog Committee** and other high-level staff — for extended periods ranging from months to years.

## Description (Wright's Account)

Based on testimony from multiple former detainees, including Tom De Vocht, Amy Scobee, Mike Rinder, Mariette Lindstein, Marc Headley, and Marty Rathbun:

- **Population**: Approximately 40–70 senior executives at peak confinement (c. 2004–2010)
- **Conditions**: No furniture (executives slept on the floor); ant-infested; hosed-down showers; inadequate food
- **"Séances"**: Daily sessions of forced public confession — detainees were required to confess to perceived failures, sabotage, and "crimes" against Scientology in front of the group; designed to extract admissions and humiliate
- **No freedom of movement**: Detainees were not free to leave and were subject to recapture if they attempted to do so
- The **"musical chairs" incident** (c. 2004): Miscavige ordered the entire group to play musical chairs to Queen's *Greatest Hits*; the loser would be permanently expelled to a worse assignment

## Key Individuals Confined

Wright names among those held in the Hole at various times: Mike Rinder, Marty Rathbun, Tom De Vocht, Amy Scobee, Mariette Lindstein, Debbie Cook, and dozens of others who held senior positions in the Church's international management structure.

Debbie Cook — who for 17 years had overseen operations at the Clearwater (Flag) base and brought in over $1.7 billion — testified in a 2012 court proceeding that she had been subjected to the Hole's conditions.

## Institutional Function

The Hole represents the extreme end of [[sea-org|Sea Org]] disciplinary culture. Below it on the formal hierarchy is the **Rehabilitation Project Force (RPF)** (punitive labor); the Hole was an informal, extra-canonical detention structure with no basis in Hubbard's published policies. Marty Rathbun describes it as Miscavige's personal instrument of control over the Church's administrative hierarchy.

## The Church's Position

The Church denied to multiple journalists that the Hole existed as described. After Debbie Cook's sworn testimony in 2012, the Church settled with her (reportedly paying a significant sum) and she agreed to cease speaking about the Church.

**Contradiction Protocol**: All positions recorded without adjudication.
