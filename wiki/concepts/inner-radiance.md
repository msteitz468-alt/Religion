# Inner Radiance (Clear Light)

**Inner radiance** (Tibetan *'od-gsal*, Sanskrit *prabhāsvara*; often "clear light") is the
**subtlest level of mind** — "the fundamental, essential nature of all our cognitive events." It
is ever-present but normally hidden by gross mental activity; it becomes manifest when the gross
mind ceases — naturally at the moment of death, or through advanced
[[dzogchen|Dzogchen]]/perfection-stage practice. In Dzogchen all states of consciousness are held
to be pervaded by inner radiance "just as a sesame seed is permeated by oil."

## Mother and Child

The [[bardo-thodol|Tibetan Book of the Dead]] turns on a "mother and child" schema:

- **Mother inner radiance** (*'od-gsal ma*) — the ground radiance that arises *naturally* at
  death, immediately after respiration ceases; it indicates the [[trikaya|dharmakāya]].
- **Child inner radiance** (*'od-gsal bu*) — the recognition of mind's nature *cultivated during
  life* by meditation.

"Buddhahood is achieved when the mother and child inner radiances conjoin" — the recognition
trained in life meets the radiance dawning at death, "like a stream meeting the main river." This
is the supreme opportunity of the [[bardo|bardo of the time of death]]; recognised, "just to
recognise this is enough."
