# OT Levels — Level-by-Level Overview

A level-by-level reference to the **Operating Thetan (OT) Levels** of [[scientology|Scientology]], drawn from [[scientology-ot-levels|Technical Bulletins Vol. XIV: The O.T. Levels]] (compiled c. 1995). Each level is a confidential **rundown** on the **Bridge to Total Freedom** above the state of [[concepts/clear|Clear]]. Access requires prior level completion and substantial fees; the full Bridge reportedly costs a minimum of $300,000–$400,000.

The OT levels exist in two parallel sequences: the **Original OT levels** (OT 1–8, developed 1966–1969) and the **New OT levels** (OT 1–8, the current Church sequence replacing most of the originals from the late 1970s onward). Both sequences appear in this volume.

**Textual limitation:** The actual GPM item lists for OT 2 and the Clearing Course are blank pages in this volume (withheld). The core handwritten Xenu "Data" document (OT 3) and the Incident I/II platens were distributed separately to students and are not reproduced as text here. The New OT 8 material is a Free Zone practitioner's memory reconstruction, not an original LRH document.

---

## The Clear State (Prerequisite)

**What it teaches:** "Clear" is not an OT level but its prerequisite. The confidential technical definition (HCOB 22 Sep 1966; HCOB 24 Sep 1978R): **"CLEAR OCCURS WHEN ONE STOPS MOCKING UP BANK, OR REALIZES THAT HE IS MOCKING IT UP OR HAS BEEN MOCKING IT UP."** Clear is described as "a negative gain" — erasure of the reactive mind. It applies only to the **1st Dynamic** (self): "you are clear on the 1st dynamic and still have a lot of work in front of you to attain OT."

The "cognition" experienced at Clear is itself contested: see [[clear-cognition-vs-implant-theory|Clear Cognition vs. Implant Theory]] for the controversy over whether the bank is self-generated or externally implanted.

**EP:** Cognition that one has been mocking up (self-generating) one's own reactive mind.

---

## OT 1 (New)

**What it teaches:** Orientation to the OT levels; confirmation that the Clear state is genuine. The practitioner performs **drills assessing other people at a distance** — an initial exercise in perceiving without direct physical contact. This level introduces the premise that a thetan, once freed from the reactive mind at Clear, can begin extending awareness beyond the immediate body.

**EP:** "Big win, persistent F/N and cognition."

---

## OT 2 / The Clearing Course

**What it teaches:** The reactive mind, Hubbard argues at this level, is structured not merely as individual traumatic engrams but as interlocking **Goals Problems Masses (GPMs)** — sequences of opposed goals that create irresolvable internal conflict. The primary goal (e.g., "to help") generates an automatic opposition goal ("not to help"), and further sub-goals in dialectical sequence, producing a "mass" of spiritual/mental weight that suppresses the practitioner's causativeness.

The critical claim: these GPM sequences are **not the product of individual life experience** but were **artificially implanted at a cosmic scale** during the **R6 implant** approximately **75 million years ago** — the same event as Incident II (see OT 3). "All CC and OT II materials are in R6 75 million years ago." (HCOB 2 Oct 1968) This makes the reactive mind's deep structure not personal but *universal* — the same patterns shared by all thetans who passed through this region of the galaxy.

The specific GPM sequences presented (named: **Electrical GPM**, **Tocky GPM**, **Big Being GPM**, **House GPM**, **Psycho GPM**, **Banky GPM**, **Forerunner GPM**, **Basic Basic GPM**, **Basic GPM**, **LP GPM**, **Body GPM**, and others) run through hundreds of pages of specific "item" dichotomies to be audited in exact order using the E-meter until each reaches a floating needle.

**Textual note:** The actual item lists (the specific dichotomies for each GPM) appear on blank pages in this volume — they were withheld from the compilation. Only the structural descriptions and names were captured.

**EP:** Erasure of the GPM charge; readiness for OT 3. See [[goals-problems-mass|Goals Problems Mass (GPMs)]] for full treatment.

## OT 3 (New; also Original OT 3)

**What it teaches:** The most notorious OT level and the theological center of Scientology cosmology. OT 3 introduces the doctrine of **[[body-thetans|body thetans]] (BTs)**: the practitioner learns that he is not a single thetan but a **composite being** — his own thetan plus enormous numbers of other thetans ("body thetans") stuck to the body and mind, who must be individually audited and released.

The cosmological framework (**the "Wall of Fire" materials**):
- **~75 million years ago**: **Xenu (or Xemu)**, ruler of the Galactic Confederacy, faced an overpopulation crisis. He rounded up billions of beings from 76 planets, transported them to Earth (then called **Teegeeack**), placed them near volcanoes, and destroyed them with hydrogen bombs. Their disembodied thetans were gathered, implanted with false memories via the **R6 implant** (including the images underlying all major world religions), and scattered.
- **~4 quadrillion years ago** (**Incident I**): A separate, universal incident shared by "all thetans in the Universe" and by beings on "21 nearby stars." It predates the Xenu event and is addressed alongside Incident II in the auditing procedure.

The BT auditing procedure requires the solo practitioner to identify BTs using the E-meter and run each through both incidents in sequence:
1. **Incident II** (~75 million years ago): the R6 volcano implant
2. **Incident I** (~4 quadrillion years ago): the universal incident

**The free-wheel warning** (HCOB 2 Oct 1968): "If the volcano bit is run as per III directions but the Inc I is not run on same thetan, R6 begins to run off on automatic, the being can't sleep for days, the body dies. That's the way it was designed." This claim — that the materials are lethal to the unprepared — is Hubbard's theological justification for the confidentiality of OT 3. The warning also binds the practitioner to strict procedural compliance.

**Note on sources:** The core handwritten "Data" document (the LRH-authored Xenu narrative proper), the Incident II and Incident I sequence platens, and the List of Volcanoes were distributed separately to students as physical documents. They are **not reproduced as text** in this volume; their parameters are extensively discussed in the HCOBs, but the narrative itself is only alluded to here.

**EP:** "No BTs left = Exterior" (1970 formulation); any "nice win" acceptable (1981 revision). Practitioners with "endless IIIs" — who cannot reach this EP — are routed to NOTs (OT 5–7).

See [[body-thetans|Body Thetans (BTs)]] for full treatment.

## OT 4

**Original OT 4 — Drug Rundown for OTs:**
Addresses the residue of drug use on the OT case — specifically, **BTs and clusters that are stuck in drug experiences** or have adopted drug-induced valences. Includes the **Valence Shifter**, a procedure for addressing entities locked into foreign identities.

- **EP (Solo component):** "Certainty of Self as a Being"
- **EP (Audited component):** Exteriorization

**New OT 4:** Continuation of OT 4 procedures within the current Church Bridge; the distinction from New OT 3 is primarily in the specific BT populations and techniques addressed.

---

## OT 5

**Original OT 5 — MEST Drills:**
Addresses the practitioner's ability to operate as an exterior thetan on **MEST** (Matter, Energy, Space, Time). The drills involve direct perception of and interaction with the physical universe while exterior to the body.

**EP:** Cognition and/or exteriorization on MEST drills.

**New OT 5 — NOTs Prerequisites:**
Serves as the entry point to the NOTs (New Era Dianetics for OTs) series. Establishes the diagnostic and procedural groundwork for the large-scale BT work to follow in OT 6–7.

---

## OT 6 / NOTs (New Era Dianetics for OTs)

**What it teaches:** The largest section of the volume (~8,500 lines; roughly 60% of the total corpus). NOTs addresses a population of BTs **distinct from those handled in OT 3**: dormant, confused, mis-identified, or collectively entangled BTs that OT 3's Incident I/II procedure did not resolve.

Key NOTs doctrines (from the NOTs Series HCOBs):

- **Valence technique**: BTs can hold collective or stolen identities ("I'm the army," "I'm us") that prevent them from blowing. The practitioner must address the BT's misidentified selfhood directly. The "we are all one" / monist worldview is explicitly labeled "an implant on the track" — a deliberately instilled aberration (NOTs Series 37).
- **Auras**: "An aura actually is a collection of BTs and clusters mocking up the aura — and sticking in it." Auras have no independent spiritual status (NOTs Series 33).
- **Somatics as attention-seeking**: BTs generate physical sensations and body pains as a mechanism to attract the practitioner's attention. "The reason why acknowledgement works is because an acknowledgement is attention. Pictures are a trick to get attention." (NOTs Series 33)
- **Universal BT fears**: "There are two fundamental things that all beings have in common... they are afraid they will become cells and molecules" — a fear of downward-spiral into unconscious matter (NOTs Series 38).
- **Language and conceptual communication**: BTs can have misunderstood words; they may not speak English. The practitioner must sometimes communicate with them "conceptually" (NOTs Series 46).
- **Generational transfer**: "BTs can be PTS to successive family members across generations." Apparent genetic inheritance of traits (physical, behavioral) is attributed to BTs misidentifying themselves as genetic material and transferring across family lines (NOTs Series 35).
- **Physical illness**: The NOTs Series explicitly attributes deafness, blindness, catatonia, cancer, chronic physical ailments, and body shape distortions to BT/cluster activity. "Illness is attributed to BTs 'being' disease states."
- **The false body**: "The body's perceived solidity is BTs/clusters in body-shape; actual biological body is 'transparent' to an OT" (NOTs Series 15).

**EP (New OT 7):** "When the Pre-OT has a transparent body and a clear area around it to some distance… and when he realized he is alive and very much himself the EP of NED for OTs has been reached. When this EP is reached then one will have achieved **'CAUSE OVER LIFE.'**"

## OT 7

**Original OT 7 — Projecting Intention:**
Structurally different from all other OT levels — this is not primarily a BT-removal rundown but a practice in **intentional telepathy**. The practitioner progresses through a graduated sequence of exercises:

1. In-session exercises ("Give me an unknown datum" — practiced within the auditing room)
2. Outdoor processes involving placing thoughts and intentions into actual strangers: "Practice placing an intention into individuals until you can successfully and easily place an intention into or on a being and/or a body."
3. Communication with plants and animals: processes continue "until you know they received your communication."

This makes OT 7 the level where claimed telepathic and intention-projection abilities are explicitly **practiced and tested** on external beings rather than on one's own mental content.

**EP:** "Rehabilitation of ability to project intention."

**New OT 7 — Solo NOTs Continuation:**
The current Church's OT 7 is simply an extended solo continuation of the NOTs process — further BT auditing toward the NOTs EP of transparent body and "Cause Over Life." (The Original OT 7 intentionality exercises were removed from the standard Bridge.)

## OT 8

**Original OT 8 (HCOB 1 Oct 1969, "SECRET" — the authentic LRH document):**
Addresses what Hubbard calls "the most plaguing question in the history of Clearing": **why do thetans compulsively create mental pictures (engrams) of bad experiences?**

Hubbard's answer locates the compulsion in a fundamental metaphysical principle about thetan psychology and **havingness**:

> "A thetan never totally gives up." When forced from a location in space, rather than abandoning it, a thetan refuses — and **mocks it up mentally**, creating a picture of that location. "This, unwittingly, gives him a picture, an engram."

Hubbard's revised definition of an engram: **"An engram therefore is a refusal to leave a place at which force was exerted to drive one away."**

Power is correspondingly redefined: **"Power is the ability to hold a position in space."**

Practical corollary: engrams can be blown without running them through beginning/middle/end (the lower-Bridge R3R technique) simply by identifying and spotting the original location ("What point (location) is unsafe?"). Hubbard notes: "Working with this you will see a door open to a higher level than Dianetic R3R. But realize that it is only for a high level thetan."

This doctrine represents a departure from lower-Bridge engram theory and reframes spiritual bondage not as traumatic content but as a metaphysical **refusal to release a position in space**.

---

**New OT 8 (Free Zone debrief, 1991 — a secondary/reconstruction source):**
Explicitly identified in this volume as a **debrief written from memory by a Free Zone practitioner** who attended the OT 8 delivery on the *Freewinds* ship. **Not an LRH-authored original.** The practitioner explicitly breaks Church security ("I am breaking security as I disagree that this should only be released to an elite").

The debrief describes OT 8 as addressing **"what truly is the relationship of the Supreme Being to each individual thetan"** — making it explicitly theological in a way the Original OT 8 is not. Preparatory processes review one's relationship to "MEST and form and life units" and one's "true source and history from its origin as the theta body, the true 8th Dynamic."

## Special Rundowns (Above or Alongside the Standard Bridge)

### L10 — Havingness Rundown
**EP:** "Freedom from self-restraint."

Addresses **havingness** — the practitioner's ability to hold, own, and sustain conditions and objects in existence. The **8th Dynamic Rundown** within L10 lists conventional religious concepts (God, prayer, gospels, sacred texts, hell, wrath of God, sutras, hymns, idols, faith, devil, miracles, paradise, sacrifice, angels) as **items to be assessed on the E-meter** and any charge erased — conventional religious beliefs are treated as potential aberrations on the case requiring clearing.

### L11 — New Life Rundown
**EP:** "A New Life."

Addresses **doingness** — restoring the practitioner's capacity to act, pursue, and accomplish. Developed from Hubbard's research into the levels above OT 8, implying further OT levels beyond 8 exist.

### L12 — Flag OT Executive Rundown
**EP:** "Certainty and unshakability as a being."

Addresses **beingness** — the practitioner's ability to occupy and hold an identity without suppression. The **L12 Character List** catalogues archetypal past-life identities (valences) to be located, assessed on the E-meter, and erased:

- **"THE ALMIGHTY"** category: God, A Goddess, The Buddha, Pan, Diana, The Father of All, The Horned God
- **"THE EVIL ONE"** category: The Antichrist, The Devil, His Satanic Majesty

Divine and demonic archetypes are treated as **whole-track case items** — accumulated past-life identities requiring clearing, not as transcendent realities. This directly conflicts with Super Power's apophatic theology; see [[scientology-god-8th-dynamic|Scientology and the 8th Dynamic]].

**Note on provenance:** The L Rundowns in this volume are explicitly identified as **reconstructions by former Church members** who delivered them before leaving the Church — not original documents from Flag Land Base. Accuracy described as high but unverified against originals.

### New Vitality Rundown
Addresses **revivication** — reanimating suppressed aspects of the thetan. A shorter, preparatory rundown.

### Bright Think Rundown
Addresses **bright-think** — the practitioner's ability to think clearly without suppression from BTs or accumulated case. Also a revivication process.

### Super Power Rundown
A five-series rundown addressing existential conditions and the **8th Dynamic (God/Infinity)**. The most theologically ambitious of the Special Rundowns.

**Series 2** contains Scientology's most striking **apophatic statement**: "God is not an object. When one labels god with an identity one immediately degrades the 8th dynamic into MEST terms. The 8th dynamic cannot be defined in terms of MEST. It can only be defined in terms of infinity which the number 8 symbolizes."

**Series 5 ("Actual Super Power Process")**: Commands include "Get the idea that you are infinite." The claimed EP is **ecstatic laughter, speechlessness, exterior state, and floating TA "up to an hour."**

**Note:** The widely reported claim that Super Power addresses "57 perceptions" does **not** appear in the Super Power materials as reproduced in this volume.

**Theological tension:** Super Power's apophatic/infinite God directly contradicts L12's treatment of God and Buddha as auditable past-life valences. Both positions appear in the same compilation without reconciliation. See [[scientology-god-8th-dynamic|Scientology and the 8th Dynamic (Controversy)]].

## Hermeneutical Note

The OT levels exemplify Scientology's defining hermeneutic: **verbatim technical application**. No passage is to be interpreted, adapted, or modified. The **"Keeping Scientology Working" policy** (HCO PL 7 Feb 1965) — reproduced in the OT 2 section of this volume — forbids any deviation. The graded, confidential access structure means *which texts one may read* is determined by Bridge position — a **gated revelation** model in which the cosmological core (Xenu, BTs, GPMs) is available only after years of preparatory auditing and payment.

This is Scientology's hermeneutical self-understanding: these are **technical instructions**, not myths or symbols, and their power depends on exact application in exact sequence.
