# Word of Wisdom

The **Word of Wisdom** is the LDS health code revealed as **D&C 89** (27 February 1833, Kirtland).

## Textual Content (§89)

- Given "not by commandment or constraint, but by revelation and the word of wisdom."
- Warnings: wine and strong drink (except sacrament wine of own make); tobacco; hot drinks.
- Positive counsel: herbs, fruit, grain (staff of life); flesh sparingly; grain for beasts.
- Promise: health, wisdom, run and not be weary; the **destroying angel** shall pass by as the children of Israel.

## Reception Note

Later LDS practice treats the Word of Wisdom as binding (temple recommend standard), though the 1833 text says "not by commandment." That shift is reception history beyond the verse — flagged, not flattened.
