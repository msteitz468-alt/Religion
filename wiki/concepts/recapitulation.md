# Recapitulation (*Anakephalaiōsis*)

Recapitulation is the soteriological theory, classically developed by [[irenaeus]], that the incarnate Word "gathers together, includes or comprises the whole of reality in Himself, the human race being included," and so reverses in his own person the fall of Adam. The term is *anakephalaiōsis*, drawn from Eph. 1:10 ("to sum up all things in Christ"), with roots also in Rom. 5:12–21 and 1 Cor. 15:22, 45 (see [[paul-apostle|Paul]]). Drawn from [[kelly-early-christian-doctrines|J.N.D. Kelly]], ch. VII.

## Irenaeus's Theory

Christ is "the second Adam" (*ho deuteros Adam*), recapitulating the first "even in the manner of His birth, being generated from the Blessed Virgin as he was from virgin earth." He "recapitulated in Himself the long sequence of mankind, and passed through all the stages of human life, sanctifying each in turn." The hinge is obedience reversing disobedience: "in obliterating the disobedience of man originally enacted on the tree, He became obedient unto death, even the death on the cross, healing the disobedience enacted on a tree by obedience on a tree." The governing formula: **"Because of His measureless love, He became what we are in order to enable us to become what He is"** — an early statement of the exchange that becomes [[apotheosis-divinization|deification]].

Kelly cautions it is "a dangerous half-truth" to reduce Irenaeus to a purely physical/incarnational theory: he equally stresses Christ's blood as "our ransom" and the cross as "the supreme and necessary expression of His obedience" (see [[ransom-to-whom]]). [[justin-martyr|Justin]] is an anticipator (Irenaeus attributes the idea to him).

## Later Echoes

[[hippolytus]]: the Word was born of the Virgin "to restore and recapitulate in Himself the original Adam," conquering death by his death. **[[methodius|Methodius of Olympus]]** retained a weakened form — Christ as new Adam — but "almost completely overlooks the positive significance of Christ's death," stressing instead that the Lord's humanity "disclosed the resurrection of the flesh."
