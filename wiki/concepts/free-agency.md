# Free Agency

**Free agency** (agency) is the LDS doctrine that humans are agents unto themselves, free to choose, and that God will not destroy that agency — a reason Satan was cast down (Moses 4:3).

## Textual Anchors

- **2 Nephi 2** — opposition in all things; enticement by good and evil; free to choose liberty/eternal life or captivity/death.
- **Moses 3:17** — at the tree: choose for thyself, yet I forbid.
- **Moses 4:3** — Satan sought to destroy the agency of man.
- **Moses 7:32** — agency given in Eden; God weeps when humans use it for violence.
- **D&C 58:26–29** — not meet that the Lord command in all things; anxiously engaged of free will.
- **Helaman 14:30–31** — Samuel: free according to the flesh; choose life or death.
- **Alma 41–42** — restoration law and justice require that choice and consequence align.
