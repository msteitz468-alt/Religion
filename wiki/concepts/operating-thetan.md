# Operating Thetan (OT Levels)

An **Operating Thetan** (OT) is, in [[scientology|Scientology]], a [[thetan|thetan]] that has achieved the ability to "operate independently of the body" — capable of acting causally on matter, energy, space, time, and life without a physical body. It represents the advanced stages of the **Bridge to Total Freedom**, beyond the [[auditing|auditing]] path that leads to [[concepts/clear|Clear]].

## Definitions

**Clear** (confidential technical definition): "CLEAR OCCURS WHEN ONE STOPS MOCKING UP BANK, OR REALIZES THAT HE IS MOCKING IT UP OR HAS BEEN MOCKING IT UP." Clear is "a negative gain" (erasure) and applies only to the 1st Dynamic: "you are clear on the 1st dynamic and still have a lot of work in front of you to attain OT." (HCOB 22 Sep 1966; HCOB 24 Sep 1978R)

**Operating Thetan** (definition): "A THETAN WHO CAN BE AT CAUSE KNOWINGLY AND AT WILL OVER THOUGHT, LIFE, MATTER, ENERGY, SPACE AND TIME, SUBJECTIVE AND OBJECTIVE." — differs from Clear by the addition of "Thought, Life" and "Subjective." (HCOB 22 Sep 1966) "The width between wog and Clear is very great. The width between Clear and OT is a bit wider."

## Structure

The **OT levels** are a graduated series of confidential rundowns above the state of Clear. Scientology does not publicly disclose their content; access requires completing earlier levels, paying substantial fees (the full Bridge reportedly costs a minimum of $300,000–$400,000), and meeting the Church's "ethical" standards.

From July 1966 onward, [[l-ron-hubbard|Hubbard]] began developing these materials. He stated they were "potentially fatal to the unprepared" — justifying their confidentiality on soteriological grounds.

## OT III and the Xenu Narrative

The most notorious confidential level is **OT III**, which contains the so-called "**Wall of Fire**" material — the central cosmological myth of Scientology that the Church neither confirms nor publicizes.

As disclosed via court records (Wollersheim case, 1985; Fishman affidavit, 1993; Netherlands Supreme Court, 2005), OT III teaches:

> **75 million years ago**, the ruler of the **Galactic Confederacy**, **Xenu (or Xemu)**, faced an overpopulation problem. He rounded up billions of beings, brought them to Earth (then called "Teegeeack"), placed them near volcanoes, and destroyed them with hydrogen bombs. Their disembodied spirits ("**body thetans**") were gathered, implanted with false memories via "R6 implants" (including the images underlying all major world religions), and scattered. These body thetans now cling to living humans, causing spiritual and psychological damage. The OT III practitioner must identify and "audit out" these accumulated body thetans.

The same narrative appears in Hubbard's *Revolt in the Stars* screenplay (1977), which he attempted to market as a Hollywood film.

**OT VIII** — the highest publicly acknowledged level — reportedly contains material claiming Jesus was "a lover of young boys" and identifies Hubbard as both the Buddha Maitreya *and* the "anti-Christ/Lucifer." The Church has called the distributed OT VIII text a "forgery"; critics note it was submitted as evidence in the Fishman federal case.

## Legal Battles Over Secrecy

The OT materials became the subject of repeated legal battles throughout the 1990s–2000s:

- **Los Angeles Times** (1985): published OT materials obtained from the Wollersheim case
- **FACTNet** (1993–95): Colorado federal court ruled OT materials are **fair use**, not trade secrets; seizure of FACTNet's computers was illegal (Judge Kane, 1995)
- **Fishman affidavit** (1993): 69 pages of OT materials entered as court exhibits in a mail fraud trial; the Church initially claimed copyright, then called them forgeries
- **Netherlands Supreme Court** (December 2005): OT materials are legal to publish (Karin Spaink case)
- **Google / Xenu.net** (2002): Scientology demanded Google remove the site; chilling effects notice remains publicly visible
- **Wikipedia ban** (May 2009): Wikipedia voted 10-0 to ban all Scientology IP addresses for editing after documented sock-puppeting about OT materials

Urban (2011) notes that by 2010, a Google search on "OT III" produced 46,300 hits and "Xenu" produced 891,000 — Scientology's information control strategy has largely failed in the Internet age.

## Theological Significance

The OT levels represent Scientology's **esoteric core** — the secret doctrine that justifies the graded, paid-access structure of the Bridge. The secrecy is theologically motivated (materials can harm the unprepared) but also commercially structured (members must purchase access level by level). This combination is what Gerry Armstrong called a "secret-selling company," and what critics like Brill and Packard argued violates the purpose of copyright law: "one must buy into the church doctrine literally and figuratively before being allowed to actually read it."

## OT 7 — Projecting Intention

The **Original OT 7** is one of Scientology's most structurally unusual levels: it is not primarily a BT-removal rundown but a practice in **intentional telepathy**. The practitioner progresses from in-session exercises to outdoor processes involving placing thoughts into actual strangers: "Practice placing an intention into individuals until you can successfully and easily place an intention into or on a being and/or a body." Processes also involve communicating with plants and animals until "you know they received your communication." This makes OT 7 the level where claimed telepathic and intention-projection abilities are explicitly practiced and tested.

## OT 8 — Original

The original OT 8 HCOB (1 Oct 1969, SECRET, "Why Thetans Mock Up") answers what Hubbard calls "the most plaguing question in history of Clearing." His argument: "a thetan never totally gives up" — when forced from a location in space, it refuses to abandon it and instead **mocks up that location** mentally, creating a picture (engram) of it. "An engram therefore is a refusal to leave a place at which force was exerted to drive one away." Power is "the ability to hold a position in space." The practical implication: engrams can be blown without running them by identifying and spotting the original location ("What point (location) is unsafe?"). This doctrine is distinct from lower-Bridge engram theory (which runs incidents through a beginning, middle, end) and is described as "only for a high level thetan."

## Scientology and the 8th Dynamic (God/Infinity)

The OT levels present two theologically contradictory positions on the 8th Dynamic (God/infinity):

1. **Apophatic** (Super Power Series 2): "God is not an object. When one labels god with an identity one immediately degrades the 8th dynamic into MEST terms. The 8th dynamic cannot be defined in terms of MEST. It can only be defined in terms of infinity which the number 8 symbolizes."

2. **Auditable past-life identities** (L12 Character List): God, the Buddha, and the Devil (along with other divine/demonic archetypes) appear as items in a list of **past-life valences** to be located, assessed, and erased. They are treated as case mass accumulated across lifetimes, not as transcendent realities.

Both positions appear in the same volume without reconciliation. See [[scientology-god-8th-dynamic|Scientology and the 8th Dynamic]] for the controversy.
