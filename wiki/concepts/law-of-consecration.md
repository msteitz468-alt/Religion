# Law of Consecration

The **law of consecration** is the early LDS economic order revealed especially in **D&C 42, 51, 78, 82, 104**: Saints consecrate property to the church, receive stewardships according to needs and wants, and place surplus in the bishop's storehouse so that the poor are cared for and members are "equal in earthly things."

## Key Notes from the Text

- "If ye are not one ye are not mine" (D&C 38:27).
- Equal according to families, circumstances, wants and needs (D&C 51).
- United order / everlasting bond; breakers face buffetings of Satan (D&C 78, 82).
- "I, the Lord, am bound when ye do what I say" (D&C 82:10).
- Later practice largely replaced by **tithing** (D&C 119–120) as the standing law of surplus for the mainline church — reception history.
