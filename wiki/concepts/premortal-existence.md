# Premortal Existence

**Premortal existence** (pre-existence) is the LDS doctrine that human spirits lived as intelligences or spirit children of God before mortal birth.

## Textual Anchors

- **Abraham 3:18–28** — eternal intelligences; God more intelligent than they all; noble and great ones chosen before birth; first and second estates; "Whom shall I send?"
- **Moses 4:1–4** — heavenly council; Satan seeks honor and to destroy agency; Beloved Son chosen.
- **Moses 6:51** — God made "men before they were in the flesh."
- **D&C 93** — intelligence was not created or made; cannot be created.
- **D&C 138:53–56** — noble and great ones (including modern prophets) prepared in the spirit world before birth.
- **Alma 13** — foreordination language for the holy order (read by LDS as premortal).

## Significance

Underwrites agency, theodicy of mortality as a proving ground, and the council-in-heaven narrative that frames the Fall and redemption. Distinctive relative to most historic Christian anthropologies that begin human existence at conception/birth.
