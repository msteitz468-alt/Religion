# The Book of Judges (NICOT) — Barry G. Webb

## What the source is

Barry G. Webb's *The Book of Judges* (2012) in the **New International Commentary on the Old Testament** (NICOT; Eerdmans). Webb (Moore Theological College, Sydney) writes an emotionally warm, narrative-critical and canonical exposition with an original Hebrew translation. The volume updates and expands his 1987 Sheffield PhD monograph. Stance: Judges is “not a nice book… rough and raw and confronting”; he aims to “let Judges be what it is instead of taming it.”

## Hermeneutical frame

Webb reads Judges **as both literature and Scripture**: New Literary Criticism (Alter tradition) + Canonical Criticism (Childs). Modified DH accepted as control; final form primary. Early-date lean; basic historicity defended. Dialogue with feminist, liberation, redaction, and ideological criticism.

**Load-bearing moves (verified):** downward spiral not flat cycle; no “rest” after Gideon; Canaanization (Block); epilogues integral; kingship need+danger; Samson = Israel; Jephthah vow = shrewd bribe + **literal sacrifice**; violence taxonomy; women both/and (Ackerman).

## Volume / work synthesis

Webb’s NICOT *Judges* is the wiki’s densest narrative-critical treatment of a Former Prophets book: warm tone, integrated reading from the 1987 monograph expanded to even-handed commentary thoroughness. The controlling literary-theological claim is the **downward spiral** — not a flat sin cycle — with Gideon as hinge (last “rest”; Moses→Aaron), Samson as Israel personified, and the dual epilogue as integral religious-then-moral chaos under the “no king / right in own eyes” refrain.

On formation, Webb accepts modified DH scaffolding while insisting the end-text is a coherent prophetic narrative. On ethics, he neither tames violence nor reduces the book to ideology: liberation war, vendetta, and civil war are distinguished; *ḥerem* is engaged canonically. On Jephthah, the re-read **corrects** a prior wiki claim: Webb is firm that the daughter was **literally sacrificed**; the vow is a calculating bribe, not an open virginity alternative.

**What this adds:** source page + coverage ledger; verbatim densification of method, spiral, Gideon hinge, Samson=Israel, epilogue integrity; one hard fidelity correction (Jephthah vow). **Tensions:** Woudstra’s anti-DH Joshua reading vs Webb’s modified DH; feminist and liberation interlocutors stay as positions.
