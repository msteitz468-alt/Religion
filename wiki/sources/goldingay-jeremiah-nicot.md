## Hermeneutical frame

Attributed to Goldingay (Intro):

1. **Three horizons** — delivery contexts; 604 Baruch scroll; post-587 expansion to final form not before 562 (near-final ~550s–540s). Persian “fourth horizon” rejected as primary.
2. **Gospels analogy** — MT/LXX like multi-Gospel situation; 604 ≈ Mark, LXX ≈ Matthew, MT ≈ Luke-Acts; all authentic.
3. **Curators and storytellers** — not “Deuteronomists”; refuse authentic/inauthentic as value judgment on material.
4. **Focus on received presentation** — poetics over genetics.
5. **Both-and theology** — judgment and hope, flexibility and finality, “which time it is.”
6. **Embodied prophet / imaged theology** — word enters body; rāʿâ as dire action/experience; pledge (*bərît*) language.

## Volume synthesis

Goldingay’s Jeremiah is a long, unified reading of the scroll as the work of a poet-persuader
mediated by curators who keep delivering Yahweh’s word after the city falls. Compositionally,
the **three horizons** and **Gospels analogy** allow him to affirm both historical Jeremiah and
post-587 growth without subordinating MT or LXX. Structurally, he treats **ch. 25 as the
central hinge/axis** (604, scroll-burning as point of no return, MT/LXX OAN placement
divergence) — a clear foil to Lalleman’s preference for a 23–29 true/false-prophecy centre.

Theologically, the commentary’s peak is the **Book of Consolation** and the **new pledge**
(31:31–34): Goldingay translates *bərît* as “pledge,” stresses husband/*bāʿal* Sinai language,
tôrâ written on the mind, and leaves Yahweh’s transforming action and Israel’s turning in
unresolved mutual relation (// Deut 30). Climax of disaster narrative: Jehoiakim’s column-by-
column burning of the scroll (36) “nails the coffin shut.” Closure (52) frames with ch. 1:
calamity short of annihilation, hope short of full renewal, Jehoiachin’s release as glimmer.

**Tensions with Lalleman TOTC (not adjudicated):** ch. 25 hinge vs 23–29 centre; Goldingay’s
more open compositional horizons vs Lalleman’s tighter 627–587 frame; “pledge” diction vs
standard “covenant.” Both affirm 604 scroll-burning as decisive.
