## What the source is

Volume VI **completes the Zohar’s commentary on Exodus** (Matt preface). Four parashot:

1. **Parashat Tetsavveh** (Exod 27:20–30:10) — ~2:180a–187b.
2. **Parashat Ki Tissa** (Exod 30:11–34:35) — census, **Golden Calf**, intercession (~2:188a–194b).
3. **Parashat Va-Yaqhel** (Exod 35:1–38:20) — Sabbath re-gift, offerings, construction theurgy (~2:195a–220a).
4. **Parashat Pequdei** (Exod 38:21–40:38) — reckonings, erection, glory fills (~2:220b–244b).

## Volume / work synthesis

Volume VI closes the Zohar’s Exodus as **rupture and provisional restoration**. After Tetsavveh’s Moses–Shekhinah and letter theurgy, Ki Tissa stages the Calf as a **sin against Mother**: *Elohim* = Glory of Israel exchanged for the bull-form garment of the Accuser; the mixed multitude (Egyptian sorcerers) catalyze the demand; Mother who usually holds the King’s arm is absent, so Moses seizes right arm, left arm, and body at Exod 32:10 until the King cannot move. The Tent moves outside the camp; death-time is rationed.

Va-Yaqhel rebuilds community without the mixed multitude’s full share, re-gifts Sabbath, and densifies Sabbath and Dwelling theurgy (with *Berikh shemeih* as later liturgical insertion). Pequdei’s reckonings culminate in the craft/erection split: **Bezalel fashions by letter-permutations; Moses alone erects** by the intention of creation’s letters. Glory fills as bridal arrayal (Exod 40:35); dual Dwelling rises; death and Samael are **withdrawn** on 1 Nisan — not abolished forever until the Messiah swallows death. Other Side still receives a regulated share so as not to accuse.

What this reingest **adds** is folio-anchored fidelity for the Calf–erection arc first filed 2026-06-07 without ledgers, plus recognition that the volume body includes **Tetsavveh** and that restoration is explicitly temporary relative to final theodicy.

Tensions held without flattening: Zoharic Calf as Mother-rejection vs plain-sense / evangelical readings of idolatry and mediation; Bezalel’s Spirit-filled craft vs Moses-only erection; *Berikh shemeih* popular liturgy vs MS absence; Dwelling’s provisional victory over death vs messianic forever (Isa 25:8).
