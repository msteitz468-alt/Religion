# The Complete Dead Sea Scrolls in English
**Geza Vermes** | Penguin Classics, 7th/revised ed., 2012 (first published 1962) | ISBN: 9780141197326

## Coverage Ledger
Read in full: lines 1–22830 (complete text including Introduction, Sections A–I, Appendix/Scroll Catalogue, Bibliography, and Endnotes). No portion deliberately omitted.

---

## Source Identity
A complete English translation of the **non-biblical Qumran scrolls** (those not already preserved in the canonical Hebrew Bible), with Introduction, section notes, and bibliography. The translation covers manuscripts from all eleven Qumran caves. Biblical manuscripts found at Qumran are noted but not translated, as standard critical editions exist. Also includes the Copper Scroll (3Q15) and the two Hebrew ostraca found in 1996.

This is Vermes's definitive personal translation, revised multiple times between 1962 and 2011. His Introduction represents his mature scholarly position on community identity, history, and theology.

## Overview and Scholarly Position

### The Essene Identification
Vermes argues confidently that the Qumran community is **the Essenes** — the identification he regards as decisively supported by classical sources (Josephus, Philo, Pliny) and internal scroll evidence. The community settled at Qumran during the reign of Jonathan Maccabaeus (153–143 BCE) after separating from a Jerusalem Temple they regarded as defiled under illegitimate priests. Rival theories (Zealot origin; Jerusalem library; country villa) are reviewed and rejected.

### The Teacher of Righteousness
An anonymous priestly figure called the **Teacher of Righteousness** (*Moreh ha-Tzedek*) founded the settled community. Vermes proposes he was a **Zadokite priest** displaced by Hasmonaean usurpation of the high priesthood. His key characteristics per the scrolls: God revealed to him all prophetic mysteries; he led the community into the wilderness; he was persecuted by the **Wicked Priest** (= Jonathan Maccabaeus, per Vermes), who attacked him on the Day of Atonement; he was slandered by the **Liar / Spouter of Lies**, a rival teacher.

### Historical Chronology
- ~160–150 BCE: Formation of proto-community ("groping for twenty years" per CD)
- ~150 BCE: Arrival of the Teacher of Righteousness
- ~153–143 BCE: Jonathan Maccabaeus = the Wicked Priest (first arose with good reputation, then apostatized)
- ~150–125 BCE: King Jonathan (4Q448) = Jonathan Maccabaeus, early career
- Kittim throughout = Romans (worship military standards; cavalry greaves identifiable with Julius Caesar)
- 68 CE: Scrolls hidden as Roman troops advance; community ends

## Key Texts Covered

### Section A: Community Texts (Sectarian)
- **Community Rule (1QS)**: Covenant renewal ceremony; entry procedures; penal code; Two Spirits doctrine; predestinarianism; solar calendar community
- **Damascus Document (CD + 4QD)**: History of the community ("plant of Aaron"); Wicked Priest; Laws for camp communities; marriage; purity; sabbath; eschatological judge
- **Messianic Rule (1QSa)**: Messianic banquet protocol; the two Messiahs (Aaron + Israel)
- **War Scroll (1QM)**: Eschatological 40-year war of Sons of Light vs. Sons of Darkness; seven battle phases; liturgical warfare
- **MMT (4Q394-9)**: Halakhic letter to Jerusalem leadership; 20+ disputed legal rulings; "deeds of the Law" language; canon reference (Torah, Prophets, David's psalms)
- **Temple Scroll (11QT)**: Longest Qumran scroll; rewritten Torah for ideal Temple and Jerusalem; 500-cubit Temple; purity laws; treason penalty = hanging alive (crucifixion)
- **Blessings (1QSb)**: Ritual blessings for Messiah of Aaron and Messiah of Israel; Isaiah 11 language for Davidic Messiah

### Section B: Hymns, Poems, Wisdom
- **Thanksgiving Hymns (1QH)**: Two cycles — Hymns of the Teacher (intense personal suffering, divine revelation) and Hymns of the Community. Teacher's hymns: divine birth metaphor; "city of refuge"; "wonderful counsellor"; "covenant" formed from the womb
- **Non-canonical Psalms (4Q380-1)**: Apocryphal Psalms attributed to Obadiah, Zedekiah
- **Apocryphal Psalms (11Q5 col. XXVII)**: Catalogue of David's 4,050 compositions; four exorcism Psalms
- **Lamentations (4Q179, 4Q501)**
- **Angelic Liturgy / Shirot Olat HaShabbat (4Q400-407, 11Q17, Masada)**: 13 songs for first 13 Sabbaths of year; descriptions of seven heavenly sanctuaries and angelic priests; Merkabah chariot description; cherubim/ophanim; "whispered divine voice" — earliest post-biblical Merkabah mysticism

### Section C: Calendrical, Liturgical, and Miscellaneous
- **Calendrical Documents (4Q319-330, 337)**: 364-day solar year; 52-week perfect cycle; Passover always Wednesday; full six-year Mishmarot tables; Otot 294-year Jubilee cycle
- **Astrological Physiognomies (4Q186, 4Q534, 4Q561)**: Light/darkness fractions predict spiritual quality from physical features
- **Words of the Heavenly Lights (4Q504-6)**: Daily prayers; recounts Exodus, covenant, exile; Israel as "first-born son"; "Thy Holy Spirit upon us"
- **King Jonathan Psalm (4Q448)**: Vermes = Jonathan Maccabaeus (not Alexander Jannaeus); early career
- **Glorification Hymns (4Q491/471b)**: "I am reckoned with the 'gods'"; Isaiah 53 allusions; eschatological high priest exalted to heavenly realm
- **Benedictions/Curses of Belial (4Q286-90)**: **Melkiresha'** named as specific Satanic name; "Amen, amen" liturgical format

### Section D: Historical and Apocalyptic
- **Historical Texts C-E (4Q331-3)**: Names Shelamzion, Hyrcanus, Kittim, Aemilius (Scaurus)
- **Messianic Apocalypse (4Q521)**: Singular Messiah; "He will heal the wounded, and revive the dead and bring good news to the poor" (Isa. 61:1); most explicit resurrection language in Qumran; parallel to Gospel tradition

### Section E: Wisdom
- **Sapiential Work A (4Q415-18, 423)**: *Raz nihyeh* ("approaching mystery"); garden of Eden typology; Korah warning
- **Songs of the Sage (4Q510-11)**: **Lilith** named among demons; exorcism function; "bastards, howlers, yelpers"
- **Beatitudes (4Q525)**: Structural parallel to Sermon on the Mount (Matthew 5) without reward clause

### Section F: Bible Interpretation (Pesharim and Related)
- **Targum of Job (11Q10)**: Oldest Aramaic Job; "sons of God" → "angels"
- **Genesis Apocryphon (1QapGen)**: Aramaic; Sarah beauty ekphrasis; Abraham's prayer against Pharaoh; Melchizedek = "Priest of the Most High God"
- **Genesis Commentary (4Q252)**: "Branch of David" + covenant of kingship; Flood adapted to 364-day calendar
- **Isaiah Commentary (4Q161-5)**: Branch of David at end of days; Interpreter of the Law; Isaiah 11
- **Nahum Commentary (4Q169)**: Only pesher naming historical figures (Demetrius, Antiochus); crucifixion as treason penalty; "furious young lion" = Alexander Jannaeus
- **Habakkuk Commentary (1QpHab)**: Kittim = Romans; Teacher = one to whom God revealed all mysteries; Wicked Priest pursues Teacher on Day of Atonement; "righteous by faith" = faithfulness to Teacher; Lebanon = Council of the Community
- **Commentary on Psalms (4Q171, 173)**: Teacher = "Priest whom God chose"; "congregation of the Poor"; Wicked Priest sought to kill Teacher
- **Florilegium (4Q174)**: Community = "Sanctuary of men" where works of the Law replace incense; Branch of David + Interpreter of the Law at end of days
- **Testimonia (4Q175)**: Four proof-texts — Prophet like Moses; Star of Jacob; Blessing of Levi; Cursed Man of Belial
- **Heavenly Prince Melchizedek (11Q13)**: Melchizedek = *elohim* / Michael; releases captives at end of 10th Jubilee on Day of Atonement; Isa. 61:1 liberation; Belial as counterpart

### Section G: Apocryphal Works
- **Jubilees fragments (4Q216-28)**: Hebrew original confirmed; 4Q219 corrects Ethiopic; **4Q225** = earliest pre-Christian attestation of Isaac's willing self-sacrifice (Akedah)
- **Book of Enoch (4Q201-12)**: Names 20 fallen angel chiefs (Shemihazah leader; Asael/Azazel); no Book of Parables; astronomical text more developed than Ethiopic; Noah birth narrative
- **Book of Giants (4Q530-3)**: Gilgamesh named; giants' dreams sent to Enoch; Manichaean parallel
- **Ages of Creation (4Q180)**: 70 weeks from Noah to Abraham; Azazel interpretation
- **Book of Noah (4Q534-6)**: Probably Noah nativity horoscope; miraculous birth
- **Testament of Levi (4Q213-14, 1Q21)**: Levi's prayer; Jacob ordains Levi; "priest of the everlasting God"
- **4Q541 (eschatological priestly figure)**: "He will atone for all the sons of his generation"; slandered by contemporaries — probable archetype for Teacher of Righteousness / Christian Servant figure
- **Testaments of Qahat (4Q542) and Amram (4Q543-9)**: Amram's vision of two Watchers — **Melkiresha'** explicitly named as "Belial, Prince of Darkness" (three names); light-ruler (likely Melchizedek) as counterpart
- **Tobit (4Q196-200)**: Confirms Aramaic original behind the long Greek recension (Codex Sinaiticus)

### Section H: Miscellanea
- **New Jerusalem (4Q554-5, 5Q15, etc.)**: Aramaic; detailed measurements of eschatological city; 12 tribal gates; Ezek. 40-48 and Rev. 21 background
- **Pseudo-Ezekiel (4Q385-388)**: Dry bones vision; Chariot/Merkabah
- **Prayer of Nabonidus (4Q242)**: Nabonidus (not Nebuchadnezzar as in Dan. 4) healed by Jewish exorcist who forgives his sins; probably older than Daniel
- **Aramaic Apocalypse / "Son of God" fragment (4Q246)**: "Son of God… son of the Most High" language paralleling Luke 1:32,35; Vermes: the last historico-apocalyptic sovereign who proclaims himself divine (like Antiochus Epiphanes), not the Davidic Messiah; eternal kingdom belongs to "people of God"
- **Copper Scroll (3Q15)**: 64 treasure hiding places; 65 tons silver + 26 tons gold; Vermes: probably real but ascription uncertain; dry, business-like style argues against fiction; copper medium likewise
- **Two Qumran Ostraca (1996)**: Property transfer with possible reference to oath-fulfillment to "Yahad" (Community)

## Hermeneutical Framework (Qumran)
The Qumran community's interpretive method is **pesher** — prophetic texts are treated as fulfilled in the community's own immediate history. Five forms of biblical commentary identified:
1. Reworked Pentateuch (4Q158, 364-7)
2. Expanded narrative (Genesis Apocryphon type)
3. Free compositions inspired by Bible
4. Pesher (prophetic fulfillment)
5. Parabiblical texts

The hermeneutical claim: "God made known all the mysteries of the words of His servants the Prophets" *to the Teacher of Righteousness*. The Teacher is the authoritative interpreter whose community lives out prophetic fulfillment.

## Key Theological Doctrines

- **Two Spirits / Predestinarianism**: All humanity divided from creation into Sons of Light (allotted to God and the Prince of Light) and Sons of Darkness (allotted to Belial/Mastema). Fate is determined before birth. Eschatological reversal at end of days.
- **Two Messiahs**: Messiah of Aaron (priestly, takes precedence at the banquet) + Messiah of Israel (Davidic/royal). Sometimes a third figure: the Prophet (Deut. 18:15).
- **Solar Calendar**: 364-day year; Passover always Wednesday; conflict with lunar Temple calendar is a cause of separation.
- **Sanctuary of Men**: Physical Temple replaced by the community itself; "works of the Law" replace sacrificial incense.
- **Cosmic Dualism**: Belial / Mastema / Melkiresha' = the Prince of Darkness and his angels vs. the Prince of Light (Melchizedek / Michael) and Sons of Light.

## Relationship to New Testament and Early Christianity
Vermes notes numerous parallels without asserting direct dependence:
- **4Q521** (resurrection + good news to the poor) parallels Matthew 11:5 / Luke 7:22 and messianic expectation
- **4Q246** ("son of God / son of the Most High") parallels Lukan Annunciation language exactly
- **Beatitudes (4Q525)** parallel Matthew 5 structurally
- **Two Spirits doctrine** underlies Johannine light/darkness dualism and Pauline flesh/spirit antithesis
- **Sanctuary of Men (4Q174)** parallels New Testament "spiritual temple" ecclesiology (1 Cor. 3:16; Eph. 2:21)
- **11Q13 Melchizedek** parallels Hebrews 7's Melchizedek Christology but with angelic rather than incarnate character
- **Glorification Hymns (4Q491/471b)** parallel the heavenly exaltation language applied to Christ
