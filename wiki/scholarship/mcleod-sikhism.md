# Sikhism — W. H. McLeod (Penguin, 1997)

A concise scholarly introduction to the Sikh tradition by **W. H. (Hew) McLeod** (1932–2009),
the foremost Western historian of Sikhism, trained at SOAS, longtime historian at the University
of Otago. The book is the **scaffolding source** for the wiki's Sikhism tradition: it surveys the
whole field — history of the Gurus and the Panth, doctrine, the Khalsa, worship, scripture, sects,
and Sikh society — and closes with translations of the core daily liturgy.

**Source:** `raw/scholarship/Sikhism (McLeod, W. H.) (z-library.sk, 1lib.sk, z-lib.sk).txt` (OCR'd text
of the Penguin edition; 372 pp.; filed into `raw/scholarship/` 2026-06-27 after ingest).

## Structure (book contents)

- **Part One — History:** (1) The Early Gurus; (2) The Developing Panth; (3) The Later Gurus;
  (4) The Growth and Expansion of the Panth, 1708 to the present.
- **Part Two — Religion:** (5) Sikh Doctrine; (6) Khalsa, Rahit and the Fight for Justice;
  (7) Worship, Ritual and Distinctive Customs; (8) Sacred Writings of the Sikhs; (9) Sikh Sects.
- **Part Three — Society:** (10) Defining a Sikh; (11) Features of Sikh Society; (12) Sikhism and
  Gender; (13) Sikhs of the Diaspora; (14) The Ultimate Authority.
- **Appendix:** translations of the scriptures — the early-morning order (Japji, Jap, Ten Savayyas),
  the evening prayer (Sodar Rahiras), Kirtan Sohila, and Ardas (the Khalsa prayer).
