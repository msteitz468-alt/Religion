## Volume / work synthesis

Volume IX is the **closing arc of the main Torah commentary**. Shelaḥ’s spies dramatize faith versus Other Side giants and the Generation of the Desert as Garden souls; *Rav Metivta* maps Heavenly Academies and the tripartite soul. Qoraḥ and Ḥuqqat densify right/left priesthood and the red heifer as Judgment theurgy. Balaq stages Balaam’s bridled mouth, the rascally *Yanuqa*, and the Peki’in child’s revival of his father. Pineḥas crowns Phinehas with peace and gilgul repair. Deuteronomy’s Shema and song prepare the land; *Idra Zuta* stages Rashbi’s death as *hillula* with Shekhinah — the literary counterpart to Moses’ death-song.

What this reingest **adds** is folio-anchored fidelity for material first filed 2026-06-07 without ledgers; corrected placement of Metivta/Yanuqa in Numbers rather than as Deuteronomy units; densification of *Idra Zuta* on [[shimon-bar-yochai|Shim’on bar Yoḥai]] alongside Vol. VIII’s *Idra Rabba*; and Metivta soul anatomy on [[zoharic-afterlife|Zoharic Afterlife]].

Tensions held: bodies-die/souls-Garden vs plain Numbers decree; Idra Zuta in running Torah commentary (Vol. IX) vs compositions volume (Vol. XI); Matt’s end of translation vs full Pritzker I–XII.
