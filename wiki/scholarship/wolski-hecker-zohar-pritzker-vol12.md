## What the source is

Volume XII completes the Pritzker Edition’s presentation of the independent **“Zoharic Compositions”** — non-running-commentary esoteric strata begun in Vol. XI (Hecker). Units: two *Heikhalot* versions, *Piqqudin* (contiguous commandments), *Raza de-Razin* (physiognomy), *Sitrei Otiyyot*, *Qav ha-Middah*, *Merkevet Yeḥezqel*, *Zohar al ha-Parashot*, and passages (mis)labeled Tosefta. Joint Introduction: material printed as *Zohar Ḥadash* is often among the **earliest** strata — a bibliographic misnomer. Matt’s series Preface in this volume marks completion of Margot Pritzker’s full-edition project.

## Volume / work synthesis

Volume XII is the **closing register** of the Pritzker “Zoharic library”: not the Companions’ running Torah midrash of Vols. I–IX, nor primarily the philosophical-allegorical MhN of Vol. X, but self-contained theosophic compositions. The **Heikhalot** pair map prayer as architectural ascent — low comprised in high — culminating in seventh-with-seventh and a full Other-Side mirror of seven impurity halls. **Piqqudin** restores a contiguous 47-commandment theurgy (general→particular knowledge of God; festival cycle through lulav-as-Adam). **Raza de-Razin** turns the body into a lettered book whose lines rewrite with deeds and *teshuvah*. **Sitrei Otiyyot** and **Qav ha-Middah** bind YHVH letters and the Lamp of Adamantine Darkness to sefirotic measure; **Merkevet** insists on looking-glass prophecy. **Zohar al ha-Parashot** densifies Joseph/Sabbath/Amalek and staff dualities; mislabeled **Tosefta** pieces micro-map sefirot, covenant rungs, and *teshuvah*.

What this reingest **adds** is coverage-ledger honesty and folio-anchored body quotes for material first filed 2026-06-07 largely from Introduction paraphrase; densification of [[heikhalot]] (holy + impurity inventories) and [[raza-de-razin]] (letter system + mutability loci); and a dedicated source page completing standards reingests for Pritzker **I–XII**.

Tensions held: *Zohar Ḥadash* “new” in print title vs earliest material (Introduction position); two Heikhalot versions as rewrite by same hand (probable, not certain — Introduction); clinical Raza vs sefirotic Zohar face (polyphony); Mantua scatter of Piqqudin vs MS contiguous form.
