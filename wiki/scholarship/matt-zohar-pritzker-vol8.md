## What the source is

Volume VIII covers the **last third of the Zohar’s Leviticus commentary** and the **first third of Numbers** (Matt preface):

1. **Parashat Qedoshim** (Lev 19:1–20:27) — holiness ethic; sexual vigilance; timed conjugal theurgy; Torah tears decrees.
2. **Parashat Emor** (Lev 21:1–24:23) — priests, festivals, *ushpizin*, counting purity.
3. **Parashat Be-Har** (Lev 25:1–26:2) — sabbatical / Jubilee.
4. **Parashat Be-Ḥuqqotai** (Lev 26:3–27:34) — blessings and curses; exile pledge.
5. **Parashat Be-Midbar** (Num 1:1–4:20) — wilderness census open.
6. **Parashat Naso** (Num 4:21–7:89) — sotah, nazirite, priestly blessing; embedded ***Idra Rabba*** (~3:127b–145a).
7. **Parashat Be-Ha’alotekha** (Num 8:1–12:16) — lamps, manna, Miriam; Torah not mere story.

## Volume / work synthesis

Volume VIII completes Leviticus’ Holiness Code and opens Numbers as **portable theurgy**. Qedoshim densifies holiness as vigilance and midnight coupling, with Torah studied for her own sake tearing up decrees. Emor’s festival cycle adds the *ushpizin* guests of faith (and the poor as their portion), four species, and calendar theurgy. Be-Har / Be-Ḥuqqotai map Jubilee and exile as Shekhinah’s pledge; Be-Midbar arrays the camp under the Name.

Naso’s climax is the ***Idra Rabba***: Rashbi and ten Companions array Arikh Anpin’s thirteen beard enhancements and Ze’eir Anpin’s full countenance so Compassion may soothe Judgment — at the cost of three Companions who die by a kiss (ten entered, seven emerged). Be-Ha’alotekha closes the volume with lamps, manna, Miriam’s eleven-letter healing Name, and R. Shim’on’s radical claim that Torah is not mere story.

What this reingest **adds** is folio-anchored fidelity for material first filed 2026-06-07 without ledgers; corrected placement of the Torah-tears-decrees unit in Qedoshim; first-class densification of *Idra Rabba* on [[shimon-bar-yochai|Shim’on bar Yoḥai]]; and clear body vs Matt-apparatus separation for *ushpizin* reception and Maimonidean framing.

Tensions held without flattening: Zoharic midnight *zivvug* and Idra anthropomorphism vs Maimonidean incorporeality; *ushpizin* body vs later Lurianic popular custom; Torah surpassing sacrifice vs still-load-bearing cult; Companion deaths as perfection vs punishment readings.
