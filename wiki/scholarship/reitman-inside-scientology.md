# Reitman, *Inside Scientology*

*Inside Scientology: The Story of America's Most Secretive Religion* (2011), by journalist **Janet
Reitman**, is the most substantial **independent narrative history** of [[scientology|Scientology]] in the
wiki's sources. It grew out of an award-winning 2006 *Rolling Stone* article and draws on extensive
interviews (including current and former members) and documents.

## Scope and Value

A largely **chronological history** from [[l-ron-hubbard|Hubbard's]] life and the 1950 launch of
[[dianetics|Dianetics]], through the founding of the [[church-of-scientology|Church]], the Sea Org, the
**Guardian's Office / Operation Snow White**, the 1993 IRS settlement, the leadership of **David Miscavige**,
the **Lisa McPherson** case, and the celebrity culture (Tom Cruise et al.). Reitman aims at **even-handed
reportage** rather than polemic, which makes it the anchor source for the wiki's neutral, NRM-as-subject
framing.

## Hermeneutical Framework

Investigative-journalistic and historical: documentary verification, multiple sourcing, and attention to the
gap between the Church's official account and the record — central to the wiki's
[[scientology-religion-or-commercial-enterprise|status controversy]] and to the contested biography of
[[l-ron-hubbard|Hubbard]].
