## What the source is

Volume I of the first complete scholarly English translation of the [[zohar|Zohar]] from a reconstructed critical Aramaic text (roughly twenty manuscripts collated against Mantua 1558–60 and Cremona 1559–60). It opens the main running Torah commentary with:

1. **Haqdamat Sefer ha-Zohar** (Introduction to the Book of Radiance) — a typical Zoharic collection of openings, not a modern preface (Zohar 1:1a–14b).
2. **Parashat Be-Reshit** (Gen 1:1–6:8) — creation as **theogony** (sefirot from [[ein-sof|Ein Sof]]), Adam’s sin as separation of [[shekhinah|Shekhinah]] from Tif’eret, generation of the Flood.
3. **Parashat Noaḥ** (Gen 6:9–11:32) — Noah as Yesod below, Flood as mirrored punishment for wasted seed / failed male–female waters, ark as refuge from the Destroyer, Babel, soul doctrine.

Arthur Green’s Introduction situates the Zohar historically and theologically (Castilian Kabbalah, literary form, sefirotic symbolism, eros, Companions, authorship positions). Matt’s Translator’s Introduction documents critical-text method and translation principles.

## Volume / work synthesis

Volume I of the Pritzker Edition does more than open the Zohar’s Torah commentary: it installs the symbolic and narrative grammar the rest of the corpus assumes. In Matt’s English, the Haqdamah stages Shekhinah as rose and bridal Assembly, the Companions as canopy attendants, and Torah innovation as theurgy that fashions heavens — while the Be-Reshit theogony re-parses Genesis 1:1 so that *Elohim* is the palace created *with* Beginning, not the biblical subject of *creatio ex nihilo*. The same run derives Hell from left-side conflict on day two, the moon’s diminishment from the two lights, and the fall as Samael-on-serpent “all is one,” culminating in Adam’s driving out of *Et* (Shekhinah). Parashat Noaḥ then reads the Flood as measure-for-measure sexual/covenant crime (upper and lower waters fail), Noah as Yesod below (ambivalent, failed intercessor versus Moses), the ark as refuge from the Destroyer, and embeds a full soul–afterlife–Hell dossier (tripartite soul, seven abodes, Kaddish theurgy).

What this reingest **adds** to the wiki is not a second parallel Zohar system but **locus-anchored fidelity** for the material first filed in 2026-06-07 without coverage ledgers or systematic verbatim grounding. Core concept pages ([[sefirot]], [[ein-sof]], [[shekhinah]], [[sitra-ahra]], [[tsaddiq]], [[zoharic-afterlife]]) and the Vol. I structure block on [[zohar]] now carry short quotes with Zohar pagination (e.g. 1:15a, 1:1a) and clear evidence-class labels (body vs Matt apparatus vs Green).
