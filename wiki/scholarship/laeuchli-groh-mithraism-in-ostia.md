## Overview

A short scholarly volume (the first in the *Garrett Theological Studies* series) reporting an
American team's fieldwork at **Ostia**. It is structured as linked essays moving from the
material remains of the Ostian mithraea outward to the social environment, then inward to
Mithraic theology (dualism), liturgy, and finally a confessional/historical comparison with
early Christianity. Its standing references are the foundational works of **Franz Cumont**,
the *Scavi di Ostia* excavation reports (Becatti), and Vermaseren's *Corpus*.
