# The Tibetan Book of the Dead — First Complete Translation

Source: `raw/texts/The Tibetan Book of the Dead First Complete Translation (Penguin Classics
Deluxe Edition) (Graham Coleman, Thupten Jinpa, Gyurme Dorje).txt` (filed into `raw/texts/`
after ingestion)

Penguin Classics Deluxe Edition. The **first complete English translation** of the full
fourteen-chapter cycle traditionally called the *Tibetan Book of the Dead* — properly the
**[[bardo-thodol|*kar-gling zhi-khro*]]** cycle ("The Profound Dharma of Natural Liberation
through the Intention of the Peaceful and Wrathful Deities"), the [[terma|treasure]] revealed
by [[karma-lingpa|Karma Lingpa]]. Includes a general Introduction (with a foreword by the
14th Dalai Lama), per-chapter introductions, and two appendices. It contains **nine
chapters not present in [[w-y-evans-wentz|W. Y. Evans-Wentz]]'s 1927 edition**, which had given
the work its English title.

## Scope Plan (ordered passes)

1. **Editorial Introduction** (lines ~156–1730) — what the cycle is; Nyingma/Dzogchen
   framing; the *terma* lineage; Karma Lingpa; Evans-Wentz reception history; Jung. ✅
2. **Chapters 1–7** (lines ~1730–6400) — preliminary practices, four-session yoga, prayer
   for union with the teacher, root verses of the six bardos, Introduction to Awareness
   (Dzogchen rigpa), habitual tendencies, negativity/obscuration, confession. ✅
3. **Chapters 8–11 (Parts One–Two)** (lines ~6400–8620) — signs of death, ritual deception
   of death, consciousness transference (phowa), and the core *Great Liberation by Hearing*:
   bardo of dying, inner radiance, bardo of reality, the Peaceful Deities. ✅
4. **Chapter 11 (Part Three)–Chapter 14** (lines ~8620–11700) — Wrathful Deities, bardo of
   rebirth, aspirational prayers, the masked drama, Liberation by Wearing. ✅
5. **Appendices, Notes** (lines ~11700–13670) — Appendix One (the 100 Peaceful & Wrathful
   Deities) and Appendix Two (mandala symbolism). ✅
6. **Glossary of Key Terms** (lines ~13678–18752) — extract key doctrinal/technical terms. ✅
