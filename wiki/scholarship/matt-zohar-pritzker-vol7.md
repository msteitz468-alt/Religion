## What the source is

Volume VII covers **more than half of the Zohar’s commentary on Leviticus** (Matt preface). Six parashot on sacrifices, priesthood, purity, and Yom Kippur / sexual holiness (Lev 1–18):

1. **Parashat Va-Yiqra** (Lev 1:1–5:26) — ~3:3b–27a (Pritzker opens at [3b]; Mantua letter-mysticism 3:2a–3b omitted).
2. **Parashat Tsav** (Lev 6:1–8:36) — perpetual fire, offerings, ordination.
3. **Parashat Shemini** (Lev 9:1–11:47) — inauguration; **Nadab and Abihu**; food laws.
4. **Parashat Tazri’a** (Lev 12:1–13:59) — childbirth purity; scaly affliction (*tsara’at*).
5. **Parashat Metsora** (Lev 14:1–15:33) — purification of the afflicted; discharges.
6. **Parashat Aḥarei Mot** (Lev 16:1–18:30) — **Yom Kippur**; Judah–Tamar theurgy; circumcision/Torah; Lilith; Lev 18.

## Volume / work synthesis

Volume VII turns the Zohar’s Torah commentary from **building and filling the Dwelling** (Vols. V–VI) into **keeping the presence** through sacrifice, priesthood, purity, and Yom Kippur. After the garden/bride banquet that greets the erected Sanctuary, *qorban* is redefined as drawing near of crowns to Compassion, and *adam* who offers must be male-and-female complete. Perpetual fire and dual altars map Judgment assuaged by a right-side priest; *zeh*/*zot* union is Aaron’s cosmic work.

The signature cautionary arc is **Nadab and Abihu**: alien fire, forced hour, unmarried half-body, and failure to link Assembly of Israel so that Matronita and King are disturbed — with Phinehas’s small *yod* and Yom Kippur liturgy as structural repair. Tazri’a’s *Al mishkavi* places Shekhinah on a foreign impure bed; *tsara’at* and food laws police the Other Side’s claim on speech and flesh. Aḥarei Mot stages the annual Accuser→Advocate drama of the two goats, the high priest’s golden knotted cord and balsam-scented entry, the three rungs Holy One–Torah–Israel, Judah–Tamar as Bride-in-exile theurgy, and Lev 18 as exposing Father’s/Mother’s nakedness when Israel’s deeds separate the divine couple — closing with Lilith, Serpent slime, and the righteous who rejoice.

What this reingest **adds** is folio-anchored fidelity (3:3b–80a) for material first filed 2026-06-07 without ledgers; clear separation of Matt preface/apparatus from body (especially golden-cord prehistory and the “all one” folk saying); and a first-class densification of [[nadab-and-abihu|Nadab and Abihu]], which previously lacked a Zoharic section.

Tensions held without flattening: Zoharic multi-cause Nadab/Abihu vs Wenham’s boundary-of-holiness hinge vs rabbinic intoxication/zeal lists; *olah* as Thought/male ascent (body) vs Shekhinah ascending (Matt preface framing); regulated Other Side goat vs Christian scapegoat typology; Torah surpassing sacrifice vs Levitical cult as still theurgically load-bearing.
