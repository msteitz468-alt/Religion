## What the source is

Volume III completes the Zohar’s main running commentary on **Genesis** (Pritzker critical English from reconstructed Aramaic). Five parashot on the close of the Jacob cycle and the Joseph cycle:

1. **Parashat Va-Yishlaḥ** (Gen 32:4–36:43) — Jacob’s return, wrestling / Peniel, Esau encounter, Dinah, Edom (~1:166a–179a).
2. **Parashat Va-Yeshev** (Gen 37:1–40:23) — Joseph sold; Judah and Tamar; dreams in prison (~1:179b–193a).
3. **Parashat Mi-Qets** (Gen 41:1–44:17) — Pharaoh’s dreams; Joseph’s rise; brothers’ first descent (~1:193b–205a).
4. **Parashat Va-Yiggash** (Gen 44:18–47:27) — Judah approaches; reunion; descent to Egypt (~1:205b–211b).
5. **Parashat Va-Yḥi** (Gen 47:28–50:26) — Jacob’s last years, blessings of the tribes, death, Joseph’s coffin (~1:216b–251a; extract title spelling *Va-Yḥi*).

## Volume / work synthesis

Volume III closes the Zohar’s Genesis commentary by staging Jacob’s return and Joseph’s career as the full drama of **Tif’eret body** and **Yesod/Tsaddiq**. Va-Yishlaḥ forces the blessings against Samael, Prince of Esau, in ash of the Other Side until dawn renames Jacob *Israel* and he arrives *shalem*; Esau is bowed past toward Shekhinah, while Edom’s kings mark impure rungs that precede holy kingship. Va-Yeshev installs Joseph as the fruiting of “generations of Jacob,” the covenant pillar who guards the sign against Potiphar’s wife, and whose sale and coat exile the tribes with Shekhinah; Judah and Tamar (with Ruth) plant the messianic line while Er/Onan’s wasted seed feeds the Sitra Aḥra. Mi-Qets reads *mi-qets* as End of the Left and Joseph’s rise as river/Yesod/*Avrekh*, with famine sealed so the Other Side cannot father alien children. Va-Yiggash joins king and Tsaddiq when Judah approaches Joseph, revives Jacob’s spirit into the Israel rung, and sends Shekhinah down to Egypt under the Life of the Worlds. Va-Yḥi densifies death and inheritance: the circumcised people of Israel are all *tsaddiqim* who inherit the Matronita; the perfect deathbed chariot (Abraham–Isaac–Jacob–Shekhinah) grounds “Jacob did not die” as sun gathered to moon; tribal blessings map sefirotic roots through Judah’s Messiah vine to Joseph’s double *aron* — coffin below and ark of the covenant above.
