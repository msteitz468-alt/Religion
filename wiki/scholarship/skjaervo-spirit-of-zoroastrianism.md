# Skjærvø, *The Spirit of Zoroastrianism*

A scholarly anthology and study of [[zoroastrianism|Zoroastrian]] religion by **Prods Oktor Skjærvø**
(Harvard Iranist), combining translated primary sources — from the **[[gathas|Gathas]]** through the
Young Avesta to the Pahlavi books — with interpretive essays. The wiki's first dedicated Zoroastrian
scholarly source (user-supplied), it grounds the Zoroastrianism pages with current philology.

## Value for the Wiki

- **Translation quality**: Skjærvø is a leading Avestan philologist; his renderings correct the dated
  Victorian [[gathas|Mills/SBE]] translation the wiki ingested as its public-domain base text — a caveat
  now noted on the Gathas page.
- **Periodization**: he carefully distinguishes the **Old Avestan** (Gathic) layer from the Young Avestan
  and the **late Pahlavi** systematizations — exactly the distinction that disciplines the
  [[zoroastrian-influence-on-abrahamic-eschatology|influence-tracing]] question (much of the fullest
  eschatology is attested only in the *late* sources).
- **Religious phenomenology**: ritual, the structure of the cosmos (the *getig*/*menog*, material/mental
  states), purity, and the moral dualism of Asha and the Lie.

## Hermeneutical Framework

Historical-critical and **philological**, with attention to oral-formulaic transmission and the ritual
*Sitz im Leben* of the texts — a corrective to both confessional and over-eager "Zoroastrian origins of
everything" readings.
