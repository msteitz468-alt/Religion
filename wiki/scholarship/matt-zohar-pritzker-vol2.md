## What the source is

Volume II continues the main running Torah commentary of the [[zohar|Zohar]] (Pritzker critical English from reconstructed Aramaic). It covers five Genesis parashot on the patriarchal narratives:

1. **Parashat Lekh Lekha** (Gen 12:1–17:27) — Abraham’s call, land, Egypt, Melchizedek, covenant of circumcision (Zohar ~1:76b/77a–96b zone).
2. **Parashat Va-Yera** (Gen 18:1–22:24) — visitors and annunciation, Sodom, Akedah.
3. **Parashat Ḥayyei Sarah** (Gen 23:1–25:18) — Sarah’s death, Machpelah, Rebekah betrothal.
4. **Parashat Toledot** (Gen 25:19–28:9) — Jacob and Esau, the stolen blessing.
5. **Parashat Va-Yetse** (Gen 28:10–32:3) — ladder, Laban, Rachel and Leah as two worlds, tribes.

No separate Green Introduction in this volume (Green’s intro is in Vol. I). Matt’s apparatus continues the philological / source-tracing notes of the series.

## Volume / work synthesis

Volume II of the Pritzker Edition carries the Zohar’s Torah commentary through the patriarchal core of Genesis. In Matt’s English, Lekh Lekha installs Abraham as Ḥesed who must *refine himself* before the land and the Name open: arousal below precedes arousal above; Egypt refines without seducing; Melchizedek joins two worlds with bread and wine; and circumcision converts a low *maḥazeh* speech-partner into a rung on which “all those rungs rest,” completing *Shaddai* and distinguishing Abram-phase Ishmael from Abraham-phase Isaac. Va-Yera then stages the theophany that follows the *brit*, the Din of Sodom tempered by imperfect Ḥesed intercession, and the Akedah as water embraced by fire — a test of Abraham, not Isaac — whose division awaits Jacob’s triad-harmonization. Ḥayyei Sarah densifies death and burial: Sarah’s sefirotic years, death concurrent with the binding, Machpelah as Garden gateway with Adam already present, and Rebekah’s entry rekindling the tent-lamp of Shekhinah. Toledot maps the twins as sacred-throne seed versus serpent/*saʿir* dross, with Rebekah’s mild din securing blessings that invert Eden’s curses while Hell enters with Esau. Va-Yetse crowns the volume with the ladder, the two wives as revealed Malkhut (*Attah*) and concealed Jubilee (*Hu*), Joseph–Benjamin as twin *tsaddiqim*, Laban’s sorcery and the Fear of Isaac oath, and Maḥanayim as the moment married Jacob with tribes is entreated by angel camps while Shekhinah waits on Benjamin to possess the house.

What this reingest **adds** is locus-anchored fidelity for patriarchal material first filed in 2026-06-07 without coverage ledgers or systematic verbatim grounding. Tensions held without flattening include: Abraham’s partial Sodom intercession versus Moses; Ishmael as necessary lower engendering versus expelled scoria; Esau’s honor of father earning dominion while his tears prolong Israel’s subjugation; Rachel’s good-intentioned theft of the *terafim* punished by never raising Benjamin; and the Akedah as cosmic completion of Ḥesed-with-Gevurah versus Isaac’s later greater tremble when Hell accompanies Esau.
