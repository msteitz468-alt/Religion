## What the source is

Volume IV opens the Zohar’s running commentary on **Exodus** (Pritzker critical English from reconstructed Aramaic). Five parashot:

1. **Parashat Shemot** (Exod 1:1–6:1) — enslavement, birth/call of Moses, burning bush (~2b–14a zone).
2. **Parashat Va-Era** (Exod 6:2–9:35) — divine Name, plagues begin (~22a–32b).
3. **Parashat Bo** (Exod 10:1–13:16) — remaining plagues, Passover, departure (~33a–44a).
4. **Parashat Be-Shallaḥ** (Exod 13:17–17:16) — Red Sea, Song, 72-letter Name, manna, Amalek (~44b–67a).
5. **Parashat Yitro** (Exod 18:1–20:23) — Jethro, Sinai theophany, Ten Commandments (~67b–94a).

## Volume / work synthesis

Volume IV opens the Zohar’s Exodus commentary as the theurgic drama of **exile, Other Side dominion, Name, and covenant**. Shemot stages Shekhinah going down to Egypt with Israel and the birth of Moses under Matronita’s light; Va-Era installs Moses as husband of the house who hears *YHVH* openly (vs patriarchal *El Shaddai*) and plagues as rung-by-rung judgment of Pharaoh the sea serpent. Bo densifies Passover as dual bloods (covenant Compassion + paschal Judgment) against the Destroyer and leaven as Other Dominion. Be-Shallaḥ reveals *yam suf* as end of rungs, the **72-letter Name** in three sefirotic arrays, the Song as Matronita’s theurgy, manna as crown of Ḥesed for scions of faith, and Amalek as Samael-assisted “dog” war above and below. Yitro completes glory when Jethro (chief of false gods) converts, then Sinai: Raḥamei first, then Gevurah; people *seeing the voices*; Decalogue five-in-five; Torah as that which makes the world “fragrantly firm.”

What this reingest **adds** is locus-anchored fidelity for the liberation–Sinai arc first filed in 2026-06-07 without coverage ledgers. Core pages ([[moses]], [[pharaoh]], [[amalekites]], [[shekhinah]], [[sitra-ahra]], [[exodus]], [[sefirot]]) now carry short quotes with Exodus-side Zohar pagination (e.g. 2:22a–24a, 2:50b–52b, 2:65a–67a, 2:81a–94a).

Tensions held without flattening include: Moses’ boldness with *Adonai* vs “husband of the house” immunity; incomplete *peri’ah* in Egypt as both haste and deficient moon; Amalek as Israel’s testing consequence and cosmic Samael war; Sinai ≫ Sea because filth removed; Jethro’s conversion as precondition of Torah glory.
