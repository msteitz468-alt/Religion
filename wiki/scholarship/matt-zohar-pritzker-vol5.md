## What the source is

Volume V continues the Zohar’s running commentary on **Exodus** immediately after the Decalogue (end of Vol. IV). Per Matt’s preface (cache lines 265–328): Mishpatim with the independent *Sava de-Mishpatim* composition; Terumah on the *mishkan* as Shekhinah / *Kallat Moshe*; and *Sifra di-Tsni’uta* (Book of Concealment), presented first plain then with commentary.

1. **Parashat Mishpatim** (Exod 21:1–24:18) — including **Sava de-Mishpatim** (Old Man of Mishpatim; ~2:94b–114a) and main-body commentary on the laws (~2:121a–126a).
2. **Parashat Terumah** (Exod 25:1–27:19) — Dwelling materials, vessels, prayer/theurgy, Sabbath liturgy (*Ke-Gavna* strand), mountain-man parable leading into Sifra (~2:126b–176b).
3. ***Sifra di-Tsni’uta*** (“Book of Concealment”; ~2:176b–179a) — five short chapters on balance, skull, emanation, male/female; plain text then annotated reprint.

## Volume / work synthesis

Volume V turns the Exodus commentary from liberation and Sinai theophany (Vol. IV) into **law, soul repair, and Dwelling**. The independent *Sava de-Mishpatim* stages Yeiva Sava’s riddles and the Torah-maiden parable, then builds the first extensive Jewish *gilgul* system: scales of destiny, Exod 21 slave-laws as Worlds of Male and Female, levirate redeemer/vessel mechanics, the sling of the childless, and resurrection that loses nothing. Main-body Mishpatim then treats holiness gradation, Gevurah-balanced justice, and the Secret of Secrets anatomy of the King through the Sinai covenant hinge.

Terumah densifies *mishkan* as Shekhinah’s body: offering as taking Matronita with divine permission; **Kallat Moshe** (Num 7:1 re-read) as her ice-breaking descent; materials, prayer, and Sabbath liturgy as theurgy; Song of Songs as Holy of Holies when Temple and moon are full; dual Dwelling with Metatron above. *Sifra di-Tsni’uta* closes the volume as condensed theosophy — balance before face-to-face, primordial kings, skull and crystalline dew, breath of the armoire, male/female, Jubilee horn restoring the complete Name — the upper “Book of Concealment” matching the manifest sanctuary below.

Tensions held without flattening include: Zoharic *mishkan* = Shekhinah theurgy vs Bruckner’s creational/microcosm and Golden-Calf chiastic reading; gilgul as theodicy and repair vs earlier rabbinic silence/resistance and evangelical sources without transmigration; Sava’s parabolic riddling vs bulk Zoharic homily and vs plain legal peshat.
