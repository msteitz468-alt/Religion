## What the source is

Volume XI is the first Pritzker volume to present a **variety of Zoharic genres** rather than continuous running Torah commentary (Vols. I–IX) or Torah *Midrash ha-Ne’lam* alone (Vol. X). Hecker’s preface (lines 268–351) states that many of these sections first appeared under the print title *Zohar Ḥadash* (“New Zohar”) — “misleading, however, in that the volume comprises some of the earliest Zoharic material.”

Verified body chapters (lines 352–4768):

1. **Midrash ha-Ne’lam on Song of Songs** — short fragment; mystical kiss / four *ruḥot*; large *shin*; non-sefirotic Ecclesiastes 12 close.
2. **Midrash ha-Ne’lam on Ruth** — large unit; soul origin/fate; near-death / Hell chambers; Shema and Grace after Meals; ten martyrs; conversion and Gentile polemic; print history (Thiengen 1559; Venice 1565; *Zohar Ḥadash* 1658).
3. **Midrash ha-Ne’lam on Lamentations** — Israel vs Babylon eulogy contest; orphaned by Divine Father and Mother; Hecker frames anti-Christian Holy Family reframe (attributed).
4. **Zohar on Song of Songs** — mature Zoharic phase; Rashbi–Elijah exchange on Song 1:1–11; Cordovero ranks among most profound; letter mysticism.
5. **Matnitin** — oracular, terse, hortatory; dense sefirotic summaries; Hecker: more likely later than early.
6. **Tosefta** — same style/tone as Matnitin (Hecker).
7. **Sitrei Torah** — mostly Genesis-linked; demonic Other Side; later MhN-style ST layers excluded from this translation.

## Volume / work synthesis

Volume XI is the Pritzker Edition’s first deliberate **genre sampler**: not another stretch of weekly parashah theurgy, but Megillot concealed midrash plus independent compositions. MhN Song is a fragment (kiss, large *shin*, Ecclesiastes 12). MhN Ruth is the volume’s practical and anthropological center — soul architecture on YHVH letters, Hell under Dumah, near-death courts, Grace after Meals and Shema praxis, conversion as spiritual drama. MhN Lamentations stages pathos as eulogy contest and divine family orphanage. Zohar on Song of Songs is the mature literary peak of the volume: Rashbi and Elijah trade ascent and Other Side repair through Song 1:1–11, culminating in letter mysticism that treats the alphabet as the fabric of romance and creation. Matnitin and Tosefta speak as oracular “Our Mishnah,” dense enough that Hecker judges them later distillates. Sitrei Torah closes with Genesis Other Side secrets and the Secret of Secrets that the Dwelling perfects the Spirit.

What this reingest **adds** is folio-anchored fidelity for material first filed 2026-06-07 without ledgers; a dedicated source page; **correction of the Idra Zuta misattribution** (not in Vol. XI); densification of Megillot pages with Zoharic registers kept separate from Longman/Lau/Goldingay; and Hell/soul densification on [[zoharic-afterlife|Zoharic Afterlife]].

Tensions held: *Zohar Ḥadash* as “new” in print vs earliest material (Hecker); Matnitin as early seed vs later summary (Hecker prefers later); MhN Ruth’s contested Zoharic status in early reception; Hecker’s anti-Christian Holy Family frame as preface attribution, not bare body; Idra Zuta remains Vol. IX, not Vol. XI.
