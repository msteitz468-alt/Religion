# Lamanites

The **Lamanites** are the Book of Mormon people associated with Laman and Lemuel and their allies after the separation from [[nephi|Nephi]] (2 Ne 5) — repeatedly enemies of the [[nephites|Nephites]], yet also major converts and the **remnant** addressed by the title page and Mormon 7.

## Identity and Nomenclature

Named for Laman; the label is political-religious more than purely genealogical (dissenters "become" Lamanites; converted Lamanites are numbered with Nephites). The text includes **curse/mark/skin** language (2 Ne 5; Alma 3; 3 Ne 2) whose reception history is highly sensitive and contested — recorded as textual claim, not modern racial theory.

## Primary Textual Appearances

- Origin in the rebellion of Laman/Lemuel; curse and scourge function (2 Ne 5).
- Ammon's mission; Anti-Nephi-Lehies (Alma 17–26).
- Samuel the Lamanite prophet to the Nephites (Hel 13–15).
- United with Nephites against Gadianton; some "curse taken" language (3 Ne 2).
- Survivors of the final wars; latter-day remnant promises.

## Theological Significance

Title-page audience; proof that the Lord remembers the whole house of Israel; inversion motif (Lamanite righteousness vs. Nephite pride). Modern LDS identification of Lamanites with indigenous American peoples is a **reception** claim under ongoing revision and dispute (see [[book-of-mormon-historicity]]).
