# Jaredites

The **Jaredites** are the prior American civilization in the [[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]], whose record is abridged in the **Book of Ether** from twenty-four gold plates found by the people of Limhi.

## Primary Textual Appearances

- Title page: people of Jared scattered at the tower when language was confounded.
- **Ether 1–3** — Jared and the brother of Jared; barges; the brother of Jared's theophany (finger then full spirit body of the premortal Christ).
- **Ether 8** — secret combinations (Akish); warning that such oaths destroyed the Jaredites and will destroy any nation that upholds them.
- **Ether 12** — faith, hope, charity; Moroni's editorial.
- **Ether 13–15** — final annihilation; Coriantumr; Ramah = Cumorah.

## Theological Significance

Narrative **mirror** of Nephite destruction; origin story for secret combinations; high Christology via the brother of Jared; American promised-land theology before Lehi.
