# Nephites

The **Nephites** are the primary covenant polity of the [[book-of-mormon|Book of Mormon]] narrative, named for [[nephi|Nephi]] after the separation from his brethren (2 Ne 5).

## Identity and Nomenclature

Self-designation "people of Nephi"; later "Nephites" as political-religious label. In 4 Nephi, for a time there are no "-ites" at all (all converted, all things common); class and sect names return with pride.

## Primary Textual Appearances

- Small-plates origin and temple culture (1–2 Nephi, Jacob).
- Kings and judges (Mosiah–Alma); wars with Lamanites and Gadianton robbers (Alma, Helaman, 3 Nephi).
- Recipients of the resurrected Christ's ministry at Bountiful (3 Nephi 11–28).
- Final destruction under Mormon and Moroni (Mormon 1–8).

## Tradition-Specific Portraits

- **LDS reading:** historical covenant people of ancient America; spiritual types for latter-day pride/prosperity cycles; ancestors (with Lamanites) of some indigenous peoples (traditional view; nuanced in modern LDS statements).
- **Critical reading:** literary construct within a 19th-century religious text.

## Theological Significance

Carriers of the brass plates and prophetic record; exemplars of the prosper/cut-off formula; both righteous remnant and object of judgment. Foil to [[lamanites|Lamanites]] and mirror of [[jaredites|Jaredite]] destruction.
