# Apostles of Bahá'u'lláh

*Populated from [[smith-concise-encyclopedia-bahai-faith|Smith, Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith (2000)]].*

Designation given by [[shoghi-effendi|Shoghi Effendi]] to a group of outstanding early Bahá'ís whose dedication, sacrifice, and teaching services were analogous to those of the apostles of Jesus. The designation is cited in connection with individual figures such as [[badi|Badí']], [[mirza-abu-l-fadl|Mírzá Abu'l-Fadl]], [[ibn-i-abhar|Ibn-i-Abhár]], and [[ibn-i-asdaq|Ibn-i-Asdaq]], among others. These individuals traveled widely to spread the Bahá'í Faith, often at great personal risk, during the ministries of Bahá'u'lláh and 'Abdu'l-Bahá. Several were martyred. Shoghi Effendi's historical work *God Passes By* provides the authoritative listing and characterization of this group.
