# Timeline of Hinduism

A chronological hub for the Hindu tradition — from its [[vedic-religion|Vedic]] roots through the classical *darśanas* and the great Vedānta and bhakti movements to modern reform. The wiki's coverage is strong for the **Vedānta schools** and the **bhakti poets**. For what was happening across other traditions in each era, see the [[comparative-timeline|Comparative Timeline of World Religions]]. Parent page: [[hinduism|Hinduism]].

> Bracketed plain names (no link) are not yet developed as wiki pages.

---

## I. The Vedic Period (c. 1500–800 BCE)

The religion of the [[vedas|Vedas]] — sacrificial ritual addressed to the devas — forms the substratum of all later Hinduism. (Treated as its own tradition page: [[vedic-religion|Vedic religion]].)

| Texts | Deities | Concepts |
|---|---|---|
| [[rig-veda|Ṛg Veda]] · [[vedas|the four Vedas]] | [[indra]] · [[agni]] · [[varuna]] · [[soma]] · [[ushas]] | [[yajna|sacrifice]] · [[rita|ṛta (cosmic order)]] |

## II. The Upaniṣadic Turn (c. 800–400 BCE)

The [[upanishads|Upaniṣads]] interiorize the sacrifice and turn to liberating knowledge — the identity of [[atman|ātman]] and [[brahman]] — establishing the framework of [[samsara]], [[karma]], and [[moksha]].

| Texts | Concepts | Themes |
|---|---|---|
| [[upanishads]] | [[brahman]] · [[atman]] · [[moksha]] · [[samsara]] · [[karma]] · [[maya|māyā]] | the [[axial-age|Axial-Age]] turn; renouncer movements |

## III. The Classical & Epic Age (c. 400 BCE – 500 CE)

The epics and the [[bhagavad-gita|Bhagavad Gītā]]; the codification of [[dharma]]; the six orthodox [[saddarshana|darśanas]] (philosophical schools) and their root-texts.

| Texts | Concepts | Schools / Themes |
|---|---|---|
| [[mahabharata]] · [[ramayana]] · [[bhagavad-gita]] · [[yoga-sutras]] · [[brahma-sutras]] | [[karma-yoga]] · [[bhakti]] · [[varnas|varṇa]] · [[gunas|guṇas]] · [[yugas]] | the [[saddarshana|six darśanas]]; the rise of [[krishna]]/[[vishnu]] and [[rama]] devotion |

## IV. The Vedānta Schools & Classical Bhakti (c. 700–1500 CE)

The great commentators on the [[brahma-sutras|Brahma Sūtras]] found the rival Vedānta schools; Tantra and [[kashmir-shaivism|Kashmir Śaivism]] flourish; the bhakti poet-saints transform popular religion.

| **Bhakti poets:** [[jayadeva]] · [[namdev]] · [[ravidas]] · [[kabir]] · [[mirabai]] · [[tulsidas]] · [[chaitanya]] | [[bhakti]] · [[sant-tradition|sant tradition]] | [[virashaiva]] · (Gauḍīya → [[iskcon]]) |
