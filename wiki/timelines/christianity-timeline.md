# Timeline of Christianity

A chronological hub for the Christian tradition, linking the figures, texts, councils, controversies, and sects developed across the wiki. Coverage is densest for the **first five centuries** (origins through Chalcedon, 451), reflecting the wiki's current scope. For the cross-tradition view of what was happening elsewhere in each era, see the [[comparative-timeline|Comparative Timeline of World Religions]]. Parent page: [[christianity|Christianity]].

> **How to read this page.** Each era gives a short orientation, then tables of the figures, texts/concepts, and councils/controversies that fall in it. Bracketed plain names (no link) are not yet developed as wiki pages.
