# Timeline of Chinese Folk Religion

A chronological hub for the diffuse popular religion of China — ancestor veneration, the worship of Heaven and a vast pantheon organized as a [[celestial-bureaucracy|celestial bureaucracy]], interwoven over time with [[daoism|Daoism]], [[confucianism|Confucianism]], and [[buddhism|Buddhism]]. For the cross-tradition view see the [[comparative-timeline|Comparative Timeline of World Religions]]. Parent page: [[chinese-folk-religion|Chinese folk religion]].

> Bracketed plain names (no link) are not yet developed as wiki pages.

---

## I. Ancient Roots: Shang & Zhou (c. 1600–256 BCE)

Oracle-bone divination, royal ancestor worship, and the worship of the supreme power [[tian-shangdi|Shangdi / Tian (Heaven)]] — the substratum of all later Chinese religion.

| Deities / Concepts | Practices | Themes |
|---|---|---|
| [[tian-shangdi|Shangdi / Tian]] · the ancestors | oracle-bone divination; ancestral sacrifice | the Mandate of Heaven; cosmic correspondence |

## II. Classical Synthesis (Han – Tang, 206 BCE – 907 CE)

The pantheon is reorganized as a [[celestial-bureaucracy|celestial bureaucracy]] mirroring the imperial state; popular religion absorbs and is absorbed by the [[daoism|Daoist]] and [[buddhism|Buddhist]] pantheons; the underworld [[diyu|Dìyù]] takes shape.

| Deities | Concepts | Themes |
|---|---|---|
| [[xi-wangmu|Xiwangmu]] · [[yan-wang|Yánwáng]] · [[tudi-gong|Tudigong]] · [[kitchen-god|the Kitchen God]] | the [[celestial-bureaucracy]]; [[diyu|the underworld]] | bureaucratic afterlife; local tutelary gods |

## III. The Medieval & Late Imperial Pantheon (Song – Qing, 960–1912)

State-canonized and locally popular deities multiply; sea-goddess and city-god cults spread; the great vernacular novels fix the popular pantheon in the imagination.

| Deities | Texts | Themes |
|---|---|---|
| [[jade-emperor|the Jade Emperor]] · [[mazu|Mazu]] · [[guanyin|Guanyin]] · [[long-wang|the Dragon Kings]] · [[nezha]] · [[sun-wukong]] · [[the-eight-immortals]] | [[investiture-of-the-gods|Investiture of the Gods]] · [Journey to the West] · [[myths-and-legends-of-china]] | imperial canonization of gods; pilgrimage and temple festivals |

## IV. Modern Chinese Religion (1912 – present)

Suppression and revival across the twentieth century; popular religion persists and flourishes in Taiwan, the diaspora, and (increasingly) mainland China.

| Status | Themes |
|---|---|
| temple religion, ancestor worship, and deity cults continue | revival after the Cultural Revolution; the cosmology of [[pangu]]/[[nuwa|Nüwa]] in popular myth |
