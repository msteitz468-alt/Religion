# Timeline of Norse Religion

A chronological hub for the religion of the Germanic and Norse peoples — the worship of the Æsir and Vanir, known from Roman observers, runic and place-name evidence, and above all the Icelandic Eddas. For the cross-tradition view see the [[comparative-timeline|Comparative Timeline of World Religions]]. Parent page: [[norse-religion|Norse religion]].

> Bracketed plain names (no link) are not yet developed as wiki pages. Dates are largely *attestation* dates.

---

## I. Proto-Germanic & Migration Age (c. 1st–7th c. CE)

The early Germanic gods, attested by Tacitus and by interpretatio Romana; [[tyr|Týr]] and [[odin|Wodan/Óðinn]] in the early pantheon.

| ----------------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| [[tyr]] · [[odin]] · [[thor]] | [[Þórr]] | Tacitus's *Germania*; runic inscriptions | the war-god and the sky-god; sacral kingship |

## II. The Viking Age (c. 793–1066 CE)

The pantheon at its height; [Blót] sacrifice, temple cult at [Uppsala], and the cosmology of the [[yggdrasil|World Tree]].

| Deities | Concepts | Themes |
|---|---|---|
| [[odin]] · [[thor]] · [[freyja]] · [[freyr]] · [[frigg]] · [[baldr]] · [[heimdall]] · [[loki]] · [[njord]] | [[yggdrasil]] · [[valhalla]] · [[norns]] · [[hel]] | Æsir and Vanir; the warrior afterlife |

## III. Christianization & the Eddas (c. 1000–1300 CE)

Conversion of Scandinavia and Iceland; the myths are preserved (in a Christian context) by [[snorri-sturluson|Snorri Sturluson]] and the Eddic poets — including the cosmic end at [[ragnarok|Ragnarök]].
