# Timeline of Islam

A chronological hub for the Islamic tradition, linking the figures, texts, and movements developed across the wiki. Coverage is strongest for the **Qurʼān and its sciences**, the **formative legal and theological schools**, and **classical philosophy and Sufism**. For what was happening across other traditions in each era, see the [[comparative-timeline|Comparative Timeline of World Religions]]. Parent page: [[islam|Islam]].

> Bracketed plain names (no link) are not yet developed as wiki pages.
