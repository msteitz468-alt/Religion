# Timeline of Buddhism

A chronological hub for the Buddhist tradition, linking the figures, texts, schools, and concepts developed across the wiki. Coverage is strong for the **Madhyamaka/Yogācāra philosophy**, the **Tibetan schools**, and the **path literature**. For what was happening across other traditions in each era, see the [[comparative-timeline|Comparative Timeline of World Religions]]. Parent page: [[buddhism|Buddhism]].

> Bracketed plain names (no link) are not yet developed as wiki pages.

[[gautama-buddha|Siddhārtha Gautama]] attains awakening and founds the monastic community; the core teaching of the [[four-noble-truths]] and [[dependent-origination]].

## III. The Rise of Mahāyāna (c. 100 BCE – 400 CE)

New [[mahayana|Mahāyāna]] sūtras and the [[madhyamaka|Madhyamaka]] philosophy of [[nagarjuna|Nāgārjuna]] reframe the goal around the [[bodhisattva]] and [[sunyata|emptiness]].

## IV. Yogācāra & Scholastic Maturity (c. 300–600 CE)
