# Timeline of the Baháʼí Faith

A chronological hub for the Baháʼí Faith, the youngest of the independent Abrahamic traditions, which arose out of [[shia-islam|Shīʻa Islam]] via the [[babi|Bábí]] movement. The wiki's Baháʼí coverage is unusually deep. For the Bábí prelude in its own right see the [[babi-timeline|Bábí timeline]]; for placement among the other traditions see the [[comparative-timeline|Comparative Timeline of World Religions]]. Parent page: [[bahai|Baháʼí Faith]].

> Bracketed plain names (no link) are not yet developed as wiki pages.

## I. The Bábí Prelude (1844–1853)

Out of the messianic expectation of [[shaykhism|Shaykhism]], the [[the-bab|Báb]] declares himself the [[qaim|Qāʼim]] in 1844 and the herald of "[[he-whom-god-shall-make-manifest|Him Whom God shall make manifest]]." See the [[babi-timeline|Bábí timeline]] for detail.

## II. The Ministry of Baháʼuʼlláh (1853–1892)

[[bahaullah|Baháʼuʼlláh]], imprisoned in the Síyáh-Chál, receives his mission; declares himself "Him Whom God shall make manifest" at the [[ridvan|Garden of Riḍván]] (1863); exiled to Baghdad, Edirne, and finally ʻAkká, where he reveals his central works.

## III. The Centre of the Covenant: ʻAbduʼl-Bahá (1892–1921)

Baháʼuʼlláh's [[covenant-bahai|Covenant]] appoints [[abdu-l-baha|ʻAbduʼl-Bahá]] as authorized interpreter and exemplar; the Faith spreads to the West.
