# Timeline of Roman Religion

A chronological hub for the religion of Rome — from the household and state cults of the archaic city, through the imperial cult and the [[mystery-cults|mystery religions]], to Christianization. For the cross-tradition view see the [[comparative-timeline|Comparative Timeline of World Religions]]. Parent page: [[roman-religion|Roman religion]].

## I. Archaic & Regal Origins (c. 753–509 BCE)

Italic and Etruscan roots; the cult of the numinous, the household gods, and the early priestly colleges.

## II. Republican Civic Religion (509–27 BCE)

The [[roman-state-cult|state cult]] administered by the pontiffs and augurs; Hellenization of the gods; [[varro|Varro's]] systematic theology and the [[three-theologies|three theologies]]; the arrival of [[magna-mater-cybele|Magna Mater]].

## III. The Imperial Age (27 BCE – c. 250 CE)

The [[imperial-cult|imperial cult]] and the [[apotheosis-divinization|deification]] of emperors; the flourishing of the [[mystery-cults|mystery religions]] ([[mithraism|Mithras]], [[cult-of-isis-roman|Isis]]); the literary mythography of [[ovid-metamorphoses|Ovid]].

## IV. Late Antiquity & Christianization (c. 250–400 CE)

Crisis, [[neoplatonism|Neo-Platonic]] revival, and the gradual triumph of [[christianity|Christianity]]; the suppression of the public cults.
