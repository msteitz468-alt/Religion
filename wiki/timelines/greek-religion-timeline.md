# Timeline of Greek Religion

A chronological hub for ancient Greek religion — the Olympian pantheon, the mystery cults, hero cult, and the philosophical theology that grew alongside and against the myths. For the cross-tradition view see the [[comparative-timeline|Comparative Timeline of World Religions]]. Parent page: [[greek-religion|Greek religion]].

> Bracketed plain names (no link) are not yet developed as wiki pages.

---

## I. Mycenaean & Archaic Origins (c. 1600–700 BCE)

Bronze-Age cult (Linear B already names Olympian gods); [[homer|Homer]] and [[hesiod-theogony|Hesiod]] fix the pantheon and its genealogy in the early Archaic period.

| Deities | Texts | Themes |
|---|---|---|
| [[zeus]] · [[hera]] · [[cronus]] · [[uranus]] · [[gaia]] | [[homer]] · [[hesiod-theogony|Theogony]] · [[homeric-hymns]] | the succession myth; the Olympian order |

## II. Archaic & Classical (700–323 BCE)

[[olympian-civic-religion|Civic Olympian religion]] (sacrifice, temple, festival) alongside the [[eleusinian-mysteries|mystery cults]] and [[greek-hero-cult|hero cult]]; philosophy begins to criticize and allegorize myth.

| Deities | Cults / Schools | Texts / Concepts |
|---|---|---|
| [[athena]] · [[apollo]] · [[poseidon]] · [[demeter]] · [[dionysus]] · [[persephone]] | [[eleusinian-mysteries]] · [[dionysian-mysteries]] · [[orphism]] · [[greek-hero-cult]] | [[orphic-hymns]] · [[fate-moirai|the Moirai (fate)]] · [[plato|Plato's]] theology |

## III. The Hellenistic Age (323–31 BCE)

Ruler cult and syncretism; the mystery religions spread across the eastern Mediterranean; the philosophical schools develop rival theologies.

| Figures | Cults / Schools | Themes |
|---|---|---|
| [[aristotle]] (the Prime Mover) | spreading [[mystery-cults|mystery cults]]; Stoic and Epicurean theology | ruler cult; [[apotheosis-divinization|apotheosis]] of rulers |

## IV. The Roman Era & Decline (31 BCE – c. 400 CE)

Greek religion is absorbed into [[roman-religion|Roman religion]] and reinterpreted by [[neoplatonism|Neo-Platonism]] ([[plotinus|Plotinus]]) before yielding to Christianization.

| Figures | Legacy | Themes |
|---|---|---|
| [[plotinus]] · [[apollodorus-library|("Apollodorus")]] | [[neoplatonism]]; the philosophical afterlife of the gods | mythography; the closing of the temples |
