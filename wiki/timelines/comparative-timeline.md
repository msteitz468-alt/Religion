# Comparative Timeline of World Religions

A synchronic hub: a chronological spine showing what was unfolding across the major religious traditions **at the same time**, with links out to the tradition overviews, per-tradition timelines, and the key figures, texts, and controversies developed across the wiki.

This is the master navigation layer. For a single tradition's internal chronology, follow its **timeline** (where built) from the tables below. Per-tradition timeline hubs are added over time; the two built so far are the [[christianity-timeline|Timeline of Christianity]] and the [[talmudic-period|Talmudic Period]] (Judaism).

> **Reading the matrix.** Rows are eras; columns are tradition-families. Each cell names the headline developments of that family in that era. The detailed per-era tables below carry the links.
