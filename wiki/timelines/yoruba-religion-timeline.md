# Timeline of Yoruba Religion

A chronological hub for the religion of the Yoruba people of West Africa — the veneration of the supreme God Olódùmarè through the *òrìṣà* (deities) and the [[ifa-corpus|Ifá]] divination system — and its vast diffusion across the Atlantic world. For the cross-tradition view see the [[comparative-timeline|Comparative Timeline of World Religions]]. Parent page: [[yoruba-religion|Yoruba religion]].

> Bracketed plain names (no link) are not yet developed as wiki pages. Yoruba religion is primarily an *oral* tradition; dates are approximate.

---

## I. Origins at Ilé-Ifẹ̀ (c. 1st millennium CE)

Yoruba religion takes shape around the sacred city of [Ilé-Ifẹ̀], the mythic site of creation; the supreme being [Olódùmarè] is worshipped through the *òrìṣà* and the wisdom of [[ifa-corpus|Ifá]].

| Concepts | Themes |
|---|---|
| [Olódùmarè]; the *òrìṣà* ([Ọbàtálá], [Ṣàngó], [Ògún], [Èṣù], [Ọ̀ṣun]); *àṣẹ* (vital force) | creation at Ilé-Ifẹ̀; destiny (*orí*) |

## II. The Ọ̀yọ́ Empire & Institutionalization (c. 1400–1800)

Under the Ọ̀yọ́ Empire, deity cults, priesthoods, and the [[ifa-corpus|Ifá]] divination corpus are elaborated into an organized religious system.

| Institutions | Texts / Concepts |
|---|---|
| *òrìṣà* priesthoods; the [babaláwo] diviners | the [[ifa-corpus|Ifá literary corpus (*odù*)]] |

## III. The Atlantic Diaspora (16th–19th c.)

The transatlantic slave trade carries Yoruba religion to the Americas, where it gives rise to major creole traditions.

| Region | Tradition |
|---|---|
| Cuba; Brazil; the wider Caribbean | [Santería / Lucumí]; [Candomblé]; òrìṣà worship in the diaspora |

## IV. Modern Yoruba Religion (19th c. – present)

Coexistence and competition with Christianity and Islam in West Africa; a global revival of òrìṣà devotion.

| Status | Themes |
|---|---|
| living practice in Nigeria, Benin, and the diaspora | revival and transnational òrìṣà religion |
