# Messianism & Eschatology Across Traditions

A cross-tradition hub for the **awaited deliverer** (messiah / saviour / mahdī / saoshyant) and the **end of the present order** (judgment, resurrection, renewal). The traditions are historically entangled — see the dedicated comparison [[zoroastrian-influence-on-abrahamic-eschatology|Zoroastrian influence on Abrahamic eschatology]]. This hub gathers and links the material without adjudicating the disputed lines of influence.

## Recurring Structures

- **An awaited deliverer** — a future figure who vindicates the righteous and defeats evil.
- **A decisive end** — [[last-judgment|judgment]], [[resurrection|resurrection of the dead]], and the renewal or destruction of the world.
- **Realized vs. future tension** — the deliverance as already-begun vs. wholly future.
- **Apocalyptic literature** — visionary disclosure of the end ([[apocalyptic-literature]], [[antichrist|the eschatological adversary]]).

## Tradition by Tradition

### Zoroastrianism (the likely fountainhead)
The [[saoshyant|Saošyant]] (future saviour), the bodily resurrection, and the [[frashokereti|frašō.kərəti]] — the final "making wonderful" in which evil is destroyed. Often argued to have shaped the Abrahamic schemes.

### Judaism
The [[davidic-covenant|Davidic]] messiah and, at Qumran, the [[two-messiahs|two messiahs]] (priestly and royal); the [[messianic-apocalypse|Messianic Apocalypse]] and [[messianic-rule|Messianic Rule]]; the resurrection of the dead and the world-to-come (*olam ha-ba*).

### Christianity
[[jesus-of-nazareth|Jesus]] as the [[son-of-man|Son of Man]]/messiah whose second coming (Parousia) brings the [[last-judgment|judgment]]; the [[antichrist|Antichrist]]; the disputed [[millennialism|millennium]]; the [[bodily-resurrection|resurrection of the body]]; and [[origen|Origen's]] universalist [[apokatastasis|apokatastasis]] vs. [[eternal-punishment|eternal punishment]].

### Islam
The [[qaim|Mahdī (the Qāʼim)]] — especially central in [[shia-islam|Shīʻa]] expectation of the Hidden Imām — alongside the return of [[jesus-of-nazareth|ʻĪsā]], the resurrection, and the Day of Judgment.

### Baháʼí
The fulfilment of all prior messianic expectations in [[bahaullah|Baháʼuʼlláh]] as "[[he-whom-god-shall-make-manifest|Him Whom God shall make manifest]]"; eschatology reread through [[progressive-revelation|progressive revelation]] and [[bahai-millenarianism|Baháʼí millenarianism]].

### Buddhism
The future Buddha [Maitreya], who will appear when the Dharma has been forgotten to teach it anew — a cyclical rather than linear eschatology.
