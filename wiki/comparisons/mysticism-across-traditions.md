# Mysticism Across Traditions

A cross-tradition hub for [[mysticism|mysticism]] — the pursuit of direct, transformative union with (or absorption into) ultimate reality, and the contemplative disciplines and apophatic theologies that attend it. This page **gathers and links** the wiki's mystical material; it does not adjudicate whether the traditions describe the same experience. For the shared philosophical root of much of it, see [[neoplatonism|Neo-Platonism]].

> This is a thematic navigation hub. Bracketed plain names lack a page yet.

## Recurring Structures

- **Apophatic vs. cataphatic** — the way of negation ([[negative-theology]], [[apophatic-theology]]) vs. affirmation; the ineffability of the ultimate ([[the-ineffable]]).
- **Emanation and return** — reality as overflow from a source to which the soul ascends ([[emanation]]); a graded hierarchy of being.
- **Stages of ascent** — purification → illumination → union; the loss of subject–object duality.
- **Esoteric/exoteric layering** — an inner meaning reserved for adepts ([[zahir-batin]], [[gnosis]], [[pardes|the Sod level]]).

## Tradition by Tradition

### Christianity
The [[apophatic-theology|apophatic]] tradition of [[pseudo-dionysius|Pseudo-Dionysius]] ([[mystical-theology]], [[on-divine-names]], [[celestial-hierarchy]]); union as [[theosis-divine-nature|deification (theosis)]] and the [[beatific-vision|beatific vision]]; the tension of the [[contemplative-and-active-life|contemplative and active lives]]. Rooted in [[neoplatonism|Neo-Platonism]] via [[augustine|Augustine]].

### Judaism
[[kabbalah|Kabbalah]] — the [[sefirot|sefirot]] and the [[ein-sof|Ein Sof]] (the Infinite) of the [[zohar|Zohar]] ([[moses-de-leon|Moses de León]]); and the earlier chariot mysticism of [[merkabah|Merkabah]]/[[maaseh-merkabah]] and the [[heikhalot|Heikhalot]] ascent literature.

### Islam
[[sufism|Sufism]] — the path to *fanāʼ* (annihilation in God); [[ibn-arabi|Ibn ʻArabī]]'s metaphysics of [[wahdat-al-wujud|the unity of being]] and the [[al-insan-al-kamil|Perfect Human]] ([[fusus-al-hikam|Fuṣūṣ al-Ḥikam]]); the [[zahir-batin|exoteric/esoteric]] layering of meaning.

### Hindu traditions
The non-dual realization of [[atman|ātman]] as [[brahman|Brahman]] in [[advaita-vedanta|Advaita Vedānta]] ([[shankara|Śaṅkara]]); and the [[kashmir-shaivism|Kashmir Śaiva]] [[pratyabhijna|recognition (pratyabhijñā)]] of [[abhinavagupta|Abhinavagupta]]; [[moksha|liberation]] as the goal.

### Buddhism
Contemplative realization of [[sunyata|emptiness]] and the nature of mind: the [[mahamudra|Mahāmudrā]] and [[dzogchen|Dzogchen]]/[[nyingthig]] traditions of [[tibetan-buddhism|Tibetan Buddhism]], and the [[samatha-vipassana|calm-and-insight]] disciplines.

### Daoism & China
[[biospiritual-cultivation|Inner cultivation]] and union with the [[dao|Dào]] through [[wu-wei|non-action]]; inner-alchemical practice in [[daoism|religious Daoism]].

### Greco-Roman / Neoplatonic
[[plotinus|Plotinus]]'s ascent to mystical union (*henōsis*) with [[neoplatonism|the One]] — the philosophical template that shaped Christian, Jewish, and Islamic mysticism alike; the [[mystery-cults|mystery religions]] as the ritual analogue.
