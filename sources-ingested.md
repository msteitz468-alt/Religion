# Ingested Sources

A readable, deduplicated bibliography of every source ingested into this wiki, grouped by
tradition and source type. Multi-volume works and multi-pass ingests are collapsed to a
single entry. This is a human-facing summary; the authoritative, dated record is
[[log|log.md]], and each source has its own summary page (with coverage ledger) under
`wiki/scholarship/`, `wiki/texts/`, or `wiki/commentators/`.
